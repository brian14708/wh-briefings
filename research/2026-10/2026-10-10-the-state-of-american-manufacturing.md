The Council of Economic Advisers (CEA) evaluated President Trump’s
impact on the manufacturing sector— reviewing his second term to date.
Reviewing a wide range of evidence, the data demonstrates that the
manufacturing sector is seeing robust growth with the Trump
Administration’s pro-growth policies.

Manufacturing jobs are reshoring in America after the Biden
Administration shipped over 200,000 American jobs overseas in its last
two years, factory construction is soaring as President Trump’s tariff
policies incentivized companies to make their products here in the
USA.  
Made-in-America manufacturing positions the United States at the
forefront of critical industries for our national security, including
aerospace, aircraft equipment, natural gas and petroleum production, and
metals. Now the world’s largest energy exporter, the United States
continues to strengthen its leadership across the energy sector, while a
new renaissance in American steel production is reinforcing domestic
industrial capacity.

The manufacturing resurgence we are witnessing reinforces America’s
dominance as a global leader, but also has positive impacts on the labor
sector as we see more American jobs, increased wages, and company
expansions that stimulate Main Street local economies and communities
nationwide.

The CEA analysis outlines how the United States is pioneering the
technology frontier, reshoring production, and President Trump’s
pro-growth, pro-investment approach is contributing to a new American
Golden Age.
