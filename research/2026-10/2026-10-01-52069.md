[whitehouse.gov/socialism](https://whitehouse.gov/socialism)

Upon taking office, the Trump Administration immediately worked to
secure the border, unleash American energy, usher in a wave of new
investment, make life affordable for working families, and right-size
the federal government. 

These policies have driven a manufacturing boom, turned America into an
energy-producing powerhouse, and made the country more affordable for
hardworking Americans. There is, of course, more to be done, but we
finally have an economy built around rewarding the people who do the
actual building.  

The Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) have offered an alternative
vision, one relying on taking resources from workers and entrepreneurs
or taxing the income of future generations to finance massive spending
today. The Council of Economic Advisers took them at their word,
examined their policy proposals, and, in the chapters that follow,
scored how much their economic agenda would cost the country.
