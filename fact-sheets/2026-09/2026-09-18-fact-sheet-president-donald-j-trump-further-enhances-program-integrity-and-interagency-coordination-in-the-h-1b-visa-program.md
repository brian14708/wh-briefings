**PROTECTING AMERICAN JOBS: **Today, President Donald J. Trump signed an
Executive Order and a Proclamation to continue to protect American
national and economic security and American workers from abuses of the
H-1B visa program.

- The Order enhances program integrity in the H-1B visa program by
  improving interagency coordination and directing enhanced scrutiny of
  H-1B visa applications where there is a heightened risk that American
  workers would be laid off.
  - The Order directs the Secretary of State, the Secretary of Labor,
    and the Secretary of Homeland Security to consult with the Secretary
    of Commerce, the Secretary of Education, and the Administrator of
    the Small Business Administration in order to receive additional
    data for the administration of the H-1B program, such as data on
    wages, industrial conditions, and employment specialization.

  <!-- -->

  - The Order directs the Secretary of State, the Secretary of Labor,
    and the Secretary of Homeland Security to consider petitioner
    employers’ recent or planned layoffs of similarly situated U.S.
    workers when considering H-1B visa applications.
- The Proclamation renews the $100,000 fee requirement for certain alien
  H-1B visa applications first imposed on September 19, 2025 to curb
  abuses that displace U.S. workers and undermine national security.

**COMBATTING H-1B ABUSES: **President Trump’s 2025 Proclamation
effectively deterred abuses in the H-1B visa program, and further action
will ensure American workers are not replaced with lower-paid foreign
labor.  

- Since the 2025 Proclamation took effect, H-1B registrations filed by
  the largest IT outsourcing firms have been reduced by 92%.
  - Before the 2025 Proclamation, unemployment among recent computer
    science graduates reached 6.1% and 7.5% for computer engineering
    graduates – more than double the rates for biology or art history
    majors. 

  <!-- -->

  - The number of foreign STEM workers in the United States had more
    than doubled between 2000 and 2019, while overall STEM employment
    only increased 44.5% during that time.  The share of IT workers with
    H-1B visas had risen from 32% in FY 2003 to over 65% in recent years
    before the 2025 Proclamation.
- The Proclamation has also caused a nearly 97% decrease in consular
  processing requests and a shift from lower-paid foreign labor toward
  higher-skilled and higher-wage workers.
- More work must be done to improve interagency coordination in the
  assessment of H-1B visa petitions.
  - Abuse of the H-1B visa program decreases wages for skilled American
    workers because the availability of cheaper foreign labor places
    downward pressure on domestic pay.

  <!-- -->

  - Many employers have laid off large numbers of highly qualified,
    highly skilled American workers, only to promptly hire large numbers
    of H-1B workers who are often lower-skilled and lower-paid.  

  <!-- -->

  - Due to the outsourcing business model of certain H-1B sponsors, many
    jobs held by H-1B workers eventually leave the United States
    entirely.

**PUTTING AMERICAN WORKERS FIRST AND RESTORING THE IMMIGRATION
SYSTEM: **After years and years of failed policies that put foreigners
above America’s own workers, President Trump has delivered on his
promise to put American workers first and fix the immigration system.

- Since returning to office, President Trump’s economic policies have
  added over one million jobs to the U.S economy, including nearly
  100,000 factory construction jobs and 58,000 manufacturing jobs since
  January 2026.
- President Trump ignited a manufacturing renaissance, with the U.S.
  manufacturing sector expanding for eight straight months after
  perpetual contraction in the Biden Administration.
- In July 2025, President Trump signed the largest tax cuts for working
  Americans in history which delivered No Tax on Overtime and No Tax on
  Tips, safeguarded millions of American jobs and over half a trillion
  dollars in wages, and established the first-ever Workforce Pell Grants
  for Americans in skilled trades.
- Under President Trump, real wages increased by $822 in 2025 and real
  household income reached its highest level in history after falling by
  thousands of dollars under the Biden Administration. 
- The Trump Administration is cracking down on visa worker abuse and
  protecting American workers from being replaced with cheap foreign
  labor, including H-1B visa fraud investigations and investigations
  into other practices that illegally favor visa holders over Americans.
- In December 2025, the Department of Homeland Security finalized a rule
  replacing the H-1B randomized lottery with a weighted system based on
  wage level to protect American workers’ wages, working conditions, and
  job opportunities. Fiscal year 2027 is the first time that the H-1B
  lottery has been decided by wage, not chance, and registrations
  dropped by almost 40%.
- The Trump Administration strengthened visa programs and their
  screenings and paused visa processing for 75 high-risk countries to
  protect the homeland and deter overstays.
- Immediately upon taking office, President Trump secured the border and
  ended the Biden Administration’s record-shattering illegal immigration
  into the U.S. The Trump Administration has deported over 3 million
  illegal aliens and for 15 straight months, no illegal aliens have been
  released into the country.
