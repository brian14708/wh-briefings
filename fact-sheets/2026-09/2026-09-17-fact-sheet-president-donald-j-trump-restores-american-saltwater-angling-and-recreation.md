**MODERNIZING MANAGEMENT AND EXPANDING PUBLIC ACCESS:** Today, President
Donald J. Trump signed an Executive Order to modernize Federal fisheries
management, empower recreational industries, and eliminate unnecessary
restrictions on American boaters and anglers. 

- The Order reduces administrative burdens by directing the Secretary of
  Commerce, the Secretary of Agriculture, and the Secretary of the
  Interior to initiate actions to suspend, revise, or rescind
  regulations that overly burden boaters, anglers, and outdoor
  businesses. Relevant agencies will also seek to transition to
  multi-year permitting for compliant charter operators and outdoor
  businesses, with a standard presumption of renewal. 
- The Order modernizes fisheries science by directing the Secretary of
  Commerce to transition from mail-in surveys to mobile electronic catch
  reporting apps, improving data accuracy and preventing improper access
  restrictions on anglers; mandating a 5-year strategic plan to
  integrate recreational economic data into seasonal quota decisions;
  and calling for the development of a real-time depredation reporting
  protocol via mobile app. 
- The Order directs the Secretary of Commerce to launch pilot programs
  for depleted stocks, including Atlantic Striped Bass, and streamline
  applicable Trust Fund projects to directly boost fish populations. 
- The Order promotes healthy habitats by directing the Secretary of the
  Interior to create the Federal Outer Continental Shelf Reef-in-Place
  Program to use decommissioned oil and gas platforms to support
  artificial reefs; directing the Make America Beautiful Again (MABA)
  Commission to establish a Restoration Coordination Working Group to
  streamline habitat restoration efforts and ensure efficient use of
  Federal resources across agencies; and directing the Secretary of
  State, the Secretary of Commerce, and the Secretary of the Interior,
  along with the United States Trade Representative to remove barriers
  to sustainable shark fisheries. 

**ELEVATING RECREATIONAL FISHING IN NATIONAL POLICY: **President Trump
is committed to removing unnecessary restrictions on saltwater anglers,
offshore boaters, and charter operators in order to enhance domestic
outdoor recreation, support American jobs, and strengthen coastal
economies. 

- America’s outdoor recreation sector generates over $1.2 trillion
  annually and supports over 5 million jobs, with recreational fishing
  being a primary driver. 
- Federal fisheries management has historically prioritized restricting
  access over empowering affiliated industries, burdening charter
  businesses and limiting public access to marine resources.
- Prohibiting or restricting recreational access is unnecessary for
  proper management when State-level partnerships and modernized data
  collection can provide accurate, real-time information for responsible
  oversight.  
- This action sets a clear policy to elevate the importance of marine
  recreational fishing across Federal ocean, coastal, and public land
  decision-making.

**DELIVERING HISTORIC ACTION FOR AMERICAN FISHERIES: **President Trump
is fulfilling his commitment to revitalizing the Nation’s maritime
economy, reversing federal overreach, and prioritizing American fishing
interests. 

- On his first day back in office, President Trump signed a Presidential
  Memorandum pausing offshore wind energy projects to preserve a robust
  U.S. fishing industry. 
- In April 2025, February 2026, and June 2026, President Trump issued
  proclamations restoring fishing access in all Marine National
  Monuments. 
- In April 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order restoring
  seafood competitiveness through deregulation, enhancing domestic
  seafood production, and establishing the America First Seafood
  Strategy. 
- In July 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order establishing
  the MABA Commission, to conserve America’s lands and waters, cut red
  tape, and drive conservation and economic growth.
- Through the strategic use of the Magnuson-Stevens Act and executive
  action, NOAA has successfully deregulated fishing operations, reversed
  decades-old closures, and maximized harvest quotas. 
  - NOAA expedited the openings of fisheries and increased catch limits
    based on the best available science, delivering financial returns to
    coastal communities.

  <!-- -->

  - NOAA continues to aggressively slash red tape and replace outdated,
    micromanaged restrictions with adaptive, flexible standards –
    maximizing profitability for the domestic fleet and keeping
    fisheries open.
- Through his leadership, President Trump continues to fulfill his
  promises to undo burdensome regulations, empower State-Federal
  partnerships, and restore access to America’s coastal waters. 
- These actions have unlocked billions of dollars in economic value and
  strengthened the outdoor recreation economy.
