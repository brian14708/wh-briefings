**EXPANDING ACCESS AND CREATING OPPORTUNITY: **Today, President Donald
J. Trump signed an Executive Order to modernize data management and data
sharing between the Departments of War and Veterans Affairs in order to
expedite veterans’ access to benefits.  

- The Order directs the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Veterans
  Affairs to update information technology systems to ensure the sharing
  of all Official Military Personnel Files and Service Treatment Records
  from the moment an individual enters into military service through the
  duration the individual receives Veterans Affairs benefits.

<!-- -->

- The Order directs the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Veterans
  Affairs to create and deploy new digital tools, using secure
  artificial intelligence and other emerging capabilities consistent
  with applicable privacy protections, to streamline veterans’
  applications for and access to benefits earned in their military
  service in a single comprehensive source.
- The Order directs the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Veterans
  Affairs to coordinate with the Director of the Office of Management
  and Budget and the Secretary of Health and Human Services to ensure
  current and future information technology contracts facilitate
  electronic sharing of personnel and medical files consistent with
  applicable privacy protections.
- The Order directs the Secretary of War, the Secretary of Labor, and
  the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to overhaul the Transition
  Assistance to improve career guidance, including job opportunities and
  training opportunities, to service members leaving active-duty service
  and to proactively connect transitioning service members with a
  veterans specialist from federal, state, or local governments who can
  help them facilitate their benefit applications.

**REMOVING ANTIQUATED SYSTEM BARRIERS TO DELIVER RESULTS: **President
Trump is removing the inefficiencies and delays that hinder our service
members and veterans from receiving the benefits and support they
deserve.

- Millions of brave men and women have served in our Armed Forces with
  courage, commitment, and sacrifice, guaranteeing our security,
  prosperity, and freedom.  These veterans deserve the best available
  services in return. 
- Federal law requires the Department of Veterans Affairs to use
  Department of War records to determine benefit eligibility for
  veterans. 
- Due to outdated systems inherited from the prior administration, the
  transfer of records takes an average of 90 to 180 days to complete,
  complicating service members’ transition from active-duty service to
  civilian life, and delaying their healthcare coverage, benefits
  processing, educational opportunities, and job training and
  placement.  
- Modern technology for electronic records management presents an
  opportunity to rectify this problem.
- Proactive sharing of military personnel and medical files is
  particularly crucial for National Guard and Reserve component
  personnel who frequently move between active duty status and Veterans
  Affairs-eligibility status.
- The current Transition Assistance Program framework often leaves
  service members confused or without guidance on how to apply benefits
  or access employment opportunity programs.   

**AGGRESSIVELY DELIVERING LIFE-CHANGING SUPPORT AND
BENEFITS: **President Trump dramatically expanded veterans’ access to
healthcare, education, and job opportunities. 

- President Trump is overhauling the outdated records management and
  transfer process, bringing it into the 21<sup>st</sup>century using
  digital solutions to rapidly connect veterans to healthcare,
  disability benefits, education and training opportunities, and the
  workforce.
- In his first term, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  supporting veterans during their transition from uniformed service to
  civilian life. This crucial Executive Order provided veterans access
  to high-quality mental healthcare and suicide prevention resources as
  they transition, with an emphasis on the one-year period following
  separation.
- In April 2025, the Office of Management and Budget and the Assistant
  to the President for Science and Technology, issued two revised
  policies to facilitate responsible AI adoption to improve public
  services.
- In May 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order to increase
  options for care, benefits, and services for our veterans. This
  included reduced wait times for Veterans Health Administration
  appointments through options such as expanded hours, weekend
  appointments, and virtual healthcare.
- In November 2025, the Trump administration eliminated the entire
  backlog for the Civilian Health and Medical Program of the Department
  of Veterans Affairs (CHAMPVA) healthcare program after inheriting more
  than 70,000 unprocessed applications from the Biden administration.
- As of July 2026, the Trump administration dropped the backlog of
  veterans waiting for VA benefits to below 70,000 for the first time
  since 2020.
- In July 2026, President Trump announced Freedom Haulers, a Department
  of Transportation-led effort implementing a fast-track program for
  military service members to attain Commercial Driver’s License
  certification.
