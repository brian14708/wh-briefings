**ELIMINATING INVASIVE TICKS AND MOSQUITOES: **Today, President Donald
J. Trump signed an Executive Order to eliminate invasive ticks and
mosquitoes in the Federal jurisdiction of Washington, D.C. through safe
and effective, non-chemical, and technology-enabled methods, and invest
in pioneering approaches to eliminate these pests throughout the
country.

- The Order directs the Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of
  Agriculture, the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency,
  and the Administrator of General Services, along with the Secretary of
  Health and Human Services, to develop and implement a campaign across
  Washington, D.C. to reduce invasive mosquito populations by 90 percent
  and the tick population by 50 percent by 2028 using safe and
  effective, non-chemical, and innovative technology-enabled methods. 
  - The campaign should remediate tick and mosquito breeding grounds on
    lands subject to Federal jurisdiction throughout D.C. using
    commonsense and technical approaches.

  <!-- -->

  - The campaign will also prioritize the use of commercial capabilities
    or cooperative agreements to reduce the population of
    disease-carrying pests in D.C.
- The Order directs the Administrator of the Environmental Protection
  Agency, with the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of Health
  and Human Services, to submit a regulatory framework for the planned
  development and release of advanced, safe, and effective tick and
  mosquito control technologies.
- The Order directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services to build
  on the existing Lyme Disease Moonshot initiative through prize
  competition announcements and additional research and development
  programs to prevent and treat tick- and mosquito-borne diseases.
- The Order also directs the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of
  the Interior, the Secretary of Health and Human Services, and the
  Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency to issue
  additional prize competition announcements or direct research programs
  for the development of non-chemical means to reduce tick- and
  mosquito-borne diseases.
- The Order directs the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of
  Health and Human Services to explore options for labeling food and
  drug products that include significant amounts of alpha-galactose,
  similar to other allergy warnings.

SAFEGUARDING AMERICANS’ HEALTH AND DEMONSTRATING INNOVATIVE PEST
CONTROL: **President Trump is addressing the invasive** **tick and
mosquito populations that spread debilitating diseases across America. 

- Foreign, invasive mosquitoes are multiplying across the United States
  and have become the dominant species in some areas, including
  Washington, D.C.—spreading disease and diminishing quality of life.
  - The expanding tick populations in the country expose Americans, our
    pets, and our livestock to debilitating and expensive health
    risks.  
- The use of commonsense, non-chemical, technology-enabled measures can
  significantly reduce the negative effects of these pests, enhancing
  enjoyment of the great outdoors for all Americans.
- Alpha-gal syndrome, a serious allergy to red meat that can develop
  after a tick bite, is increasingly common in America due to the spread
  of ticks to new areas of the United States.  
- This new model, localized to Washington, D.C., can later be applied
  nationwide as targeted, innovative, non-chemical pest control for the
  whole nation.
- Americans should be able to enjoy the beauty and health of Our
  Nation’s Capital without the threat of widespread invasive ticks and
  mosquito populations. President Trump is Making D.C. Safe and
  Beautiful Again.

**MAKING AMERICA HEALTHY, SAFE, AND BEAUTIFUL:** President Trump is
committed to Making America Healthy Again and leading the world in Gold
Standard Science, with multiple initiatives emanating out of Washington,
D.C. 

- In February 2025, President Trump created the Make America Healthy
  Again Commission to tackle the rise of chronic illness.
- In March 2025, President Trump established the Make D.C. Safe and
  Beautiful Task Force to make Washington, D.C. what it should be—the
  pride of every American to whom it belongs. In August 2025, the
  President declared a crime emergency in Washington, D.C. 
  - Just over a year later, thousands of criminals have been removed
    from the streets, homeless encampments are cleared, parks are
    reclaimed, and crime has plummeted.

  <!-- -->

  - This model has been replicated across the country in cities such as
    Memphis, TN, and violent crime nationwide is down to historic lows
    not seen in decades.
- In July 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order establishing
  the Make America Beautiful Again (MABA) Commission to ensure that our
  Nation’s next 250 years are beautiful, successful, and secure. 
- President Trump signed multiple Executive Orders that improved the
  safety and security of biological research, ended dangerous
  gain-of-function research, restored Gold Standard Science as the
  cornerstone of Federal scientific research, and rebuilt public trust
  in science.
