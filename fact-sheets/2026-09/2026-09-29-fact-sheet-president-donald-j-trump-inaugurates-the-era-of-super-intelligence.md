**USHERING IN SUPER INTELLIGENCE:** Today, President Donald J. Trump
signed an Executive Order to replace the term “Artificial Intelligence”
(“AI”) with “Super Intelligence” (“SI”) in the executive branch to
recognize the continuously advancing technological frontier and the
limitless promise it offers the American people.

- The Order directs all departments and agencies to use the terms “Super
  Intelligence” and “SI” in official correspondence, public
  communications, policy documents, and non-statutory documents within
  the executive branch and to no longer acknowledge the terms
  “Artificial Intelligence” or “AI”.
- The Order directs the Assistant to the President for Science and
  Technology (APST) to propose a Federal definition of “Super
  Intelligence” and “SI” that reflects the state of this extraordinary
  frontier technology and identify any additional executive action
  necessary to implement the proposed definition.

**RECOGNIZING THE IMMENSE OPPORTUNITY OF SI**: The United States is the
world leader in Super Intelligence, and the Federal government should
use a name that reflects the full promise and opportunity of these
technologies.

- The United States is the birthplace of Artificial Intelligence and is
  pushing this frontier forward, having led the world in its development
  and deployment for over 70 years since its inception as a field of
  research.
- The name Super Intelligence conveys the true capabilities of the
  technologies being developed today, unlocking new forms of human
  creativity and empowering Americans to achieve what was previously
  impossible—not simply imitating or automating human intelligence as is
  implied by the term “Artificial Intelligence.”
- Using the name Super Intelligence will ensure departments and agencies
  remain focused on expanding the capabilities and applications of these
  technologies to empower Americans, strengthen our economy, and improve
  the lives of the American people.

**ADVANCING THE FRONTIER OF AMERICAN INNOVATION:** President Trump has
taken decisive action to ensure that the United States leads and wins in
Super Intelligence, and that all Americans benefit from Super
Intelligence and its vast economic benefits. 

- Immediately upon returning to office, President Trump eliminated the
  Biden Administration’s overreaching and harmful SI policies,
  unleashing a new era of innovation. 
- Under President Trump’s leadership, the Federal Government has
  announced more than $5 billion for the Genesis Mission, a national
  effort to harness Super Intelligence to accelerate breakthroughs in
  science, medicine, energy, and manufacturing.
- In July 2025, the White House released *America’s AI Action Plan*,
  identifying more than 90 Federal actions to win the SI race by
  accelerating SI innovation, building American SI infrastructure, and
  extending U.S. SI leadership around the world.
- In March 2026, the White House unveiled a commonsense, comprehensive
  National Super Intelligence Policy Framework with legislative
  recommendations to ensure that every American benefits from this
  technological revolution.
