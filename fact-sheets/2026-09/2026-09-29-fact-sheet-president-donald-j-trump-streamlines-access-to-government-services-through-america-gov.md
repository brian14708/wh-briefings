**REDESIGNING GOVERNMENT SERVICES FOR AMERICANS: **Today, President
Donald J. Trump launched America.gov, a single digital point of entry
for Americans to access Federal government information or services
online, and signed an Executive Order to ensure Federal agencies
integrate with America.gov.

- America.gov is a secure, intelligent, and service-oriented single
  point of entry through which an individual can sign in, communicate in
  plain language, receive accurate answers, and, where authorized and
  technically available, complete government transactions without having
  to navigate multiple Federal agencies’ websites.
  - America.gov implements modern technology and Super Intelligence (SI)
    to dramatically improve how the Federal government serves Americans,
    all while preserving personal privacy.

  <!-- -->

  - Right now, Americans can ask any question on America.gov and get an
    up-to-date answer. Later this year, Americans will be able to
    complete tasks like passport renewal and Medicare enrollment on
    America.gov, without being sent back into a maze of websites. More
    services will be roll out onto America.gov as agencies integrate
    with it. 
- The Order directs the Administrator of General Services, the
  Administrator of the National Design Studio, and the Director of the
  Office of Management and Budget to establish and operate America.gov
  as the single point of entry for Americans to access covered services
  online, and to integrate Login.gov with America.gov.
  - Covered services include public-facing Federal services that serve
    more than 100,000 users in a 12-month period and can be accessed or
    applied for online. Tax filing services provided by the Internal
    Revenue Service and national-security-sensitive services provided by
    the Department of War and the Intelligence Community are excluded.
- The Order directs the Director of the Office of Management and Budget
  to issue guidance to executive agencies to ensure they implement their
  covered services into America.gov. 

**MODERNIZING HOW GOVERNMENT WORKS FOR AMERICANS: **The Trump
Administration is making it easier and more accessible for you and your
family to find answers and get things done within the Federal
government.  

- For generations, Americans seeking essential services from the Federal
  government have spent hours of their time navigating systems organized
  around the Federal bureaucracy rather than the needs of the people.
  - Americans spend around 10 billion hours each year dealing with
    federal paperwork. The American people deserve better.
- Federal services should work the way you do, not against
  you.  America.gov gives power back to the people, returns Americans’
  time, and restores the respect and the dignity they deserve from their
  government. 
- American innovation and modern technology allow the Federal government
  to use simplicity, speed, accuracy, and security to help Americans
  complete essential interactions with the Federal government. 

**  
REFORMING GOVERNMENT TO WORK FOR YOU: **President Trump has consistently
prioritized innovation and efficiency, ensuring that Federal programs
are efficient, transparent, and focused on delivering real value to
taxpayers—making government smaller, smarter, and work for the American
people.

- President Trump has driven sweeping reforms to eliminate waste, reduce
  bureaucracy, and restore accountability across the Federal
  government. 
- Under the Trump Administration, the backlog of veterans waiting for
  benefits has dropped by 74%, demonstrating the Trump Administration’s
  dedication to making government efficient and responsive to those it
  serves.
- Vice President Vance’s Fraud Task Force has uncovered over $260
  billion in fraud since January 2025, including billions found in
  Medicare, and is holding criminals accountable for defrauding
  taxpayers.
- In August 2025, President Trump launched the “America by Design”
  initiative and established the National Design Studio to enhance the
  usability and aesthetics of Federal digital and physical services.
  - The National Design Studio has helped government agencies and
    programs, such as TrumpRx, Trump Accounts, and the Federal Employees
    Retirement System, modernize their websites to improve Americans’
    experiences with the Federal government.
- In February 2026, President Trump
  launched [TrumpRx.gov](https://trumprx.gov/), where American patients
  can access large discounts on many of the most popular and
  highest-priced medicines in the country, paying prices in line with
  the MFN price.  TrumpRx has saved American patients more than $700
  million on medicines, including massive discounts on popular
  medications used to treat obesity and infertility.
- In September 2026, President Trump announced the Working Families
  Obamacare Refunds, returning $500 to nearly 1 million Americans from
  Biden Administration Obamacare overcharges. This action lowers
  healthcare costs for Americans and gives the money to the people, not
  big insurance companies.
