**PROTECTING EXECUTIVE PRIVILEGES FROM CONGRESSIONAL OVERREACH TO SERVE
THE AMERICAN PEOPLE: **Today, President Donald J. Trump signed an
Executive Order* *to ensure Federal contractors and subcontractors
protect the Executive Branch’s interest and rights, and ensure
economical and efficient work.

- The Order directs all federal contractors and subcontractors to: 
  - Notify the White House and related contracting agencies of any
    pending and future congressional requests on contractors’ work or
    communications with the Executive Branch;

  <!-- -->

  - Coordinate with the White House and related contracting agencies on
    appropriate responses and relevant privileges; and

  <!-- -->

  - Abide by instructions from the White House or related contracting
    agencies on withholding privileged communications deemed
    privileged. 
- The Order directs Federal contractors to ensure that subcontractors
  working on federal contracts also comply with the Order.
- The Order directs all Executive Agencies to promptly inform all
  contractors and subcontractors of their obligations in this Order.

**PROTECTING CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND PROMOTING THE ECONOMY AND
EFFICIENCY: **President Trump is ensuring that Americans’ confidential
information and national and homeland security work is protected, and
that the Executive Branch is able to serve the American people
efficiently and economically.

- The Executive Branch must be able to work closely with its employees
  and contractors on sensitive matters without concern that confidential
  information may be released to unauthorized third parties, even by
  federal contractors from a congressional request.
  - If the Executive Branch cannot be assured that communications with
    contractors will remain confidential, federal agencies will be
    deterred from safely and freely sharing sensitive information with
    them, which will deadlock critical government programs.
- The Constitution’s Executive Branch privileges protect its information
  from disclosure. These privileges are vital to the Executive Branch’s
  ability to function and serve the American people. 
- Certain congressional leaders have threatened to intrude on these
  Executive prerogatives by seeking confidential Executive branch
  information from Executive agencies and harassing private companies
  that contract with the federal government.
  - Congress cannot seek information from Executive branch agencies,
    employees, or federal contractors without a valid legislative
    purpose that does not intrude on core Executive functions or force
    the disclosure of privileged information without meeting narrow
    exceptions.
- A lack of coordination between federal contractors and the Executive
  Branch substantially undermines the ability of contractors to provide
  economical and efficient work to the American people.
