**ENFORCING PROMPT CAPITAL SENTENCES: **Today, President Donald J. Trump
signed a Memorandum to pursue capital punishment for heinous federal
crimes and expedite the enforcement of capital sentences.

- The Memorandum directs the Attorney General to pursue Federal
  jurisdiction and seek the death penalty for particularly heinous and
  egregious Federal capital crimes including:
  - murder in the first degree of a minor;

  <!-- -->

  - murder committed in the course of rape, sexual abuse, sexual
    exploitation of a child, or sex trafficking; and

  <!-- -->

  - murder committed in the course of kidnapping or human trafficking. 
- This Memorandum expands the list of crimes covered by President
  Trump’s Day One Executive Order that directed the Attorney General to
  pursue Federal jurisdiction and seek the death penalty for certain
  federal capital crimes involving the murder of a law-enforcement
  officer and capital crime committed by an alien illegally present in
  this country.
- The Memorandum directs the Attorney General to ensure that federally
  imposed sentences of death are carried out as expeditiously as
  possible, including:
  - prioritizing the prompt resolution of direct appeals, petitions for
    writs of habeas corpus, and other post-conviction proceedings; 

  <!-- -->

  - opposing dilatory litigation tactics;

  <!-- -->

  - seeking expedited judicial consideration wherever appropriate; and

  <!-- -->

  - eliminating unnecessary administrative delays.

**ENSURING JUSTICE IS CARRIED OUT FOR HEINOUS CRIMES: **Far too often,
capital sentences are delayed for years, if not decades, stalling the
justice their victims’ families deserve.

- A prolonged delay in carrying out a death sentence is a grave offense
  to the justice system that denies a victim ‘s family the closure they
  deserve.  
- Capital sentences imposed upon the worst of the worst criminals are
  too often frustrated by years, or even decades, of litigation.  
- The United States Department of Justice must take every step to ensure
  that those who commit the most heinous crimes promptly face the full
  consequences of their actions.
