**ADDRESSING BEEF AFFORDABILITY WITH TARGETED ACTION: **Today, President
Donald J. Trump signed a Proclamation to temporarily increase the
quantity of lean beef trimmings that are imported with no above-quota
tariff to ensure affordable beef for American consumers in light of
current supply challenges. This action includes key safeguards to
maximize cost savings while protecting American ranchers from foreign
imports and giving space for American ranchers to grow the domestic
herd. 

- The Proclamation temporarily expands the existing quantity of lean
  beef trimmings that are subject to an in-quota tariff rate, to produce
  ground beef, allowing trading partners eligible to ship beef to the
  United States to take advantage of lower, in-quota tariff rates.
- This expanded quota has the following limitations:
  - It only applies for 90 days, beginning on September 1, 2026.

  <!-- -->

  - It only allows 100,000 tons per month. (USDA projects U.S. beef
    production in 2026 to be over 11 million tons.)

  <!-- -->

  - It only applies to lean beef trimmings to combine with U.S. beef for
    ground beef.

  <!-- -->

  - It encourages beef to be sold at a 25% discount from the going
    import price.
- The Proclamation does not modify any commitments related to beef for
  countries with a free trade agreement with the United States, and does
  not apply to countries with country-specific beef quotas.

**FURTHER RESPONDING TO REDUCED U.S. BEEF AVAILABILITY AND HIGHER
DOMESTIC DEMAND: **President Trump is taking decisive action to respond
to market disruptions and natural disasters in order to ensure adequate
supply and affordable prices of beef products for American families.

- The Biden administration put American agriculture last and delivered
  one of its most severe beef supply crises in decades, with domestic
  cattle herds at their lowest level in 75 years. Years of pursuing
  radical climate activists’ misguided policies and inciting inflation
  that left farmers with historically high input costs put enormous
  pressure on American agriculture and the ranchers who feed our
  country.
  - The Trump administration has been working around the clock to help
    rebuild the American beef industry – for the first time since 2018,
    the number of cattle in the U.S. is increasing and ranchers are
    retaining heifers at higher rates. 
- Since President Trump’s Proclamation in February addressing the
  elevated beef prices for U.S. consumers, including the elevated price
  of ground beef, many factors—including necessary restrictions on live
  animal imports from Mexico in order to mitigate the spread of the New
  World Screwworm, drought conditions across cattle-producing regions
  affecting grazing lands, and reduced feed and forage availability due
  to ongoing wildfire conditions—have put downward pressure on the size
  of the U.S. domestic herd. 
  - For example, the necessary closure of southern ports in response to
    the New World Screwworm to protect the American herd resulted in a
    loss of hundreds of thousands of metric tons of beef being produced
    in the United States.
- As a result of these and other factors, the U.S. Department of
  Agriculture (USDA) forecasts U.S. beef output will fall by around four
  percent from 2025 levels.
- The United States is the world’s largest consumer of beef by volume
  and ranks second globally in per capita beef consumption, underscoring
  beef’s importance to the American diet.
  - As Americans eat more beef than ever, it is necessary and
    appropriate to ensure that the supply of beef products will be
    sufficient to meet U.S. demand at affordable and reasonable prices. 
- The President is taking temporary action to strengthen the Nation’s
  supply of affordable beef for American consumers while these supply
  challenges persist.
  - This temporary action will increase beef supply by roughly 10% over
    current projections – an amount that will deliver economic relief to
    American consumers, will only compete with cull cow markets, and
    will not significantly impact the fed cattle market. 

  <!-- -->

  - This action will not make up for the entire quantity of beef that
    would have been in the U.S. marketplace without the necessary
    closure of the U.S. – Mexican border to delay the incursion of New
    World Screwworm. President Trump continues to support and uplift
    America’s ranchers as they rebuild from years of neglect from the
    Biden administration. 

**DELIVERING LOWER COSTS FOR AMERICAN FAMILIES WHILE SUPPORTING OUR
RANCHERS: **Since returning to office,** **President Trump has moved
swiftly to restore affordability, fight to reverse the Biden
Administration’s inflation crisis, and strengthen America’s farmers,
ranchers, and food supply. 

- Under four years of Biden, workers lost nearly $3,000 in purchasing
  power—meaning inflation rose faster than wages. Under President Trump,
  even after accounting for higher prices, real wages grew by $822 in
  2025, putting more money in the pockets of hard-working Americans,
  with more savings on the way from further dergulation.  
- From day one, President Trump began halting proposed Biden-era
  regulations, which have saved Americans over $211 billion, or $2,461
  per family of four.
- In July 2025, President Trump signed the largest tax cuts in American
  history, providing families with thousands more in their pockets each
  year.
  - The Working Families Tax Cuts various tax provisions, such as the
    now-permanent small-business deduction and death tax exemption, and
    the first increase in reference prices in over 10 years, make the
    ranching industry more attractive for investment, boosting
    profitability for ranchers now and into the future.
- Since taking office, the Trump administration began implementing a
  robust plan to protect the American herd from the threat of New World
  Screwworm. Certain southern ports remain closed to live animal imports
  from Mexico as the United States pursues a phased and monitored
  reopening to increase the beef supply with robust, overlapping
  safeguards. 
- In October 2025, the Trump Administration announced a suite of actions
  to strengthen the American beef industry, reinforcing and prioritizing
  the American rancher’s critical role in the national security of the
  United States.
- In December 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order to stop
  price fixing, anti-competitive behavior, and foreign influence that
  drives up grocery prices and threatens the security of America’s food
  supply.
- In February 2026, President Trump issued a proclamation to temporarily
  increase imports and reduce beef prices for American consumers.
- In June 2026, President Trump signed an Executive Order to accelerate
  American agriculture innovation, empowering farmers and ranchers and
  bolstering the strength and security of our food supply.
- Today’s action reflects The Trump administration’s continued focus on
  driving down costs for all Americans. 
- The Trump Administration has strengthened the farm safety net,
  expanded crop insurance, cut red tape, and reduced costs for farmers.
- President Trump is committed to reducing risk for cattle producers,
  supporting new ranchers across the country, and ensuring ranchers are
  prosperous now and into the future.
