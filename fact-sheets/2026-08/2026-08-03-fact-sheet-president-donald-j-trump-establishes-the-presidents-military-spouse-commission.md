**STRENGTHENING MILITARY READINESS: **Today, President Donald J. Trump
signed an Executive Order establishing the Military Spouse Commission to
strengthen military readiness by improving the quality of life for
military spouses and families.  

- The Order establishes the Commission to advise and assist the
  President on policies affecting military spouses and military
  families.
- The Military Spouse Commission will be chaired by the spouse of the
  Secretary of War. An Executive Director of the Commission will be
  appointed by the President. The Spouse of the Secretary of Homeland
  Security, and 20 other military spouses of the Service Secretaries,
  the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and each Service’s senior enlisted leader
  will serve as members of the Commission. 
- The Order directs the Commission to engage directly with military
  spouses, identify challenges affecting military families, and develop
  policy recommendations to address housing, employment, healthcare,
  education, child care, and deployment-related support. 
- The Order directs the Commission to provide annual recommendations to
  the President to improve the quality of life for military spouses
  while supporting the highest standard of military readiness. 

**PRIORITIZING MILITARY EXCELLENCE: **President Trump recognizes that
military spouses are essential to the strength, readiness, and retention
of our military.  

- Military spouses provide critical support that enables America’s
  service members to accomplish their mission and defend the
  Nation. Strong military families help build a stronger military, a
  more resilient fighting force, and a safer and more secure America.
- Frequent relocations, lengthy deployments, and the unique demands of
  military life create significant challenges for military spouses,
  including barriers to housing, employment, healthcare, education and
  child care, and deployment-related support.  
- By addressing these challenges, the Commission will help strengthen
  military families, improve retention, and ensure America’s armed
  forces remain ready to meet any challenge. 

**DELIVERING FOR MILITARY FAMILIES: **President Trump has rebuilt
America’s military and ensured that families who support our service
members receive the recognition and support they deserve. 

- During his first term, President Trump expanded employment
  opportunities for military spouses by improving occupational license
  portability, increasing remote and flexible work opportunities, and
  enhancing access to Federal employment.  
- In 2018, President Trump signed the Veterans and Transition Act, to
  allow military spouses to keep the same state of residency as their
  service member to reduce challenges associated with frequent military
  relocations.  
- In January 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order to expand
  education freedom for families, including giving military families
  greater flexibility to use Department of War education funds to send
  their children to the school that best meets their needs. 
- In January 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order to
  reinstate service members who were dismissed for refusing the COVID
  vaccine, with full back pay and benefits. To date, 345 service members
  who were unjustly discharged under the since-rescinded COVID-19
  vaccine mandate have been reinstated. 
- In December 2026, President Trump established the $1,176 Warrior
  Dividend, recognizing the extraordinary sacrifices of America’s
  service members while providing meaningful financial support to the
  families who serve alongside them.
