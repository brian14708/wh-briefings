**ENDING DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION PRACTICES: **In 21 months back
in office, President Donald J. Trump has removed wasteful and
unmeritocratic DEI programs from government, education, and the private
sector. President Trump promised it and delivered – DEI is dead.

- On day one, President
  Trump [rescinded](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/initial-rescissions-of-harmful-executive-orders-and-actions/) Orders
  from the previous administration that injected DEI into our
  institutions, replacing hard work, merit, and equality with a divisive
  and dangerous preferential hierarchy, and signed an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/ending-radical-and-wasteful-government-dei-programs-and-preferencing/) ending
  radical and wasteful DEI programs and preferencing in the Federal
  government.
- In January 2025, President Trump signed a Presidential
  Memorandum [terminating](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/01/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-ends-dei-madness-and-restores-excellence-and-safety-within-the-federal-aviation-administration/) a
  Biden Administration Federal Aviation Administration hiring policy
  that prioritized diversity, equity, and inclusion over safety and
  efficiency.
- In January 2025, President Trump signed an Executive
  Order [protecting](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/01/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-protects-civil-rights-and-merit-based-opportunity-by-ending-illegal-dei/) the
  civil rights of all Americans and expanding individual opportunity by
  terminating radical DEI preferencing in federal contracting and
  directing federal agencies to relentlessly combat private sector
  discrimination.
- In January 2025, President Trump signed an Executive
  Order [eliminating](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/01/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-restores-merit-and-lethality-to-americas-armed-forces/) race-
  and sex-based discrimination within the armed forces to restore merit
  and lethality to America’s fighting force.
- In February 2025, President
  Trump [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-removes-dei-from-the-foreign-service/) a [Presidential
  Memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/removing-discrimination-and-discriminatory-equity-ideology-from-the-foreign-service/) removing
  DEI from the Foreign Service. 
- In March 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  to [ensure](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-empowers-parents-states-and-communities-to-improve-education-outcomes/) that
  programs or activities receiving any remaining Department of Education
  funds will not advance DEI or gender ideology.
- In March 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  to [restore](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-restores-truth-and-sanity-to-american-history/) truth
  and sanity to American history, including the Smithsonian Institution.
- In April 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  to [overhaul](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-reforms-accreditation-to-strengthen-higher-education/) the
  higher education accreditation system, ensuring colleges and
  universities deliver high-quality, high-value education free from
  unlawful DEI discrimination and ideological overreach.
- In April 2025, President Trump signed an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/04/reinstating-common-sense-school-discipline-policies/) ensuring
  school discipline policies are based on objective behavior, not DEI. 
- In April 2025, President Trump signed an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/04/restoring-equality-of-opportunity-and-meritocracy/) eliminating
  the use of disparate-impact liability – the legal theory that
  differences in outcomes among races, sexes or similar groups indicate
  unlawful discrimination – in various contexts to ensure equal
  treatment under the law.
- In May 2025, President Trump signed an Executive
  Order [ending](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-ends-the-taxpayer-subsidization-of-biased-media/) the
  taxpayer subsidization of NPR and the PBS after NPR described its DEI
  practices as “inseparable” from its content.
- In July 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order to facilitate
  the rapid and efficient buildout of data center infrastructure
  and [revoke](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-accelerates-federal-permitting-of-data-center-infrastructure/) a
  Biden-era Executive Order that would have saddled SI data center
  development on Federal lands with pages of DEI and climate
  requirements.
- In July 2025, President Trump signed an Executive
  Order [protecting](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-prevents-woke-ai-in-the-federal-government/) Americans
  from biased SI outputs driven by ideologies like DEI at the cost of
  accuracy.
- In August 2025, President Trump signed an Executive
  Order [stopping](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-stops-wasteful-grantmaking/) unelected,
  unaccountable bureaucrats from wasting taxpayer dollars on frivolous
  DEI grants.
- In December 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  to [end](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-protects-american-investors-from-foreign-owned-and-politically-motivated-proxy-advisors/) the
  outsized influence of proxy advisors that prioritize radical political
  agendas, including DEI, over investor returns.
- In December 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order
  to [protect](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-ensures-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial-intelligence/) American
  SI innovation from an inconsistent and costly compliance regime,
  including State laws that force companies to embed DEI into their
  models.
- In March 2026, President
  Trump [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-addresses-dei-discrimination-by-federal-contractors/) an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/addressing-dei-discrimination-by-federal-contractors/) eliminating
  racially discriminatory “diversity, equity, and inclusion”  practices
  by Federal contractors and their subcontractors, ensuring merit-based
  and efficient contracting and employment.
