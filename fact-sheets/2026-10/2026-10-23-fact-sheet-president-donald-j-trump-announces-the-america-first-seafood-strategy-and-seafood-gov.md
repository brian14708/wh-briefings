**SECURING AMERICAN SEAFOOD DOMINANCE: **Today, President Donald J.
Trump’s Administration unveiled the historic [America First Seafood
Strategy](https://www.usda.gov/sites/default/files/documents/america-first-seafood-strategy.pdf)
and launched [Seafood.gov](https://seafood.gov), implementing a plan of
action for the future of America’s seafood industry.

- The Strategy recognizes U.S. fishermen as essential to America’s
  economic strength, food security, coastal prosperity, and national
  resilience. 
  - The Strategy prioritizes rebuilding infrastructure by reducing
    regulatory barriers to expansion and modernization, thereby
    encouraging investment in processing jobs such as those that provide
    the primary source of income to hundreds of rural Alaska
    communities.

  <!-- -->

  - The Strategy identifies how the Trump Administration is helping
    restore the profitability of U.S. fishermen, especially Maine
    lobster fisheries, and protecting them from unfair competition,
    including by strengthening import certification and compliance,
    engaging in regulatory reform, pursuing partnerships with industry,
    and seeking expanded market access for American seafood abroad. 
- The Trump Administration is launching a new website, Seafood.gov – a
  one-stop destination for U.S. fishermen and consumers to access clear
  information about federal seafood initiatives and the science- and
  health-based reasons to choose American seafood.
- In another step forward for U.S. fishermen and America’s seafood
  industry as a whole, beginning today, the Food and Drug Administration
  (FDA) is proposing a voluntary “U.S. Caught Seafood” label so American
  consumers and those around the world can clearly identify seafood that
  is caught and harvested by American fishermen. The label is available
  for download on Seafood.gov. 
- Additionally, today the President is making available more than $5
  billion in guaranteed loans through the United States Department of
  Agriculture (USDA) to expand American dominance in modernized meat and
  seafood processing.

**PUTTING AMERICA’S FISHERMEN FIRST: **President Trump is committed to
building the future of American seafood in the United States by ensuring
that American fishermen, American workers, and American industry can
thrive.

- For too long, Federal seafood policy focused on regulation and market
  contraction rather than production and growth. The America First
  Seafood Strategy reverses that approach.
- American fishermen operate under the highest standards in the world,
  while compliance costs climb and fishing grounds narrow due to
  burdensome overregulation from States and previous Administrations.
- In many waters outside the United States, illegal, unreported, and
  unregulated (IUU) fishing, mislabeled imports, and heavily subsidized
  foreign seafood produced under weak labor, environmental, and safety
  standards have undercut American businesses that play by the rules and
  deliver the best products.
- Permitting barriers to private investment in working waterfronts, cold
  storage, and shoreside facilities have led to significant loss in U.S.
  seafood processing capacity.

**DELIVERING HISTORIC WINS FOR AMERICA’S FISHERIES: **These actions
build on President Trump’s actions to revitalize the U.S. fishing
industry, delivering historic economic growth and regulatory relief and
prioritizing American seafood producers. These actions have unlocked
billions of dollars in economic value and secured vital domestic supply
chains.

- In April 2025, President Trump signed an Executive Order restoring
  American seafood competitiveness through deregulation, combating
  unfair trade practices, enhancing domestic seafood production and
  exports, cooperative research, and creation of the America First
  Seafood Strategy.
- In April 2025, February 2026, and June 2026, President Trump issued
  proclamations restoring commercial fishing access in all Marine
  National Monuments, restoring hundreds of thousands square miles in
  the Atlantic and Pacific oceans to America’s fishermen.
- In April 2026, President Trump’s USDA launched the Office of Seafood
  to help the American seafood industry navigate access to USDA programs
  and support.
- Through the strategic use of the Magnuson-Stevens Act and Executive
  action, NOAA has successfully removed red tape, reversed decades-old
  closures, and maximized harvest quotas.
