**PRIORITIZING SOLUTIONS FOR WOMEN’S HEALTH: **Today, President Donald
J. Trump signed an Executive Order, led by First Lady Melania Trump, to
strengthen research, prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and care for
women and girls throughout every stage of life.

- The Order establishes the White House Women’s Health Task Force to
  ensure the Federal Government pursues a comprehensive approach to
  women’s health that recognizes evolving health needs from infancy,
  childhood, and adolescence through pregnancy, midlife, menopause, and
  aging, and supports necessary policy and regulatory reforms to ensure
  timely and stage-specific prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and care
  for women.
- The Order directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services and the
  Director of the National Institutes of Health to establish a Women’s
  Health Division within the National Institutes of Health to strengthen
  the Federal Government’s ability to coordinate research, address gaps
  in scientific understanding, and accelerate the translation of
  discoveries into better health outcomes.
- The Order rescinds the initiatives of the prior Administration in
  order to broaden beyond research and prioritize translating research
  into improved clinical care that benefits women and girls.

**ENSURING AMERICAN WOMEN RECEIVE THE BEST SCIENTIFICALLY-SUPPORTED
MEDICAL ADVICE IN THE WORLD: **President Trump is prioritizing the
health and longevity of America’s women and girls and putting research
into action. American women deserve answers, and this Administration is
committed to improving women’s health outcomes.

- While previous administrations have invested in women’s health
  research, the Trump Administration is enhancing, streamlining, and
  elevating Federal efforts regarding both research and its translation
  into health solutions.
- Despite considerable advances in biomedical science, many diseases and
  conditions that disproportionately or uniquely affect women have not
  received the level of research, clinical attention, and treatment that
  other diseases and conditions have received.  
- More research is needed in critical aspects of women’s health,
  including cardiovascular and heart disease, cancer, stroke,
  Alzheimer’s Disease, autoimmune and inflammatory conditions,
  osteoporosis, mental health, chronic pain, endometriosis, fibroids,
  polyendocrine metabolic ovary syndrome (PMOS), infertility, maternal
  health, menopause, neurological disorders, aging, and more.  
- Improving health outcomes for women requires coordinated Federal
  leadership, bolstered scientific investment, and accelerated
  translation of research into clinical and community practice.  

**DELIVERING ON PROMISES FOR AMERICAN WOMEN: **President Trump and his
Administration are advancing women’s health to improve health outcomes
in all phases of life and ensuring they can access more affordable
healthcare by lowering costs and taking on special interest that raise
Americans’ healthcare costs.  

- In less than two years, President Trump has begun reshaping how
  America eats, moves, farms, and thinks about health to address the
  root causes of America’s escalating health crisis. Earlier this year,
  the Administration launched the new Dietary Guidelines for Americans
  with sections for pregnant and lactating women, marking the most
  significant reset of federal nutrition policy in decades.
- In his historic Working Families Tax Cuts Act, President Trump
  expanded access to health savings accounts for up to ten million
  people on Obamacare, took the most significant actions to reduce
  healthcare fraud and abuse in history, and deployed a record $50
  billion investment to improve rural healthcare.
  - 46 states have made investments in maternal health, including
    specific investments in telehealth and remote patient monitoring for
    maternal care, obstetric workforce development, and postpartum care.
- In November 2025, the Food and Drug Administration initiated the
  removal of broad and misleading “black box” warnings on Hormone
  Replacement Therapy products for menopause.
- The Trump Administration has reached 26 deals with pharmaceutical
  manufacturers, covering nearly 90% of the branded drug market, to get
  Americans the same low prices for prescription drugs that people in
  other countries pay. The Council of Economic Advisers estimates
  President Trump’s MFN deals will save Americans $600 billion
  prescription drugs over the next decade.
  - In February 2026, President Trump launched TrumpRx.gov, where
    patients can access large discounts in line with the MFN price on
    many of the most popular and highest-priced medicines in the
    country. Since its launch, patients have saved over $700 million on
    medicines through TrumpRx.gov.
  - TrumpRx.gov is delivering massive savings on some of the most
    commonly used fertility medications in the country to help families
    grow. Many patients struggling with infertility pay for their
    medicines out-of-pocket, but now they can save more than $2,000 per
    cycle of fertility drugs, on average.
- In May 2026, President Trump launched Moms.gov to support new and
  expecting mothers, bringing together information intended to help
  families navigate pregnancy and early parenthood.
