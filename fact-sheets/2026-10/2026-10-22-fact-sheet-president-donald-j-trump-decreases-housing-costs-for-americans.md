**LOWERING COSTS AND EXPANDING ACCESS TO HOMEOWNERSHIP: **Today,
President Donald J. Trump signed an Executive Order to lower the costs
of single-family homeownership and mortgages and promote innovation
throughout the housing market.

- The Order directs the Director of the Federal Housing Finance Agency
  (FHFA) to create a pilot program to cancel borrowers’ mortgage
  insurance premiums proactively once they have satisfied their equity
  requirements based on the current market value, which will eliminate a
  large monthly expense for qualifying Americans across the country.  
- The Order establishes the Task Force on Manufactured Housing
  Innovation, co-chaired by the Secretary of Housing and Urban
  Development and the FHFA Director, to increase the quality of and
  lower the cost of building a home by facilitating the use of modern
  manufacturing methods in the production of factory-built housing
  eligible for federal housing programs.
- The Order directs the FHFA Director to reduce homeowners’ refinancing
  costs by expanding the FHFA’s title insurance waiver pilot into a
  permanent program to allow more borrowers to utilize cost-friendly
  alternatives to title insurance and to reduce the closing costs of
  refinancing and monthly expenses associated with homeownership. 
- The Order directs the Secretary of Agriculture and Secretary of
  Veterans Affairs to reduce interest rates on single-family home loans
  made by their respective Departments.

**RESTORING THE AMERICAN DREAM OF HOMEOWNERSHIP: **President Trump is
removing unnecessary red tape and making homeownership more affordable
for all Americans.

- Homeownership is a vital pillar of our great Nation, and President
  Trump and his Administration are working tirelessly to cut burdensome
  costs and red tape to make the American dream of homeownership more
  affordable for all Americans. 
- Unnecessary regulations and outdated mortgage processes have made
  homeownership unaffordable and driven up closing costs. 

**  
DRAMATIC ACTION TO DECREASE HOUSING COSTS: **President Trump has
undertaken an aggressive agenda to tackle the housing and affordability
challenges facing American families and make the dream of homeownership
accessible again.

- In January 2026, President Trump signed an Executive Order preventing
  large institutional investors from buying single-family homes that
  could otherwise be purchased by families, and called on Congress to
  codify these policies.
- In January 2026, President Trump directed Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac
  to purchase $200 billion in mortgage-backed securities to further
  drive down borrowing costs.
- In March 2026, President Trump signed an Executive Order reducing
  regulatory burdens that drove up mortgage costs, limited access for
  creditworthy borrowers, and weakened community bank participation in
  lending.
- President Trump’s crackdown on H-1B visa abuse and illegal immigration
  has helped lower home prices and rental costs, respectively, for
  everyday Americans.
- The Trump Administration eliminated burdensome insurance requirements
  for federally backed mortgages, cutting costs and increasing the
  availability of low-cost insurance policies for consumers.
- The Working Families Tax Cuts doubled the investment limit for Fannie
  and Freddie’s Low Income Housing Tax Credit, boosting housing supply
  in rural and underserved communities. 
- President Trump continues to focus on ways to provide more relief to
  Americans from the economic and financial mismanagement of the Biden
  era and failed Democrat policies, so families can prosper and achieve
  the American Dream of owning a home.
