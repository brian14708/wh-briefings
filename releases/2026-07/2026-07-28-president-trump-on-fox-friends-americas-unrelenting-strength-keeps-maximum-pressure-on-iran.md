**This morning** [**on *Fox &
Friends***](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2082106417004093653)**,
President Donald J. Trump delivered a clear, uncompromising update on
U.S. operations against Iran.** President Trump detailed total naval
control of the Strait of Hormuz, unmatched U.S. intelligence and strike
readiness, and a firm strategy that keeps maximum pressure on the
Iranian regime while leaving the door open to a deal that serves
American interests.

- **President Trump underscored Iran’s bad-faith tactics in
  negotiations:** “For some reason, we can be in the midst of a
  beautiful discussion and they’ll come out and say, ‘We’re not talking’
  or ‘we didn’t discuss nuclear.’ Well, that’s all we discussed because
  they’ll never have a nuclear weapon. They understand that.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2082083997329322270?s=20))
- **President Trump confirmed U.S. intelligence and readiness to act on
  Pickaxe Mountain:** “Because of Space Force, we have the greatest
  cameras in the world focused — we know exactly what’s going on. But
  no, I heard Bibi announce that. I said, ‘Why don’t you just tell it to
  me? Why do you have to announce it to the world?’ I know exactly
  what’s going on at Pickaxe. It’s not a big problem. We took out their
  nuclear sites and we’ll have to take out Pickaxe if we don’t make a
  deal. If we don’t make a deal, we’ll take it out very easily.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2082084919958733226?s=20))
- **President Trump addressed different perspectives while affirming his
  clear strategy:** “You have a lot of competing factions. You have a
  group of people that want it to be obliterated in the truest sense of
  the word, then you have a group that says, ‘This is actually a good
  time to make a deal for them. If they don’t make a deal, then I go
  back and I finish the job’ — but that’s going to take them forever to
  rebuild. Already, it’ll take them many years to ever to rebuild.”
  ([Watch](https://youtu.be/ljWT1iqbobo?si=aJPd_KoondmV093l&t=370))
- **President Trump detailed America’s overwhelming capacity to
  dismantle Iranian infrastructure if necessary:** “I could take out
  most of their bridges in less than an hour — less than an hour — but
  it takes them ten years to build a bridge. Bridges take the longest
  and power plants are second. I can knock out the power plants; within
  one day, all of their power plants would be gone… I think about 91
  million people without power, without bridges — they have to live —
  and it’s a very, very delicate balance. So, I think we have a very
  strong position right now. They know I’m going to do that if they
  don’t make a deal… If I can avoid doing that, I’d like to do that.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2082086102014263513?s=20))
- **President Trump contrasted decades of prior inaction with his
  decisive leadership:** “For 50 years, other Presidents should have
  done this job and they never did anything. Obama actually paid them
  billions of dollars — the opposite! He thought he could bribe his way
  into friendship and they went out and tried to develop nuclear
  missiles and nuclear weapons from it… For 50 years, other Presidents
  should have done this job. It would have been a lot easier.”
  ([Watch](https://youtu.be/ljWT1iqbobo?si=DJfwyfXtjAS9dvzf&t=505))
- **President Trump asserted total U.S. control of the Strait of
  Hormuz:** “We have a blockade up there right now, and only ships that
  we want to get through get through. Iran doesn’t control the Strait.
  We control the Strait. Now, they can drop a couple of mines in there
  and screw things up, but we control the Strait. Our Navy is
  incredible; not one ship has gotten to Iran during any time where we
  had the blockade.”
  ([Watch](https://youtu.be/ljWT1iqbobo?si=Cp0JFjUOeiNvbY03&t=610))
- **President Trump emphasized the blockade’s effectiveness and zero
  tolerance for broken deals:** “We have a blockade that’s so powerful
  that nobody can get through. They tried, three or four of them tried
  over the last week. But we opened the blockade because we had the
  confines of a deal… then we put it back because they broke the deal.
  They break the deal. We cannot have them break deals anymore.”
  ([Watch](https://youtu.be/ljWT1iqbobo?si=oBsPcBHgDN3FUXob&t=655))
