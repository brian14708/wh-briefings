**President Donald J. Trump convened his Cabinet today for the first
live, televised Cabinet meeting at Camp David in American history.** The
session showcased the Trump Administration’s rapid progress in restoring
American strength, security, prosperity, and common sense after years of
Democrat-driven decline.

**President Trump opened the meeting by highlighting some of his
Administration’s signature achievements for American families.**

- “When we took office, we inherited a total catastrophe. The last
  Administration created the worst inflation in 48 years, the most
  dangerous and insecure border in history, and exploding trade deficits
  like we have never seen before — rampant crime, men in women’s sports,
  transgender mutilization of your children, and chaos and bedlam all
  over the world… In less than 18 months, we’ve turned around four years
  of disaster and we now have the [strongest
  border](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/07/16/trump-administration-delivers-14-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  in American history… The murder rate saw the single largest drop ever
  recorded, to the
  [lowest](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/crime-plummets-another-historic-low-under-president-trump/)
  in 125 years.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083216418955202796))
- “On July 4th, we launched Trump Accounts, a key provision of our Great
  Big Beautiful Bill. These tax-free investment savings accounts have
  already been activated by [over seven million
  children](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/bessent-trump-account-signups-reach-7-million.html).
  These are investments made in our future.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083217841050808498))
- “With my [Most Favored
  Nation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/research/2026/05/savings-from-most-favored-nation-drug-pricing-policy/)
  policy… we’re achieving Record discounts on prescription drugs. The
  prescription drugs were going through the roof for many years and now
  we’re getting record discounts, with price discounts of 300%, 400%,
  500%, 600%.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083218537221329373))
- “We’re also making an unprecedented investment in the United States
  Armed Forces… \[We’re\] looking forward to getting $1.5 trillion next
  year… and the good news is all of that equipment is made in the USA.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083219215822082137?s=20))

**Vice President JD Vance**
[**reported**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083221163803607501?s=20)
**on the major progress in the Administration’s aggressive campaign
against fraud.**

- “Just since the beginning of the Fraud Task Force that I started under
  the President’s leadership and direction, we have identified $230
  billion of fraud that’s being perpetrated against the American people
  — and we have halted already $56 billion of that.”
- “Just today, Mr. President, we will announce 17 new anti-fraud actions
  that will total about a third of a billion dollars and that’s added to
  the $230 billion that we’ve already uncovered.”

**Secretary of State Marco Rubio**
[**outlined**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083223470381420713)
**decisive steps to restore American leadership and sovereignty
abroad.**

- “The International Criminal Court is an illegitimate international
  organization. They’ve made themselves illegitimate because they claim
  that even if you’re not a member of that court, they can come after
  you. What that literally means in the future is that American
  servicemen, political leaders and others could find themselves under
  indictment by this International Criminal Court… We’ve now begun an
  [effort](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/07/state-department-launches-campaign-to-dismantle-international-criminal-courts-threat-to-american-sovereignty)
  to really try to bring that court to heel.”
- “60 countries came to Washington and were part of an
  [effort](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/trump-administration-unleashes-global-campaign-to-crush-radical-left-terrorism/)
  where we’re shedding light on far-left terror and violence.”
- “The deal with Lebanon and Israel is unprecedented… This is the [first
  time](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/14/nx-s1-5783933/lebanon-and-israel-hold-first-direct-talks-in-decades-as-israeli-invasion-digs-in)
  the Israelis and the Lebanese government have spoken at the official
  level in close to 30 years.”
- “As you announced last night, Mr. President, this disarmament
  [deal](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117011270507732212)
  that you brought about \[on Hamas\] is another big step, as well. It’s
  something a lot of people thought would never happen.”
- “For the first time in 15-20 years, the overwhelming majority of the
  countries in the Western Hemisphere are now led by pro-American
  leaders and pro-American governments. It’s an extraordinary
  achievement.”

**Secretary of War Pete Hegseth**
[**described**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083224426489778353)
**how the Department of War is rebuilding a lethal, merit-based
military.**

- “When you rip out DEI, when you rip out social engineering and social
  justice and you put in merit, and then you untie the hands of
  commanders to enforce discipline and accountability, to get back to
  basics, then you get historic reenlistment inside our ranks. Our best
  people, Mr. President, are staying because they want to be in that
  kind of Department. And then historic
  [recruitment](https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4365687/fy25-sees-best-recruiting-numbers-in-15-years/)
  record among young Americans. Every month, our department
  [breaks](https://www.livenowfox.com/news/military-recruiting-surge-hegesth-army-navy-air-force)
  records.”
- “We are committed in working with Congress to get [$1.5
  trillion](https://x.com/SecWar/status/2052396775797891417?lang=en).
  But this is not the same way we’ve funded our department in the past.”
- “So far, $75 billion in private
  [investment](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4411124/department-of-war-announces-groundbreaking-of-new-munitions-campus-in-indiana/)
  from defense companies who are now investing in new plants, new
  equipment, new assembly lines to build the munitions of the future
  faster than ever possible. That’s saving taxpayer dollars.”

**Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent**
[**touted**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083225306773516608)
**the successes of President Trump’s economic agenda in delivering real
gains for Americans.**

- “The [Working Families Tax Cuts](https://www.whitehouse.gov/obbb/) —
  which, I will add, every Democrat voted against, every Democrat voted
  against — it is the perfect barbell for this economy. On one side, you
  have full expensing for factories, for equipment, for farm structures.
  On the other side, your four signature policies — promises made,
  promises kept — No Tax on Tips, No Tax on Overtime, No Tax on Social
  Security, deductibility of auto loans if you buy an American-made
  car.”
- “Core inflation is low. Consumer confidence is strong. We have the
  [highest](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/us-consumer-sentiment-rises-to-five-month-high-on-broad-pickup)
  consumer confidence — it came out today — in five months.”
- “These Trump Accounts are the most successful program launch in
  government history. We’ve seen [seven million
  families](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/bessent-trump-account-signups-reach-7-million.html)
  sign up for them; we’re going to get to open enrollment and we will
  have 70 million families who have signed up for this.”
- “We had the
  [biggest](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0441) tax
  refund season ever.”
- “America is ushering in the greatest reshoring wave in our history. In
  June, our manufacturing sector
  [expanded](https://www.industryweek.com/the-economy/news/55388128/june-ism-report-manufacturing-activity-expands-for-sixth-straight-month)
  for the sixth straight month. We’ve got construction jobs and those
  are going to weave in to manufacturing jobs.”
- “Real wages are up $1,000… the bottom 25% of wage earners, just like
  in your first term, are
  [outperforming](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/paycheck-boost-gives-low-income-workers-a-breather-bfbd0313)
  the top 25%… In the past year, that group had wage increases of
  [5.5%](https://www.wsj.com/economy/jobs/paycheck-boost-gives-low-income-workers-a-breather-bfbd0313#:~:text=New%20federal%20data,the%20same%20period.)
  — three times what the top end has done.”

**Secretary of Health and Human Services Robert F. Kennedy, Jr.,**
[**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2083225928663003320)
**that TrumpRx is already delivering huge savings and paving the way to
the world’s lowest drug prices.**

- “By the time you leave office, assuming that’s in 2028, 95% of the
  drugs that are available to Americans — they will be paying the lowest
  prices in the world.”
- “TrumpRx, just in the short time since we’ve put it out, has saved
  Americans [$700
  million](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/americans-save-more-than-700m-prescription-medications-through-trumprx-white-house-says).
  We now have [800
  drugs](https://www.livenowfox.com/news/trumprx-list-expanded-again-tops-800-drugs-president-says)
  on there. We have
  [deals](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-expansion-of-trumprx-gov-to-bring-americans-transparency-and-choice-on-everyday-medicines/)
  with Amazon, with GoodRx. We are adding more and more drugs every
  day.”
