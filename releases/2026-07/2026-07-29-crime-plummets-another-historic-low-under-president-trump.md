title: Crime Plummets to Another Historic Low Under President Trump
---
 
**Under President Donald J. Trump, violent crime is collapsing at a pace
unmatched in modern American history.** A [new mid-year
report](https://counciloncj.org/homicide-likely-reaching-a-new-historic-low-as-most-crime-rates-continue-to-decline-at-mid-year/)
confirms that homicides are again on track to reach the lowest level in
more than a century — building on last year’s
[extraordinary](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/01/icymi-murder-rate-plunges-to-125-year-low-as-president-trump-restores-law-and-order/)
[progress](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/02/president-trump-returned-our-nation-to-law-and-order/)
and delivering on one of President Trump’s [core
promises](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/11/promises-made-promises-kept-one-year-later/#:~:text=Protecting%20public%20safety)
to restore law and order.

**Americans see the results every day: safer neighborhoods, fewer
victims, and the decisive end of the crime surge that scarred the last
decade.** This is not accidental; it is the direct result of the Trump
Administration’s relentless efforts to enforce the rule of law, back the
police, and surge resources into communities long abandoned by
Democrats.

**In the first half of 2026 vs. the same period last year:**

- Homicides are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  18% — on pace for the lowest level in at least 126 years.
- Carjackings are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  47%.
- Motor vehicle thefts are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  20%.
- Robberies are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  17%.
- Residential burglaries are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  13%.

**Compared to the peaks of the past nine years:**

- Homicides are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  51%.
- Carjackings are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  73%.
- Residential burglaries are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  56%.
- Motor vehicle thefts are
  [down](https://counciloncj.org/crime-trends-in-u-s-cities-mid-year-2026-update/)
  51%.

****The steepest homicide drops have come in cities where the Trump
Administration stepped in after Democrat leaders failed to act:****

- In Memphis, murders are
  [down](https://jasher.substack.com/p/murder-in-the-us-fell-dramatically)
  more than 43% so far this year — after the city
  [recorded](https://www.memphispolice.org/news/memphis-sees-significant-crime-reduction-in-2025-focus-shifts-to-sustaining-progress/)
  fewer than 200 murders last year for the first time since 2019.
- In Washington, D.C., murders are
  [down](https://jasher.substack.com/p/murder-in-the-us-fell-dramatically)
  more than 42% so far this year — following a 60%
  [plunge](https://nypost.com/2026/01/06/us-news/jeanine-pirro-touts-60-drop-in-homicides-in-dc-since-trumps-crime-crackdown/)
  in murders last year.
- In New Orleans, murders are
  [down](https://jasher.substack.com/p/murder-in-the-us-fell-dramatically)
  more than 32% so far this year — after the city’s homicide rate
  [fell](https://orleansda.com/new-orleans-homicide-rate-at-lowest-level-in-50-years-da-williams-and-partners-outline-2026-strategy-to-sustain-crime-reductions/)
  last year to its lowest in nearly 50 years.
- In Los Angeles, murders are
  [down](https://jasher.substack.com/p/murder-in-the-us-fell-dramatically)
  more than 18% so far this year — after the city
  [saw](https://lacity.gov/news/los-angeles-police-departments-2025-crime-data-report-released)
  a nearly 20% drop last year.
