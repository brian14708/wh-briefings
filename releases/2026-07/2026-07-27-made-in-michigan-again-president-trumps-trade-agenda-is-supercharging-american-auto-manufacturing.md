**President Donald J. Trump returns to Michigan today to visit the
General Motors Proving Ground in Milford and celebrate the roaring
comeback of American auto manufacturing under his leadership.** Since
taking office, President Trump has ignited an automotive renaissance —
onshoring production, restoring factory jobs, and making vehicles in
Michigan again.

### **Record Investments. Record Sales. American Jobs.**

Michigan is locking in tens of billions in new capital and thousands of
high-paying jobs as companies respond to President Trump’s America First
policies:

- General Motors is
  [investing](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2026/apr/0429-general-motors-us-manufacturing-investments-6-billion.html)
  $6 billion in U.S. manufacturing — including a major
  [expansion](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2025/jun/0611-plants.html)
  at its Orion Township assembly plant — with
  [plans](https://investor.gm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/q2-2026-letter-shareholders)
  to continue onshoring significant production. GM is also
  [adding](https://www.freep.com/story/money/cars/general-motors/2026/03/30/gm-overtime-shift-flint-assembly-pickup/89384715007/)
  an overtime shift at its Flint plant amid a surge in pickup truck
  demand.
- Ford Motor Company is
  [investing](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2025/ford-affordable-electric-vehicle-platform-midsize-electric-truck)
  $3 billion in its BlueOval Battery Park in Marshall, creating 1,700
  new jobs. The company is also
  [adding](https://patch.com/michigan/dearborn/ford-adding-hundreds-jobs-dearborn-truck-plant)
  a third shift at its Dearborn Truck Plant as the F-150 continues to
  [dominate](https://www.focus2move.com/world-best-selling-pick-up/)
  global pickup truck sales.
- Stellantis is
  [investing](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-388-million-investment-metro-detroit-hub/)
  $388 million to build a state-of-the-art “megahub” in Van Buren
  Township, $140 million to
  [expand](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-announces-13-billion-investment-in-four-states/#:~:text=Stellantis%20also%20plans%20to%20invest%20%24130%20million%20in%20the%20Detroit%20Assembly%20Complex%20to%20produce%20the%20next%2Dgeneration%20Dodge%20Durango%20beginning%20in%202029.)
  its Detroit plant, and $100 million to [add
  production](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-announces-13-billion-investment-in-four-states/#:~:text=In%20Michigan%2C%20the,retool%20the%20plant.)
  at its Warren plant.
- Detroit Diesel is
  [recalling](https://uaw.org/after-uaw-tariff-push-detroit-diesel-to-add-third-shift-hire-more-and-recall-laid-off-workers/)
  laid-off workers and adding a third shift.

**The results are already clear on the showroom floor.** 2025 marked the
[strongest
year](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cox-automotive-forecast-despite-q4-slowdown-new-vehicle-sales-expected-to-hit-16-3-million-in-2025--up-nearly-2-from-2024-best-result-since-2019--302645028.html)
for [new vehicle
sales](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/us-auto-sales-defy-regulatory-uncertainty-rise-2-2025-2026-01-05/)
since 2019. Ford
[posted](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/06/ford-f-2025-annual-sales.html)
its best annual U.S. sales in six years. General Motors
[delivered](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/05/gm-posts-5point5percent-us-sales-gain-in-2025-while-jeep-ends-annual-declines.html)
soaring overall volume and its
[strongest](https://investor.gm.com/static-files/bd576095-7337-4596-bfd2-cb7b3b5b336a)
SUV performance in decades. Jeep sales
[rose](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/05/gm-posts-5point5percent-us-sales-gain-in-2025-while-jeep-ends-annual-declines.html)
for the first time since 2018.

### **America First Tariffs Bring Production — and Paychecks — Home**

The boom stems directly from President Trump decisive tariffs that
reward American workers and production:

- President Trump
  [imposed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/03/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-adjusts-imports-of-automobiles-and-automobile-parts-into-the-united-states/)
  a 25% tariff on passenger vehicles and light trucks built outside the
  U.S.
- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-incentivizes-domestic-automobile-production/)
  an order rewarding domestic assembly by reducing the impact of parts
  tariffs on U.S.-built vehicles.
- President Trump
  [extended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-addresses-the-threat-to-national-security-from-imports-of-medium-and-heavy-duty-vehicles-parts-and-buses/)
  a 25% tariff to medium- and heavy-duty trucks manufactured abroad.

### **A Broader Manufacturing Boom Across Michigan**

Michigan is winning massive investments in advanced manufacturing,
energy, aerospace, and technology:

- OpenAI, Oracle, and Related Digital are
  [investing](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-environment-watch/dte-consumers-advance-plans-for-power-hungry-data-centers-in-michigan/)
  more than $7 billion in a major data center campus in Saline Township.
- Pratt Industries
  [pledged](https://www.prattindustries.com/latest-news/growth/anthony-pratt-5-billion-investment/)
  $5 billion to reindustrialize America, with thousands of jobs headed
  to Michigan workers.
- Corning is
  [expanding](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/corning-1-point-5-billion-solar-wafer-plant-expansion-richland-township-michigan/746900/)
  with a $1.5 billion investment, adding more than 400 advanced
  manufacturing jobs.
- Chobani is
  [investing](https://www.chobani.com/newsroom/our-news/chobani-strengthens-commitment-to-norton-shores)
  $567 million to expand its La Colombe plant, creating hundreds of new
  jobs.
- GE Aerospace is
  [investing](https://wwmt.com/news/local/ge-aerospace-invests-60m-in-west-michigan-with-10m-for-grand-rapids-avionics-muskegon)
  $60 million to boost aircraft engine and avionics production at its
  Michigan facilities.
- Saginaw Control & Engineering is
  [investing](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55370952/saginaw-control-engineering-to-spend-50-million-on-michigan-expansion)
  $50 million to expand in Thomas Township.
- Adrian Steel is [investing](https://www.adriansteel.com/news/) $43.4
  million to significantly grow its operations.
- American Rheinmetall is
  [investing](https://www.rheinmetall.com/en/media/news-watch/news/2025/09/2025-09-11-american-rheinmetall-invests-$31-7-million-in-modernising-the-group-s-site-in-michigan)
  $31.7 million in its Michigan facilities.
