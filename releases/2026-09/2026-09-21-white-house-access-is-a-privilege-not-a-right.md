**Last week, President Donald J. Trump**
[**removed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2101025120399626488)
**CNN, MS NOW, and Politico from the White House grounds after**
[**years**](https://www.newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/curtis-houck/2019/01/21/weekend-cnn-msnbc-spend-over-53-minutes-trying-smear-covington)
[**of**](https://www.newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/geoffrey-dickens/2019/08/12/cant-stop-wont-stop-unhinged-msnbcers-keep-comparing-trump)
[**false**](https://newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/bill-dagostino/2025/08/12/cnn-and-msnbc-have-cited-falsified-statistics-73-times-over-last)
[**reporting**](https://www.newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/brad-wilmouth/2025/10/12/msnbc-spreads-fake-news-ice-zip-tied-children-fox-debunks)**.**
The First Amendment protects their right to publish; it does not entitle
them to a hard pass, briefing room seat, or place in the press pool.

Access changes are nothing new. What is new, however, is the sudden
claim that it becomes sacred only when the outlets are friendly to the
Radical Left. The rule was never “every outlet gets in.” It was always
“our side stays in.”

## **For years, Democrat Administrations have restricted, punished, and locked out the press.**

**2009:** The Obama White House
[branded](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/president-obamas-feud-with-fox-news/#:~:text=%22FOX%20News%20often%20operates%20almost%20as%20either%20the%20research%20arm%20or%20the%20communications%20arm%20of%20the%20Republican%20party%2C%22%20said%20Anita%20Dunn%2C%20White%20House%20communications%20director.)
Fox News as the “research arm” and “communications arm” of the
Republican Party,
[barred](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/president-obamas-feud-with-fox-news/#:~:text=And%20the%20White,from%20the%20press.)
it from a round of exclusive Sunday show interviews,
[instructed](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/sep/20/republicans-back-trumps-ban-liberal-news-outlets/?srsltid=AU7gw4WwQY1I5lRou1KcOWHI0_IoZb7dSPwWyCcvUBrqIP20mlWsbmIw)
agencies to “skip” including it in pooled coverage,
[derided](https://x.com/WesternLensman/status/2101764070500426219) it as
“really not news,” and
[undertook](https://www.nytimes.com/2009/10/12/business/media/12fox.html)
an all-out effort to isolate the organization. Barack Hussein Obama now
[calls](https://www.newsweek.com/videos/obama-reacts-to-trump-white-house-media-ban-its-unimaginable)
a Fox ban “unimaginable,” but his White House [tried
it](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/obama-glosses-over-his-own-attempt-to-block-media-from-white-house-as-he-slams-trump/)
— and his spokesman admitted they were willing to do it.

**2010:** The Obama Justice Department
[labeled](https://www.nytimes.com/2013/05/21/us/politics/white-house-defends-tracking-fox-reporter.html)
Fox News correspondent James Rosen a possible “co-conspirator” in a
criminal leak case, secretly
[monitored](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fox-news-reporter-secretly-monitored-by-obama-administration-court-documents/)
his emails and phone calls, and tracked his movements — an
“unprecedented” level of surveillance and intimidation of a journalist
over his reporting.

**2011:** Obama White House officials
[restricted](https://www.mediaite.com/online/white-house-refuses-event-access-to-boston-herald-calls-out-mitt-romneys-front-page-op-ed/)
the *Boston Herald* from coverage —
[writing](https://www.mediaite.com/online/white-house-refuses-event-access-to-boston-herald-calls-out-mitt-romneys-front-page-op-ed/#:~:text=I%20tend%20to%20consider%20the%20degree%20to%20which%20papers%20have%20demonstrated%20to%20covering%20the%20White%20House%20regularly%20and%20fairly%20in%20determining%20local%20pool%20reporters.)
that pool slots depended on covering Obama “fairly” — after the paper
ran a front page Mitt Romney op-ed.

**2011:** After a *San Francisco Chronicle* reporter recorded protesters
interrupting an Obama fundraiser, White House officials
[threatened](https://www.politico.com/story/2011/04/wh-sf-chronicle-spar-over-video-053960#:~:text=But%20Chronicle%20reporter,its%20transparency%20mantra.)
to strip the outlet from access and warned of retaliation if the paper
reported on it.

**2012:** The Obama Justice Department secretly
[seized](https://www.ap.org/media-center/ap-in-the-news/2013/govt-obtains-wide-ap-phone-records-in-probe/)
two months of telephone records covering more than 20 Associated Press
lines used by more than a dozen journalists — targeting them over their
reporting.

**2023:** The Biden White House
[blocked](https://nypost.com/2023/05/08/why-are-white-house-staff-afraid-to-let-a-post-reporter-question-biden/)
*New York Post* reporter Steven Nelson from covering the President’s
only daytime public event — despite empty seats in the room — after the
reporter pressed Biden on his family’s shady business dealings.

[restricted](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/flashback-biden-also-changed-white-house-press-pool-cutting-off-more-than-440-reporters-credentials)
more than 440 reporters from White House access through a new,
retroactive press credentialing process — with independent and
conservative journalists taking a heavy share of the hit. The White
House Correspondents’ Association barely objected.

**2008:** In the Obama campaign’s final days, senior campaign officials
[pulled](https://abcnews.com/Politics/Vote2008/story?id=6156794&page=1)
reporters from three prominent outlets off the traveling press plane —
restricting them from coverage after the papers endorsed Obama’s
opponent.

**2021:** The same media now suing once urged a ban on Fox News. CNN’s
Brian Stelter
[repeatedly](https://x.com/mazemoore/status/2101316732862247250)
[floated](https://www.newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/pj-gladnick/2026/09/21/rewind-brian-stelter-suggested-fox-news-could-be-banned-white-house)
removing Fox News from the White House press tool as a “political tool,”
and later even [suggested](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0MxhlmlFZUc)
Fox News be dropped from cable altogether.

During the first 100 days of President Trump’s second term, the [Media
Research
Center](https://www.newsbusters.org/blogs/nb/rich-noyes/2025/04/28/tv-news-assaults-2nd-trump-admin-92-negative-coverage)
found that 92% of coverage by mainstream outlets was negative compared
to nearly 60% positive coverage during the same period under Biden.
That’s more like a political operation with cameras than a free press
holding power to account.

**No President is required to host a hostile operation on the grounds.**
Access has always been a privilege — and President Trump is applying
that rule.
