**President Donald J. Trump**
[**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2104640636297388141)
**a $15 billion Mesabi Metallics steel complex is** [**coming to
Iowa**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/mined-melted-and-poured-in-america-president-trump-reverses-years-of-anti-mining-policy/)
**— the largest steel plant ever built in the United States as more
industrial capacity comes home.** The project will create 1,750 new,
permanent jobs, support up to 6,000 construction jobs, and generate an
estimated $95 billion in U.S. economic output over construction and the
first decade of operations.
