**Today, President Donald J. Trump**
[**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2104640636297388141)
**one of the largest** [**steel
plants**](https://www.wsj.com/politics/trump-to-unveil-planned-15-billion-iowa-steel-project-051bc7d8)
**in American history: a $15 billion Mesabi Metallics mill in Iowa.**
The project will create 1,750 permanent Iowa jobs, support up to 6,000
construction jobs, and is projected to add $95 billion to the U.S.
economy over the next decade.

The mill will produce 7.5 million tons of steel per year in its first
phase, rising to about ten million tons, using iron ore from Mesabi
Metallics’ newly opened Minnesota mine — the [first new iron ore
mine](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2100980576060637520) in the
United States in 50 years.

**This is the kind of project Democrats spent years making impossible.**
The Obama Administration
[blocked](https://www.nytimes.com/2016/12/15/us/boundary-waters-minnesota-mining.html)
a proposed northern Minnesota copper-nickel mine by denying its federal
lease renewals in 2016. The Biden Administration
[canceled](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2022/01/26/biden-administration-cancels-twin-metals-leases)
those same leases in 2022, then
[banned](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/biden-harris-administration-protects-boundary-waters-area-watershed)
mining for 20 years across 225,504 acres of the Superior National
Forest. It
[revoked](https://apnews.com/article/new-range-mine-tribe-water-pollution-minnesota-075463042956fa4e89d576c5cae8f663)
a key federal water permit for another long-stalled copper-nickel mine.
It
[blocked](https://www.politico.com/news/2024/04/16/biden-set-to-block-mining-road-in-alaska-wilderness-00152592)
a 211-mile road in Alaska that would’ve opened a major copper and cobalt
district. In 2024, it piled [new
rules](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/03/06/2024-02305/national-emission-standards-for-hazardous-air-pollutants-taconite-iron-ore-processing)
onto taconite iron-ore processing and coke ovens — the plants that feed
American steel — while tariff loopholes kept foreign steel flowing in.

**President Trump reversed those policies — and American mining is
roaring ahead.**

- In 2025, the U.S.
  [surpassed](https://economy.ac/news/2026/08/202608289698) Japan in
  crude steel production for the first time since 1999 and became the
  world’s third-largest steel producer. In fact, raw steel output is up
  9% since President Trump returned to office.
- Nearly 6,000 steelworker jobs have been added after almost 10,000 were
  lost under the prior Administration.
- American mills now supply 84% of the U.S. finished steel market, up
  from 77% in 2024.
- Steel imports are
  [falling](https://www.steel.org/2026/09/steel-imports-up-3-4-in-august-vs-july/)
  as tariffs, permitting reforms, and energy dominance bring production
  home.
- New mill orders hit a record $17.3 billion in July 2026; domestic
  shipments hit a record $16.9 billion that same month.
- U.S. manufacturing has expanded for eight straight months.
