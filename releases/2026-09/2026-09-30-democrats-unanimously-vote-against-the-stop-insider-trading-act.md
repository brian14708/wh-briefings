title: Democrats UNANIMOUSLY Vote AGAINST the Stop Insider Trading Act
---
 
President Trump stands with the American people— no lawmaker, regardless
of party or seniority, should utilize insider information for personal
profit. In his [State of the Union Address to
Congress](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2026493022335054138?s=20)
earlier this year, President Trump called for the passage of the Stop
Insider Trading Act without delay to ensure that Members of Congress
cannot profit from insider information.  
  
*“As we ensure that all Americans can profit from a rising stock market,
let’s also ensure that members of Congress cannot corruptly profit using
inside information… Did Nancy Pelosi stand up? Doubt it. **Pass the Stop
Insider Trading Act without delay.**”* **–** [***President Trump, State
of the Union
Address***](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2026493022335054138?s=20)  
  
Yet, today [EVERY SENATE DEMOCRAT
BLOCKED](https://x.com/BillMelugin_/status/2105340426769064399?s=20)
this legislation to root out corruption in Washington and ban Members of
Congress, their spouses, and their dependent children from purchasing
individual stocks from publicly traded companies. Members of Congress
shouldn’t be buying individual stocks while writing laws and making
decisions that directly move financial markets. This is commonsense.

Republicans and President Trump are UNITED behind the Stop Insider
Trading Act – critical legislation that would deliver the most
significant change to stock trading rules in over a decade after
President Obama’s STOCK Act failed to hold members accountable. Look no
further than [Nancy
Pelosi](https://www.newsmax.com/newsfront/nancy-pelosi-paul-pelosi-networth/2025/06/24/id/1216327/)
as proof that a stock trading ban is necessary to stop corruption at the
highest levels and restore the public’s trust.

When lawmakers consistently outperform stock trading
[benchmarks](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/congress-hold-hearing-member-stock-trading-amid-pelosis-reported-130-m-windfall?msockid=2ac6c3f27a1c60101941d5cb7b3161f3),
Americans are rightly asking questions and demanding transparency.
[Democrats](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6120528-senate-blocks-gop-stock-bill/)
are now [on the record](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2026280) opposing
this commonsense legislation that nearly [90% of
Americans](https://publicconsultation.org/united-states/stock-trading-by-members-of-congress/)
support.

In July, House Democrats voted almost unanimously against advancing this
legislation.
