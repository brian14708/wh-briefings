**Today, President Donald J. Trump joined leaders of Denmark and
Greenland to**
[**sign**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2102424080586801322?s=20)
**an unprecedented**
[**agreement**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/09/agreement-between-the-government-of-the-united-states-of-america-and-the-government-of-the-kingdom-of-denmark-together-with-the-government-of-greenland-to-amend-and-supplement-the-agreement-of-27-apri/)
**giving the United States lasting control over security interests in
Greenland.** The landmark agreement — which comes at no cost to American
taxpayers — forever guarantees America the ability to defend the
continent, includes permanent basing rights, and bars any U.S. adversary
from establishing a base, military presence, or sensitive investment
without America’s approval.

**The agreement is being hailed at home and around the world as a
decisive victory for American security and a stronger NATO in the
Arctic. Here’s what they’re saying:**

[President Donald J.
Trump](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2102412927298863402): “As I
announced just days ago, the U.S. has also reached an unprecedented
agreement regarding the Northern Frontier of North America — the large
and strategically vital piece of land known as Greenland. This
agreement… gives the United States permanent control over security and
all other needs on that territory. We will have the complete ability to
do what is necessary to defend the American continent.”

[Danish Prime Minister Mette
Frederiksen](https://www.youtube.com/live/0b4Ly8E8Jt4?si=uw2I_YthqnZBYIic&t=209):
“This day represents a milestone in our longstanding cooperation. 75
years ago, we entered into an agreement about the defense of Greenland.
Today, we are signing an even bigger and better agreement — a deal that
can last forever. This deal is good for the United States, it’s good for
Greenland, for Denmark, for the NATO alliance, and therefore, also good
for Europe. In other words, we are strengthening our common security,
boosting our common deterrence, showing our adversaries who we are — and
we are doing it together.”

[Prime Minister of Greenland Jens-Frederik
Nielsen](https://www.youtube.com/live/0b4Ly8E8Jt4?si=qfOZY72wR4HMzHkf&t=554):
“We are, have been, and will remain a steadfast friend of the United
States and a part of the Western alliance. Your security is our security
and our security is your security. We are truly pleased with the result
that strengthens our shared security and strengthens our bond as close
allies. This agreement is historical.”

[Danish Minister for Foreign Affairs Lars Løkke
Rasmussen](https://x.com/larsloekke/status/2102423991008854120?s=20):
“An important moment today. Greenland, Denmark and the United States
have signed an agreement strengthening security in the Arctic and North
Atlantic. It’s been a bumpy ride. But here we are – with a deal in hand
that we can all be very satisfied with. We share a long history. And we
share a responsibility for the security of our region. Today, we take
another important step together – with full respect for the sovereignty
and territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Denmark and the Greenlandic
people’s right to self-determination. History binds us together. Our
future demands that we stand together.”

Rutte](https://x.com/SecGenNATO/status/2102426380667338991): “Great to
