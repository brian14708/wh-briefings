> ***“America added 162,000 jobs in August — triple economists’
> expectations. The private sector has now created over one million jobs
> under President Trump, whose reindustrialization agenda continues to
> drive manufacturing and factory construction job growth. America is in
> the middle of a historic investment boom, and the August jobs report
> is the latest data point showing how Americans are benefitting from
> the Trump administration’s pro-growth policies.  The best is yet to
> come with even more job, wage, and economic growth in store for
> everyday Americans.”***  
> — White House spokesman Kush Desai

**President Donald J. Trump’s agenda is working — and the latest jobs
report proves it.** Factories are hiring, construction is booming, wages
are rising, and the private sector — not government — is driving the
boom.

### **Here’s what you need to know:**

- **Jobs, Jobs, Jobs:** The U.S. economy
  [added](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2095854419359281378)
  162,000 jobs in August — nearly triple economists’ expectations
  (again) — for the second-largest monthly gain of President Trump’s
  second term.
- **Main Street Wins:** More than one million private-sector jobs have
  been created in President Trump’s second term, while federal
  employment remains at its
  [lowest](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2095866386081882563)
  share of the workforce on record.
- **Factory Comeback:** After years of Biden-era decline, manufacturers
  [added](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2095864529691300102?s=20)
  16,000 jobs last month — the strongest monthly gain in three years —
  and 58,000 jobs this year,
  [powered](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/09/04/business/jobs-report-economy/3683418f-d5b9-5beb-ae28-cf0adcb1ce3b?smid=url-share)
  by huge demand for pharmaceutical and defense production, data center
  construction, and semiconductor plants.

- **Construction Boom:** Construction
  [added](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/09/04/business/jobs-report-economy/f29f1aca-00f1-54fb-8b8e-c3c6e0620844?smid=url-share)
  22,000 jobs last month, with factory construction jobs up nearly
  100,000 in President Trump’s second term as companies make products in
  America again.
- **Opportunity Rising:** Labor force participation increased at its
  [strongest](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/live-blog/2026-09-04/us-employment-report-for-august-live-updates?srnd=homepage-americas#:~:text=The%20participation%20rate%20%E2%80%94%20the%20share%20of%20the%20population%20that%20is%20working%20or%20looking%20for%20work%20%E2%80%94%20edged%20up%20to%2061.6%25%20in%20August%2C%20marking%20the%20first%20improvement%20in%20almost%20a%20year.)
  level in almost a year last month.College graduate unemployment
  remains a rock-solid 2.7%, while black unemployment
  [posted](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/09/04/business/jobs-report-economy/58b6fd1b-b9e7-5134-a930-3f0eeda92efb?smid=url-share)
  its second monthly decline.
- **Paychecks Climbing:** Private-sector workers saw average weekly
  earnings rise last month — up 3.7% over the year.
