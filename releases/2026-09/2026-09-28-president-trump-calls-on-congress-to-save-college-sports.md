title: President Trump Calls on Congress to Save College Sports
---
 
President Donald J. Trump is calling on Congress to pass the [Protect
College Sports
Act](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103951521369203166?s=20) and
bring clarity, stability, and accountability back to college athletics.

Just this
[weekend,](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2103874908019212323?s=20)
President Trump was at Neyland Stadium for the blockbuster
Texas–Tennessee college football game where the crowd [erupted in
celebration](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103957704121225218?s=20)
of his attendance. President Trump has tremendous respect for the
determination and strength of our great athletes and the traditions that
make college sports uniquely American. That is why President Trump has
been a champion for this legislation and fighting to protect this proud
American institution—supporting hundreds of thousands of
student-athletes.

Years of litigation and uncertainty over NIL, eligibility, transfers,
and revenue sharing have created a system without clear, consistent
rules. The Administration is calling on Congress to establish reasonable
guardrails and clear rules and put the [Protect College Sports
Act](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117056206935468126) on
President Trump’s desk.

We cannot turn back the clock, but we can protect the future of college
sports and make sure opportunities remain available to student-athletes
across America.

President Trump believes wholeheartedly that college sports are worth
fighting for—for the athletes, the schools, the fans, and America.
Scholarships, roster spots, women’s and Olympic sports, and the
traditions millions of Americans cherish are on the line.

**President Trump has been leading this fight to protect college sports
and America’s athletes.**

- In April, President Trump signed an additional [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/urgent-national-action-to-save-college-sports/)
  to restore order, fairness, and stability to college athletics. He has
  also brought athletes, coaches, schools, and sports leaders to the
  White House to address the challenges facing college sports.
- In 2025, President Trump signed an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-saves-college-sports/)
  to protect student-athletes and collegiate athletic scholarships and
  opportunities, including in Olympic and non-revenue programs, and the
  unique American institution of college sports.
- The Trump Administration
  [rescinded](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-rescinds-biden-11th-hour-guidance-nil-compensation)
  the Biden Administration’s overly burdensome and profoundly unfair NIL
  guidance that treated NIL agreements between schools and students like
  financial aid to be distributed between male and female athletes
  proportionally under Title IX. 
- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/keeping-men-out-of-womens-sports/)
  an executive order to protect women’s sports by keeping biological men
  out of them.
- President Trump convened a college sports roundtable at the White
  House with leading sports figures from across the sports landscape to
  address the future of the industry.

**The greatest threat is inaction and uncertainty—Congress must act now
to establish clear, fair rules for NIL and protect the future of college
athletics.**
