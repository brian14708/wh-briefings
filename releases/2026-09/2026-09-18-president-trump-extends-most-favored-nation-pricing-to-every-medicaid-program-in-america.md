**For decades, Presidents have promised to cut prescription drug prices,
but only President Donald J. Trump has delivered.** Prescription drug
prices have now recorded their sharpest annual drop in more than 60
years — and those lower prices now reach every Medicaid program in
America.

### **President Trump is the first President to actually *lower* prescription drug prices.**

Official data [confirms](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)
the largest annual decline in more than 60 years — the steepest drop
since 1963 — with prices
[falling](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/pharmacy/prescription-drug-prices-post-steepest-annual-drop-since-1963/)
every month of 2026.

### **President Trump ended the era of Americans paying the highest drug prices on Earth.**

Through [Most Favored
Nation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/05/delivering-most-favored-nation-prescription-drug-pricing-to-american-patients/) pricing,
26 of the world’s largest drugmakers — covering 90% of the branded U.S.
market — have agreed to bring U.S. prices in line with the lowest paid
by comparable developed nations. These agreements are
[projected](https://wgme.com/news/nation-world/trump-announce-new-drug-pricing-deals-healthcare-announcement-white-house-oval-office-prescription-medicine-medicare-medicaid-executive-order-most-favored-nation-trump-rn-healthcare-affordability-midterms-pharmaceuticals-biotech)
to save Americans more than $600 billion.

### **Every state Medicaid program in the nation now gets Most Favored Nation drug prices.**

All 50 states, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico are in,
unlocking nearly [$65
billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/research/2026/05/savings-from-most-favored-nation-drug-pricing-policy/)
in Medicaid drug savings over the next decade — real relief for
low-income families, children, seniors, and the disabled, and another
big win for taxpayers.

### **TrumpRx is already putting hundreds of millions back in patients’ pockets.**

The platform has delivered more than [$700
million](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/americans-save-more-than-700m-prescription-medications-through-trumprx-white-house-says) in
savings and now covers more than 1,000 branded and generic medicines.
GLP-1 drugs that once cost over $1,000 a month start as low as $149;
eligible Medicare seniors pay just $50 a month, with more than 500,000
seniors already
[saving](https://www.medicaldaily.com/medicare-glp1-bridge-500000-enrollees-demand-2027-478776)
$216 million. Fertility drugs, inhalers, insulin, cholesterol
treatments, and dozens of other high-cost drugs have been slashed 50–90%
or more.

### **President Trump is bringing drug manufacturing home and securing our supply chain.**

The same 26 companies have
[committed](https://www.pharmaceuticalcommerce.com/view/mfn-program-nine-drugmakers-manufacturing-investment)
about $170 billion to U.S. manufacturing and R&D and are
[stocking](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-deal-with-nine-additional-pharmaceutical-manufacturers-to-lower-drug-prices-for-americans/)
the Strategic Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients Reserve — cutting
America’s dependence on foreign supply chains for essential medicines.
