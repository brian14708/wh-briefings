title: President Trump, First Lady Conclude Historic China State Visit
---
 
**Today, President Donald J. Trump and First Lady Melania Trump
concluded a historic state visit with His Excellency Xi Jinping,
President of the People’s Republic of China, and Madame Peng Liyuan.**
This marked the first visit to the White House by a Chinese President in
more than a decade — and the first time a Chinese leader has made two
formal state visits to the United States.

As President Trump
[said](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117332812545704813),
“The meeting was one of Friendship, Strength, and Success, for both
China and the U.S.A… Much has been, and will be, accomplished.”

- **President Trump and the First Lady**
  [**welcomed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103124768522408038?s=20)
  **President Xi and Madame Peng with an official state arrival
  ceremony:** “President Xi, Madame Peng, and the members of the Chinese
  delegation, I am delighted to welcome you to the beautiful White
  House.”
- **President Trump and the First Lady hosted a full official state
  welcome, including a gift exchange,** [**Silent Drill Platoon
  review**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103137493902364695?s=20)**,**
  [**military
  review**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103148747245146320?s=20)**,
  and**
  [**flyover**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103143469585727997)
  **— a commanding display of American strength and hospitality.**
- **President Trump and President Xi**
  [**held**](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2103204224553361513/photo/4)
  **productive** [**bilateral
  discussions**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103503225651392766?s=20)**:**
  “I think our farmers are going to be very happy — a lot of very
  positive things happened… Great things for both countries. It’s just
  been very, very productive.”
- **President Trump**
  [**presented**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103274357904871629)
  **President Xi and Madame Peng with a custom-made statue of a bald
  eagle at the state dinner:** “The bald eagle is a national symbol of
  the United States and an embodiment of the free and soaring spirit of
  America — and we hope it will find a nice place, a wonderful place, in
  Beijing.”
- **President Trump**
  [**toasted**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103274867705679978)
  **President Xi and Madame Peng at the state dinner:** “May all of our
  people know a future of harmony, peace, and success.”
- **President Trump**
  [**gave**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103206709695004903)
  **President Xi an exclusive tour of Marine One and the new South Lawn
  helipad:** “He likes granite. He’s an expert on stone, aside from many
  other things, and he loves good granite.”
- **President Trump and the First Lady**
  [**hosted**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103502515585372429)
  **tea for President Xi and Madame Peng:** “We’ve had a tremendous
  visit from two spectacular people, and we really enjoyed it — and I
  think we’ve made tremendous strides. We’ve made great strides. Very
  positive for both countries.”
- **President Trump and the First Lady**
  [**hosted**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2103514929496010807?s=20)
  **President Xi and Madame Peng at the National Archives in recognition
  of the 250th anniversary of American independence.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

This state visit showcased the strength of the United States, advanced
American economic interests, and made one thing unmistakable: peace and
prosperity are strongest when America leads from a position of power.
