  Prove It.
---
 
**One year ago, President Donald J. Trump**
[**created**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/09/restoring-law-and-order-in-memphis/)
**the Memphis Safe Task Force and ordered a full-scale federal surge
into one of America’s** [**most
violent**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/09/president-trump-announces-bold-action-to-crush-violent-crime-in-memphis/)
**cities.** President Trump directed coordination of more than 30
federal, state, and local agencies in making arrests, clearing warrants,
seizing illegal firearms, and saturating neighborhoods that Democrats
had long surrendered to gangs and chaos.

One year later, the results are undeniable — and the city is being
reclaimed from the violent crime and chaos that once
[plagued](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/09/memphis-welcomes-president-trumps-action-to-restore-public-safety/)
its residents.

### **The numbers:**

- Overall crime in Memphis is
  [down](https://data.memphistn.gov/apps/7fe3d1d471984096ad287080e3cd5e60/explore)
  nearly 36% from last year.
  - Murders are down 36%.

  <!-- -->

  - Sexual assaults are down 28%.

  <!-- -->

  - Motor vehicle thefts are down 57%.

  <!-- -->

  - Robberies are down 42%.

  <!-- -->

  - Shoplifting is down 44%.

  <!-- -->

  - Aggravated assaults are down 26%.
- The city is [on pace](https://archive.is/u6uZX) for a 25-year low in
  overall crime.
- Across Shelby County, violent crime is
  [down](https://memphiscrime.org/the-stats/) more than 30% and major
  property crime is down more than 40%.

### **Task Force results as of September 15, 2026:**

- 12,747 arrests, including:
  - 131 arrests for homicide.

  <!-- -->

  - 1,363 arrests for narcotics.

  <!-- -->

  - 1,276 arrests for firearms violations.

  <!-- -->

  - 125 arrests for sex offenses.

  <!-- -->

  - 1,408 arrests of known gang members.
- 2,160 illegal firearms taken off city streets.
- 189 missing children recovered.

This follows an already historic drop in 2025 versus 2024, when overall
crime fell 28% — including a 26% drop in murders and 31% drop in
robberies. In 2025,
Memphis [recorded](https://www.memphispolice.org/news/memphis-sees-significant-crime-reduction-in-2025-focus-shifts-to-sustaining-progress/) fewer
than 200 murders for the first time since 2019.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

In 2024, Memphis posted the [highest violent crime
rate](https://www.wsmv.com/2025/09/05/this-tennessee-city-has-highest-violent-crime-rate-country-see-where-your-home-ranks/#:~:text=Memphis%20had%20the%20highest%20rate%20of%20violent%20crime)
and [highest property crime
rate](https://www.security.org/resources/most-dangerous-cities/) in
America. Its murder rate
[topped](https://www.newsweek.com/major-cities-higher-murder-rates-chicago-2123275#:~:text=Memphis%2C%20Tennessee%20(40.6))
Chicago, Baltimore, Detroit, and Washington, D.C., while its overall
crime rate ran [344%
higher](https://www.security.org/resources/most-dangerous-cities/) than
the national average.

**That era is over.** President Trump rejected the excuses, restored law
and order, and once again proved that decline is a choice.
