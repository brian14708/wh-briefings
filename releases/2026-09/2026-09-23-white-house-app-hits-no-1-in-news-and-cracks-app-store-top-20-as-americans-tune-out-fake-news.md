**As of September 22, 2026, the official White House mobile app reached
No. 1 in the News category on the App Store and climbed into the Top 20
among all apps.** The app previously hit No. 1 in News and, at launch,
soared as high as No. 4 across the entire App Store. More and more
Americans are gravitating to the app because they want the news straight
from the source, not filtered through networks that have spent years
distorting the record of President Donald J. Trump.

That demand is rising as legacy networks fall in line behind CNN, MS
NOW, and Politico, outlets whose White House credentials were
[pulled](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/white-house-access-is-a-privilege-not-a-right/)
after a long record of false reporting. Other networks have now withheld
pool coverage in solidarity. The American people are not waiting on
those cameras; they are going straight to the Presidency.

### **Americans are choosing the source over the spin.**

Launched in March 2026, the [official White House
app](https://www.whitehouse.gov/app/download/) carries a 4.9 out of 5
average rating on the App store and more than two million downloads
across the App Store and Google Play. It has now reclaimed the No. 1
News ranking in the country and broken into the overall Top 20. That is
proof the American people want unfiltered access to the Presidency.

### **With the official White House app, Americans can:**

- Get news direct from the source: policy initiatives, key victories,
  and major developments with no filter and no spin, plus official
  social feeds, photos, and videos in one place.
- Receive real-time updates and instant push notifications from the
  President, press briefings, and breaking White House news the moment
  they happen.
- Watch live events and the full daily schedule, including speeches,
  bill signings, state visits, and historic moments as they unfold.
- Dive into a dynamic media library of highlights and stay connected on
  the latest policy breakthroughs.
- Send their voice and feedback directly to the Administration.

### **TrumpTV: the Administration’s biggest moments, 24/7.**

The ranking surge overlapped with the launch of
[TrumpTV](https://www.whitehouse.gov/live), a major success. Americans
can now watch the Trump Administration’s biggest moments all in one
place. Top videos, major remarks, and must-see highlights are streaming
24/7 and updated in real time. Stay informed and stay up to date.
TrumpTV is available on
[WhiteHouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov/live/),
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4gNgHfZ-v4), and the
[official White House app](https://www.whitehouse.gov/app/download/).
