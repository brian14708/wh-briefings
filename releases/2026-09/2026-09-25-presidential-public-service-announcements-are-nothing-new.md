title: Presidential Public Service Announcements Are Nothing New
---
 
**The attacks on President Donald J. Trump’s public service
announcements are highly dishonest.** The announcements are very clearly
not campaign ads; President Trump is not on the ballot and there is no
call to action. Instead, the announcements are a reminder for Americans
to love their country and know why it’s worth defending — at home, at
the border, and abroad.

That’s not partisanship — that’s a government that still believes in the
country it serves.

### **Administrations of both parties have used public service announcements to explain policy and make the case for the nation’s direction:**

- The G.W. Bush Administration ran a [national television
  campaign](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-302504#:~:text=Similar%20to%20the,for%20more%20information.)
  explaining the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and
  Modernization Act to seniors. It also
  [bought](https://www.ourmidland.com/news/article/White-House-Airing-Super-Bowl-Ads-7080786.php)
  [Super Bowl
  airtime](https://www.nydailynews.com/2002/01/31/white-house-spends-34m-for-super-ads/)
  for anti-drug and anti-terrorism [public service
  announcements](https://www.wltx.com/article/news/nation/youtube-showcases-bushs-anti-drug-campaign/101-382795056).
- The Obama Administration used national advertising to promote
  [Obamacare
  subsidies](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/obama-administration-will-advertise-healthcare-subsidies-to-combat-perceptions-of-unaffordability-of-aca-health-plans/)
  and [open
  enrollment](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/regulatory/obama-admin-to-blast-tv-social-media-ads-before-open-enrollment),
  and a [digital
  campaign](https://www.nytimes.com/2015/05/19/us/critics-hear-epas-voice-in-public-comments.html#:~:text=The%20White%20House,Instagram%2C%20Vine%2C%20Pinterest.%E2%80%9D)
  to sell its Waters of the United States rule.
- The Biden Administration poured hundreds of millions of dollars into
  “[We Can Do
  This](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2021/04/01/biden-pro-vaccine-tv-ads/)”
  — television, digital, radio, and print spots — to drive a nationwide
  public health message.
- It’s not just modern Administrations. President Woodrow Wilson’s
  [Committee on Public
  Information](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/how-woodrow-wilsons-propaganda-machine-changed-american-journalism-180963082/)
  [used](https://wwichangedus.org/topics/selling-the-war/) posters,
  films, and speakers to rally the country. President Franklin D.
  Roosevelt’s [Office of War
  Information](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/executive-order-9182-consolidating-certain-war-information-functions-into-office-war)
  [produced](https://www.marshallfoundation.org/articles-and-features/marshall-and-the-office-of-war-information/)
  films, radio, and posters to promote the war effort.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

When prior Administrations utilized the airwaves, it was
[called](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/white-house/biden-administration-formally-launches-national-vaccination-outreach-campaign-n1262236)
“public education.” When this Administration promotes pride in our
country, many of those same voices [call
it](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/09/24/ad-boosting-trump-paid-by-taxpayers-airing-la-chicago/)
“propaganda.”

**Patriotism isn’t partisan.** These public service announcements are
unapologetically American, clearly disclosed, and aimed at the country —
not a line on the ballot.
