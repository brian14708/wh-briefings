## **Securing America’s Borders and Putting Americans First**

*President Trump ended Democrats’ open borders disaster, established the
strongest border in history, and restored American sovereignty.*

- The Trump Administration immediately
  [ended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/securing-our-borders/)
  “catch-and-release” — and has not released a single illegal alien into
  the country for [15 straight
  months](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/trump-administration-delivers-15-straight-months-zero-releases).
- The U.S.
  [recorded](https://abcnews.com/US/us-1st-time-50-years-experienced-negative-net/story?id=129175522)
  negative net migration in 2025 for the first time in at least half a
  century.
- More than three million migrants have [left the
  country](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/dhs-touts-most-secure-border-us-history-amid-departure-nearly-3m-illegal-immigrants) in
  President Trump’s second term — the largest reduction in illegal
  immigration in modern history — with the illegal alien population
  [down](https://cis.org/Report/Census-Bureau-Survey-Shows-Total-ForeignBorn-Down-29-Million)
  by at least 2.3 million.
- Under President Trump, asylum is now granted in [just
  7%](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/09/us/politics/trump-miller-immigration-judges-purge.html#:~:text=Asylum%20grant%20rates%20have%20plummeted) of
  cases — a historic low — plummeting from over 50% rubber-stamped under
  Biden.
- The Trump Administration has
  [stopped](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/us-blocks-10k-narcoterrorists-terror-watchlist-swells-85k-2025)
  more than 10,000 narcoterrorists from entering the country.
- ICE has
  [arrested](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/06/24/making-america-safe-again-ice-hits-milestone-more-10000-gang-members-arrested-under)
  more than 10,000 illegal alien gang members.
- President Trump
  [designated](https://www.state.gov/foreign-terrorist-organizations)
  major foreign cartels as terrorist organizations, including Tren de
  Aragua, MS-13, and Clan del Golfo.
- President Trump signed the [Laken Riley
  Act](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-signs-laken-riley-act-law-first-legislative-victory-new-administration),
  [HALT Fentanyl
  Act](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/07/president-trump-signs-halt-fentanyl-act-into-law/),
  and [Secure America Act](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hLBirtH9iE)
  into law — mandating detention of violent illegal alien criminals,
  permanently classifying fentanyl-related substances as Schedule I
  drugs, and delivering the largest border enforcement investment in a
  generation.
- Fentanyl trafficking at the southern border
  [collapsed](https://foxbaltimore.com/news/nation-world/kristi-noem-visits-california-to-tout-historic-efforts-at-the-southern-border-homeland-security-cbp-illegal-drugs-fentanyl-immigration-trump)
  56% and fentanyl-linked deaths
  [plunged](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/exclusive-fentanyl-deaths-plunge-22-trump-white-house-touts-crackdown-deadly-drug-crisis)
  22% in a single year.
- The Trump Administration
  [cut](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/07/taxpayer-funded-benefits-are-for-american-citizens-not-illegals/)
  illegals off taxpayer benefits,
  [revoked](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/08/state-department-revokes-more-than-175000-visas)
  more than 175,000 visas from foreign nationals,
  [carried](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/07/20/dhs-and-department-justice-announce-move-revoke-us-citizenship-10-naturalized)
  out the largest denaturalization effort in history, and
  [launched](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/trump-admin-shuts-down-270-driving-schools-fraud-crackdown)
  a nationwide crackdown on illegal alien CDL fraud to get unvetted
  drivers off America’s roads.
