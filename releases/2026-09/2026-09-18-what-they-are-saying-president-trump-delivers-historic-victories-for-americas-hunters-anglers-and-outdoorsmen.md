President Donald J. Trump is delivering on his promises to America’s
outdoorsmen by cutting red tape, removing burdensome and unnecessary
restrictions levied by previous Administrations and expanding America’s
great outdoor recreation sector that generates over $1.2 trillion
annually and supports over 5 million jobs. 

Last night, the President signed two Executive Orders
to [bolster](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-reinvigorates-americas-hunting-heritage/) American
hunting
and [revive](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-restores-american-saltwater-angling-and-recreation/) American
saltwater angling. Industry leaders and stakeholders nationwide hailed
the President’s  leadership and commitment to restoring Americans’
access to Federal lands, revitalizing our Nation’s outdoor recreation
economy, while conserving and protecting America’s abundant natural
resources.

**[Rocky Mountain Elk Foundation President and CEO Kyle

Trump and his administration for recognizing the vital role hunters play
in sustaining wildlife and for issuing the Reinvigorating America’s
Hunting Heritage Executive Order that strongly aligns with RMEF’s
mission to ensure the future of elk, other wildlife, their habitat and
our hunting heritage. We are honored by the president’s invitation to
attend the White House ceremony where the policy was announced.

The order’s focus on expanding public access, actively managing habitat,
facilitating wildlife movement and habitat connectivity, strengthening
cooperation with state wildlife agencies, supporting science-based
management of game populations, addressing predators and other
population-limiting factors, and combatting invasive species closely
reflects RMEF’s work for more than four decades. These priorities are
central to our mission, and we look forward to working with the
administration, federal agencies and state partners as the order is
implemented.”
