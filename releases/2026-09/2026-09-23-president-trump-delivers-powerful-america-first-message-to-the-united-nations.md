In his address to the United Nations, President Donald J.
Trump [touted](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2102392985828880599?s=20) his
record of historic results that have reshaped world order for America’s
benefit and made our nation safer and more prosperous.

President Donald J. Trump is ensuring America is respected on the world
stage again and holding the U.N. accountable after years of weak and
failed Democrat leadership under the Biden Administration that put
America last.

**Under President Donald J. Trump’s leadership, America is back and
stronger than ever before. **

- The Trump Administration saved over $1 billion by [cutting
  down](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2102126745755205819?s=20) the
  U.N. bureaucracy, terminated the blank checks and endless peacekeeping
  missions, forced a return to the basics of its founding mission, and
  advanced the United States’ interests.
- President
  Trump [imposed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/imposing-sanctions-on-the-international-criminal-court/) sanctions
  on the International Criminal Court for its outrageous overreach, and
  the State
  Department [launched](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/07/state-department-launches-campaign-to-dismantle-international-criminal-courts-threat-to-american-sovereignty) a
  campaign to dismantle the court’s threat to American Sovereignty.
- President
  Trump [withdrew](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/01/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-withdraws-the-united-states-from-international-organizations-that-are-contrary-to-the-interests-of-the-united-states/) the
  United States from 66 international organizations that no longer
  served American interests, including the UN Framework Convention on
  Climate Change and the Global Forum on Migration and Development.
- The Trump
  Administration [thwarted](https://x.com/SecRubio/status/1980003383130959910?s=20) the
  U.N.’s efforts to impose a secret global carbon tax that would have
  raised shipping costs on Americans
  to [fund](https://x.com/SecRubio/status/1979209616425644482?s=20) progressive
  climate pet projects.
- President
  Trump [withdrew](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/withdrawing-the-united-states-from-and-ending-funding-to-certain-united-nations-organizations-and-reviewing-united-states-support-to-all-international-organizations/) the
  United States and its funding from the U.N. Human Rights Council
  (UNHRC) for its protection of human rights abusers and for attacking
  the United States for securing our borders and deporting criminal
  illegal aliens. President Trump
  also [withdrew](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/withdrawing-the-united-states-from-and-ending-funding-to-certain-united-nations-organizations-and-reviewing-united-states-support-to-all-international-organizations/) the
  United States and its funding from the United Nations Relief and Works
  Agency (UNRWA) for its close ties to Hamas.
- The United Nations Security
  Council [endorsed](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2025/11/17/un-vote-gaza-trump-plan/) President
  Trump’s 20-point peace plan for Gaza and
  officially [approved](https://press.un.org/en/2025/sc16225.doc.htm) the
  Board of Peace, which now has 30 countries on its board. 
- This year, the United Nations Security Council
  overwhelmingly [voted](https://press.un.org/en/2026/sc16315.doc.htm) to
  condemn Iran’s terror attacks on commercial
  shipping, [receiving](https://usun.usmission.gov/explanation-of-vote-on-a-un-security-council-resolution-on-the-situation-in-the-middle-east-2/) more
  co-sponsors from U.N. members than any Security Council resolution in
  history.
- President Trump
  is [defending](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2102414622674325604?s=20) American
  industries from the U.N.’s attempts to interfere with innovation and
  stifle the growth of the new industrial revolution powering
  unprecedented economic development
