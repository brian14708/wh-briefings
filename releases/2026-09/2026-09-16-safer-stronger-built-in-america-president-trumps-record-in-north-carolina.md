**President Donald J. Trump returns to North Carolina today with a
simple message: North Carolinians are better off with Republican

crime down, put money back in workers’ pockets, and unleashed a wave of
advanced manufacturing, pharmaceutical, aerospace, and AI investment
across the Tar Heel State.

## **Making North Carolina Safe Again**

***The Trump Administration is restoring law and order — and the numbers
prove it.***

- Nationwide, 2025 saw the [largest annual
  drop](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-drop-in-violent-crime-in-american-history/)
  in violent crime on record — a trend that continues to accelerate this
  year.
- In North Carolina, crime
  [fell](https://www.ncdps.gov/division/governors-crime-commission/trends-reported-crime-nc-january-june-2026/download?attachment)
  across all four major categories in the first half of this year
  compared with the same period last year, including a 12.4% drop in
  murders, an 18% drop in robberies, and a 23% drop in motor vehicle
  thefts. Violent crime and motor vehicle thefts also
  [fell](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/explorer/crime/crime-trend)
  in 2025 compared with 2024, including an 18% drop in homicides, a 21%
  drop in motor vehicle thefts, a 20% drop in robberies, and a 14% drop
  in aggravated assaults.
- In Charlotte, overall crime
  [fell](https://www.charlottenc.gov/cmpd/News-Information/Crime-Statistics-Report)
  9% in 2025 and violent crime fell 21%, including a 13% drop in
  homicides, a 24% drop in robberies, and a 20% drop in aggravated
  assaults. In the first half of 2026, overall crime fell another 16%,
  violent crime fell 8%, and property crime fell 17%.
- In Raleigh, violent crime and property crime
  [dropped](https://cityofraleigh0drupal.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/drupal-prod/COR23/rpd-crime-data-26q2.pdf)
  in the second quarter of this year compared with last year, including
  a 33% drop in homicides.
- In Wilmington, overall crime
  [fell](https://www.wect.com/2026/03/26/give-us-praise-give-us-criticism-wilmington-police-report-drop-2025-crime-statistics/)
  16% in 2025, with violent crime down 17% and motor vehicle theft down
  22%.
- The Trump Administration is swiftly removing the worst of the worst
  criminal illegal aliens from North Carolina, with [Operation
  Charlotte’s
  Web](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/operation-charlottes-web-ice-arrests-criminal-illegal-alien-who-killed-15-year-old#:~:text=DHS%20launched%20Operation,1%2C300%20illegal%20aliens)
  alone producing more than 1,300 arrests.

## **Results for All North Carolinians**

***President Trump is fighting for North Carolina workers, honoring the
state’s military legacy, and delivering where previous Administrations
failed.***

- **Protecting North Carolina’s Furniture Industry:** President Trump
  [imposed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/09/adjusting-imports-of-timber-lumber-and-their-derivative-products-into-the-united-states/)
  Section 232 tariffs on imported wood products, including 25% on
  upholstered wooden furniture, 25% on kitchen cabinets and vanities,
  and 10% on softwood timber and lumber.
- **Honoring North Carolina’s Military Legacy:** In 2025, the Trump
  Administration
  [restored](https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4063467/defense-secretary-renames-fort-liberty-as-fort-bragg-honoring-wwii-soldier/)
  Fort Bragg — the U.S. Army’s
  [largest](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/us/2025-02-10/fort-bragg-liberty-hegseth-16791540.html)
  post — to its [original
  name](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1932535772785295803?s=20),
  honoring Private Roland L. Bragg, who earned a Silver Star and Purple
  Heart for his courage at the Battle of the Bulge.
- **Delivering After Hurricane Helene:** After the previous
  Administration botched the response, the Trump Administration has
  [provided](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260914/recovery-progress-continues-nearly-two-years-after-helene)
  more than $7.5 billion in federal disaster recovery funding to help
  North Carolina rebuild.
- **Recognizing the Lumbee Tribe:** President Trump
  [delivered](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2002073623360934077?s=20)
  full federal
  [recognition](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/federal-recognition-of-the-lumbee-tribe-of-north-carolina/)
  to the great Lumbee Tribe of North Carolina — fulfilling a
  [promise](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2001808338355441893) he
  made on the campaign trail.
