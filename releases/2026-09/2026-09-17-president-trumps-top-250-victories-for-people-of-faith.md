PROTECTED RELIGIOUS LIBERTY

1.  **President
    Trump** [**established**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/establishment-of-the-white-house-faith-office/)** the** **historic** **White
    House Faith Office** – the first White House office focused
    exclusively on faith and located in the West Wing – reporting to the
    President and housed in the Domestic Policy Council.
2.  **President
    Trump **[**created**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/establishment-of-the-white-house-faith-office/)** Centers
    for Faith** with Faith Directors or Faith Liaisons in every federal
    department and agency.
3.  **President
    Trump **[**established**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/05/establishment-of-the-religious-liberty-commission/)** the
    Religious Liberty Commission **to secure and promote religious
    liberty for Americans of all faiths, appointing leaders of every
    major faith to the [Religious Liberty
    Commission](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/05/president-trump-announces-religious-liberty-commission-members/) and
    its [advisory
    boards](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/05/president-donald-trump-names-advisory-board-members-to-the-religious-liverty-commission/).
4.  **The Religious Liberty
    Commission **[**held**](https://www.justice.gov/religious-liberty-commission/resources)** a
    year-long series of hearings **with almost 100
    witnesses, [culminating](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/president-trumps-religious-liberty-commission-delivers-historic-report-draft) in
    a historic report to the President with recommendations on how this
    Administration can protect religious liberty for future
    generations. 
5.  **The Department of
    Justice **[**issued**](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/acting-attorney-general-blanche-issues-updated-guidance-strengthen-federal-religious-liberty)** guidance** to
    all departments and agencies, directing them to protect religious
    liberty in all programs, policies, and practices. 
6.  **President
    Trump **[**established**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/eradicating-anti-christian-bias/)** a
    new Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias** to [investigate
    and
    dismantle](https://static.foxnews.com/foxnews.com/content/uploads/2025/09/anti-christian-bias-report.pdf) the
    systematic discrimination against Christians that occurred under the
    previous Administration.
7.  **The Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias released
    a **[**report**](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/task-force-publishes-report-eradicating-anti-christian-bias-and-restoring-religious-liberty)** and **[**documentary**](https://bydawnsearlylightfilm.com/)** **detailing
    the Biden Administration’s radical efforts to persecute Christians
    and highlighting how President Trump is ensuring Christians are free
    to live out their faith. 
8.  **President Trump issued an **[**executive
    order**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/additional-measures-to-combat-anti-semitism/)** to
    combat anti-Semitism** on our campuses and in our streets.
9.  **The Department of Health and Human
    Services **[**created**](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-announces-restructuring-of-its-office-for-civil-rights.html)** a
    Conscience and Religious Freedom Division** to defend religious
    liberty and enforce conscience protections in healthcare.
10. **President
    Trump **[**ordered**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-directs-repeal-of-regulations-that-are-unlawful-under-10-recent-supreme-court-decisions/)** the
    repeal of all regulations that violate religious liberty **under new
    U.S. Supreme Court religious liberty precedents. 
11. **The Department of
    Education **[**released**](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-issues-guidance-prayer-and-religious-expression-public-schools)** prayer
    guidance **emphasizing that students and school officials have a
    right to pray in school as an expression of individual faith and
    that parents have the right to direct the education and upbringing
    of their children. 
12. **The Office of Personnel Management issued guidance** [protecting
    religious
    expression](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/latest-memos/protecting-religious-expression-in-the-federal-workplace/) and [supporting
    religious
    accommodations](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/latest-memos/reasonable-accommodations-for-religious-purposes/) for
    all federal employees, including [in relation to
    telework](https://www.justice.gov/olc/media/1414536/dl).
