title: One Year Later, President Trump Has Delivered on Making D.C. Safe Again
---
 
One year after President Donald J. Trump
[declared](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/declaring-a-crime-emergency-in-the-district-of-columbia/)
a crime emergency in the District of Columbia and
[unleashed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-declares-a-crime-emergency-to-restore-safety-in-the-district-of-columbia/)
the Make D.C. Safe and Beautiful Task Force, the
[results](https://www.usmarshals.gov/news/press-release/make-dc-safe-and-beautiful-task-force-marks-one-year-of-relentless-action)
are undeniable.

**The nation’s capital is no longer a Democrat-run nightmare of murder
and lawlessness.** [Violent
predators](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trumps-dc-crime-operation-puts-safety-families-first-arrests-surge-past-1800-fbi-director),
[gangbangers](https://www.justice.gov/usao-dc/pr/twelfth-and-final-member-21st-and-vietnam-crew-sentenced-101-months-narcotics-weapons),
[drug
traffickers](https://www.justice.gov/usao-dc/pr/fbi-arrests-seven-alleged-members-dc-fentanyl-and-pcp-drug-trafficking-conspiracy),
and [sex
offenders](https://www.facebook.com/USMSHQ/posts/us-marshals-conducted-a-sex-offender-operation-as-part-of-the-makedcsafeagain-in/788442497100711/)
have been removed from the streets. Homeless encampments
[cleared](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/dc-concerns-grow-trump-moves-extend-federal-control-clear-homeless-encampments).
Parks [reclaimed](https://www.nps.gov/nama/learn/news/fountainwork.htm).
Crime driven toward [historic
lows](https://wjla.com/news/local/dc-homicide-crime-data-year-police-health-fbi-bowser-reduction-council-criminal-justice-report-security-initiatives-efforts).

**The** [**Task Force**](https://safedc.gov/) **— an unprecedented
partnership of more than 20 federal and local agencies, backed by the
National Guard — has** [**delivered unrelenting
enforcement**](https://www.usmarshals.gov/news/press-release/make-dc-safe-and-beautiful-task-force-marks-one-year-of-relentless-action)**:**

- Over 16,000 arrests
- 35 homicide suspects locked up
- More than 100 known gangbangers removed
- Over 3,200 drug-related arrests
- Nearly 2,000 illegal firearms seized
- 25 missing children returned home

**Under President Trump’s leadership, violent crime has been**
[**smashed**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/crime-plummets-another-historic-low-under-president-trump/)
**to levels not seen in decades.** Homicides have
[plunged](https://www.justice.gov/usao-dc/dc-safe-beautiful-task-force-0).
Carjackings, robberies, and overall crime have
[dropped](https://mpdc.dc.gov/dailycrime) sharply. What was once among
America’s [most dangerous
cities](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/08/fact-yes-d-c-crime-is-out-of-control/)
is now on a clear path to become the safest and most beautiful capital
in the world.

President Trump
[promised](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/08/president-trump-is-delivering-on-his-campaign-promise-to-make-d-c-safe-again/)
to dismantle the rampant [crime
crisis](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/08/fact-yes-d-c-crime-is-out-of-control/)
Democrats allowed to fester — and he kept that promise.

**One year later, the record is clear: President Trump made D.C. safe
again.**
