title: Under President Trump, U.S. Factories Expand at Fastest Clip in More Than Four
  Years
---
 
**U.S. manufacturing activity
[surged](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/us-manufacturing-activity-expands-at-strongest-pace-since-2022)
in July at the strongest rate in more than four years — driven by
soaring demand, record production, and a wave of new hiring.** This is
the latest clear result of President Donald J. Trump’s economic agenda
delivering an industrial renaissance across America.

The Institute for Supply Management Manufacturing PMI shows the sector
[expanding](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/july/)
for the seventh consecutive month — a sharp reversal after contraction
in every single month of the previous Administration (outside a
temporary post-pandemic bounce). New export orders flipped firmly back
into expansion, proving the world is once again demanding American-made
goods.

> ***[***Bloomberg’s Lisa Abramowicz on
> X***](https://x.com/lisaabramowicz1/status/2084303546854285407)***:
> “US manufacturing is booming, expanding at the fastest pace since 2022
> and beating expectations in many metrics for the month of July. Omar
> Sharif of Inflation Insights points out that the ISM production index
> rose by the most for any July since 1951.”******

**The Federal Reserve
[confirms](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/BeigeBook_20260715.pdf)
the boom is widespread: manufacturing activity, payrolls, and wages are
rising nationwide.** Manufacturing wages climbed
[4.2%](https://www.aprio.com/insights-events/6-manufacturing-distribution-insights-from-q2-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-you-ins-article-md/)
year-over-year — [beating](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/pdf/cpi.pdf)
inflation and putting more money in the pockets of American workers. The
Philadelphia Fed survey
[shows](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/regional-economic-analysis/mbos-2026-07)
general activity and new orders at nearly five-year highs.

**This turnaround is no accident.** President Trump’s Working Families
Tax Cuts have [powered](https://nam.org/issues/tax/) the revival —
supporting six million American jobs, protecting $540 billion in worker
wages, and preserving $1.1 trillion in U.S. economic activity.

**The results are stacking up:**

- The U.S. economy is
  [projected](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow)
  to grow at a blistering 6.2% annualized rate in Q3 2026.
- Non-residential fixed investment — the engine of long-term growth —
  rocketed ahead 11.7% last quarter, the fastest since 2023.
- Orders for business equipment are [on
  track](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/us-core-capital-goods-orders-climb-by-more-than-expected)
  for an all-time high in 2026.
- Capital goods imports now exceed 40% of all U.S. goods imports — an
  historic peak as factories retool and expand.
- 83,000 factory construction jobs have been added since President Trump
  took office.
- More than 18,000 manufacturing jobs have been created in 2026 alone,
  reversing years of decline.

**Factories are humming. Workers are earning more. Investment is pouring
in. Exports are rising.** This is the manufacturing renaissance
President Trump promised — and delivered.
