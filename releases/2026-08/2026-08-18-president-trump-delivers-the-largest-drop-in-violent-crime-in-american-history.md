**President Donald J. Trump and his Administration have delivered the
most dramatic decline in violent crime in American history.** According
to the FBI’s newly released [Uniform Crime Report for
2025](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/fbi-releases-2025-reported-crimes-in-the-nation-statistics),
America is safer than it has been in generations.

### **The Largest Annual Drop in Violent Crime Ever Recorded:**

- Violent crime plunged 9.3% in 2025 — the largest annual decline on
  record.
- Murder dropped 18.1% — tying the lowest rate in recorded history.
- Robbery fell 18.5% — resulting in nearly 38,500 fewer robberies.
- Rape declined 7.6% — resulting in more than 10,000 fewer victims.
- Aggravated assault fell 7.2% — resulting in over 62,700 fewer attacks.
- Property crime dropped 12.4% — the second-largest decline in history,
  with nearly 750,000 fewer offenses.

**These numbers — in line with the** [**Council on Criminal
Justice**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/crime-plummets-another-historic-low-under-president-trump/)**,**
[**Major Cities Chiefs
Association**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/icymi-violent-crime-keeps-falling-in-biggest-u-s-cities/)**,
and**

[**sources**](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/interactive/2026/crime-rates-us-cities/)
**— mark the decisive reversal of the crime crisis Democrats left
behind.**

Meanwhile, preliminary
[data](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/resources/reports/UCR_Summary_of_Reported_Crimes_in_the_Nation_2025.pdf#page=4)
for the first half of 2026 shows the decline continuing and deepening,
with violent crime down an additional 10.6%, murder down another 23%,
rape down 18.6%, and robberies down 19.6% compared to the same period
last year.

### **Under President Trump and Director Kash Patel, the FBI has unleashed unprecedented enforcement:**

- Nearly 54,000 violent crime arrests (up 90%), including more than
  31,000 violent criminals and gang members (up 30%).
- Nine of the FBI’s Ten Most Wanted fugitives
  [captured](https://www.foxnews.com/us/fbi-captures-top-ten-most-wanted-fugitive-alleged-sinaloa-cartel-ties-patel-says)
  — more than twice as many as during the entire previous four-year
  Administration — alongside
  [four](https://x.com/FBIDirectorKash/status/2087953045384495343) of
  the FBI’s Most Wanted Fraudsters.
- More than 5,000 child predators and human traffickers arrested (up
  13%).
- More than 11,000 child victims located and identified (up 21%).
- Record disruptions across criminal, cyber, counterterrorism,
  counterintelligence, and international operations — taking down
  hundreds of cybercriminal enterprises, espionage rings, and terrorist
  networks.
