  Coming Home'

Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to Texas with a record to match
the visit: tax relief in workers’ pockets, the most secure border in a
generation, safer streets, and a surge of private investment creating
new American jobs across the state.

### **Historic Tax Relief for Texas Workers, Families, Seniors**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts put money back in Texans’
pockets.**

- **No Tax on Overtime:** Approximately [one in
  four](https://dallasexpress.com/state/everything-bigger-in-texas-average-4344-tax-refund-boosted-by-trumps-historic-tax-relief/) Texas
  workers no longer pay federal income taxes on qualifying overtime pay.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** About four million Texas
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Texas-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Texas taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$4,344](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  — the second-highest in the nation.
- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Texas [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) about
  $281 million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program
  funding.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=45) 547,000
  Texas jobs, $107 billion in state GDP, and $51 billion in workers’
  wages.

### **Safer Streets Across Texas**

**Violent crime is falling across the state as a result of President
Trump’s law-and-order agenda.**

- Violent crime
  [fell](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/crimereports/25/2025cit.pdf)
  13% last year, including a 13% drop in murder and 11% drop in rape.
- The top 25 most populous jurisdictions in Texas all posted
  [lower](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/crimereports/25/2025cit.pdf#page=8)
  overall crime rates than the year before.
- In the first half of 2026, Arlington homicides
  [fell](https://counciloncj.org/homicide-likely-reaching-a-new-historic-low-as-most-crime-rates-continue-to-decline-at-mid-year/)
  60% from the first half of 2025.
- America’s Safest Big Cities recently
  [placed](https://smartasset.com/data-studies/americas-safest-big-cities-2026-study)
  Plano, Laredo, and Arlington in the national Top 20 — with Plano and
  Laredo in the Top 10.

### **The Most Secure Border in a Generation**

**President Trump ended the Biden-era border crisis and is reimbursing
Texas for the costs it carried.**

- The Trump Administration [has not
  released](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/08/13/trump-administration-delivers-15-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  a single illegal alien at the border for 15 straight months through
  July.
- Southern border apprehensions in July were [94%
  lower](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/08/13/trump-administration-delivers-15-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  than the monthly average under Biden.
- Refugee admissions in the first ten months of this fiscal year are
  [down almost
  90%](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/08/17/refugee-admissions-to-the-us-are-dropping-sharply-in-trumps-second-term/#:~:text=The%20federal%20government%20has%20admitted%2010%2C258%20refugees%20through%20the%20first%2010%20months%20of%20fiscal%20year%202026%2C%20which%20ends%20on%20Sept.%2030.%20That%E2%80%99s%20down%20from%2038%2C102%20refugees%20in%20fiscal%202025%20and%20100%2C034%20in%20fiscal%202024.%20(President%20Joe%20Biden%20was%20in%20office%20for%20part%20of%20fiscal%202025%20and%20all%20of%20fiscal%202024.))
  from 2024.
- The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [allocated](The%20Working%20Families%20Tax%20Cuts%20invest%20$46.5%20billion%20to%20complete%20hundreds%20of%20miles%20of%20the%20border%20wall%20and%20deploy%20advanced%20surveillance%20technology%20alongside%20it.)
  $46.5 billion to finish the border wall.
- Texas is set to
  [receive](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/24/texas-border-reimbursement-cornyn-abbott-operation-lone-star/)
  $7.5 billion from the State Border Security Reinforcement Fund —
  established in the Working Families Tax Cuts — to recoup costs from
  the Biden-era border crisis.

### **Protecting Texas Communities**

**President Trump is delivering for Texas farmers, ranchers, and
everyday families.**

- **Water for the Valley:** In January 2026, USDA and the State
  Department
  [secured](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/02/03/statement-secretary-state-marco-rubio-and-secretary-agriculture-brooke-rollins)
  Mexico’s commitment to deliver 350,000 acre-feet per year from the Rio
  Grande — meeting the 1944 treaty average and repaying prior-cycle
  debt.
- **Protecting Ratepayers:** After President Trump’s landmark Ratepayer
  Protection Pledge, the Public Utility Commission of Texas and ERCOT
  [moved to
  shield](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-puc-and-ercot-actions-protecting-residential-ratepayers-from-data-center-costs)
  Texas families from the cost of data center expansion.
- **Screwworm Response:** The Trump Administration
  [funded](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-federal-funding-to-strengthen-nws-inspection-capacity)
  additional Texas Animal Health Commission inspectors and a new [$750
  million sterile-fly
  facility](https://texasscorecard.com/state/texas-receives-federal-funding-to-combat-screwworm/),
  which [broke
  ground](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/04/17/usda-and-us-army-corps-engineers-break-ground-new-texas-sterile-fly-production-facility)
  in April at Moore Air Base in Edinburg.
