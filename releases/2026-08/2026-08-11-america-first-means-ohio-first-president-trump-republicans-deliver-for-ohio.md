**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to the Buckeye State for the
Patriot Games finals — celebrating 250 years of American independence by
honoring the excellence, grit, and competitive spirit of America’s
youth.**

As he visits Ohio, one thing is undeniable: under President Trump and
Republican leadership, Ohio is thriving. Since returning to office,
President Trump has delivered historic tax relief, driven down crime,
unleashed American energy, and attracted massive private investment —
putting Ohio families, workers, seniors, and communities first.

### **Historic Tax Relief for Ohioans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of hardworking Ohioans.**

- **No Tax on Overtime:** More than 1.2 million Ohio workers
  [claimed](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=More%20than%201.2%20million%20Ohio%20filers%20claimed%20the%20overtime%20deduction%2C%20totaling%20about%20%243.4%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20about%2029.4%20million%20filers%20claimed%20the%20deduction%20totaling%20almost%20%2492.7%20billion.)
  $3.4 billion in No Tax on Overtime deductions.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** More than 1.4 million Ohio seniors
  [paid](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=About%201.4%20million%20Ohioans%20benefitted%20from%20the%20senior%20deduction%2C%20with%20the%20total%20amount%20of%20deductions%20claimed%20approaching%20%247.9%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20over%2035%20million%20seniors%20benefitted%20from%20the%20deduction%20with%20more%20than%20%24193%20billion%20claimed.)
  zero federal taxes on their benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Ohio’s average tax refund reached
  [$3,308](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/).
- **Investments in Rural Health:** Ohio
  [secured](https://governor.ohio.gov/media/news-and-media/governor-dewine-announces-ohios-rural-health-transformation-program-award)
  more than $200 million in first-year funding under the Working
  Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation Program.
- **Protecting Ohio’s Economy:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [protected](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf)
  208,000 Ohio jobs, preserved [$37
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in state GDP, and safeguarded [$19
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in wages for Ohioans.

### **Safer Communities**

**Violent crime is falling across Ohio as President Trump’s law and
order agenda takes hold.**

- Statewide homicides
  [dropped](https://www.daytondailynews.com/local/crime/new-fbi-data-crime-rate-drops-across-us-ohio/article_bf55b67f-c59b-57a8-87da-831ababfb790.html)
  16.1% last year.
- In Columbus, homicides
  [hit](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2026/01/08/2025-homicides-crime-trends-ohio)
  a 16-year low in 2025 and have
  [continued](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  to decline in 2026
- In Cincinnati, homicides
  [fell](https://spectrumnews1.com/oh/columbus/news/2026/02/03/cincinnati-sees-drop-in-crimes-in-2025)
  more than 6% in 2025 and
  [dropped](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  another 19% in the first half of 2026 compared to the same period last
  year.
- In Cleveland, homicides
  [fell](https://www.police1.com/police-recruitment/cleveland-crime-drops-as-police-see-356-rise-in-cadet-applications)
  16%, rapes fell 28%, auto theft fell 26%, and robberies fell 24%
  in 2025. In the first half of 2026, rapes declined another 8% and
  robberies another 21%.

### **Billions in New Private Investment**

**President Trump’s America First economic agenda is delivering major
private-sector wins for Ohio.**

- Aerospace manufacturer Electra
  [announced](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/electra-to-usher-in-the-next-era-of-aviation-with-advanced-production-facility-in-springfield-ohio#:~:text=at%20the%20Farnborough%20International%20Airshow%20in%20England)
  an $850 million investment in Springfield, creating 1,975 new jobs.
- Swiss paper manufacturer Weidmann
  [announced](https://www.journal-news.com/local/manufacturer-plans-150m-investment-at-former-middletown-paper-mill-creating-56-jobs/article_0d0c3615-cd66-58b6-8485-e725f2651142.html)
  a $150 million investment in Middletown, creating dozens of new
  full-time jobs.
- Automaker Stellantis
  [announced](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/ohio-success/stellantis-chooses-ohio-as-part-of-major-us-expansion)
  a $400 million investment in its Toledo Assembly Complex, creating
  over 900 jobs.
- Corrugated packaging company Pratt Industries
  [announced](https://www.prattindustries.com/latest-news/growth/anthony-pratt-5-billion-investment/)
  a $5 billion U.S. investment, with significant expansion targeted for
  Ohio.
- Property developer DAMAC
  [announced](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2025/01/trumps-20b-data-center-investment-announcement-underscores-the-race-for-data-center-capacity)
  a $20 billion U.S. investment, with Ohio positioned for projects. 

### **Advancing Energy Dominance**

- The Trump Administration has
  [formed](https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-department-energy-ensuring-affordable-energy-access-ohio-while-powering-future/)
  a public-private partnership with SoftBank Group and SB Energy to
  build 10 gigawatts of new power generation in southern Ohio, ensuring
  corporations — not Ohio families — will bear the cost of supporting
  data center growth. As part of the deal, SB Energy is providing $40
  million in community benefits.

**President Trump and Republicans are delivering concrete results for
Ohio families, workers, seniors, and communities — proving once again
that America First means Ohio First.**
