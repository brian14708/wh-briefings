**Today, Karoline Leavitt completes her final day as the 36th White
House Press Secretary — the youngest person to ever hold the position
and among the best ever to do it.** She leaves the James S. Brady
Briefing Room after a tenure defined by command, clarity, and an
unrelenting defense of President Donald J. Trump and the America First
agenda.

President Trump [called
her](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2087624257446572154) “one of
the best White House Press Secretaries in the History of the Office”
— and that’s the legacy she leaves behind.

### **Here are only a few of our favorite moments:**

- **She**
  [**refused**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2011874658694676942)
  **to let reporters turn the briefing room into a stage for activism:**
  “You’re a left-wing hack. You’re not a reporter. You’re posing in this
  room as a journalist… You and the people in the media who have such
  biases but fake like you’re a journalist — you shouldn’t even be
  sitting in that seat.”
- **She**
  [**rejected**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1932854614044426358)
  **questions that didn’t deserve the dignity of longer replies:** “What
  a stupid question.”
- **She**
  [**responded**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1884310924359786785?s=20)
  **with facts:** “All of them — because they illegally broke our
  nation’s laws and therefore, they are criminals.”
- **She**
  [**declared**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1904949934987543003?s=20)
  **this Administration would not be lectured by those who failed the
  country:** “We are not going to be lectured about national security
  and American troops by Democrats and the mainstream media, who turned
  the other cheek when the Biden Administration, because of their
  incompetence, left 13 service members dead in Afghanistan and not a
  single person in the previous Administration was held accountable.”
- **She** [**drew a red
  line**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1961137676989423691?s=20)
  **on this Administration’s personnel and purpose:** “He identifies
  pregnant women as ‘pregnant people,’ so that’s not someone who we want
  in this Administration anyway. If people are not aligned with the
  President’s vision and the Secretary’s vision to make our country
  healthy again, then we will gladly show them the door.”
- **She**
  [**dismissed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1901688046950494413?s=20)
  **a low-level French politician’s demand for the Statue of Liberty
  with history:** “It’s only because of the United States of America
  that the French are not speaking German right now.”
- **She**
  [**named**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1904947160178725156?s=20)
  **the playbook when Democrats and their Fake News allies tried to
  manufacture a crisis:** “If this story proves anything, it proves that
  Democrats and their propagandists in the mainstream media know how to
  fabricate, orchestrate, and disseminate a misinformation campaign
  quite well.”
- **She**
  [**rebuked**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1991581750464799058)
  **a foreign leader who attacked the United States and the President:**
  “I saw the South African President running his mouth a little bit
  against the United States and the President of the United States
  earlier today, and that language is not appreciated by the President
  or his team.”
- **She**
  [**documented**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1965474588214894615)
  **the Democrat Crime Crisis with the record, not spin:** “There is
  crime in all states but… if you look at the list of the top 20
  high-crime cities in the United States, every single one, with the
  exception of one, is run by a Democrat.”
- **She**
  [**exposed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1910749050363650401?s=20)
  **the media’s complicity in the Biden cover-up:** “I can tell you
  there was certainly a lack of transparency from the former President,
  from the entire former Administration, and frankly, a lot of people in
  this room when it came to the health and the competence of the former
  President of the United States, Joe Biden. There was one of the
  greatest coverups and, frankly, political scandals this nation has
  ever seen.”
- **She**
  [**spoke**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1961117590467514686)
  **directly to Americans abandoned by failed Democrat leadership:**
  “This is our message to Americans in Democrat-run cities nationwide:
  decline is a choice. You don’t have to live in constant fear of being
  robbed, raped, or murdered. Your leaders are lying to you — and they
  have been failing you for decades.”
- **She**
  [**rejected**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2041936161027833870?s=20)**,
  without hesitation, suggestions that President Trump stood on weaker
  moral ground than the Iranian regime:** “The President absolutely has
  the moral high ground over the Iranian terrorist regime — and for you
  to even suggest otherwise is, frankly, insulting.”
- **She**
  [**exposed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1887211477771428138?s=20)
  **the endless hypocrisy of the Radical Left:** “I would just say a
  strong message to Democrats who are out there pretending to be
  outraged about the long list of crap that this Administration is
  cutting — federal waste in funding, like $2 million for sex changes in
  Guatemala, $6 million to fund tourism in Egypt… Democrats are outraged
  that the American people want their tax dollars going to good uses,
  not stuff like this — but then they’re very quiet about the fact that
  there are still North Carolinians and people in California who have
  lost everything and in the last four years the federal government did
  nothing to help them.”
- **She**
  [**delayed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2048813158630838353?s=20)
  **maternity leave after the attempted assassination targeting
  President Trump:** “As I told many of you on Friday afternoon, I
  thought that would be my last time taking your questions until after
  my maternity leave — but given the attempted assassination of the
  President… I felt it was prudent to be here today to answer your
  questions.”
- **She**
  [**opened**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1884307337453986284?s=20)
  **the briefing room to new media — and made clear this White House
  would take questions from anyone:** “It’s essential to our team that
  we share President Trump’s message everywhere.”
- **She**
  [**welcomed**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1924879329118347514)
  **children into the briefing room on Take Our Sons and Daughters to
  Work Day — and answered them with the same honesty she brought to the
  press corps:** “I think the most fun part about my job is doing things
  like this with all of you in the briefing room and answering so many
  great questions. I think the hardest part of my job is also doing
  things like this in the briefing room!”
- **And when a question deserved nothing more, she** [**gave
  it**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2024193734234038735?s=20)
  **nothing more:** “You’re kidding, right?”
