**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to South Carolina to
celebrate the powerful results of America First leadership.** Under
President Trump and Republicans, South Carolina is thriving: the largest
tax cuts for working families in a generation, the most secure border in
history, plunging crime rates, unleashed American energy, and a surge of
private investment creating jobs and opportunity across the Palmetto
State.

## **Historic Tax Relief for Working Families and Seniors**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts put real money back into
the pocket of South Carolina workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Overtime:** [One in
  four](https://x.com/SenatorTimScott/status/1946593431217660229) South
  Carolina workers no longer pay federal income taxes on qualifying
  overtime pay.
- **No Tax on Social Security**: Approximately one million South
  Carolina
  seniors [benefit](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=About%201.4%20million%20Ohioans%20benefitted%20from%20the%20senior%20deduction%2C%20with%20the%20total%20amount%20of%20deductions%20claimed%20approaching%20%247.9%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20over%2035%20million%20seniors%20benefitted%20from%20the%20deduction%20with%20more%20than%20%24193%20billion%20claimed.)
  from the new senior deduction.
- **Bigger Refunds:** South Carolina taxpayers saw average tax refunds
  reach [$3,476](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/).
- **Rural Health Investments:** South
  Carolina [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) more
  than $200 million in first-year funding through the Rural Health
  Transformation Program.
- **Protecting South Carolina’s Economy:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=42)
  86,000 South Carolina jobs, $15 billion in state GDP, and $8 billion
  in workers’ wages.

## **Safer Communities**

**President Trump’s law-and-order agenda is delivering safer streets
across South Carolina as violent crime falls sharply.**

- Statewide
  homicides [dropped](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/explorer/crime/crime-trend) more
  than 18% in 2025 — and fell another 21% in the first half of 2026
  compared to the same period the prior year.
- Statewide aggravated assaults
  [fell](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/explorer/crime/crime-trend)
  over 12% in 2025 and 18% in the first half of 2026.
- Statewide rapes
  [declined](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/explorer/crime/crime-trend)
  over 12% in 2025 and 23% in the first half of 2026.
- Statewide robberies
  [dropped](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/#/pages/explorer/crime/crime-trend)
  over 21% in 2025 and 24% in the first half of 2026.
- In Charleston, overall crime
  [plunged](https://www.charleston-sc.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1625)
  36% in 2025, with homicides, aggravated assaults, robberies, and sex
  offenses all well
  [below](https://www.charleston-sc.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1625#docaccess-715460d05e8a64c9d72630e9c62bcc35c160e32391a9a017b640acf4568d3f77)
  the five-year average.

## **Billions in New Private Investment**

**President Trump’s America First economic agenda — tax cuts, energy
dominance, secure borders, and pro-growth policies — has ignited a
private investment surge in South Carolina.**

- Eaton
  [announced](https://www.eaton.com/us/en-us/company/news-insights/news-releases/2025/eaton-invests-in-new-south-carolina-transformer-manufacturing.html)
  a $340 million investment to expand U.S. production of critical
  transformers in Jonesville.
- NorthMark Strategies
  [announced](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/04/22/cutting-edge-supercomputing-facility-a-2-8b-investment-planned-for-spartanburg-county/)
  a $2.8 billion investment to build a state-of-the-art high-performance
  computing center in Spartanburg.
- TS Conductor
  [announced](https://thebusinessdownload.com/ts-conductor-to-open-134m-facility-in-south-carolina-bringing-462-clean-energy-jobs/)
  a $134 million investment to open an advanced manufacturing facility
  in Hardeeville.
- Rolls-Royce
  [announced](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/15/rolls-royce-south-carolina.html)
  a $75 million investment to expand its manufacturing facility in
  Aiken.
- Century Aluminum
  [announced](https://www.postandcourier.com/business/industry/sc-century-aluminum-tariffs-trump-berkeley-county/article_dc587485-e451-44a9-af47-b0ae50ed01f8.html)
  a $50 million investment to restore its Berkeley County plant to full
  production for the first time in a decade.
- Scout Motors
  [announced](https://wach.com/news/local/scout-motors-to-invest-additional-300-million-in-south-carolina-site-facility-investments-supplier-jobs-blythewood-richland-county-manufacturer-cars-electric-vehicles)

  Production Center.
- Coastal Precast Systems
  [announced](https://www.greenvillebusinessmag.com/stories/coastal-precast-systems-announces-laurens-county-operation,29529)
  a $9 million investment to establish operations in Laurens County.
- Georg Utz, Inc.
  [announced](https://www.plasticstoday.com/packaging/georg-utz-invests-40m-in-south-carolina-facility)
  a $40 million investment to build its first South Carolina
  manufacturing facility in Sumter County.
- Ferrara Candy Company
  [announced](https://www.ferrara.com/us/en/ferrara-candy-company-selects-orangeburg-county-new-manufacturing-site)
  a $675 million investment to establish its first South Carolina
  operation in Orangeburg County.
- Red Metals
  [announced](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-06/red-metals-establishes-first-copper-manufacturing-operation-charleston-county)
  a $70 million investment to launch its first copper manufacturing
  operation in Charleston County.
- AmbioPharm
  [announced](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-06/ambiopharm-expands-aiken-county-operations)
  a nearly $119 million investment to expand peptide manufacturing and
  development in Aiken County.
- Octapharma
  [announced](https://www.octapharma.com/news/press-release/2026/octapharma-to-build-1-5-billion-state-of-the-art-critical-care-manufacturing-facility-in-rock-hill-sc)
  a $1.5 billion investment to build a state-of-the-art critical care
  manufacturing facility in Rock Hill.

President Trump and Republicans are proving once again that America
First means South Carolina First — delivering results for families,
workers, seniors, and every community across the Palmetto State.
