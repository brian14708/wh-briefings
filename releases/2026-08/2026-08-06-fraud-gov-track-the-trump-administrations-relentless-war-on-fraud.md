President Donald J. Trump and Vice President JD Vance are waging war on
the fraudsters and scammers who have looted hardworking Americans for
far too long.

**Today, that war becomes fully transparent at**
[**fraud.gov**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fraud/) **— a new website
where Americans can track every action, every takedown, and every dollar
recovered as part of the unrelenting effort.**

The [White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud](https://x.com/WHFraudTF)
is moving to shut off the money, crush criminal networks, and deliver
swift justice. Since the Task Force began its work, it has
[uncovered](https://www.dailywire.com/news/exclusive-white-house-launches-fraud-website-to-highlight-successes-of-task-force#:~:text=Since%20January%202025%2C%20the%20task%20force%20has%20uncovered%20%24229.9%20billion%2C%20according%20to%20the%20website.)
nearly $230 billion in fraud,
[stopped](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2085012752729264405?s=20)
$56 billion in fraudulent payments that would have gone into the pockets
of criminals,
[enforced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fraud/#:~:text=%24-,55.5B,-FLAGSHIP%20ACTIONS%20%C2%B7%20AUGUST)
more than $55 billion through indictments, settlements, and civil
penalties, and secured hundreds of indictments and convictions.
