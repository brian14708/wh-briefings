**Canada has been ripping off the United States for decades — and
President Donald J. Trump is**
[**done**](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117156429202842827)
**letting them get away with it.** Last week, the U.S.
[offered](https://x.com/USTradeRep/status/2091015298929332326) Canada
the most preferential market access of any country on Earth, with deep
cuts on steel, aluminum, autos, lumber, and more. Instead of
partnership, Canada chose unreasonable demands, walk-backs, and flat-out
rejection.

### **The record of Canadian abuse is clear and deliberate:**

- **FACT: Canada is joined only by the People’s Republic of China in
  choosing retaliation over negotiation.** Their continued
  discriminatory treatment of U.S. commerce has burdened American
  workers, farmers, and businesses.  
- **FACT: Canada alone**
  [**imposed**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-motor-vehicles/#:~:text=3.%C2%A0%20Since%20April,of%20the%20increase.)
  **discriminatory 25% tariffs and company-specific quotas on U.S. motor
  vehicles — measures applied to no other country.** As a result, U.S.
  vehicle exports to Canada
  [crashed](https://x.com/USTradeRep/status/2079323971623776471) 22%
  over the last year.  
- **FACT: Canada**
  [**banned**](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwyp9g7p5p9o#:~:text=Canadian%20provinces%20began%20removing%20US%20liquor%20off%20their%20shelves%20in%20February.%20As%20of%20December%2C%20only%20two%20out%20of%2010%20provinces%20are%20selling%20American%20booze.)
  **American wine, beer, and spirits in nearly every province and
  territory — while other countries have faced no such restrictions.**
  As a result, U.S. alcohol exports to Canada
  [collapsed](https://x.com/USTradeRep/status/2079323968364859888) 81%
  in a single year.  
- **FACT: Canada locks out U.S. dairy with** [**tariff-rate
  quotas**](https://www.idfa.org/news/idfa-statement-on-potential-u-s-tariff-on-canadian-dairy-products)
  **far more**
  [**restrictive**](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/economy/article-oecd-criticizes-canadas-supply-management-system/)
  **than those**
  [**given**](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/economics/2026/08/20/why-is-the-us-mad-about-who-sells-cheese-in-canada/#:~:text=Canada%20currently%20gives,of%20all%20types.%E2%80%9D)
  **to Europe, plus** [**over-quota
  tariffs**](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2025/01-99/01-99-2025-eng.pdf#page=93)
  **of nearly 300%** — rates so extreme they function as a [near-total
  ban](https://www.idfa.org/news/idfa-statement-on-potential-u-s-tariff-on-canadian-dairy-products)
  and rank among the
  [highest](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce8kvm84lxmo) agricultural
  tariffs in the developed world.  
- **FACT: Canada has extracted a persistent average annual goods**
  [**trade
  deficit**](https://ustr.gov/countries-regions/americas/canada) **of
  roughly $50 billion from the U.S. over the last decade — while
  refusing reciprocal access.  **
- **FACT: Canada**
  [**targeted**](https://www.wsj.com/world/americas/canada-certifies-gulfstream-jets-following-trumps-threats-0edf48ba?eafs_enabled=false)
  **American aerospace manufacturer Gulfstream for years.** They
  effectively prohibited the sale of its G500, G600, G700, and G800
  models while shielding its own competitor —
  [until](https://www.reuters.com/world/transport-canada-certifies-gulfstream-g700-g800-jets-after-trump-spat-2026-02-24/)
  President Trump
  [intervened](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/115980983698005555).  
- **FACT: Canada’s protectionist barriers —** [**discriminatory auto
  quotas**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-motor-vehicles/)**,**
  [**alcohol
  bans**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-alcoholic-beverages/)**,
  and** [**dairy
  lockouts**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-dairy/)
  **— have**
  [**hammered**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-alcoholic-beverages/)
  **American companies.** This has cost U.S. producers [billions in lost
  sales](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united-states-with-respect-to-alcoholic-beverages/),
  forced layoffs, and [driven market
  share](https://x.com/i/grok?conversation=2092265068880650465#:~:text=https%3A//www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article%2Dtrump%2Dauto%2Dtariffs%2Dcanada%2Dretaliation%2Dexports/)
  to foreign competitors who face none of the same restrictions.  
- **FACT: Without the United States, Canada could not survive.** Canada
  sends roughly
  [three-quarters](https://ustr.gov/countries-regions/americas/canada#:~:text=Canada%20exported%20over%20three%2Dquarters%20of%20its%20goods%20to%20the%20United%20States%20and%20imported%20almost%20half%20of%20its%20goods%20from%20the%20United%20States.)
  of all its goods exports to America — and the U.S. market is where
  Canada gets the overwhelming majority of its money and economic
  oxygen.  
- **FACT: Canada’s failed trade policies are driving its own
  manufacturers south.** A [recent
  survey](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/four-in-10-canadian-manufacturers-have-moved-or-plan-to-move-production-to-the-u-s-kpmg-survey-finds-833778639.html)
  found 42% of Canadian manufacturers have already moved or are planning
  to move production to the U.S.  
- **FACT: Canada is doubling down on tariffs targeting American
  workers.** Canada just
  [announced](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/08/list-of-products-from-the-united-states-subject-to-counter-tariffs-effective-september-8-2026.html#wb-auto-4)
  an additional $27.6 billion in tariffs on American businesses —
  including a 50% tariff on American steel and aluminum, a 25% tariff on
  American fish, and a 25% tariff on American tools.  
- **FACT: The U.S. economy is approximately** [**13 times
  larger**](https://www.statista.com/statistics/527955/north-america-gross-domestic-product-forecast/)
  **than the Canadian economy and**
  [**home**](https://www.census.gov/popclock/) **to over** [**eight
  times**](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710000901)
  **as many people.** The United States has the clear leverage.
