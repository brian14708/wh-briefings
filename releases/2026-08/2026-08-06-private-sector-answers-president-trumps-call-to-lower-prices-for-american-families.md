President Donald J. Trump inherited an economy battered by years of
inflation that crushed working families. Through bold action to restore
American energy dominance, slash red tape, and secure fair trade, the
Trump Administration is quickly rebuilding the foundation for lasting
prosperity.

**That leadership is producing results.** Across the country, private
sector companies are responding to President Trump’s call by delivering
tangible price reductions on everyday goods and services — putting money
back in the pockets of American workers and families.

## Groceries and Everyday Essentials

Major retailers are cutting prices on staples that hit household budgets
hardest — putting real money back in the pockets of American families.

- **Giant Eagle**
  [reduced](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/09/3324816/0/en/GIANT-EAGLE-LOWERS-PRICES-ON-KEY-GROCERY-STAPLES-TO-HELP-CUSTOMERS-SAVE-THROUGHOUT-THE-SUMMER-SEASON.html?_sp=45c07a26-1602-441c-9732-2144431ac56a.1784516987318)
  prices by an average of more than 10% on over 300 top products,
  including proteins, produce, and pantry favorites.
- **Walmart**
  [lowered](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/07/06/walmart-and-sams-club-lower-prices-to-help-customers-make-the-most-out-of-summer)
  prices on thousands of everyday items nationwide, including ground
  beef, fresh corn, paper plates, beverages, and more.
- **Sam’s Club**
  [cut](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/07/06/walmart-and-sams-club-lower-prices-to-help-customers-make-the-most-out-of-summer)
  prices on more than 250 household essentials.
- **Meijer**
  [reduced](https://newsroom.meijer.com/2026-08-03-Meijer-Thanks-Midwest-Families-with-Six-Weeks-of-Discounts-on-More-than-1,000-Everyday-Favorites)
  prices on more than 1,000 popular grocery and household items on a
  rotating basis.
- **Target**
  [reduced](https://corporate.target.com/press/release/2025/11/target-announces-price-reductions-on-food-and-essentials-and-nationwide-food-donation-to-support-fam)
  prices on thousands of groceries, household items, and other
  essentials.
- **Kroger** is
  [planning](https://nypost.com/2026/05/25/lifestyle/kroger-slashes-prices-on-thousands-of-items/)
  its biggest price cuts in years on thousands of items.
- **Albertsons**
  [accelerated](https://www.inkl.com/news/7-grocery-price-cuts-albertsons-is-making-to-win-back-shoppers)
  targeted price reductions and launched its “New Lower Price” program,
  cutting base prices by at least 8% or $1 on items across meat,
  seafood, dairy, pantry staples, and other goods.
- **H-E-B**
  [dropped](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/business/article/walmart-kroger-heb-grocery-prices-22085962.php#:~:text=H%2DE%2DB%2C%20headquartered%20in%20San%20Antonio%2C%20announced%20in%20March%20that%20it%20is%C2%A0lowering%20prices%20on%20thousands%20of%20items%20throughout%20its%20stores.)
  prices on thousands of items across its stores.
- **C&S Wholesale Grocers**
  [slashed](https://www.cswg.com/cs-wholesale-grocers-lowers-prices-on-thousands-of-items-across-midwest-hometown-grocery-stores/?_sp=5f5df14d-9118-47bf-9c20-b000e1810045.1786037235872)
  prices on thousands of everyday items at Family Fare, VG’s Grocery,
  Martin’s Super Markets, and Piggly Wiggly locations across eight
  Midwestern states.
- **Stop & Shop**
  [lowered](https://libn.com/2026/05/18/stop-and-shop-cuts-prices-long-island-stores/)
  prices on thousands of items across nearly every department in
  hundreds of stores throughout the Northeast.
- **Cub Foods**
  [reduced](https://www.startribune.com/target-cub-walmart-cutting-prices-groceries-cautious-consumers-snap/601523113#:~:text=Cub%20Foods%20has%20reduced%20prices%20by%20about%2040%25%20on%20more%20than%2030%20grocery%20items%2C%20including%20bread%20and%20rotisserie%20chicken.)
  prices by about 40% on dozens of grocery items across its Midwestern
  locations.
- **Piggly Wiggly Food for Less**
  [cut](https://massmarketretailers.com/piggly-wiggly-food-for-less-rolls-out-affordable-protein-initiative/)
  prices on meat and other staples across its 14 stores.
- **Costco**
  [dropped](https://www.usatoday.com/story/grocery/stores/2026/06/08/costco-drops-prices-popular-kirkland-products/90459418007/)
  prices for many of its most popular Kirkland Signature items across
  multiple categories.
- **PepsiCo**
  [lowered](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/pepsico-makes-iconic-snacks-more-affordable-ahead-of-super-bowl)
  retail prices by up to 15% on major snack brands.

## Prescription Drugs

President Trump’s Most Favored Nation approach is delivering historic
cuts to prescription drug prices, with 17 of the world’s largest
drugmakers agreeing to bring U.S. prices in line with the lowest paid by
comparable developed nations.

- **Novo Nordisk**
  [slashed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-major-developments-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/)
  prices on Ozempic and Wegovy to as low as $199–$350 per month and
  [reduced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-major-developments-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/)
  the price of common insulin treatments by 75% through TrumpRx.
- **Pfizer**
  [offered](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/global-pharma-companies-that-have-publicly-announced-trump-drug-pricing-2026-05-04/#:~:text=The%20company%20(PFE,high%20as%2085%25.)
  average savings of 50% (up to 85%) on primary care treatments and
  select brands via TrumpRx.
- **Amgen**
  [lowered](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/global-pharma-companies-that-have-publicly-announced-trump-drug-pricing-2026-05-04/#:~:text=Amgen%20(AMGN.O,off%20list%20price.)
  prices on cholesterol drug Repatha, migraine drug Aimovig, and
  arthritis treatment Amjevita by 60–80%.
- **Eli Lilly**
  [lowered](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-major-developments-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/)
  the price of migraine treatment
  [Emgality](https://trumprx.gov/p/emgality) by approximately 60% and
  diabetes treatment [Trulicity](https://trumprx.gov/p/trulicity) by
  approximately 61%.
- Multiple additional manufacturers — including
  [Merck](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-largest-developments-to-date-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [AstraZeneca](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-second-deal-to-bring-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [Bristol Myers
  Squibb](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-largest-developments-to-date-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [Gilead](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-largest-developments-to-date-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [Sanofi](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-largest-developments-to-date-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [GSK](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/12/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-largest-developments-to-date-in-bringing-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/),
  [EMD
  Serono](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-actions-to-lower-costs-and-expand-access-to-in-vitro-fertilization-ivf-and-high-quality-fertility-care/),
  [Johnson &
  Johnson](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-reaches-agreement-with-u-s-government-to-improve-access-to-medicines-and-lower-costs-for-millions-of-americans-delivers-on-u-s-manufacturing-and-innovation-investments),

## Energy

Key energy providers and local networks are delivering targeted savings
that lower costs for consumers.

- The **Freedom Fuel Network** — a coalition of 25 gas stations across
  the greater Philadelphia area —
  [lowered](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/first-freedom-fuel-gas-station-opens-trump-backed-discounts-roll-out)
  prices by approximately 50 cents per gallon.
- **Georgia Power** is
  [lowering](https://www.georgiapower.com/news-hub/press-releases/georgia-psc-approves-plan-to-lower-overall-rates.html)
  rates for residential customers by $50/year, delivering total savings
  of nearly $300 million for all customers.
- **Alliant Energy**
  [implemented](https://corridorbusiness.com/alliant-ceo-cedar-rapids-data-centers-will-help-enable-five-year-rate-freeze/#:~:text=and%20a%20five%2Dyear%20freeze%20on%20electrical%20rates%2C%20according%20to%20Alliant%20CEO%20Lisa%20Barton.)
  a five-year rate freeze for residential customers in Iowa.
- **NiSource** is
  [set](https://www.nisource.com/news/article/nisource-announces-first-2026-quarter-results)
  to return $1.4 billion to customers in Indiana through agreements with
  Amazon and Alphabet.
- **Entergy** is
  [set](https://www.entergy.com/wp-content/uploads/AWS_WinMississippi_001.pdf)
  to return $2 billion to customers in Mississippi through an agreement
  with Amazon.
- **DTE Energy**
  [announced](https://www.dteenergy.com/us/en/newsroom/2026/DTE-Energy-intends-to-pause-future-electric-rate-requests-following-upcoming-filing-as-data-centers-come-online.html)
  it will pause electric rate increases for at least two years.

## Automobiles

American automakers are making vehicles more affordable for hardworking
families.

- **Ford**
  [extended](https://www.foxbusiness.com/retail/ford-rolls-out-nationwide-employee-pricing-mark-americas-250th-anniversary)
  employee pricing to all U.S. customers.
- **Stellantis**
  [offered](https://www.autonews.com/stellantis/an-stellantis-declaration-of-deals-sale-0305/)
  buyers 0% financing and deferred monthly payments for 90 days.
- **Chevrolet**
  [revived](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/topic/us/en/2026/jun/0624-heartbeat-america.html)
  its classic “Heartbeat of America” campaign, offering 90 days of
  deferred monthly payments and multi-year 0% APR financing options.
