**A new** [**mid-year crime
report**](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
**delivers more clear proof: President Donald J. Trump’s law-and-order
agenda is crushing violent crime across America.**

According to the Major Cities Chiefs Association, homicides in the
nation’s largest cities are down 17.2% from the first half of last year
— alongside sharp declines in robberies, rapes, aggravated assaults, and
overall violent crime. That translates to nearly 14,200 fewer reported
violent crimes in just six months.

**The results are especially striking in Democrat-run cities where the
Trump Administration has aggressively surged federal resources and
National Guard support after years of soft-on-crime failure.** In
Washington, D.C., homicides are down 43.9%. In Memphis, homicides are
down 43.7%. In New Orleans, homicides are down nearly 25%.

This builds directly on last year’s already
[historic](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/01/icymi-murder-rate-plunges-to-125-year-low-as-president-trump-restores-law-and-order/)
[progress](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/02/icymi-violent-crime-plummets-across-major-u-s-cities/),
when murders and other violent crime plummeted to levels not seen in
more than a century — and is the latest in a mountain of data all
pointing to the same unmistakable reality. The Council on Criminal
Justice
[finds](https://counciloncj.org/homicide-likely-reaching-a-new-historic-low-as-most-crime-rates-continue-to-decline-at-mid-year/)
the national murder rate is on pace to hit the lowest level in at least
126 years, while crime data analyst Jeff Asher
[reports](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2086810228998213731) the
2026 murder rate is tracking to be “by far the lowest ever recorded.”

**This is the Trump Effect in full force.**
