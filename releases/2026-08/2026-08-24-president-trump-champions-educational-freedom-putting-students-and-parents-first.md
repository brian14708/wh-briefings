**Today, President Donald J. Trump**
[**welcomes**](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/08/23/trump-school-choice-rose-garden/)
**students, parents, and education advocates to the White House to
celebrate the start of a new school year.** Under President Trump’s and
Secretary of Education Linda McMahon’s leadership, this Administration
has delivered an unparalleled record of expanding educational freedom,
restoring parental rights, returning power to the states, and putting
American students first.

### **Expanding School Choice**

- President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts
  [created](https://hechingerreport.org/whats-a-tax-credit-scholarship-the-details-behind-the-first-national-school-voucher-program/)
  the first-ever nationwide school choice program, delivering a federal
  [Education Freedom Tax
  Credit](https://www.ed.gov/media/document/education-freedom-tax-credit-fact-sheet-113147.pdf)
  of up to $1,700 for scholarships covering tuition, books, and other
  costs at private and public schools.
  - Nearly 52 million students nationwide are
    [eligible](https://www.federationforchildren.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/07.27.2026-EFTC-2-Pager-v2-2.pdf),
    with [30+ states](https://x.com/afcpress/status/2091885687641415943)
    already opting in or signaling their intent to participate.
- The Trump Administration
  [delivered](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-announces-release-of-record-500-million-charter-schools-programs)
  the largest investment in charter schools in history — $500 million
  through the Charter Schools Program — to expand high-quality options
  nationwide.
- President Trump
  [directed](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/department-of-education-encourages-states-expand-education-choice-leveraging-flexibilities-title-i-funds)
  federal agencies to maximize school choice,
  [enabling](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/expanding-educational-freedom-and-opportunity-for-families/)
  states to use Title I funds and other flexibilities to support
  parental decision-making.

### **Returning Education to the States**

- President Trump
  [directed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/improving-education-outcomes-by-empowering-parents-states-and-communities/)
  the Secretary of Education to dismantle the bloated federal
  bureaucracy and return authority to states and local communities,
  empowering them to expand freedom and opportunity after decades of
  [failed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/03/on-education-president-trump-knows-we-can-do-better/)
  central control.
- The Trump Administration
  [approved](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-approves-iowas-returning-education-states-waiver)
  the nation’s first Returning Education to the States waivers, freeing
  tens of millions of dollars from red tape and directing them straight
  to classrooms and students.
- The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-awards-unprecedented-number-of-education-innovation-and-research-grants-improve-literacy)
  a record number of grants to advance literacy, expand access to
  credentialed school psychologists, emphasize science-of-reading
  approaches, integrate AI in education, and demand real academic
  results over ideology.

### **Ending Radical Gender Ideology and Restoring Parental Rights**

- President Trump signed an [Executive
  Order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/keeping-men-out-of-womens-sports/)
  barring biological boys from girls’ sports and private spaces,
  prompting
  [school](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/transgender-students-terminate-protections-education-department-trump-20260406.html)
  [systems](https://www.cbsnews.com/baltimore/news/anne-arundel-schools-amend-gender-identity-policy-trump-administration-threat/)
  and
  [athletic](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/piaa-transgender-trump-executive-order/)
  [associations](https://www.vpm.org/news/2025-02-11/vhsl-ban-trans-students-girls-sports-youngkin-trump-executive-order/)
  nationwide to end the practice.
- The Trump Administration
  [launched](https://www.google.com/url?sa=i&source=web&rct=j&url=https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-educations-office-civil-rights-initiates-18-title-ix-investigations&ved=2ahUKEwjgv6fW-aeWAxXWgCsGHaGjGUMQ0YISegoIAggACAAIERAD&opi=89978449&cd&psig=AOvVaw0HIrQASWJpka-NRjzFBTmK&ust=1787065974997000)
  dozens of Title IX investigations into schools that allowed males in
  female sports and intimate facilities, enforcing biological reality
  and holding institutions accountable.
- The Trump Administration
  [cracked](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/trump-administration-cracks-down-school-districts-secretly-transitioning-students-and-hiding-it-parents)
  [down](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-launches-investigation-maine-department-of-education-alleged-ferpa-violations)
  on schools that “transition” students behind parents’ backs,
  [rescinded](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-rescinds-illegal-title-ix-resolution-agreements)
  unlawful Title IX guidance that punished districts for failing to use
  preferred pronouns, and reinforced parental rights under federal law —
  restoring fairness for female athletes, privacy for young women, and
  parents’ fundamental authority.

### **Eliminating Waste, Fraud, and Divisive Indoctrination**

- The Trump Administration
  [terminated](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-secretary-of-education-linda-mcmahon-celebrates-president-trumps-historic-first-year#:~:text=Eliminating%20over%20%242%20billion%20in%20divisive%20and%20unnecessary%20spending.%20%C2%A0)
  more than $2 billion in wasteful “diversity, equity, and inclusion”
  spending, shifting focus from dividing students by race and identity
  to uniting them around merit and excellence.
- The Trump Administration
  [ended](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/06/23/hhs-grants-sexually-explicit-minors/)
  $67 million in grants that funded sexually explicit material for
  minors, rejecting inappropriate and inaccurate ideological curricula
  with no place in classrooms.
- By enforcing immigration laws, the Trump Administration
  [reduced](https://thefederalist.com/2025/12/18/large-school-systems-see-plummeting-foreign-language-students-thanks-to-immigration-enforcement/)
  the strain of non-English-speaking students on American public
  schools, allowing resources to prioritize American citizens.

### **Supporting Every Child**

- First Lady Melania Trump’s [Fostering the
  Future](https://fosteringthefuture.gov/) initiative expands support
  and educational opportunities for children leaving foster care through
  scholarships, university partnerships, savings accounts, and a global
  coalition — delivering pathways to independence, skills, and the
  American Dream.
- The Trump Administration
  [launched](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-opens-title-ix-investigation-los-angeles-unified-school-district)
  a Title IX investigation into the Los Angeles Unified School District
  for allegedly protecting predator teachers accused of sexual
  misconduct.
- The Trump Administration
  [strengthened](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-issues-guidance-prayer-and-religious-expression-public-schools)
  protections for prayer in public schools, reinforcing every student’s
  constitutional rights.
