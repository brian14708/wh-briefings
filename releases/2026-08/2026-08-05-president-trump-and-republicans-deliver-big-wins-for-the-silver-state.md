title: President Trump and Republicans Deliver Big Wins for the Silver State

**From the largest tax cut for working families in American history to
the most secure border on record, Nevada is winning again under
President Donald J. Trump and Republican leadership.** Since returning
to office, President Trump has cut taxes, driven down crime, secured the
border, opened federal lands, unleashed American energy, and attracted
billions in private investment to Nevada.

### **Historic Tax Relief for Working Families and Seniors**

- **No Tax on Tips:** More than 440,000 Nevada tipped workers —
  including those in Las Vegas, the
  [birthplace](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/04/in-nevada-president-trump-celebrates-no-tax-on-tips-real-money-to-working-americans/')
  of this landmark policy — saved an average of $10,000 this filing
  season.
- **No Tax on Overtime:** 150,000 Nevada workers saved an average of
  $3,000.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** 500,000 Nevada seniors paid zero
  federal taxes on their benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Nevada’s average tax refund rose 15% to
  [$4,193](https://www.nbcrightnow.com/national/average-tax-refunds-by-state-and-county/collection_f257c0aa-6345-5a10-acd4-7957eeedb269.html#5:~:text=Nevada%20had%20an%20average%20refund%20of%20%244%2C193%2C%20with%201.08%20million%20refunds%20issued%20out%20of%201.6%20million%20total%20returns%20%E2%80%94%20an%20overall%20refund%20rate%20of%20roughly%2069.6%25.)
  — among the
  [highest](https://www.nbcrightnow.com/national/average-tax-refunds-by-state-and-county/collection_f257c0aa-6345-5a10-acd4-7957eeedb269.html#5)
  in the nation.
- **Investments in Rural Health:** Nevada
  [secured](https://www.gov.nv.gov/press-releases/nevada-announces-first-round-of-grants-under-new-rural-health-transformation-program/#:~:text=Nevada%20was%20awarded%20%24180%20million%20for%20the%20first%20year%20of%20this%20program.%20%C2%A0)
  nearly $180 million in first-year funding under the Working Families
  Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation Program.
- **Protecting Nevada’s Economy:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  safeguarded [$10
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in state GDP and [$5
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in wages for Nevadans.

### **Safer Communities and More Affordable Housing**

- Violent crime is
  [down](https://online.fliphtml5.com/LasVegasMetroPolice/2025-Annual-Report-GfTT/#p=1)
  in [Las
  Vegas](https://online.fliphtml5.com/LasVegasMetroPolice/2025-Annual-Report-GfTT/#p=1)
  and
  [statewide](https://nevadacrimestats.nv.gov/tops/report/violent-crimes/nevada/2025)
  compared to the final year of the previous Administration — and is
  continuing its downward trend.
- The Trump Administration [has not
  released](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/trump-administration-delivers-14-straight-months-zero-releases#:~:text=Trump%20administration%20delivers%2014%20straight%20months%20of%20zero%20releases%20at%20the%20border,-Release%20Date&text=WASHINGTON%20%E2%80%94%20Today%2C%20the%20U.S.%20Department,of%20historically%20low%20border%20crossings.)
  a single illegal alien into the United States in 14 consecutive
  months, while the U.S.
  [recorded](https://abcnews.com/US/us-1st-time-50-years-experienced-negative-net/story?id=129175522')
  negative net migration in 2025 for the first time in more than five
  decades and more than three million illegal aliens have left the
  country since President Trump returned to office.
- As a result, home prices in Las Vegas — one of the [top
  20](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2019/03/11/us-metro-areas-unauthorized-immigrants/)
  metro areas with the largest illegal alien population — have dropped
  nearly 4% since President Trump took office, the
  [largest](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/las-vegas-home-prices-post-largest-drop-in-the-nation-d39d6ecd)
  decline among major U.S. cities. Statewide, home prices are
  [down](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/42/nv/) 2.1% over last year.

### **Billions in New Private Investment**

- Apple
  [announced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/08/apple-600-billion-commitment-to-boost-u-s-manufacturing/)
  a $600 billion investment over four years, with Nevada targeted for
  significant expansion. Amazon
  [invested](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/job-creation-and-investment/amazon-economic-impact-report-2025)
  a record $340 billion across the United States in 2025, including
  customer fulfillment centers and AI and cloud infrastructure projects
  in Nevada. Vantage Data Centers
  [announced](https://vantage-dc.com/news/vantage-data-centers-invests-3-billion-to-deliver-ai-campus-in-growing-nevada-market/)
  a $3 billion AI data center campus in Storey County. Crocs
  [committed](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/08/15/crocs-invest-80-million-las-vegas-fulfillment-center/#:~:text=Crocs%20to%20invest%20%2480%20million%20in%20Las%20Vegas%20fulfillment%20center)
  $80 million to expand its Las Vegas fulfillment center. First Majestic
  [announced](https://www.firstmajestic.com/investors/news-releases/first-majestic-announces-restart-plan-for-jerritt-canyon-gold-mine)
  a $75 million investment to restart the Jerritt Canyon Gold Mine in
  Elko County.

### **Opening Federal Lands, Advancing Energy Dominance**

- The Trump Administration
  [launched](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/icymi-secretary-burgum-hud-secretary-turner-announce-joint-task-force-reduce-housing)
  a joint task force [alongside](https://www.hud.gov/news/hud-no-25-067)
  Gov. Lombardo to open vast tracts of federal land in Nevada for
  residential development, while
  [fast-tracking](https://nevadacurrent.com/2025/06/03/3-nevada-geothermal-projects-fast-tracked-under-trumps-quickie-environmental-review-process/)
  several major energy projects to further the American Energy Dominance
  agenda.

### **Strengthening National Defense**

- The FY2027 Air Force budget request
  [includes](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/air-force-doubles-construction-budget-2027-f-47-facilities/#:~:text=%24730%20million%C2%A0for%20new%20hangar%20and%20support%20spaces%20for%20the%C2%A0F%2D47%20fighter.%20The%20facilities%2C%20to%20be%20built%C2%A0at%20Nellis%20Air%20Force%20Base%2C%20Nev.%2C%20anticipate%20the%20F%2D47%E2%80%99s%20first%20flight%20in%202028.)
  $730 million for new hangars and support facilities at Nellis Air
  Force Base to host America’s newest sixth-generation fighter jet.

**President Trump and Republicans are delivering results for Nevada
families, workers, seniors, and communities — proving once again that
America First means Nevada First.**
