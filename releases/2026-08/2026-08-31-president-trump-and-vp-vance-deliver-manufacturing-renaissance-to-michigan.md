title: President Trump and VP Vance Deliver Manufacturing Renaissance to Michigan
---
 
**Vice President JD Vance is in Michigan today to mark the Trump
Administration’s unprecedented crackdown on fraud and the comeback of
American manufacturing under the leadership of President Donald J.
Trump.** Since taking office, the Trump Administration has onshored
production, restored factory jobs, and put vehicles back on Michigan
assembly lines — while delivering tax relief that leaves more money in
workers’ pockets and driving a sharp decline in crime across the state.

### **Record Investments. Record Sales. American Jobs.**

*Michigan is locking in tens of billions in new capital and thousands of
high-paying jobs as companies respond to the Trump Administration’s
America First agenda.*

- General Motors is
  [investing](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2026/apr/0429-general-motors-us-manufacturing-investments-6-billion.html)
  $6 billion in U.S. manufacturing — including a major
  [expansion](https://news.gm.com/home.detail.html/Pages/news/us/en/2025/jun/0611-plants.html)
  at its Orion Township plant — and is
  [onshoring](https://investor.gm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/q2-2026-letter-shareholders)
  more production to meet demand for American-built vehicles. GM is also
  [adding](https://www.freep.com/story/money/cars/general-motors/2026/03/30/gm-overtime-shift-flint-assembly-pickup/89384715007/)
  an overtime shift at Flint as pickup demand surges.
- Ford Motor Company is
  [investing](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2025/ford-affordable-electric-vehicle-platform-midsize-electric-truck)
  $3 billion in its BlueOval Battery Park in Marshall, creating 1,700
  new jobs. Ford
  [added](https://patch.com/michigan/dearborn/ford-adding-hundreds-jobs-dearborn-truck-plant)
  a third shift at the Dearborn Truck Plant as the F-series continues to
  [dominate](https://www.focus2move.com/world-best-selling-pick-up/)
  global pickup sales.
- Stellantis is
  [investing](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-388-million-investment-metro-detroit-hub/)
  $388 million in a state-of-the-art “megahub” in Van Buren Township,
  $140 million to
  [expand](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-announces-13-billion-investment-in-four-states/#:~:text=Stellantis%20also%20plans%20to%20invest%20%24130%20million%20in%20the%20Detroit%20Assembly%20Complex%20to%20produce%20the%20next%2Dgeneration%20Dodge%20Durango%20beginning%20in%202029.)
  its Detroit plant, and $100 million to [add
  production](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/stellantis-announces-13-billion-investment-in-four-states/#:~:text=In%20Michigan%2C%20the,retool%20the%20plant.)
  at Warren.
- Detroit Diesel is
  [recalling](https://uaw.org/after-uaw-tariff-push-detroit-diesel-to-add-third-shift-hire-more-and-recall-laid-off-workers/)
  laid-off workers and adding a third shift.
- 2025 marked the [strongest
  year](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cox-automotive-forecast-despite-q4-slowdown-new-vehicle-sales-expected-to-hit-16-3-million-in-2025--up-nearly-2-from-2024-best-result-since-2019--302645028.html)
  for [new vehicle
  sales](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/us-auto-sales-defy-regulatory-uncertainty-rise-2-2025-2026-01-05/)
  since 2019. Ford
  [posted](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/06/ford-f-2025-annual-sales.html)
  its best annual U.S. sales in six years. General Motors
  [delivered](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/05/gm-posts-5point5percent-us-sales-gain-in-2025-while-jeep-ends-annual-declines.html)
  soaring overall volume and its
  [strongest](https://investor.gm.com/static-files/bd576095-7337-4596-bfd2-cb7b3b5b336a)
  SUV performance in decades. Jeep sales
  [rose](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/05/gm-posts-5point5percent-us-sales-gain-in-2025-while-jeep-ends-annual-declines.html)
  for the first time since 2018.

*Michigan is winning in advanced manufacturing, energy, aerospace, food
production, and the infrastructure of the AI economy.*

- OpenAI, Oracle, and Related Digital are
  [building](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-environment-watch/dte-consumers-advance-plans-for-power-hungry-data-centers-in-michigan/)

  than $7 billion, with thousands of construction jobs and hundreds of
  permanent positions.
- Pratt Industries
  [pledged](https://www.prattindustries.com/latest-news/growth/anthony-pratt-5-billion-investment/)
  $5 billion to reindustrialize America, with thousands of jobs headed
  to Michigan workers.

  [investing](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/corning-1-point-5-billion-solar-wafer-plant-expansion-richland-township-michigan/746900/)

  manufacturing jobs.

  hundreds of new jobs and sharply increasing purchases of Michigan
  milk.
- GE Aerospace is
  [investing](https://wwmt.com/news/local/ge-aerospace-invests-60m-in-west-michigan-with-10m-for-grand-rapids-avionics-muskegon)

- Adrian Steel is [investing](https://www.adriansteel.com/news/) $43.4
  million to significantly grow its operations.
- American Rheinmetall is
  [investing](https://www.rheinmetall.com/en/media/news-watch/news/2025/09/2025-09-11-american-rheinmetall-invests-$31-7-million-in-modernising-the-group-s-site-in-michigan)
  $31.7 million in its Michigan facilities.

### **Historic Tax Relief for Michigan Workers and Families**

*President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts put money back in
Michiganders’ pockets.*

- **No Tax on Social Security:** About 1.7 million Michigan
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Michigan-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction.
- **No Tax on Overtime and No Tax on Tips:** Roughly a quarter of
  Michigan employees regularly work overtime and can benefit — so
  workers keep more of what they earn.
- **Made-in-America Auto Loan Interest:** Families who buy a vehicle
  assembled in the U.S. can deduct the interest — a tax cut that doubles
  as industrial policy.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Michigan taxpayers saw average refunds
  of [$3,508](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/).
- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Michigan [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) about
  $173 million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program
  funding.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=24) 170,000
  Texas jobs, $30 billion in state GDP, and $15 billion in workers’
  wages.

### **Michiganders are Safer**

*The Trump Administration is making Michigan safe again — and the
numbers tell the success.*

- So far in 2026, violent offenses are
  [down](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/) compared to the same
  period last year — with homicide down 19%, rape down 18%, and robbery
  down 17%.
- In 2025, violent crime and motor vehicle thefts all
  [fell](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/webapp/) compared to the
  previous year.
- In 2025, Detroit
  [recorded](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/01/08/detroit-violent-crime-drops-to-lowest-level-in-decades-homicides-fall-below-200-for-first-time-since-1965/)
  the fewest homicides since 1964 and the first time the city fell below
  200 killings in six decades.
