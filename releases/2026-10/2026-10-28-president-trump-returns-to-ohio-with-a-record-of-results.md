title: President Trump Returns to Ohio With a Record of Results
---
 
**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to the Buckeye State to
showcase a record of results that puts Ohio workers, families, seniors,
farmers, and communities first.** Under the leadership of President
Trump and Republicans, Ohioans are benefiting from historic tax relief,
falling crime, surging private investment, stronger rural healthcare,
and new resources for the state’s defense industry.

## **Delivering Historic Tax Relief for Ohioans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of hardworking Ohio workers, families, and
seniors.**

- **No Tax on Overtime:** More than 1.2 million Ohio workers
  [claimed](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=More%20than%201.2%20million%20Ohio%20filers%20claimed%20the%20overtime%20deduction%2C%20totaling%20about%20%243.4%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20about%2029.4%20million%20filers%20claimed%20the%20deduction%20totaling%20almost%20%2492.7%20billion.)
  $3.4 billion in No Tax on Overtime deductions in 2026.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** More than 1.5 million Ohio seniors
  [benefited](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=About%201.4%20million%20Ohioans%20benefitted%20from%20the%20senior%20deduction%2C%20with%20the%20total%20amount%20of%20deductions%20claimed%20approaching%20%247.9%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20over%2035%20million%20seniors%20benefitted%20from%20the%20deduction%20with%20more%20than%20%24193%20billion%20claimed.)
  from the new senior deduction in 2026, with the average senior
  expected to pay zero federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Ohio taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,308](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=37)
  208,000 Ohio jobs, $37 billion in state GDP, and $19 billion in wages
  for Ohioans.
- **Lowering Taxes for Small Businesses and Family Farms:** The tax cuts
  permanently [locked
  in](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/INVESTS-IN-RURAL-AMERICA.pdf)
  the increased death tax exemption (indexed for inflation), 100%
  expensing, a doubled Section 179 limit, and the 20% small business
  deduction. They also expanded crop insurance and raised reference
  prices for the first time in over a decade.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare Across Ohio**

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources to expand access,
modernize care, and improve outcomes in Ohio’s rural communities.**

- **Rural Health Transformation:** Ohio
  [secured](https://governor.ohio.gov/media/news-and-media/governor-dewine-announces-ohios-rural-health-transformation-program-award)
  more than $200 million in first-year funding under the Working
  Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation Program.
- **Modernizing Care:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/trump-administration-announces-92-million-coordinate-rural-hospital-networks-improve-maternal-care)
  $92 million across 22 rural Ohio hospitals to support coordinated care
  networks, maternal health services, and equipment and facilities
  upgrades.

## **Making Ohio Communities Safer**

**Violent crime is falling across Ohio as President Trump’s law and
order agenda takes hold.**

- Statewide homicides
  [dropped](https://www.daytondailynews.com/local/crime/new-fbi-data-crime-rate-drops-across-us-ohio/article_bf55b67f-c59b-57a8-87da-831ababfb790.html)
  16.1% last year.
- **Columbus**
  [hit](https://www.axios.com/local/columbus/2026/01/08/2025-homicides-crime-trends-ohio)
  a 16-year low in homicides in 2025, with [further
  declines](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  in 2026.
- **Cincinnati** saw homicides
  [fall](https://spectrumnews1.com/oh/columbus/news/2026/02/03/cincinnati-sees-drop-in-crimes-in-2025)
  more than 6% in 2025 and
  [drop](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  another 19% in the first half of 2026 compared with the same period
  the year before.
- **Cleveland** recorded double-digit
  [drops](https://www.police1.com/police-recruitment/cleveland-crime-drops-as-police-see-356-rise-in-cadet-applications)
  in 2025: homicides down 16%, rapes down 28%, auto thefts down 26%, and
  robberies down 24%. In the first half of 2026, rapes fell another 8%
  and robberies another 21%.

## **Unleashing Private Investment and Job Creation**

**President Trump’s pro-growth, America First policies are drawing major
private investment and new jobs across the Buckeye State.**

- **Nvidia** is
  [financing](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html)
  up to $105 billion for a new Super Intelligence data center in Pike
  County.
- **Electra** is
  [investing](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/electra-to-usher-in-the-next-era-of-aviation-with-advanced-production-facility-in-springfield-ohio)
  $850 million to open its first EL9 Ultra Short aircraft production
  facility in Springfield, creating 1,975 new jobs.
- **Stellantis** is
  [investing](https://www.13abc.com/2025/10/15/new-midsize-truck-be-assembled-toledo-part-nearly-400-million-stellantis-investment/)
  $400 million in its Toledo Assembly Complex, creating over 900 jobs.
- **Bayer** is
  [investing](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/bayer-to-invest-22-billion-in-a-new-manufacturing-site-in-ohio)
  $2.2 billion to build a pharmaceutical manufacturing campus in New
  Albany, creating about 600 permanent jobs and 1,500 construction jobs.
- **Honda** is
  [investing](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/honda-plans-invest-25-bln-build-hybrid-vehicle-plant-ohio-nikkei-says-2026-09-24/)
  up to $2.5 billion to build a new hybrid vehicle production plant in
  Ohio. 
- **Hikma Pharmaceuticals** is
  [investing](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/hikma-pharmaceuticals-announces-major-expansion-in-ohio-creating-350-new-jobs-and-investing-267-million)
  $267 million to expand its manufacturing facilities in Columbus and
  Bedford, creating 350 new jobs.
- **Weidmann** is
  [investing](https://www.journal-news.com/local/manufacturer-plans-150m-investment-at-former-middletown-paper-mill-creating-56-jobs/article_0d0c3615-cd66-58b6-8485-e725f2651142.html)
  $150 million to renovate and expand a historic former paper mill in
  Middletown, creating dozens of new full-time jobs.
- **Kreate** is
  [investing](https://www.plasticstoday.com/injection-molding/kreate-announces-100-million-ohio-manufacturing-facility-investment)
  $100 million for a new consumer and professional plastic products
  manufacturing facility in Tiffin.
- **Kimberly-Clark** is
  [investing](https://businessjournaldaily.com/a-factory-for-the-future/)
  $800 million to build its first new U.S. plant in 37 years in Warren.
- **Vallourec** is
  [investing](https://businessjournaldaily.com/vallourec-invests-48m-in-youngstown-expansion/)
  $48 million to expand its operations in Youngstown.

## **Powering Growth and Protecting Communities**

**President Trump is securing reliable energy and delivering for
communities that were neglected under the previous Administration.**

- **Protecting Ratepayers:** The Trump Administration
  [formed](https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-department-energy-ensuring-affordable-energy-access-ohio-while-powering-future/)
  a public-private partnership with SoftBank Group and SB Energy to
  build 10 gigawatts of new power generation in southern Ohio, ensuring
  corporations — not Ohio families — pay the costs of data center
  growth. The deal includes $40 million in community benefits from SB
  Energy.
- **Standing Up for East Palestine:** After years of inaction, the Trump
  Administration
  [launched](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/nih-long-term-health-research-east-palestine-ohio-train-disaster.html)
  a five-year, $10 million research initiative to study the long-term
  health impacts of the 2023 train derailment and ensure residents
  receive the medical attention they need.
- **Revitalizing American Steelmaking:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-500-million-award-revitalize-american-steelmaking)
  $500 million to
  [support](https://www.cincinnati.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/21/jd-vance-announces-grant-middletown-steel-plant/91308063007/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=false&gca-epti=z1157xxp003950l001050c003950u003942e1157xxv001942&gca-ft=180&gca-ds=sophi)
  a $1 billion investment at Cleveland-Cliffs’ Middletown Works facility
  in Middletown, protecting 2,300 jobs and strengthening our domestic
  steel supply chain.

## **Strengthening Ohio’s Defense Industry**

**President Trump is expanding Ohio’s role in national security and
high-tech defense manufacturing.**

- In October, President Trump
  [announced](https://thehill.com/newsletters/defense-national-security/6146728-cleveland-to-be-home-of-next-submarine-repair-center/)
  that Cleveland will be
  [home](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2109801412066443765?a=cmtp3_OzD8JOlMq4-eY&s=20)
  to the new U.S. Submarine Component Repair Center.
- The first Ohio-built Fury fighter drone for the U.S. Air Force [rolled
  off](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/first-ohio-built-fury-rolls-off-andurils-arsenal-1-production-line)
  the line in July at Anduril’s Arsenal-1 plant in Pickaway County — a
  $910.5 million project expected to create more than 4,000 jobs.
- The FY 2026 NDAA
  [provided](https://www.moreno.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/moreno-delivers-over-72m-in-defense-wins-for-ohio/)
  more than $72 million for Ohio defense projects, including $60 million
  for work at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base.
