title: White House Appoints 2026-2027 Class of White House Fellows
---
 
Today, the President’s Commission on White House Fellows announced the
appointment of 11 exceptional Americans who will serve as White House
Fellows for a one-year term. The 2026-2027 Class of White House Fellows
represents a cross-section of professions including law enforcement,
business, academia, and five branches of the military.

The White House Fellows program was created in 1964 by President Lyndon
B. Johnson because “freedom, in its deepest sense, requires
participation – full, zestful, knowledgeable participation”. Through
high-level work experience, an educational program, and community
service, the program provides gifted and highly motivated young
Americans with first-hand experience in the process of governing the
Nation and a sense of personal involvement in the leadership of society.
Each White House Fellow serves to remind the nation that government
exists of the people, by the people, and for the people.

Through a vigorous and highly competitive selection process, the
Commission selects patriotic Americans who demonstrate unusual ability,
high moral character, outstanding motivation, broad capacity for
leadership, and exceptional promise of future development. Selected
individuals spend one year working as full-time, paid fellows to senior
White House Staff, Cabinet Secretaries, and other top-ranking government
officials.

The application for the 2027-2028 class of White House Fellows will open
on November 2, 2026. For more information on how to apply, please visit
[whitehousefellows.gov](/Users/EGPohl1/AppData/Local/Microsoft/Windows/INetCache/Content.Outlook/495HJTTY/whitehousefellows.gov).   

**2026-2027 Class of White House Fellows:**

**Manuel Barrantes is from Bentonville, Arkansas, and is placed at the
Department of Labor.** Born in Nicaragua, he received a scholarship to
attend Harding University in Searcy, Arkansas. He spent nine years at
Walmart and Sam’s Club, beginning as an hourly associate and advancing
through leadership roles spanning store operations, merchandising,
product, and e-commerce. Most recently, he served as a director in
Walmart U.S. Last Mile Delivery, leading efforts across operations and
technology to support independent drivers who shop for and deliver
customer orders. His work has centered on the customer experience, a key
part of Walmart’s goal to be America’s favorite place to shop. Beyond
work, Manuel has mentored entrepreneurs through nonprofits, helping them
take practical steps toward launching businesses. He participated in
Leadership Benton County and the Workmatters Institute. His leadership
and service have been recognized by Arkansas Business 20 in Their 20s,
the Northwest Arkansas Business Journal’s Fast 15 and Forty Under 40,
and the Spirit of Bentonville Award. Manuel earned dual B.B.A. degrees
in Business Management and Global Economic Development from Harding
University and is nearing completion of an M.S. in Artificial
Intelligence at the University of Colorado Boulder.

**Steven D. Christopher is from Fort Myers, Florida, and is placed at
the Department of Agriculture.** A Lieutenant Colonel, Steve was
commissioned in 2007 with academic and athletic distinction from the
U.S. Air Force Academy as an Electrical Engineering major and Wings of
Blue Parachute Team Cadet Commander. He has served in reconnaissance,
flight test, formal training, and remote-piloted aircraft units and has
commanded at the Detachment and Squadron levels. Steve also graduated
with distinction from the Defense Language Institute’s Chinese-Mandarin
course, was an Olmsted Scholar in Asia, and was a National War Fellow at
Harvard Kennedy School’s Belfer Center for Science and International
Affairs. As a Foreign Area Officer, he served as the State Department’s
Deputy Division Chief for South China Sea Affairs. Prior to his current
position, Steve was the Squadron Commander of the Department of the Air
Force’s Indo-Pacific Military Personnel Exchange Program, where he led
172 personnel across 13 countries. Steve is a graduate of Euro-NATO
Joint Jet Pilot Training and a Senior Pilot with more than 2,200 flying
hours, including over 1,400 hours of combat experience in the RQ-170,
MC-12W, and MQ-1B.

**Victor Hooper is from Santa Clarita, California, and is placed at the
Department of Housing and Urban Development.** Victor is a Sergeant II
with the Los Angeles Police Department (LAPD), where he protects and
serves the Los Angeles community. He serves as the officer in charge of
the LAPD’s Leadership Unit, which delivers advanced leadership training
to middle managers from domestic and international law enforcement
agencies. Throughout his career, Victor has held numerous specialized
roles as both a patrol officer and supervisor. He previously served as
an infantryman in the U.S. Army’s 75th Ranger Regiment, 2nd Battalion,
during the early years of the Global War on Terror. He deployed overseas
multiple times and earned the US Army Ranger Tab, Combat Infantryman
Badge, and Combat Parachutist Badge. Victor is deeply committed to
continuous improvement and professional excellence. He earned a
bachelor’s degree in Criminal Justice from California State University,
Bakersfield, and a master’s degree in Forensic Science from Oklahoma
State University. In his spare time, he volunteers with his local parks
and recreation department and supports various charitable organizations.

**Christopher Huff is from San Antonio, Texas, and is placed at the
Small Business Administration.** A Lieutenant Colonel, Chris is a United
States Marine Corps officer whose career has taken him from leading
Marines in combat to flying military aircraft across the globe and
advising senior leaders on national security. He has served as an
infantry officer, AH-1Z Cobra attack helicopter pilot, and MV-22 Osprey
tiltrotor pilot, deploying to Afghanistan, the Indo-Pacific, and the
Horn of Africa. He recently served at Headquarters Marine Corps,
supporting Department of the Navy and Marine Corps leadership with
operational and strategic assessments of global military operations.
Chris is committed to public service and lifelong learning. He attends
the United States Army War College, is currently completing his second
master’s degree with the Naval Postgraduate School, and earned his
undergraduate degree from the Rawls College of Business at Texas Tech
University. His professional interests center on national security,
strategic competition, emerging technologies, and strengthening the
institutions that sustain the constitutional republic of the United
States.

**Blake Hunnewell is from New York City, and is placed at the
International Development Finance Corporation.** A Major in the Army,
Blake most recently served as Special Assistant to the Assistant
Secretary of War for Special Operations and Low-Intensity Conflict
advising senior leaders on irregular warfare, counterterrorism, and
special operations. As a U.S. Army Special Forces officer and defense
policy professional, he previously served as a policy advisor in the
Office of the Under Secretary of War for Policy, where his experience
spanned special operations counterterrorism and homeland defense. He has
led conventional and special operations units across Europe and Africa.
Blake is an International Strategy Forum Fellow, played rugby for the
West Point Black Knights, and has research interests in national
security, leadership, and emerging technologies. Blake holds a B.S. in
Mechanical Engineering from the United States Military Academy at West
Point, an M.A. in International Affairs from King’s College London, and
a Master of International Public Policy from Johns Hopkins SAIS as a
U.S. Space Force Schriever Scholar. He lives in Washington D.C. with his
wife and young son.

**Sophie Lekas McCraith is from Chicago, Illinois, and is placed at the
National Energy Dominance Council.** She is a Lieutenant in the United
States Navy, serving as a Submarine Warfare Officer. Her most recent
assignment was aboard the ballistic missile submarine USS WYOMING (SSBN
742), where she completed three strategic deterrent patrols and
qualified as a Department of Energy Nuclear Engineer. As Communications
Officer and Officer of the Deck, she directed highly classified message
processing, complex planning for joint exercises, and safe navigation
through the East Coast’s most challenging waterways. Sophie has
published research applying feedback control to additive manufacturing
in peer-reviewed engineering journals and international conference
proceedings. An accomplished violinist and oboist, she is passionate
about performing and sharing music with her community. Sophie earned a
Master of Science in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford,
England, and a Bachelor of Science with Distinction in Aerospace
Engineering from the United States Naval Academy, where she was a
two-time National Collegiate Boxing Champion and the first female
four-time Brigade Boxing Champion.

**Cliff Rhoten is from Wimer, Oregon, and is placed at the Department of
the Interior. ** Cliff is a fifth-generation Oregonian and
first-generation college graduate whose career spans the private sector,
military service, and nonprofit leadership.  He recently led a $200
million global industrial portfolio at 3M, where he also held corporate
strategy roles.  Prior to joining 3M, Cliff served as an active-duty
lieutenant in the U.S. Navy.  Across five overseas deployments, he led
Sailors and Marines while assigned to a guided-missile frigate, coastal
riverine squadron, and Marine Corps ANGLICO.  Cliff has served as Vice
Chairman of the Board of Rise, Inc., a nonprofit that provides
vocational services to more than 2,500 people with disabilities each
year.  Cliff helped fund his education as a wildland firefighter and
earned an M.B.A. from the Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern
University and a B.A. in Accounting from George Fox University.  He is a
current Term Member of the Council on Foreign Relations.

**Nicholas Rogers is from Boca Raton, Florida, and is placed at the
Department of Health and Human Services.** He is a Lieutenant Commander
in the U.S. Coast Guard, specializing in maritime counter smuggling, law
enforcement, search and rescue, and finance. He most recently commanded
Coast Guard Cutter RICHARD DIXON out of San Juan, Puerto Rico, where he
served as on-scene commander for numerous search-and-rescue and
counternarcotics operations, seizing $60 million in narcotics and saving
more than 400 mariners in distress. His cutter was hand selected to lead
multiple humanitarian initiatives including the armed response that
reopened Haiti’s capital port to humanitarian aid, medical supplies, and
fuel following the government’s request for assistance during civil
unrest and a cholera outbreak. He previously served as an Assault Force
Commander of a Direct Action Section within the Coast Guard’s Maritime
Security Response Team, where he led an advanced interdiction team
combating terrorism-sponsored weapon and lethal aid shipments. He holds
a B.S. in Marine and Environmental Science from the U.S. Coast Guard
Academy, an M.B.A. from the Wharton School, and a Master in Law from
Penn Carey Law.

**Kyle Saleeby is from Destin, Florida, and is placed at the National
Security Council.** He was previously the Research Program Director for
the Georgia Tech Manufacturing Institute, where he led three
multidisciplinary research teams to design, develop, and deploy critical
manufacturing technologies to strengthen U.S. industrial competitiveness
and national security. As an engineer, Kyle is passionate about
data-driven manufacturing and workforce development, translating
research innovations into practical solutions for industry and
government partners. As an inventor, he holds patents for AI-driven
process optimization and novel manufacturing methods and earned GT’s
Research Program Impact Award. As an educator, he teaches precision
manufacturing techniques, mentoring the next generation of manufacturing
leaders. Beyond research leadership, he founded the Advanced
Manufacturing Pathways program, introducing high school students
toengineering, additive manufacturing, and data-driven design through
fabrication and racing of 3D-printed pinewood derby cars. Kyle earned a
B.S. in mechanicalengineering from MIT, followed by M.S. and Ph.D.
degrees from Georgia Tech.  

**Emily Tucker is from White Lake, Michigan, and is placed at the White
House Office of Science and Technology Policy.** With a 16-year military
career, including 13-plus years of active-duty Army service, Em served
in logistics and information operations roles supporting special
operations units. She deployed as a convoy commander in Afghanistan in
2012, multiple domestic hurricane responses, and to Eastern Europe in
2022 as an information operations officer. She is a Naval War College
graduate and now serves as a Major Promotable in the Army Reserve, where
she is a Strategic Planner on the Joint Staff, J-39. Em’s military
experience is complemented by her private sector career, focusing on
defense work at McKinsey & Company in Washington, D.C., after
transitioning through the SkillBridge program as a member of the firm’s
first firmwide cohort. Em’s passions include national security, defense
technology, and innovation. An advocate for veterans transitioning out
of uniform, she mentors junior colleagues navigating the path between
military service and the private sector, and serves as a coach for both
the Honor Foundation and the First Tee. She began her career as a golf
professional, having earned her PGA certification from Methodist
University.

**Elise Willding is from Dallas, Texas and is placed at the Department
of War.** Elise is the Founder and Principal of M Partners, an advisory
firm providing financial and strategic advice across the full capital
structure, including debt and equity financing, recapitalizations,
restructurings, acquisitions, divestitures, and special situations.
Prior to founding M Partners, Elise spent seven years with Bain &
Company advising corporate and private equity clients spanning
industries from industrials to higher education on strategy, mergers and
acquisitions, operating model design, and organizational transformation.
After Bain, Elise served as Chief Strategy Officer of a middle-market,
private equity-backed home services platform where she led the
integration of seven operating companies, structured and managed
transformation initiatives, and served as an executive coach. Elise
holds an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School and both a Master of
Science in Systems Engineering and a Bachelor of Science in Management
Science from Southern Methodist University. Elise received SMU’s
distinguished M Award for leadership and service.
