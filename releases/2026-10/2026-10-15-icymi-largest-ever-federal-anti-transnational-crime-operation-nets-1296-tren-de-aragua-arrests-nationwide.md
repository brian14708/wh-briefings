**President Donald J. Trump is systematically dismantling the
bloodthirsty Tren de Aragua gang that Biden’s open borders allowed to
pour into American communities.** The Trump Administration has launched
the largest federal campaign against transnational crime in U.S. history
— identifying, arresting, and removing these vicious foreign criminals
and driving violent crime down nationwide.

**The *Washington Examiner***
[**highlights**](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/national-security/4765683/largest-federal-anti-transnational-crime-operation-tren-de-aragua-arrests/)
**the Trump Administration’s latest effort:**

*“The Trump administration has waged an aggressive campaign against
Venezuelan gang Tren de Aragua, whose members entered the United States
through the vulnerable U.S.-Mexico border during the Biden
administration.*

*The White House, Department of Homeland Security, and Department of
Justice launched Homeland Security Task Forces in 2025 with the goal of
identifying, finding, and removing criminal rings and terrorist
organizations embedded in U.S. communities, particularly Tren de
Aragua.*

*It is the largest campaign against transnational crime that the
government has undertaken in history and one of the reasons violent
crime rates overall have dropped precipitously nationwide since 2025…*

*Task force data publicly released for the first time to the Washington
Examiner showed law enforcement arrested 1,296 Tren de Aragua gang
members between Jan. 20, 2025, and Oct. 8, 2026, and charged nearly 350
Tren de Aragua members and associates with federal crimes.”*
