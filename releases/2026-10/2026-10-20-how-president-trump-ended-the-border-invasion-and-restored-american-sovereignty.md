**President Donald J. Trump was returned to office with a clear mandate
to end the invasion that Democrats allowed to overwhelm communities,
drain public resources, and endanger public safety.** In less than two
years, the Trump Administration has delivered the largest reduction in
the illegal alien population in modern history, driven illegal crossings
to an all-time low, and enacted sweeping reforms that finally put
American citizens first.

## **Historic Progress: The Numbers**

**The Trump Administration reversed years of open border policies and
produced the largest sustained drop in the illegal alien population in
modern American history.**

- The illegal alien population has been
  [reduced](https://cis.org/Report/Census-Bureau-Survey-Shows-Total-ForeignBorn-Down-29-Million)
  by at least 2.3 million, with more than three million migrants leaving
  the country during President Trump’s second term — the largest
  reduction in illegal immigration in modern history.
- Nearly 1.2 million illegal aliens have been deported or removed, and
  nearly 1.3 million illegal alien arrests have been carried out.
- The United States
  [recorded](https://abcnews.com/US/us-1st-time-50-years-experienced-negative-net/story?id=129175522)
  negative net migration in 2025 for the first time in at least 50
  years.
- Catch-and-release was
  [ended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/securing-our-borders/),
  delivering [17 straight
  months](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  of zero releases at the border and [zero Border Patrol
  releases](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  in FY2026 — the first fiscal year in U.S. history with that result.
- Illegal southern border crossings were
  [cut](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  by 95% from Biden-era levels in FY2026,
  [recording](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  the lowest number of illegal border crossings in American history.
- Nationwide encounters in FY2026 were the
  [lowest](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest)
  in agency history (under 380,000), compared with roughly 2.7 million
  per year under Biden.
- Entries by asylum seekers at U.S.-Mexico entry points have been
  [slashed](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2108676307110555817/photo/1)
  by 99.9%.
- Asylum is now
  [granted](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/04/09/us/politics/trump-miller-immigration-judges-purge.html#:~:text=Asylum%20grant%20rates%20have%20plummeted)
  in just 7% of cases, down from more than 50% under Biden.
- The immigration court backlog has been
  [reduced](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/pr/eoir-announces-significant-immigration-court-milestones)
  from more than 4.18 million cases to under 3.09 million — the
  [sharpest
  decrease](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/pr/eoir-announces-significant-immigration-court-milestones#:~:text=sharpest%20decrease%20in%20caseload%20in%20EOIR%E2%80%99s%20history.)
  in the Executive Office for Immigration Review’s history.
- More than 12,100 gang members have been arrested, including more than
  1,500 Tren de Aragua affiliates and nearly 2,000 MS-13 affiliates,
  along with more than 2,200 known or suspected terrorists (more than
  2,000 of whom have been removed).
- Nearly one million pounds of narcotics have been
  [seized](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  through Customs and Border Protection in FY2026 (63% above the prior
  four-year average), and more than 624,000 pounds have been interdicted
  by the U.S. Coast Guard, including more than 260,000 pounds of cocaine
  in [Operation Pacific
  Viper](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/24/us-coast-guard-surpasses-260000-total-pounds-cocaine-seized-operation-pacific-viper)
  alone — preventing over 219 million lethal doses from entering
  American communities.
- Fentanyl trafficking at the southern border
  [collapsed](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/09/30/cbp-reports-drug-seizures-surge-again-august)
  by 56%, and fentanyl-linked deaths
  [plunged](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/exclusive-fentanyl-deaths-plunge-22-trump-white-house-touts-crackdown-deadly-drug-crisis)
  22% in a single year.
- Drug overdoses have
  [fallen](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/10/16/trump-oversees-more-than-40-percent-drop-in-drug-overdose-deaths-since-peak-under-biden/)
  by more than 40% from the Biden-era peak.
- America recorded its
  [lowest](https://blogs.cdc.gov/nchs/2026/07/02/7934/) overall death
  rate ever in 2025, driven [in large
  part](https://www.wsj.com/health/u-s-death-rate-falls-to-its-lowest-point-on-record-9b7777c5#:~:text=The%20main%20driver%20of%20the%20record%20low%20was%20a%20continued%20drop%20in%20drug%20overdose%20deaths%2C%20said%20Farida%20Ahmad%2C%20a%20health%20scientist%20at%20the%20CDC%20and%20one%20of%20the%20report%E2%80%99s%20co%2Dauthors.)
  by [sharp
  declines](https://www.newsweek.com/why-us-death-rate-has-fallen-record-low-12154744#:~:text=Although%20the%20provisional%20mortality%20report%20does%20not%20identify%20a%20single%20cause%20for%20the%20record%2Dlow%20death%20rate%2C%20Ahmad%20pointed%20to%20falling%20overdose%20deaths%20as%20a%20significant%20factor.)
  in drug fatalities.
- Migration from majority-Muslim countries has been
  [cut](https://www.cato.org/blog/legal-immigration-majority-muslim-nations-was-cut-96-2024)
  by 96% since 2024 and overall migration [roughly in
  half](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/10/18/us/trump-immigrant-visas-decline.html),
  with visa issuances down 89% for Africa and 81% for the Middle East.
- Border-related deaths
  [fell](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest#:~:text=President%20Trump%E2%80%99s%20policies,the%20Biden%20Administration.)
  nearly 80% in FY2026 compared to the Biden Administration
- International student enrollment is
  [projected](https://www.nafsa.org/fall2026outlook) to decrease by
  another 111,000 compared to last year.
- More than 300,000 visas have been
  [revoked](https://nypost.com/2026/10/20/us-news/over-300k-visas-revoked-so-far-in-trumps-second-term-state-department-announces/),
  including at least 4,000
  [tied](https://nypost.com/2026/10/20/us-news/over-300k-visas-revoked-so-far-in-trumps-second-term-state-department-announces/#:~:text=At%20least%204%2C000%20visas%20were%20yanked%20from%20foreign%20nationals%20%E2%80%9Cwho%20engage%20in%20or%20facilitated%20birth%20tourism%2C%E2%80%9D%20the%20official%20noted.)
  to birth tourism and 6,000 from [foreign
  students](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/rubios-state-department-yanks-more-than-6k-student-visas-due-assault-burglary-support-terrorism)
  who committed crimes or have ties to terrorism. More than 700 visas
  from Chinese nationals have been
  [revoked](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/10/16/trumps-birth-tourism-task-force-chinese/)
  by President Trump’s Birth Tourism Prevention Task Force since it
  launched in August.
- More than 157,000 unaccompanied alien children who were lost under the
  Biden Administration — often to sex trafficking, forced labor, and
  other atrocities — have been
  [located](https://www.ntd.com/homan-says-157000-missing-unaccompanied-immigrant-children-located_1175219.html).

## **Securing the Southern Border**

**On Day One, the Trump Administration declared a national emergency,
restored physical barriers, and deployed the military to restore control
at the southern border.**

- President Trump
  [declared](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/declaring-a-national-emergency-at-the-southern-border-of-the-united-states/)
  a national border emergency on Day One and
  [deployed](https://www.northcom.mil/BorderSecurity/) thousands of
  military personnel to protect the southern border against invasion.
- Border wall construction was immediately
  [ordered](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/declaring-a-national-emergency-at-the-southern-border-of-the-united-states/)
  to resume. Approximately 290 miles of new border barriers have been
  constructed, including 131 miles of primary wall, with $34.3 billion
  [obligated](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  from the Working Families Tax Cuts Act for Smart Wall projects.
- The successful Remain in Mexico policy was
  [reinstated](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-administration-reinstating-remain-mexico-program-2025-01-21/).
- The Trump Administration
  [expanded](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/interactive/2026/09/21/how-trump-turned-refugee-bureau-into-410-million-deportation-operation/)
  third-country removal agreements with dozens of nations to expedite
  deportations, enabling the
  [removal](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/journal/feature/us-third-country-deportations)
  of more than 25,000 illegal aliens to countries other than their own.
- The failed Biden-era CBP One app was
  [repurposed](https://www.dhs.gov/cbphome) into a self-deportation
  tool.
- The Pentagon
  [created](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/pentagon-congress/2025/05/02/defense-department-designates-second-military-zone-on-southern-border/)
  National Defense Areas along the border in New Mexico and Texas, where
  illegal crossers can be charged with trespassing on a military
  installation.
- Visa processing was
  [paused](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/immigrant-visa-processing-updates-for-nationalities-at-high-risk-of-public-benefits-usage.html)
  for 75 high-risk countries, visa interview waiver categories were
  [tightened](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/interview-waiver-update-sept-18-2025.html)
  to require most applicants to attend in-person interviews, and a new
  visa integrity fee along with several other new fees was
  [implemented](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/18/visa-integrity-fee-what-to-know-about-new-travel-fee-to-enter-the-us-.html)
  to enhance the integrity of the visa application process and deter
  overstays.
- Temporary Protected Status was
  [terminated](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R49346.html) for
  13 countries, including
  [Somalia](https://abcnews.go.com/Politics/dhs-announces-termination-protected-status-somalis-after-group/story?id=129167236#:~:text=The%20Trump%20administration%20announced%20Tuesday,was%20%22barely%20a%20country.%22),
  [Venezuela](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/dhs-terminates-2021-designation-of-venezuela-for-temporary-protected-status),
  and
  [Haiti](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/temporary-protected-status/temporary-protected-status-designated-country-haiti#:~:text=ALERT%3A%20Secretary%20of%20Homeland%20Security%20Kristi%20Noem%C2%A0has%20terminated%20Temporary%20Protected%20Status%20for%20Haiti%20and%20TPS%20benefits%20will%20no%20longer%20be%20in%20effect%20starting%20at%2011%3A59%20p.m.%20on%20Feb.%203%2C%202026.%20Additional%20information%20is%20available%20in%20the%C2%A0Federal%20Register%20notice.).
- Refugee resettlement was immediately
  [suspended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/realigning-the-united-states-refugee-admissions-program/)
  and the number of refugees admitted was dramatically
  [reduced](https://www.npr.org/2025/10/30/g-s1-95823/trump-administration-sets-lowest-ever-cap-on-refugee-admissions).
- Illicit fentanyl and its core precursors were
  [designated](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/12/designating-fentanyl-as-a-weapon-of-mass-destruction/) as
  Weapons of Mass Destruction, enabling harsher penalties, expanded
  seizure authorities, and treating this poison with the full force of
  national security tools.
- The de minimis loophole was
  [closed](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/cbp-ready-enforce-end-de-minimis-loophole-securing-borders-and),
  ending the flow of millions of uninspected packages that traffickers
  used to smuggle fentanyl precursors and finished product into the
  country.
- Migration through the Darién Gap
  [collapsed](https://www.dailywire.com/news/how-policy-not-walls-quieted-the-darien-gap-and-no-one-is-talking-about-it)
  by more than 99%, choking off a major transit route used under the
  prior administration.

## **Expanding Interior Enforcement and Accelerating Deportations**

**The Trump Administration has expanded enforcement capacity,
accelerated removals, and targeted the criminal networks that exploit
the border.**

- Immigration court hearings have been
  [accelerated](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/26/nx-s1-5830474/trump-immigration-courts-mega-masters)
  while welcoming the
  [largest-ever](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/eoir-announces-77-immigration-judges-and-5-temporary-immigration-judges)
  class of new immigration judges.
- The immigration enforcement workforce has been
  [expanded](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/01/03/ice-announces-historic-120-manpower-increase-thanks-recruitment-campaign-brought),
  including more than
  [doubling](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/01/03/ice-announces-historic-120-manpower-increase-thanks-recruitment-campaign-brought)
  the number of ICE agents and [record
  high](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/border-patrol-sets-record-21471-agents-board)
  Border Patrol staffing.
- Federal immigration detention capacity has been
  [dramatically](https://www.foxnews.com/us/ice-ramps-up-deportation-push-92600-new-beds-38-3b-expansion)
  [expanded](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/article/trump-immigrant-detention),
  including opening the
  [largest](https://time.com/7310657/ice-immigration-detention-texas-lone-star-lockup/)
  migrant detention facility in U.S. history.
- Deportation flights have been
  [accelerated](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/immigration/4614339/ice-deportations-soared-in-may/)
  to record highs, and a record was set for the highest number of
  illegal aliens arrested in a month in August.
- More than 2,500 agreements have been
  [secured](https://www.ice.gov/identify-and-arrest/287g) with state and
  local law enforcement under Immigration and Nationality Act Section
  287(g).
- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/01/29/president-trump-signs-laken-riley-act-law)
  the Laken Riley Act into law, requiring detention of illegal aliens
  charged with serious crimes.
- President Trump
  [invoked](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2025/03/16/alien-enemies-act-venezuela-el-salvador-prison/)
  the Alien Enemies Act to expeditiously deport violent illegal alien
  gang members.
- Over ten Latin American cartels were
  [designated](https://www.state.gov/foreign-terrorist-organizations) as
  terrorist groups, including Tren de Aragua, MS-13, and Clan del Golfo.
- Major immigration enforcement operations were launched in cities
  around the country, including [Metro
  Surge](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/02/04/dhs-reaches-more-4000-arrests-illegal-aliens-including-murderers-sex-offenders-gang),
  [Midway
  Blitz](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/09/08/ice-launches-operation-midway-blitz-honor-katie-abraham-target-criminal-illegal),
  and [Catahoula
  Crunch](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/12/03/dhs-launches-operation-catahoula-crunch-new-orleans-targeting-criminal-illegal).
- The Victims of Immigration Crime Engagement Office was
  [re-launched](https://dailycaller.com/2025/04/09/trump-admin-relaunches-voice-office/)
  to assist Americans harmed by immigrant crime after it was shuttered
  under Biden.
- The practice of conducting neighborhood investigations of potential
  new citizens was
  [restored](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/dhs-strengthens-integrity-in-nations-immigration-system-returns-to-commonsense-legal-immigration#:~:text=We%20restored%20the%20practice%20of%20conducting%20neighborhood%20investigations%20of%20potential%20new%20citizens%20to%20verify%20their%20claims%20and%20ensure%20we%20get%20a%20full%20picture%20of%20their%20character.)
  to verify their claims and ensure a full picture of their character.
- Enforcement of the federal requirement that illegal aliens register
  with the government was
  [resumed](https://www.cozen.com/news-resources/publications/2025/alien-registration-requirement),
  with criminal penalties for those who refuse.
- So-called sanctuary policies were
  [terminated](https://www.axios.com/2025/08/15/trump-dc-bondi-sanctuary-policies-immigration)
  in Washington, D.C., while
  [moving](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-protects-american-communities-from-criminal-aliens/)
  to strip federal funding from sanctuary jurisdictions.
- A [stipend
  program](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/05/05/dhs-announces-historic-travel-assistance-and-stipend-voluntary-self-deportation)
  and [fine
  program](https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2025/10/21/exclusive-trumps-dhs-issues-nearly-10-billion-fines-illegals-so-they-self-deport/)
  were launched to incentivize self-deportation and reduce costs.

## **Tightening Legal Migration and Protecting American Workers**

**The Trump Administration has reformed legal immigration pathways,
ended abuses of temporary programs, and restored priority for American
workers and wages.**

- Entry of H-1B aliens was
  [restricted](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-suspends-the-entry-of-certain-alien-nonimmigrant-workers/)
  unless their petition is accompanied by a $100,000 payment, the random
  selection process for H-1B registrations was
  [ended](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/dhs-changes-process-for-awarding-h-1b-work-visas-to-better-protect-american-workers),
  and H-1B lottery registrations were [driven
  down](https://www.ellis.com/resources/uscis-reaches-h1b-cap-fy-2027)
  38% for FY2027 under the new merit-based system, with no second
  selection planned.
- Bonds of up to $15,000 were
  [implemented](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-to-demand-bonds-of-up-to-15000-for-visa-applications-from-12-more-countries)
  for visa applications from citizens of high-risk countries, and
  student visa applicants are
  [required](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/department-of-state-announces-social-media-vetting-for-student-visa-applicants/)
  to make their social media public for screening.
- The practice of granting automatic extensions of employment
  authorization documents was
  [ended](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/dhs-ends-automatic-extension-of-employment-authorization).
- Access to immigrant work permits was
  [restricted](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/dhs-advances-rule-nixing-automatic-renewal-of-work-permits),
  prioritizing U.S. workers and tightening security.
- Employers’ ability to pay substandard wages to foreign workers in
  certain visa programs was
  [ended](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/eta/eta20260326-0) to
  protect American workers.
- A fixed time period of program completion or four years, whichever is
  shorter, was
  [established](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/2025/08/dhs-posts-notice-of-proposed-rulemaking-establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and)
  for foreign students and exchange visitors.
- The data schools must provide on students studying in the U.S. was
  significantly
  [expanded](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/03/2025-16875/agency-information-collection-activities-student-and-exchange-visitor-information-system-sevis),
  including information on programs of study, funding sources,
  graduation dates, and more.
- The $1 million Trump Gold Card was
  [launched](https://cbsaustin.com/news/nation-world/golden-ticket-to-the-us-applications-go-live-for-trumps-new-1-million-visas)
  as a path to residency for vetted, high-value applicants.
- A new policy was
  [established](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/us-citizenship-and-immigration-services-will-grant-adjustment-of-status-only-in-extraordinary)
  that generally requires aliens in the U.S. temporarily to return to
  their home countries to apply for a green card.
- The citizenship test was
  [reformed](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/uscis-unveils-first-changes-to-naturalization-test-in-multi-step-overhaul-of-american-citizenship)
  to better assess aliens’ understanding of American history,
  government, and values, and to better ensure aliens meet all
  eligibility requirements.
- Naturalization fees were
  [raised](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/23/2026-12542/naturalization-application-fee-adjustments)
  so American taxpayers are no longer subsidizing the process.
- An unprecedented denaturalization effort was
  [launched](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/denaturalization-us-citizens-fraud-crimes-trump-administration/)
  targeting those who fraudulently obtained U.S. citizenship.
- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/ending-birth-tourism/)
  an Executive Order ending birth tourism and launched a dedicated Birth
  Tourism Prevention Task Force that has already revoked thousands of
  visas.

## **Protecting Taxpayers, Communities, and the Rule of Law**

**The Trump Administration has closed access to taxpayer benefits for
illegal aliens, strengthened verification systems, and secured legal
authority to enforce immigration law.**

- Illegal aliens were
  [banned](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/prwora-hhs-bans-illegal-aliens-accessing-taxpayer-funded-programs.html)
  from more than a dozen taxpayer-funded healthcare programs.
- Foreign nationals — including green card holders — were
  [banned](https://www.sba.gov/article/2026/03/09/sba-bans-foreign-nationals-accessing-sba-backed-loans)
  from accessing Small Business Administration loans intended for
  American entrepreneurs.
- Government-backed mortgage financing for illegal aliens and H-1B visa
  holders was [ended](https://www.hud.gov/news/hud-no-25-048).
- The human trafficking hotline was
  [upgraded](https://www.dailysignal.com/2025/09/22/exclusive-trumps-hhs-revamps-human-trafficking-hotline-after-biden-admins-provider-missed-calls/).
- Use of federal assistance and grant funding to provide services to
  illegal aliens was
  [prohibited](https://www.regulations.gov/document/OMB-2026-0034-0001).
- Illegal aliens were
  [blocked](https://dailycaller.com/2025/07/10/exclusive-trumps-ed-dept-ends-free-tuition-for-illegals/)
  from accessing federal funds for higher education programming.
- Illegal aliens were
  [barred](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0321) from
  abusing refundable individual income tax credit benefits.
- Data sharing programs on illegal aliens receiving taxpayer-funded
  health insurance benefits were
  [commenced](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/12/29/trump-admin-can-share-immigrants-medicaid-data-ice-judge-rules-00707716).
- The financial system was
  [secured](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0523)
  against illegal aliens by urging financial institutions to detect and
  report illicit activity.
- A new remittance tax was
  [implemented](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-proposed-regulations-on-the-new-remittance-transfer-tax-established-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill)
  on money sent out of the country by illegal aliens.
- More than 206 million benefits eligibility checks were
  [conducted](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/brochures/USCIS_2025_EndofYearReview_508.pdf)
  in 2025.
- The Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) program was
