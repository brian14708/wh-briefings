**President Donald J. Trump and Vice President JD Vance are aggressively
shutting down the waste, fraud, and abuse that has drained billions from
American taxpayers.** As head of the White House Task Force to Eliminate
Fraud, Vice President Vance this week [called
on](https://x.com/JDVance/status/2110752082399555799) officials at every
level of government to join him in a pledge to protect vulnerable
Americans, keep taxpayer-funded benefits for Americans only, stop fraud
before the money goes out, and hold fraudsters accountable.

behind the pledge:**
