title: "WHAT THEY ARE SAYING: President Trump Puts America\u2019s Fishermen First\
  \ and Secures American Seafood Dominance"
---
 
President Trump has delivered historic wins for America’s fishing
industry by putting American fishermen first, unlocking billions of
dollars in economic growth, revitalizing the industry, and providing
critical regulatory relief.

Just one week ago, the Trump
Administration [unveiled](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-the-america-first-seafood-strategy-and-seafood-gov/) its
historic America First Seafood Strategy and
launched [Seafood.gov](http://seafood.gov/), to **ensure that American
fishermen, workers, industry, and coastal communities thrive from Alaska
to Maine**. The Trump Administration’s strategy is simple: end federal
policies that punished American producers and rewarded foreign
dependence. 

President Trump wasted no time in implementing important elements of his
America First Seafood Strategy by announcing the new “U.S. Caught
Seafood” voluntary label and making available more than $5 billion in
guaranteed loans to expand American meat and seafood processing. 

**Here’s what they’re saying:**

  Lutnick**](https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/trump-administration-releases-america-first-seafood-strategy):
  “The species managed in U.S. waters are American icons – from Maine
  lobster to Gulf shrimp, from Alaskan salmon to Florida stone crab,
  American seafood is a rich part of U.S. history and our vibrant
  coastal economy. The America First Seafood Strategy ensures that the
  future of seafood in the United States will be built by American
  fishermen, American workers, and American industry—not by foreign
  imports.”
