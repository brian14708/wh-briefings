President Donald J. Trump is delivering on his promise to restore law
and order. According to the [DOJ’s National Crime Victimization
Survey](https://us.list-manage.com/TA2FtW6Kzcq?e=e070a83827&c2id=b10bf9502296de100c94915c3befd22c),
violent crime PLUMMETED in 2025 under President Trump compared to 2024
and the lawlessness Americans witnessed during the Biden Administration.
**Under President Trump, Americans have seen the largest one-year
reduction in violent crime in the report’s history. **  
  
This is the direct result of having President Trump at the helm— a
leader who respects LAW AND ORDER and puts the safety and security of
the American people first.  After securing the border, deporting
dangerous criminal illegal aliens, respecting our law enforcement
officers, and cracking down on drug trafficking, crime is dropping at a
historic rate. 

Meanwhile, as President Trump makes our communities safer, Radical
Democrats continue to put criminals over law-abiding Americans, backing
policies like cashless bail, abolishing prisons, open borders, and
dangerous sanctuary city policies that threaten public safety.   
  
The results prove that President Trump and Republicans are delivering
safer streets, where Radical Democrats delivered lawlessness, open
borders and no respect for the rule of law in this country.
