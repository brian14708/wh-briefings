  and Stronger Communities

**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to the Magnolia State to
highlight a proven record of results that put Mississippi workers,
families, seniors, farmers, and communities first.** Under the
leadership of President Trump and Republicans, Mississippians are
benefiting from historic tax relief, surging private investment and job
creation, stronger rural healthcare, major support for farmers, and new
resources for the state’s defense and shipbuilding industry.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Mississippians**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of Mississippi workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 500,000 Mississippi
  seniors
  [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Mississippi-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf)
  from the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay
  zero federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Mississippi taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$4,018](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026 — among the highest in the nation.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=26) 46,000
  Mississippi jobs, $8 billion in state GDP, and $4 billion in workers’
  wages.
- **Lowering Taxes for Family Farms and Small Businesses:** The tax cuts
  [made](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/INVESTS-IN-RURAL-AMERICA.pdf)
  the increased death tax exemption permanent and indexed it for
  inflation,
  [made](https://home.treasury.gov/wftc/fact-sheets/Delivering%20Relief%20to%20America%27s%20Heartland.pdf)
  100% expensing permanent, doubled the Section 179 limit, made the 20%
  small business deduction permanent, expanded crop insurance, and
  [increased](https://www.fb.org/intel/markets/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-final-agricultural-provisions)
  reference prices for the first time in over a decade.
- **Strengthening the Farm Safety Net:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [delivered](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/news-events/news/10-19-2026/usda-boosts-mississippi-agricultural-economy-4691-million-safety-net)
  more than $469 million in farm safety net and conservation program
  payments to Mississippi farmers, producers, and landowners — part of
  the first meaningful investment in these programs in decades.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare Across Mississippi**

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources to expand access,
modernize care, and improve outcomes in Mississippi’s rural
communities.**

- **Rural Health
  Transformation:** Mississippi [secured](https://governorreeves.ms.gov/governor-reeves-announces-mississippi-awarded-nearly-206-million-to-strengthen-healthcare-in-rural-communities/) $206
  million in first-year
  [funding](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states)
  from the Working Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation
  Program — one of the largest awards among all states — to expand
  access, strengthen the workforce, and streamline care.
- **Modernizing Care Delivery:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/trump-administration-announces-104-million-transform-rural-healthcare-across-mississippi-through)
  more than $104 million through 167 grants for Mississippi rural health
  technology upgrades, specialty and maternal care, and telehealth.
- **Advancing Telehealth:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.wicker.senate.gov/2026/09/08/wicker-hyde-smith-guest-welcome-over-4-million-for-mississippi-telehealth-efforts/)
  the University of Mississippi Medical Center $4.25 million to renew
  its status as a federal Center of Excellence in Telehealth — one of
  only two in the country — expanding remote care for rural and
  underserved communities.

**President Trump’s pro-growth, America First agenda is driving major
private investment and job creation throughout the Magnolia State, from
advanced manufacturing to logistics, shipbuilding, and technology.**

- **ALO** is
  [investing](https://magnoliatribune.com/2026/10/12/alo-investing-over-176-million-in-southaven-for-national-distribution-center/)
  $176 million to open a national distribution center in Southaven,
  creating more than 400 jobs.
- **Gulf Ship** is
  [investing](https://magnoliatribune.com/2026/09/02/gulf-ship-investing-44-million-to-expand-gulfport-shipyard/)
  $44 million to modernize its Gulfport shipyard, creating 121 jobs.
- **Raytheon** is
  [investing](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55401779/raytheon-completes-50m-expansion-of-electronic-warfare-and-radar-systems-manufacturing-facility)
  $50 million to expand its electronic warfare and radar systems
  manufacturing facility in Forest, creating 100 jobs.
- **Southwire** is
  [investing](https://www.southwire.com/blogs/starkville-expansion) $256
  million to expand its Starkville operations, creating 128 jobs.
- **Aluminum Dynamics** is
  [investing](https://mississippi.org/news/aluminum-dynamics-to-develop-recycled-aluminum-slab-center-in-lowndes-county/)
  $200 million to develop a recycled aluminum slab center in Lowndes
  County, creating 100 jobs.
- **SIP Manufacturing** is
  [investing](https://www.wlbt.com/2026/07/22/sip-manufacturing-announces-100m-investment-southaven/)
  more than $100 million to build a production, packaging, and shipping
  facility in Southaven, creating 187 jobs.
- **Amazon** is
  [investing](https://governorreeves.ms.gov/amazon-continues-mississippi-expansion-announcing-plans-to-invest-a-total-of-25-billion-across-the-magnolia-state/)
  an additional $12 billion in part to expand the company’s data center
  operation in Madison County, creating 700 jobs, in addition to a $3
  billion
  [project](https://mississippi.org/news/governor-tate-reeves-announces-amazon-plans-to-invest-3-billion-in-vicksburg/)
  in Vicksburg announced last year.
- **Southwark Metal Manufacturing Company** is
  [investing](https://mississippi.org/news/southwark-metal-manufacturing-company-expanding-operations-in-desoto-county/)
  $29 million to expand its operations in DeSoto County.
- **Siemens Energy** is
  [investing](https://mississippi.org/news/siemens-energy-expanding-presence-in-rankin-county-constructing-new-facility/)
  $300 million to build a new manufacturing facility for electrical grid
  components in Pearl, creating up to 300 jobs.
- **xAI** is
  [investing](https://governorreeves.ms.gov/tech-leader-xai-investing-more-than-20-billion-in-southaven/)
  $20 billion to build a data center in Southaven, creating hundreds of
  jobs.
- **BWC Terminals** is
  [investing](https://mississippi.org/news/bwc-terminals-locating-terminal-facility-in-pascagoula/)
  $316 million to locate a terminal facility in Pascagoula.
- **Hood Industries** is
  [investing](https://mississippi.org/news/hood-industries-expanding-operations-in-waynesboro/)
  $245 million to construct a new advanced sawmill in Waynesboro.

## **Rebuilding Communities and Upgrading Infrastructure**

**President Trump is delivering federal resources to help Mississippi
recover from storms, reduce congestion, and strengthen freight movement
that supports economic growth.**

- **Helping Mississippi Rebuild:** In August 2026, President Trump
  [approved](https://governorreeves.ms.gov/president-trump-approves-major-disaster-declaration-for-tropical-storm-arthur/)
  Mississippi’s disaster declaration request for counties impacted by
  Tropical Storm Arthur, unlocking individual and public assistance.
  Earlier federal disaster assistance was
  [approved](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260702/president-donald-j-trump-approves-major-disaster-declaration-mississippi)
  following May 2026 severe storms, including funds for temporary
  housing and home repairs, low-cost loans for uninsured losses, and
  other recovery programs.
- **Expanding Critical Corridors:** In October 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [awarded](https://www.wicker.senate.gov/2026/09/28/wicker-announces-44-million-roadway-investment-for-desoto-county/)
  a $44 million grant to
  [expand](https://transportationtodaynews.com/news/38871-mississippi-dot-receives-44m-grant-to-improve-state-freight-corridor/)
  the I-55/I-69 corridor in DeSoto County from four to ten lanes, add
  ramp and turning lanes, and lengthen acceleration and deceleration
  lanes — reducing congestion on a vital freight route that serves tens
  of thousands of vehicles daily.
- **Upgrading Mississippi Roads:** In July 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [awarded](https://www.hydesmith.senate.gov/hyde-smith-511-million-will-fund-three-major-transportation-projects-rankin-hancock-harrison)
  $51.1 million in federal BUILD grants to improve U.S. Highway 80 in
  Brandon, expand truck parking along I-10 in Hancock County, and
  upgrade Mallett Road in D’Iberville.
- **Modernizing Freight Rail:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.hydesmith.senate.gov/355-million-grant-awarded-modernize-121-year-old-escatawpa-river-swing-bridge)
  Mississippi Export Railroad up to $35.5 million to
  [modernize](https://www.tallahatchienews.ms/355-million-grant-awarded-modernize-121-year-old-escatawpa-river-swing-bridge-6adad26275709')
  and automate a 121-year-old swing bridge, upgrade track, improve
  crossings, and raise track in flood-prone areas — strengthening
  reliability for Gulf Coast industries and ports.

## **Putting Mississippi Farmers First**

**President Trump is delivering direct support, infrastructure upgrades,
and market opportunities for Mississippi agriculture.**

- **Emergency Assistance:** The Trump Administration has
  [delivered](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/emergency-commodity-assistance-program/dashboard)
  more than $145 million to nearly 4,300 Mississippi farmers and
  producers through the Emergency Commodity Assistance Program. Nearly
  3,700 Mississippi producers have also
  [received](https://publicdashboards.dl.usda.gov/t/FPAC_PUB/views/FSA-DAFP-FBADashboard/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity?%3Aembed=y&%3AisGuestRedirectFromVizportal=y)
  more than $147 million in payments as part of a $12 billion [national
  bridge
  program](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
  for trade disruptions and higher input costs left by the Biden
  Administration. Hundreds of Mississippi producers have also
  [received](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/farmers.gov/viz/FSA-DAFP-ASCFPublic/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity)
  more than $8 million in funding through the Assistance for Specialty
  Crop Farmers program.
- **Support for Ranchers:** In September 2026, President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-supports-americas-ranchers/)
  two Executive Orders to back American ranchers — one [directing
  action](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history)
  to help producers protect herds from wolves and other predators and
  advancing country-of-origin labeling transparency, and another
  [strengthening](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history)
  enforcement of the Packers and Stockyards Act to expand market access
  without compromising food safety.

## **Investing in Mississippi’s Military and Defense Industries**

**President Trump is delivering new resources to strengthen
Mississippi’s shipbuilding and defense capabilities, supporting both
national security and high-quality jobs.**

- In October 2026, the Navy
  [awarded](https://magnoliatribune.com/2026/10/19/ingalls-awarded-99-million-contract-modification-for-uss-lyndon-b-johnson-sea-trials/)
  a $99 million contract to Ingalls Shipbuilding in Pascagoula for sea
  trials of the *USS Lyndon B. Johnson*.
- In April 2026, the Navy
  [awarded](https://news.usni.org/2026/04/28/navy-awards-282-9m-ffx-frigate-contract-to-hiis-ingalls-shipbuilding)
  a $283 million contract to begin work on the Navy’s new FF(X) frigate
  combatant ships.
- In February 2026, Mississippi
  [secured](https://mississippitoday.org/2026/02/12/defense-spending-mississippi-bill/)
  $528 million in NDAA funding, including $53 million for critical
  upgrades at Columbus Air Force Base, Camp Shelby, Navy Reserve Center
  Meridian, and Key Field Air National Guard Base.
