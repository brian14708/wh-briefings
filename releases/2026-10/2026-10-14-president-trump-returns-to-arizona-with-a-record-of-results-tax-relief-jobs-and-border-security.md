**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to the Grand Canyon State for
a visit to Bullhead City.** He arrives with a clear record of America
First results that put Arizona workers, families, seniors, and
communities first — delivering tax relief, unleashing major private
investment, ending the Biden-era border crisis, driving down violent
crime, and securing Arizona’s critical water resources.

### **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Arizonans**

*President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are delivering direct
relief to Arizona workers, families, and seniors.*

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 1.3 million Arizona
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Arizona-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Arizona taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,659](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Arizona [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) $167
  million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program funding.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=5) 119,000
  Arizona jobs, $22 billion in state GDP, and $11 billion in workers’
  wages.

### **Unleashing an Investment Boom in Arizona**

*President Trump’s agenda of tax cuts, energy dominance, secure borders,
and pro-growth policies has attracted major private capital and
high-quality jobs to Arizona.*

- Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company is
  [expanding](https://www.enr.com/articles/63349-tsmc-arizona-megaproject-grows-to-265-billion)
  advanced semiconductor manufacturing at its Phoenix facility with a
  $200 billion investment.
- EVelution Energy is
  [building](https://kyma.com/news/top-stories/2026/05/19/cobalt-facility-planned-for-yuma-county-moves-forward/)
  an $850 million cobalt processing facility in Yuma County, creating
  3,300 jobs.
- Amkor Technology is
  [constructing](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55314765/amkor-technology-invests-2-billion-to-build-semiconductor-manufacturing-facility-in-arizona)
  a $2 billion semiconductor packaging and test facility in Peoria,
  creating 2,200 jobs.
- Hadrian is
  [establishing](https://www.gpec.org/news/press-releases/hadrian-announces-mesa-manufacturing-software-hub/)
  a $200 million large-scale manufacturing and software hub in Mesa,
  creating 350 jobs.
- Cyclic Materials is
  [developing](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250423172110/en/Cyclic-Materials-to-Invest-Over-USD-%2420M-in-its-First-Commercial-Facility-in-the-United-States)
  its first $20 million commercial facility in Mesa.

### **Securing the Southern Border**

*President Trump ended the Biden-era border crisis and restored control
at the southern border.*

- The Trump Administration [has not
  released](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/16/trump-administration-delivers-16-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  a single illegal alien into the country at the border for 16
  consecutive months.
- Southern border apprehensions in August were [94%
  lower](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/16/trump-administration-delivers-16-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  than the monthly average under Biden.
- Refugee admissions in the first ten months of this fiscal year are
  [down almost
  90%](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/08/17/refugee-admissions-to-the-us-are-dropping-sharply-in-trumps-second-term/#:~:text=The%20federal%20government%20has%20admitted%2010%2C258%20refugees%20through%20the%20first%2010%20months%20of%20fiscal%20year%202026%2C%20which%20ends%20on%20Sept.%2030.%20That%E2%80%99s%20down%20from%2038%2C102%20refugees%20in%20fiscal%202025%20and%20100%2C034%20in%20fiscal%202024.%20(President%20Joe%20Biden%20was%20in%20office%20for%20part%20of%20fiscal%202025%20and%20all%20of%20fiscal%202024.))
  from 2024.
- The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [allocated](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/press/rep/releases/the-one-big-beautiful-bill-makes-america-safe-again)
  $46.5 billion to complete the border wall.
- Fentanyl and other drug trafficking are
  [down](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/16/trump-administration-delivers-16-straight-months-zero-releases-border),
  with CBP seizing 73% more drugs this fiscal year through August than
  in the same period of FY 2024, and 61% more than the average for the
  same period over the prior four years.
- The Trump Administration
  [transferred](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/department-interior-announces-land-transfer-department-navy-national-defense-arizona)
  285 acres of public land in Yuma County along the U.S.-Mexico border
  to the Department of the Navy to establish a National Defense Area in
  direct support of border security operations.

### **Making Safer Streets Across Arizona**

*Violent crime is falling as President Trump’s law-and-order agenda
delivers real results for Arizona communities.*

- Violent crime
  [fell](https://azcrimestatistics.azdps.gov/tops/report/violent-crimes/arizona/2025)
  13% statewide last year, including a 13% drop in homicides, an 11%
  drop in aggravated assaults, a 24% drop in robberies, and a 10% drop
  in sexual assaults.
- In Mesa, homicides
  [fell](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  33% and rapes fell 15% in the first half of this year compared with
  the same period last year.
- In Phoenix, homicides
  [fell](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  22% and robberies fell 19% in the first half of this year compared
  with the same period last year.
- In Tucson, homicides
  [fell](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  24% and robberies fell 10% in the first half of this year compared
  with the same period last year.

### **Protecting Arizona’s Water Resources**

*The Trump Administration is making targeted investments to strengthen
Arizona’s water supply and long-term resilience.*

- In July 2025, the Bureau of Reclamation
  [announced](https://www.usbr.gov/newsroom/news-release/5202) a $179
  million investment to design and construct the North Gateway Advanced
  Water Purification Facility in Phoenix.
- In May 2025, the Trump Administration
  [finalized](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-secures-18-short-term-agreements-boost-colorado-river-conservation)
  18 short-term System Conservation Implementation agreements with water
  entitlement holders in Arizona and California to increase conservation
  of Colorado River System water.
