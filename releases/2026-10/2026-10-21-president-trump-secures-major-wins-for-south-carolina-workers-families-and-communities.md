  Communities

**President Donald J. Trump returns to South Carolina today to highlight
a proven record of results that put workers, families, seniors, and
communities first.** Under President Trump and Republican leadership,
South Carolinians are seeing historic tax relief, surging private
investment and job creation, stronger rural healthcare, and major
investments in infrastructure, military readiness, and public safety.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking South Carolinians**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of Palmetto State workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately one million South
  Carolina seniors
  [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/South-carolina-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf)
  from the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay
  zero federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** South Carolina taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,476](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=42) 86,000
  South Carolina jobs, $15 billion in state GDP, and $8 billion in
  workers’ wages.
- **Opportunity Zones:** In September 2026, Gov. Henry McMaster
  [submitted](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-09/gov-mcmaster-completes-south-carolinas-opportunity-zone-submission-us-treasury)
  South Carolina’s new Opportunity Zone nominations to the Treasury,
  made possible by the [permanent
  extension](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0550) of
  the program — unlocking new private capital and economic opportunity
  for communities statewide.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare Access**

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources to expand access and
modernize care in South Carolina’s rural communities.**

- **Rural Health Transformation:** South
  Carolina [secured](https://www.scdhhs.gov/communications/south-carolina-awarded-200-million-rural-health-transformation-program-funding) $200
  million in first-year
  [funding](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states)
  through the Working Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation
  Program.
- **Modernizing Care Delivery:** In September, the state
  [awarded](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-09/south-carolina-announces-recipients-rural-health-transformation-program-funding)
  $167.3 million in Rural Health Transformation Program grants to 228
  rural providers for primary care, maternal care, pediatrics, and
  behavioral health — removing barriers and building long-term capacity
  through facility modernization, technology upgrades, and workforce
  development.

## **Investing in South Carolina Communities**

**President Trump is delivering targeted federal resources that
strengthen roads, military installations, disaster recovery, and
educational opportunity.**

- **Strengthening Roads and Bridges:** In July 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [awarded](https://www.scdot.org/inside/SCDOTPress/2026/scdot-receives--23-5-million-federal-grant-for-statewide-bridge-.html)
  South Carolina a $23.5 million grant to replace 18 aging bridges
  across nine counties.
- **Modernizing Military Installations:** The FY 2027 NDAA
  [authorized](https://mace.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-nancy-mace-delivers-major-wins-south-carolina-and-americas-warfighters)
  $161.7 million for a nuclear power training facility expansion at
  Joint Base Charleston and extended funding totaling $166.7 million for
  new recruit barracks at Parris Island.
- **Delivering Disaster Recovery:** In October 2026, President Trump
  [approved](https://governor.sc.gov/news/2026-10/gov-mcmaster-announces-approval-federal-cost-share-adjustment-hurricane-helene)
  South Carolina’s request to raise the federal share of Hurricane
  Helene Public Assistance recovery costs from 75% to 90%.
- **Supporting HBCUs:** In September 2026, the Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.scott.senate.gov/media-center/press-releases/sen-tim-scott-applauds-u-s-department-of-education-investment-in-south-carolinas-hbcus/)
  nearly $10 million to eight South Carolina HBCUs, led by $2.4 million
  to South Carolina State.

## **Making South Carolina Safe Again**

**President Trump and Republicans are delivering results that reduce
crime and restore safety for South Carolina families.**

- Violent crime
  [fell](https://www.facebook.com/FBIColumbiaSC/posts/new-uniform-crime-reporting-numbers-from-2025-show-big-drops-in-crime-across-the/122342895632011761/)
  11% and property crime
  [fell](https://www.facebook.com/FBIColumbiaSC/posts/new-uniform-crime-reporting-numbers-from-2025-show-big-drops-in-crime-across-the/122342895632011761/)
  14% last year, including sharp drops in murders (17%), robberies
  (19%), aggravated assaults (9%), and burglaries (15%).
- In Charleston, overall crime
  [plunged](https://www.charleston-sc.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1625#docaccess-715460d05e8a64c9d72630e9c62bcc35c160e32391a9a017b640acf4568d3f77)
  36% in 2025, with homicides
  [down](https://www.charleston-sc.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1625#docaccess-715460d05e8a64c9d72630e9c62bcc35c160e32391a9a017b640acf4568d3f77)
  29%, aggravated assaults down 16%, robberies down 24%, and sex
  offenses down 39% from the five-year average.
- At least [72 law enforcement
  agencies](https://www.ice.gov/identify-and-arrest/287g#:~:text=287(g)%20Participating,g)%20Participating%20Agencies),
  including the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division and the South
  Carolina Department of Public Safety, have signed [287(g)
  agreements](https://www.ice.gov/identify-and-arrest/287g) with ICE to
  identify, detain, and remove violent illegal alien criminals from the
  state.
