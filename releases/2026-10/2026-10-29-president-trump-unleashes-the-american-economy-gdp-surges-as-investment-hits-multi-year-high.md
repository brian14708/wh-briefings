  Multi-Year High'

**The U.S. economy**
[**grew**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2115803576920637441)
**at a strong annual rate of 3.4% in the third quarter of 2026 — the
strongest pace in a year.** President Donald J. Trump’s America First
agenda is delivering as promised: faster growth, historic levels of
investment, and an economy that continues to outperform the so-called
“experts.”

**Here’s what you need to know:**

- **Businesses are investing big in America.** Private investment in
  plants and equipment surged at a 9.4% rate in the third quarter,
  accelerating from an already strong second quarter. So far this year,
  investment is growing more than twice as fast as it did in 2025 and
  three times faster than the weak 2.3% rate in Biden’s final year. That
  means more factories, more manufacturing, more jobs, and more
  production here at home.
- **Business investment just posted its strongest quarter in nearly 15
  years.** With
  [growth](https://x.com/byHeatherLong/status/2115794550321000614) of
  almost 13% in the third quarter, it’s a clear signal that companies
  are betting on American workers, factories, and production under
  President Trump.
- **Strong momentum is building.** Over the past three quarters, the
  economy has grown at a 2.7% rate — faster than the Blue Chip consensus
  [forecast](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow)
  (2.4%) and well above the Congressional Budget Office’s
  [projection](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62105) (2.2%).
- **The “experts” were wrong — again.** 72% of the economists
  [surveyed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/us-economy-grows-a-faster-than-expected-3-4-most-in-a-year)
  by Bloomberg underestimated today’s growth number, once again missing
  the strength of the Trump economy.
- **American consumers remain resilient.** Consumer spending
  [grew](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/business/economy/us-economy-gdp.html#:~:text=Consumer%20spending%2C%20which%20accounts%20for%20more%20than%20two%2Dthirds%20of%20economic%20output%2C%20grew%20at%20a%203.8%20percent%20rate.)
  at a solid 3.8% rate last quarter as American families spend with
  confidence and businesses expand.
- **America is investing in its future.** Capital goods — the equipment
  and machinery that build American industry — now make up a record
  share of U.S. imports. At the same time, consumer goods fell to just
  17% of goods imports, tied for the lowest level on record. Combined
  with the surge in domestic investment, this shows President Trump’s
  policies are helping rebuild American productive capacity.

**President Trump’s agenda of lower taxes, fewer regulations, energy
dominance, and America First trade are fueling a historic investment
boom that is powering strong growth.** Businesses are betting on
America, and the foundation of the future is being built right here at
home.
