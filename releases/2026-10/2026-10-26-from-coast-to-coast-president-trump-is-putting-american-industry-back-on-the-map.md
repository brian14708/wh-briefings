title: From Coast to Coast, President Trump Is Putting American Industry Back on the
  Map
---
 
**President Donald J. Trump is delivering an America First surge of
manufacturing, energy, agriculture, semiconductor, shipbuilding, and
national security investments that are bringing production home,
expanding domestic capacity, and locking in American jobs.** These
commitments build on the trillions in [private
investment](https://www.whitehouse.gov/investments/) the Trump
Administration has secured since taking office — proving that when
America puts itself first, results follow.

**As the Administration moves across the country, it is delivering
concrete wins for American communities:**

- **GEORGIA:** Audi is
  [investing](https://www.audi.com/en/press-releases/audi-to-establish-first-us-production-site-in-georgia-investing-4-billion-and-creating-4500-jobs-18420)
  $4.2 billion to build its first-ever U.S. production site near Macon —
  a [direct
  result](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-24/volkswagen-plans-new-audi-plant-in-georgia-handing-trump-a-win)
  of President Trump’s trade agenda. The plant will create 4,500 jobs
  and have an annual production capacity of 200,000 vehicles,
  strengthening American dominance in auto manufacturing.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2114148679351169136)*)*
- **FLORIDA:** The Trump Administration is
  [investing](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2113720027581173884?s=20)
  $275 million to recruit, hire, and retain at least 5,000 new nurses in
  elder care facilities nationwide, expanding the workforce that serves
  American seniors.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2113720027581173884?s=20)*)*
- **IOWA:** The Trump Administration is
  [invoking](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-use-defense-production-act-funding-restart-american-refinery-and-expand)
  the Defense Production Act to release $150 million to expand U.S.
  refining capacity — boosting domestic production of diesel and
  gasoline to strengthen energy security and lower costs.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2113437844811165855?s=20)*)*
- **NEBRASKA:** The Trump Administration is
  [investing](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/10/22/trump-administration-announces-major-investment-american-fertilizer-manufacturing-strengthen)
  $1.3 billion in domestic fertilizer manufacturing, with TalusAg
  [opening](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/10/22/trump-administration-announces-major-investment-american-fertilizer-manufacturing-strengthen#:~:text=TalusAg%20%E2%80%93%20Iowa%2C%20Kentucky,depend%20on%20it.)
  two facilities in Nebraska to deliver lower-cost, American-made
  fertilizer to farmers.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2113304731863142865?s=20)*)*
- **NORTH CAROLINA:** The Trump Administration is
  [investing](https://www.wolfspeed.com/company/news-events/news/wolfspeed-announces-conditional-30-year-loan-commitment-from-us-department-of-war-to-advance-domestic-wide-bandgap-supply-chain/)
  up to $1.5 billion to expand semiconductor production, while Sibelco
   [expands](https://www.sibelco.com/en/news/sibelco-and-u-s-department-of-commerce-sign-letter-of-intent-for-up-to-56-million-in-chips-act-funding-to-advance-high-purity-quartz-innovation-for-semiconductor-manufacturing-in-the-united-states)
  production of high-purity quartz and related materials essential to
  semiconductors in Mitchell County — advancing America’s semiconductor
  supply chain.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2112708953125577026)*)*
- **KANSAS:** The Trump Administration is
  [launching](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/10/19/trump-administration-announces-8-billion-harvest-fuel-relief-program)
  the Harvest Fuel Relief Program, providing farmers with up to $8
  billion in support to offset elevated diesel costs during the current
  harvest season and protect American agriculture.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2112353730041422330?s=20)*)*
- **MONTANA:** The Department of War is
  [establishing](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-announces-montana-super-range-midterm-rally-pentagon-test-advanced-war-technology)
  a new super range for testing advanced weapons and technologies —
  including autonomous weapons, space and electronic warfare
  capabilities, supersonics, and hypersonics — to ensure American
  military superiority.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110558543866732889)*)*
- **KENTUCKY:** The Department of War is
  [advancing](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4628105/department-of-wars-office-of-strategic-capital-signs-80-million-conditional-loa/)
  an $80 million investment to revitalize pharmaceutical manufacturing
  with Antheia at a facility in Winchester, restoring critical drug
  production to American soil.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2111535296244326857)*)*
- **ALASKA:** Taiwan is
  [investing](https://apnews.com/article/trump-alaska-sullivan-midterm-election-0542aaa0e516c1c6057a2f13e9163b3f#:~:text=During%20Tuesday%E2%80%99s%20rally,provide%20further%20details.)
  [$4.4
  billion](https://alaskapublic.org/news/politics/elections/2026-10-13/trump-rally-brings-pardons-promises-and-endorsements-to-wasilla#:~:text=agreed%20to%20invest-,%244.4%20billion,-.%20Taiwan%20signed%20a)
  in Alaska’s mega-gas project, while the Federal Communications
  Commission is
  [investing](https://apnews.com/article/trump-alaska-sullivan-midterm-election-0542aaa0e516c1c6057a2f13e9163b3f#:~:text=Trump%20also%20said,on%20certain%20vehicles.)
  an additional $162 million to expand high-speed internet across the
  state.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110186587036537208?s=20)*)*
- **OHIO:** Cleveland will be the
  [home](https://thehill.com/newsletters/defense-national-security/6146728-cleveland-to-be-home-of-next-submarine-repair-center/)
  of the new U.S. Submarine Component Repair Center, strengthening
  America’s undersea naval industrial base.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2109801412066443765)*)*
- **MARYLAND:** The Navy and Anduril are
  [investing](https://www.anduril.com/news/anduril-u-s-navy-invest-usd6-6-billion-to-expand-america-s-submarine-industrial-base)
  $6.6 billion to build Arsenal-2, a new software-defined shipyard in
  Baltimore — the largest new American shipbuilding push in generations.
  *(*[*Watch*](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2107566035507593450)*)*

**These investments demonstrate the Trump Effect in action: bringing
jobs and production home, supporting American workers and farmers,
strengthening energy and national security, and expanding the skilled
workforce America needs.** The Trump Administration will continue
delivering results that rebuild American industry and put America First.
