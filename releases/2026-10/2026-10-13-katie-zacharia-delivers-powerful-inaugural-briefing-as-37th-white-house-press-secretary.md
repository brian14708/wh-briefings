**Today, Katie Zacharia delivered a commanding** [**inaugural press
briefing**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g7we6gecwnc) **as the 37th
White House Press Secretary.** The briefing, which generated
[wall-to-wall
coverage](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110004871613280464?s=20),
served as a strong introduction to her tenure and highlighted the Trump
Administration’s record of delivering historic successes for the
American people.

- **Katie opened the briefing with clear purpose:** “I am so honored to
  work for President Trump and the American people as the new White
  House Press Secretary. I want to thank President Trump for his trust
  that he has placed in me. I have wanted to work for him for a very
  long time.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110005546522669137?s=20))
- **Katie detailed President Trump’s second-term accomplishments on
  costs and much more:** “President Trump is the first President in
  modern history to actually lower prescription drug prices, delivering
  the biggest drop in 63 years.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110008138682544489?s=20))
- **Katie reaffirmed the Administration’s commitment to broader media
  access:** “My predecessor, Karoline Leavitt, did an incredible job and
  I completely agree with the choice of a New Media Seat — we are
  absolutely going to keep it and we are going to expand on it to the
  extent possible. We love new media, and we want to be able to embrace
  it.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110007073178370340?s=20))
- **Katie announced the continued rapid pace of President Trump’s
  midterms travel:** “You can expect more of the midterm blitz. Just
  now, we’ve added Kentucky and we have a few other stops as he goes
  down to Florida — so it’ll be Georgia, Kentucky, and Florida.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110007857202520463?s=20))
- **Katie spoke about her family and the Trump Administration’s support
  for working parents:** “We have four kids, ranging from 12, 10, 8,
  and 6. Three of them are boys, so we have a handful… President Trump
  is the most family-friendly President we’ve ever had. He has hired
  more women with families, and the fact that he knew I had four kids
  and knew that this job would be demanding, and still placed his trust
  in me, shows that I know I’m capable of being able to step up to
  that.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2110009668332408985?s=20))
