title: President Trump Is Putting American Industry, Jobs, and Paychecks Back on the
  Map
---
 
**President Donald J. Trump is delivering on his promise to the American
worker: build it here, power it here, and put paychecks back in the
communities Washington left behind.** In a matter of weeks, President
Trump has locked in a run of steel, nuclear, gas, shipbuilding, and
medical manufacturing commitments that will hire American workers and
strengthen American security.

- **President Trump**
  [**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2104640636297388141)
  **that Mesabi Metallics will build a** [**$15 billion steel
  complex**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/mined-melted-and-poured-in-america-president-trump-reverses-years-of-anti-mining-policy/)
  **in Iowa — the largest steel plant ever built in the United States.**
  Fed by American iron ore and driven by the Trump Administration’s
  steel tariffs, the fully integrated mine-to-mill complex will create
  1,750 new, permanent jobs, support up to 6,000 construction jobs, and
  generate an estimated $95 billion in economic output.
- **President Trump and Anduril**
  [**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2107566035507593450)
  **Arsenal-2, the biggest new American shipyard push since World War
  II, now moving forward in Maryland.** The yard will produce critical
  Virginia-class components and large assemblies, create about 3,100
  direct jobs, and support more than 11,000 indirect jobs. Anduril is
  investing $3.7 billion in private capital; the Navy is putting up to
  $2.9 billion behind it as part of the Trump Administration’s push to
  reshore America’s defense industrial base.
- **President Trump**
  [**unveiled**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2105398496077521257)
  **a South Korean energy investment package of up to $200 billion in
  the United States.** The package
  [includes](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2105398496077521257)
  as much as $120 billion to build eight large-scale nuclear plants, $54
  billion tied to the Alaska LNG pipeline, and $22.3 billion for a
  six-gigawatt natural gas power facility in Texas.
- **Vice President Vance**
  [**announced**](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/trump-admin-invoking-defense-production-act-new-alaska-power-grid-project-touted-vance)
  **that the Department of Energy will invest $150 million to advance a
  major new transmission line in Alaska.** The project includes 223
  miles linking the Beluga power station on the southern coast with
  Healy in the Interior, a corridor serving ports and nearly
  three-quarters of the state’s population.
- **Becton Dickinson**
  [**announced**](https://news.bd.com/2026-10-06-BD-and-U-S-Government-Launch-Landmark-Partnership-to-Expand-U-S-Manufacturing-and-Strengthen-Americas-Healthcare-Resilience)
  **a $19 billion U.S. investment under a landmark partnership with the
  Trump Administration, including $3 billion to expand American
  manufacturing.** BD will produce 100% of the needles it sells in
  America with American-made steel and expand domestic output of
  essential products.
- **AstraZeneca**
  [**announced**](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/astrazeneca-opens-new-massachusetts-rd-center-part-1-billion-investment-2026-10-05/)
  **a $1 billion investment in Massachusetts under the $50 billion
  investment commitment it made in its Most Favored Nation**
  [**agreement**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-second-deal-to-bring-most-favored-nation-pricing-to-american-patients/)
  **with the Trump Administration.** The company is expanding its
  domestic research and manufacturing operation and growing its domestic
  workforce.
