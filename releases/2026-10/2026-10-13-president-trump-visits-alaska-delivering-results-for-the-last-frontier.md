**Today, President Donald J. Trump travels to Alaska.** He returns to
the Last Frontier with a proven record of results that put Alaskans
first: lower taxes, responsible development of the state’s vast energy
and mineral resources, safer communities, strong support for fisheries,
and major investments that create jobs and reinforce America’s Arctic
security.

### **Tax Relief That Puts Money Back in Alaskans’ Pockets**

*President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are delivering direct
relief to Alaska workers, families, and seniors.*

- **No Tax on Tips:** Approximately
  [14,000](https://www.facebook.com/SenDanSullivan/posts/thanks-to-the-obbb-alaskan-workers-will-keep-more-of-their-hard-earned-paychecks/1309264993899614/) Alaska
  workers no longer pay federal income taxes on tips.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** About 100,000 Alaska
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Alaska-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Alaska taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,690](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Alaska [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) about
  $272 million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program
  funding.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=4) 11,000
  Alaska jobs, $2 billion in state GDP, and $1 billion in workers’
  wages.

### **Unleashing Alaska’s Energy, Minerals, and Economic Potential**

*President Trump is reversing years of restrictions, unlocking
responsible development of Alaska’s oil, gas, and critical minerals.*

- In October 2026, the Trump Administration
  [secured](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/trump-admin-invoking-defense-production-act-new-alaska-power-grid-project-touted-vance)
  $150 million in Defense Production Act funding for a major new power
  line that will lower energy costs for 75% of Alaskans.
- In September 2026, President Trump
  [unveiled](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-30/trump-set-to-unveil-200-billion-of-south-korean-us-investments?utm_campaign=heatmap_am&&utm_medium=email&&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9zKvsz1JKIFPirgOzScfIPWw1dNt19pYe7KqVx3qulWcxnPndfDq1HbzeKJI3QCHiNhvcsVABQizDrhyFs8Nk4P2GjerDLw2KGFH8NvOFpxS2ABrE&&_hsmi=442026268&&utm_content=442026268&&utm_source=hs_email)
  a $200 billion South Korean investment in U.S. energy that includes
  $54 billion to construct a new natural gas pipeline in Alaska.
- In December 2025, the Trump Administration
  [reopened](https://www.blm.gov/blog/2026-01-06/progress-public-lands-blm-2025-trump-administration-accomplishments-jan-20-Dec-31-2025)
  nearly 82% of the National Petroleum Reserve-Alaska to oil and gas
  leasing.
- In October 2025, the Trump Administration
  [opened](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-takes-bold-steps-expand-energy-local-control-and-land-access-alaska)
  1.56 million acres of the Coastal Plain to oil and gas leasing,
  reversing Biden-era restrictions.
- In October 2025, President Trump
  [approved](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-approves-ambler-road-project-to-unlock-alaskas-mineral-potential/)
  the Ambler Road Project, providing essential access to the Ambler
  Mining District’s large deposits of copper, cobalt, gallium,
  germanium, and other critical minerals.
- In June 2025, the Trump Administration
  [advanced](https://alaskabeacon.com/2025/06/17/trump-administration-revives-plan-to-open-most-of-arctic-alaska-land-unit-to-oil-development/)
  plans to open an additional seven million federally managed acres on
  the North Slope to new oil development.
- In January 2025, President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/unleashing-alaskas-extraordinary-resource-potential/)
  an Executive Order directing federal agencies to prioritize
  development of Alaska’s oil, gas, minerals, and other natural
  resources.

### **Protecting Alaska’s Communities, Fisheries, and Veterans**

*President Trump has moved quickly to restore historic names, support
Alaska’s vital fishing industry, and expand care for the state’s
veterans.*

- On his first day in office, President Trump
  [restored](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/restoring-names-that-honor-american-greatness/)
  the name Mount McKinley, reversing the Obama Administration’s 2015
  [change](https://www.nps.gov/dena/learn/historyculture/mountain-name.htm)
  and returning the designation that stood from 1917.
- The Trump Administration
  [ensured](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/sustainable-fisheries/administration-commercial-fishing-industry-wins)
  Alaska groundfish fisheries opened on time, protecting a total value
  of $301.1 million for Gulf of Alaska fisheries and $2.64 billion for
  Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands fisheries.
- The Trump Administration
  [implemented](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/sustainable-fisheries/administration-commercial-fishing-industry-wins)
  regulatory and data collection reforms that allowed the timely opening
  of the Alaska halibut fishery.
- The Department of Veterans Affairs
  [awarded](https://www.va.gov/alaska-health-care/news-releases/va-awards-4069354-million-in-grants-to-fight-veteran-homelessness-in-alaska/)
  over $4 million in grants to community organizations supporting Alaska
  veterans who are homeless or at risk of homelessness.
- In September 2026, the VA
  [opened](https://www.va.gov/alaska-health-care/stories/alaska-va-opens-walk-in-clinic-for-faster-service/)
  a new walk-in clinic at the Colonel Mary Louise Rasmuson Campus in
  Anchorage.
- The Trump Administration
  [delivered](https://www.va.gov/alaska-health-care/news-releases/alaska-va-upgrades-health-care-infrastructure/)
  funding for critical infrastructure upgrades at the Alaska VA
  Healthcare System as part of a record $4.8 billion in nationwide VA
  spending.

### **A Military Construction Boom Across Alaska**

*President Trump and Republicans in Congress have directed major new
investments into Alaska’s bases, creating jobs and modernizing
facilities that strengthen U.S. security in the Arctic.*

- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/5371/all-actions)
  the Continuing Appropriations, Agriculture, Legislative Branch,
  Military Construction and Veterans Affairs, and Extensions Act, 2026,
  which
  [included](https://www.murkowski.senate.gov/press/release/murkowski-secures-investments-for-alaska-in-appropriations-bills):
  - $208 million for new barracks at Fort Wainwright in Fairbanks.
  - $46 million for a base supply complex at Joint Base
    Elmendorf-Richardson (JBER) in Anchorage.
  - $152 million for the next phase of the Joint Integrated Test and
    Training Center at JBER.
  - $45 million to complete the Air Force runway extension at JBER.
  - $6.7 million to finish planning and design for a permanent party
    dormitory at Eielson Air Force Base.
  - $7.7 million to begin planning and design for a new Army dining
    facility at Fort Wainwright.
- President Trump
  [signed](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1071/all-actions)
  the FY 2026 National Defense Authorization Act, which delivered
  additional Alaska funding,
  [including](https://www.sullivan.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/final-fy-2026-defense-bill-includes-19-sullivan-provisions-prioritizes-alaska/):
  - $82 million for the Air Force Joint Integrated Test & Training
    Center at JBER.
  - $80 million for upgraded Army barracks at Fort Wainwright.
  - $46 million for an Army Reserve Base Supply Complex at JBER.
  - $46 million for an Army Reserve Maintenance Facility at JBER.
  - $15 million for a Base Civil Engineering Pavements and Grounds
    Facility for the Alaska Air National Guard at Eielson.
  - $13.2 million for design of an Air Force Consolidated Munitions
    Complex at Eielson.
  - $8 million for design of an Army dining facility at Fort Wainwright.
  - $1.75 million for design of an Air Force Coal Thaw Shed Addition at
    Eielson.
- The FY 2027 NDAA, which has
  [passed](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/8800/text)
  the House,
  [includes](https://www.sullivan.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/sullivan-secures-56-provisions-historic-26-billion-for-alaska-in-fy-2027-defense-authorization/)
  $2.6 billion for Alaska and launches the multi-year, $7 billion
  “Fighter Town USA” recapitalization at JBER. The project will
  construct 26 modern facilities, position JBER as one of the most
  strategically important bases in the world, create up to 4,000
  private-sector jobs, and bring additional military personnel and
  families to Alaska.

### **Safer Streets Across Alaska**

*Violent crime is falling in Alaska as President Trump’s law-and-order
agenda delivers results for Alaska communities.*

- The statewide murder rate
  [fell](https://dps.alaska.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2025-Crime-in-Alaska.pdf)
  13% last year.
- Juneau recorded a 6% decline in total criminal offenses last year.
