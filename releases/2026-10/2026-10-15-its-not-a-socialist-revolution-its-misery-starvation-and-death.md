romanticize one of history’s most dangerous and lethal ideologies.**

test](https://www.newsweek.com/ready-to-join-the-american-socialist-revolution-take-our-quiz-and-find-out-12557895)”
that asks about oat milk, shorter workweeks, and tote bags — then scores
readers on how ready they are for an “American socialist revolution.”

**There’s nothing lighthearted about the reality that campaign is trying
to hide.** Socialist and communist regimes have killed [some 100 million
people](https://www.wheaton.edu/academics/academic-centers/wheaton-center-for-faith-politics-and-economics/resource-center/articles/2020/the-death-and-rise-of-socialism#:~:text=Over%20the%20last,Marxist%20ideals.2)
through engineered famines, mass executions, and slave labor camps.

**That same ideology is now being sold to Americans by the Democrat
Socialists of America and the Radical Left that dominates the Democrat
Party.** Their agenda — Medicare for All, open borders, mass amnesty,
abolishing police and prisons, and the Green New Deal chief among them —
carries a [staggering
price](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Real-Cost-of-Socialism-In-America-Summary.pdf#page=3).

A new
[report](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Real-Cost-of-Socialism-In-America-Report.pdf)
from the Council of Economic Advisers puts the ten-year cost of the
socialist agenda at [$49
trillion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/socialism/), or as much as $400,000
per American household —
[financed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Real-Cost-of-Socialism-In-America-Summary.pdf#page=2)
by massive tax hikes that slash wages by nearly 30%, shrink economic
output, and drive inflation sky-high. Mortgage rates would soar past
12%, millions of jobs would vanish, and productivity would collapse as
empty shelves, rationed care, and the slow grind of state control
replace the abundance of American exceptionalism.

third-world country.

best**](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116803704330293684)**:
“America the Beautiful will NEVER be a Communist Country!!!”**
