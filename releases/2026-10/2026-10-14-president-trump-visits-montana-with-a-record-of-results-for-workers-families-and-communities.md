title: President Trump Visits Montana with a Record of Results for Workers, Families,
  and Communities
---
 
**Today, President Donald J. Trump heads to Montana for a stop in
Kalispell.** He arrives with a proven record of America First policies
that put Montana workers, families, seniors, and communities first —
delivering historic tax relief, unleashing private investment and job
growth, securing energy dominance, building critical minerals
independence, and protecting public lands and rural communities.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Montanans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting money back in
the pockets of Montana workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 200,000 Montana
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/montana-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay zero
  federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Montana taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,304](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=28) 20,000
  Montana jobs, $4 billion in state GDP, and $2 billion in workers’
  wages.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare**

**President Trump is bolstering access to care in Montana’s rural and
frontier communities.**

- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Montana [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) $233
  million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program funding from
  the Working Families Tax Cuts — the fourth-highest award among all
  states.
- **Rural EMS:** The Trump Administration
  [announced](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/trump-administration-announces-8-7-million-new-ambulances-lifesaving-medical-equipment-upgrades)
  $8.7 million in September 2026 from the Rural Health Transformation
  Program for new ambulances and lifesaving equipment at 79 rural
  Montana EMS agencies, including three tribal governments.
- **Behavioral Health:** Montana
  [secured](https://news.mt.gov/Governors-Office/Governor-Gianforte-Announces-Montana-Secures-Federal-Funding-to-Establish-Behavioral-Health-Clinics)
  federal approval in May 2026 to stand up certified community
  behavioral health clinics at roughly 20 sites statewide, replacing
  short-term grants with permanent funding.

## **Growing Montana’s Manufacturing Base**

**President Trump’s pro-growth agenda is driving private investment and
job creation across the state.**

- Janicki Industries
  [selected](https://www.janicki.com/janicki-selects-great-falls-montana/)
  Great Falls for an $800 million advanced manufacturing campus,
  creating more than 1,000 jobs within five years and more than 2,000
  jobs at completion.
- Montana
  [ranked](https://news.mt.gov/Governors-Office/Governor-Gianforte-Announces-Montana-Ranks-as-Top-10-State-for-Job-Growth)
  ninth in the nation for job growth during the first year of President
  Trump’s second term.
- Under Republican leadership, Montana’s unemployment rate stood at
  [3.2%](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MTUR) in August — well below
  the national average and ranking among the lowest in the country.

## **Driving American Energy Dominance**

**President Trump’s energy dominance agenda has reversed the previous
Administration’s restrictions and unlocked major coal development that
supports high-paying jobs and reliable power.**

- **Spring Creek Mine (Big Horn County):** In March 2025, the Department
  of the Interior
  [approved](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-advances-energy-independence-spring-creek-mine-expansion-approval)
  an expansion that extends the mine’s operating life by 16 years,
  enables production of 39.9 million tons of coal, and supports hundreds
  of jobs.
- **Rosebud Mine (Rosebud and Treasure counties):** Interior
  [approved](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-department-approves-coal-mine-expansion-montana-supporting-american-energy)
  an expansion that recovers 33.75 million tons of federal coal, extends
  operations through 2039, supports more than 300 high-paying jobs, and
  supplies the Colstrip and Rosebud power plants, which together
  generate 1,500 megawatts of electricity.
- **Bull Mountains Mine:** Interior
  [approved](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-approves-bull-mountains-mining-plan-modification)
  a mining plan modification that authorizes 22.8 million tons of
  federal coal and 34.5 million tons of adjacent non-federal coal,
  extends the mine by up to nine years, supports more than 250 jobs, and
  enables significant exports to U.S. allies.

## Building America’s Critical Minerals Supply Chain

**President Trump is restoring domestic production of critical minerals
and ending reliance on foreign adversaries.**

- United States Antimony Corporation (USAC) has
  [earned](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4421101/department-of-war-invests-27m-for-the-domestic-excavation-extraction-processing/)
  millions in Department of War grant milestones for its Thompson Falls
  smelter expansion under a $27 million Defense Production Act award.
- USAC
  [resumed](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101538/000110465926097708/tm2622899d2_ex99-1.htm)
  mining at Stibnite Hill and is processing ore at its newly acquired
  Radersburg mill, restoring domestic antimony production for the first
  time in decades.
- Montana antimony is
  [refilling](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101538/000165495425011011/uamy_ex991.htm)
  the National Defense Stockpile under a five-year, $245 million
  contract with the Defense Logistics Agency.

## Returning Control of Lands and Water to Montanans

**President Trump is putting decision-making back in the hands of
Montanans while delivering critical infrastructure.**

- **Grizzly Bears:** The Fish and Wildlife Service
  [proposed](https://zinke.house.gov/media/press-releases/zinke-applauds-usfws-decision-grizzly-bears)
  a rule in July 2026 giving Montana and other Western states greater
  flexibility to manage grizzlies in the Yellowstone ecosystem and other
  recovery areas where populations have met or exceeded recovery
  benchmarks, while maintaining Endangered Species Act protections.
- **Fort Peck Water:** President Trump
  [signed](https://www.daines.senate.gov/2026/09/28/daines-downing-bill-protecting-rural-water-infrastructure-signed-by-president-trump-officially-law/)
  the Fort Peck rural water reauthorization in September 2026, keeping
  clean water construction on schedule for the Fort Peck Reservation and
  Roosevelt, Sheridan, Valley, and Daniels counties.
- **Crow Tribe Water Rights:** Congress
  [passed](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/240)
  the Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Amendments Act in September
  2026 and sent it to President Trump’s desk, giving the tribe
  flexibility to finish its water system and hydropower project.

## **Rebuilding America’s Nuclear Deterrent at Malmstrom AFB**

**President Trump is modernizing the land-based leg of the nuclear triad
in Montana.**

- The Air Force and Army Corps of Engineers [broke
  ground](https://www.army.mil/article/293332/usace_u_s_air_force_partners_fortify_malmstrom_national_defense_with_gateway_to_sentinel_groundbreaking)
  in May 2026 on a $26 million commercial entrance facility that clears
  the way for nearly $1 billion in Sentinel construction work at
  Malmstrom.
- Work is
  [advancing](https://www.malmstrom.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4461198/sentinel-program-hits-key-milestone-begins-groundwork-at-former-montana-missile/)
  on surveying and preparing sites across central Montana’s missile
  fields to replace the aging Minuteman III with the new Sentinel ICBM.
