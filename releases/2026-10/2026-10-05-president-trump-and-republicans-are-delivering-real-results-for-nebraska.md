**Today, President Donald J. Trump travels to Grand Island, Nebraska.**
Under President Trump and Republican leadership, families in the
Cornhusker State are keeping more of what they earn, farmers and
ranchers are getting direct support, private investment is expanding
production, and crime is falling in the state’s largest cities.

## **Historic Tax Relief for Nebraskans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts stopped the largest tax
hike in American history and put money back in Nebraskans’ pockets.**

- **No Tax on Tips, No Tax on Overtime:** Qualified tips and overtime
  pay are now deductible from federal income taxes, benefiting hundreds
  of thousands of Nebraska service workers, truckers, manufacturers, and
  hourly employees.
- **No Tax on Social Security:** More than 300,000 Nebraska seniors can
  [benefit](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-ceo-frank-j-bisignano-visits-ohio-to-tout-working-families-tax-cuts-provisions-on-no-tax-on-car-loan-interest-no-tax-on-overtime-enhanced-deduction-for-senior-citizens#:~:text=About%201.4%20million%20Ohioans%20benefitted%20from%20the%20senior%20deduction%2C%20with%20the%20total%20amount%20of%20deductions%20claimed%20approaching%20%247.9%20billion.%20Nationally%2C%20over%2035%20million%20seniors%20benefitted%20from%20the%20deduction%20with%20more%20than%20%24193%20billion%20claimed.)
  from the new deduction that eliminates federal income tax on Social
  Security benefits for the vast majority of recipients.
- **Bigger refunds:** Nebraskans’ average federal tax refund reached
  [$3,378](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/).
- **Investments in rural health:** Nebraska
  [secured](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states)
  nearly $219 million in [first-year
  funding](https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/Rural-Health-Transformation.aspx)
  under the Working Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation
  Program — the eighth-largest award in the country — to modernize care
  in rural communities.
- **Protecting Nebraska’s economy:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [protected](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=37)
  37,000 Nebraska jobs, preserved [$7
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in state GDP, and safeguarded [$3
  billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/president-trumps-working-families-tax-cuts-ignite-manufacturing-renaissance-across-all-50-states/)
  in wages for Nebraskans.

## **Standing with Nebraska Farmers and Ranchers**

**President Trump’s America First agenda is delivering real dollars and
new market access to the producers who feed the country.**

- **Year-round E15:** In April 2025, President Trump’s EPA
  [granted](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-fortifies-domestic-fuel-supply-provides-americans-relief-pump-approving-nationwide)
  the emergency waiver that cleared summer sales of E15 nationwide,
  answering the direct
  [request](https://governor.nebraska.gov/governor-pillen-celebrates-epa-e15-waiver)
  of Nebraska and other Midwestern states and expanding the market for
  Nebraska ethanol and corn.
- **Direct aid:** USDA has
  [delivered](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/emergency-commodity-assistance-program/dashboard)
  $622 million to 36,000 Nebraska Farmers through the Emergency
  Commodity Assistance Program for the 2024 crop year. Nebraska farmers
  have also
  [received](https://www.nefb.org/news/a-bridge-over-troubled-waters)
  more than $632 million in Farmer Bridge Assistance payments — part of
  the $12 billion [national bridge
  program](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
  for trade disruptions and higher input costs left by the Biden
  Administration — with nearly
  [33,000](https://www.nefb.org/news/a-bridge-over-troubled-waters#:~:text=Figure%205%20shows,applied%20for%20payments.)
  Nebraska producers applying. They remain
  [eligible](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/07/09/trump-administration-announces-expedited-congressionally-mandated-disaster-assistance-farmers)
  for the $16 billion Supplemental Disaster Relief Program covering 2023
  and 2024 crop losses.
- **Ranchers first:** In September 2026, President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-supports-americas-ranchers/)
  two Executive Orders backing American ranchers. One [directs
  action](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history#:~:text=Today%E2%80%99s%20Executive%20Orders%3A-,SUPPORTING%20AMERICA%E2%80%99S%20RANCHERS,origin%20labeling%20for%20beef%20products%20through%20new%20regulations%20and%20legislative%20recommendations.,-PROMOTING%20FAIR%20COMPETITION)
  to help producers protect herds from wolves and other predators, and
  advances country-of-origin labeling transparency. The second
  [orders](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history#:~:text=PROMOTING%20FAIR%20COMPETITION,unnecessary%20red%20tape.)
  tougher enforcement of the Packers and Stockyards Act and expands
  ranchers’ ability to get cattle to market without compromising food
  safety.

## **New Investment Across Nebraska**

**President Trump’s economic agenda is delivering major private-sector
investment and expansion.**

- Eaton is
  [investing](https://www.eaton.com/us/en-us/company/news-insights/news-releases/2026/eaton-expands-operations-in-nebraska-with-new-manufacturing-facility.html)
  more than $30 million in a new Bellevue facility to expand U.S.
  production of medium-voltage switchgear for data centers, creating
  more than 200 new jobs.
- Becton Dickinson is
  [investing](https://news.bd.com/2026-01-13-BD-Announces-110-Million-to-Support-U-S-Pharmaceutical-Supply-Chain-for-Biologic-Drugs)
  $110 million to expand prefillable syringe production at its Columbus
  plant, creating about 120 new jobs, on top of its earlier $35 million
  [investment](https://investors.bd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/897/bd-to-invest-35-million-in-nebraska-facility-to-expand-prefilled-flush-syringe-manufacturing-to-support-u-s-health-care)
  at the same plant.

## **Safer Communities for Nebraskans**

**President Trump’s commitment to law and order is producing real drops
in crime in Nebraska’s largest cities.**

- In Omaha, crime is
  [down](https://police.cityofomaha.org/opd-crime-statistics#docaccess-c0dc8dd417e4964b60ff28e2f1229670)
  from last year, including an 11% drop in total crime, a 12% drop in
  property crime, a 33% drop in criminal homicide, a 21% drop in rape, a
  31% drop in robbery, and a 29% drop in auto theft.
- In Lincoln, violent crime is
  [down](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/266/police/crime-analysis/weeklystats.pdf)
  15%, robbery is down 31%, aggravated assault is down 16%, murder is
  down 75%, and property crime is down 9%.
