FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Today at the *Science: A New Golden Age* Summit, President Donald J.
Trump presented National Medals of Science and National Medals of
Technology and Innovation, our nation’s highest honors for exemplary
achievement and leadership in science and technology, to six of
America’s greatest living innovators.  
   
Since 1963, the President of the United States has bestowed the National
Medal of Science on those deserving of special recognition for their
outstanding contributions to science in service of the United States.
Since it was first awarded in 1985, the National Medal of Technology and
Innovation has recognized American innovators whose vision, intellect,
creativity, and determination have strengthened America’s economy and
improved our quality of life.

President Trump is committed to restoring U.S. global scientific and
technological leadership for a New Golden Age, from harnessing super
intelligence for scientific discovery to ushering in a nuclear
renaissance; from ensuring American space superiority to strengthening
America’s quantum advantage.

These medalists have pioneered breakthroughs in science and engineering
for the new age of super intelligence, from fundamental advances in
general-purpose parallel computing and semiconductor design to
engineering and business leadership in building the foundations of
hyperscale infrastructure in the U.S., around the world, and even into
space. Their work has advanced American global technology leadership for
the 21<sup>st</sup> Century and laid the foundation for a new Golden Age
of Science.

The recipients are listed below. 

**Recipients of the National Medal of Science**

- Elon Musk – “For engineering achievements of extraordinary ambition
  and scale that have pushed the frontiers of space exploration,
  intelligent systems, communications, and beyond, renewing American
  technological leadership and opening a new chapter in American
  manufacturing.”
- Sergey Brin – “For the development of foundational algorithms for
  information retrieval and data processing that revolutionized how the
  world accesses information across science, commerce, and in the course
  of everyday life.” 
- Jensen Huang – “For advancing the graphics processing unit into a
  general-purpose computing architecture through innovations in
  processor design and software, transforming massively parallel
  computation and powering breakthroughs in scientific computing and
  super intelligence.”
- Lisa Su – “For a lifetime of leadership in semiconductor engineering
  and high-performance computing through innovations in processor
  architecture and chiplet-based integration that powered the world’s
  first exascale supercomputer and opened new frontiers in computational
  performance.”

**Recipients of the National Medal of Technology and Innovation**

- Michael Dell – “For advancing a vision of widespread adoption of
  personal and enterprise computing; for his technical and business
  management skills building a worldwide technology company; and for his
  support for the next generation of American scientists, engineers, and
  technologists.”
- Satya Nadella – “For his transformative leadership of a critical
  American technology company, which led to its rejuvenation for a new
  era of cloud computing, and for his construction of the hyperscale
  infrastructure that enabled the training and global deployment of
  American super intelligence.”

** **“Each of these medalists has transformed their field or industry,
laying the foundations for technologies we already can barely imagine
living without,” said **Michael Kratsios, Assistant to the President and
Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy**.
“While we in government work to deliver the efforts a New Golden Age of
Science will require, we know that scientific progress and technological
innovation depend on the insight and character of individuals.
Innovators like these six awardees are the architects of the future.”

**About the Science: A New Golden Age Summit:**

[*Science: A New Golden
Age*](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Science-A-New-Golden-Age.pdf),
released in July 2026 by White House Office of Science and Technology
Policy Director Michael Kratsios, is the first comprehensive rethinking
of the U.S. science and technology enterprise since Vannevar Bush’s
1945 *Science: The Endless Frontier.*

The report observes significant changes to how scientific discovery
happens today, particularly the increased role of industry in driving
R&D and the close integration of applied and foundational research. It
provides concrete recommendations for how government, industry,
academia, and philanthropy can seize the opportunities presented by
these changes to accelerate scientific discovery and empower the
Nation’s best and brightest scientists. Core recommendations include
strengthening support for early-career scientists, reviving the national
pursuit of ambitious scientific missions, launching new funding and
organizational models for transformative research, building the
metascience infrastructure to continuously improve the research
enterprise, and scaling SI-driven scientific discovery through the
Genesis Mission.

The *Science: A New Golden Age* Summit convenes leading scientists and
innovators to examine these changes and translate the report’s ideas
into the next chapter of American scientific progress. Throughout the
day, the Trump Administration will provide major announcements, in
coordination with private sector partners and research institutions, to
realize the vision of *Science: A New Golden Age* and the Kyoto Vision
for a Golden Age of Science.
