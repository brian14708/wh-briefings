*New Analysis
[Confirms](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
President Trump’s Pro-Growth Policies Are Driving American Manufacturing
Resurgence*

President Donald J. Trump’s America First agenda is revitalizing
American manufacturing, delivering historic investments, driving up
wages, and igniting a blue-collar BOOM.

After the Biden Administration lost more than 200,000 American
manufacturing jobs in its final two years due to reckless Democrat
polices, President Trump is delivering on his promise to put American
workers first.

The results speak for themselves. New analysis by the Council of
Economic Advisors (CEA) reports that wages are rising faster than
inflation, domestic manufacturing is expanding, and the blue-collar jobs
lost under the Biden Administration are rightly returning back to U.S.
soil.

Wages are outpacing inflation under President Trump, reversing the
$3,000 annual decline in real wages under Biden. Since President Trump
took office, blue-collar workers are seeing even bigger gains:
manufacturing wages are up [$2,000 and construction wages
$2,800](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf). 

Demand for factory construction workers is surging, hiring rising, and
layoffs down over the past year. Overtime pay is also up— thanks to
President Trump’s no-tax-on-overtime policy, workers are now keeping
more of what they earn.

The benefits of President Trump’s pro-growth agenda and successful
tariff policies extend beyond factory floors. This resurgence is
strengthening America’s position in critical industries essential to our
national security, including aerospace, aircraft equipment, metals, and
natural gas and petroleum production. 

A stronger manufacturing sector means more opportunities for American
workers, higher wages, and business expansion that supports Main Street
economies who support communities across the country. 

## **BY THE NUMBERS**

- **AMERICAN MANUFACTURING JOBS SURGING:** Manufacturing [employment is
  up](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
  72,000 in 2026, including a 101,000 job increase in durable goods,
  reversing losses of more than 200,000 manufacturing jobs during the
  Biden Administration’s last two years. Since President Trump took
  office, [construction
  employment](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
  has grown by 100,000, including [more
  than](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
  111,000 additional factory construction jobs.
- **RISE IN ANNUAL WAGES:** Inflation-adjusted wages for blue-collar
  manufacturing workers have risen an average of $2,400 more per year
  since President Trump took office. Construction workers’ real wages
  are up
  [ $3,800](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf).
- **HIGHEST BUSINESS GROWTH IN 5 YEARS:** U.S. business growth has
  reached its highest level in more than five years, while manufacturing
  expands and American goods exports are on track for a record year.
- **NO TAX ON OVERTIME:** American construction workers are keeping more
  of what they earn thanks to No tax on overtime, with more than 29
  million Americans claiming the deduction this filing season.
- **TRILLIONS IN INVESTMENTS SECURED:** President Trump [has
  secured](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
  trillions of dollars in investment commitments across the United
  States, supporting domestic production, factory expansion, and
  American jobs.
- **SMALL BUSINESS GROWTH:** Small businesses and local economies [are
  feeling](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-State-of-American-Manufacturing_Oct2026.pdf)
  the benefits of President Trump’s manufacturing boom.
