  Seniors, and Veterans

**President Donald J. Trump returns to Florida today to highlight a
clear record of results that puts hardworking Americans first.** Under
President Trump and Republican leadership, Floridians are seeing
historic tax relief, a surge in private investment and job creation,
stronger rural healthcare, accelerated infrastructure and environmental
progress, expanded support for veterans, and safer communities.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Floridians**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of Sunshine State workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 4.8 million Florida
  seniors
  [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Florida-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf)
  from the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay
  zero federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Florida taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$4,433](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026 — the highest of any state.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=11) 399,000
  Florida jobs, $73 billion in state GDP, and $36 billion in workers’
  wages.
- **Expanding Workforce Opportunity:** In September 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [approved](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-approves-workforce-pell-grant-programs-florida)
  Florida’s Workforce Pell Grant program, enabling students at Eastern
  Florida State College and other institutions to use Pell Grant funding
  for high-demand careers such as firefighting, corrections, and
  emergency management.

## **Unleashing Private Investment and Job Creation**

**President Trump’s pro-growth, America First policies are attracting
significant private capital and creating high-quality jobs across
Florida.**

- **Johnson & Johnson** is
  [investing](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-invests-more-than-1-billion-to-strengthen-u-s-vision-manufacturing-in-jacksonville-florida)
  $1 billion to upgrade manufacturing and packaging technologies and
  build a new state-of-the-art distribution facility in Jacksonville.
- **Williams International** is
  [investing](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2025/state-florida-and-williams-international-announce-billion-dollar-investment)
  $1 billion to build a high-volume aviation gas turbine engine
  manufacturing facility in Okaloosa County.
- **Blue Origin** is
  [investing](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2026/governor-ron-desantis-announces-600m-blue-origin-manufacturing-expansion-500-high)
  $600 million to expand its Rocket Park campus at Cape Canaveral
  Spaceport.
- **Lupin** is
  [investing](https://www.lupin.com/media/press-releases/lupin-announces-plans-to-build-a-new-state-of-the-art-manufacturing-facility-in-coral-springs-florida)
  $250 million to construct a new pharmaceutical manufacturing plant in
  Coral Springs.
- **Chick-fil-A** is
  [investing](https://www.csx.com/index.cfm/about-us/csx-served-chick-fil-a-distribution-center-breaks-ground-in-central-florida/)
  $150 million to build a new distribution center in Winter Haven.
- **Monin** is
  [investing](https://zephyrhills-community.com/posts/monin-to-build-111-million-flavor-plant-in-zephyrhills-adding-100-jobs)
  $111 million to build a manufacturing plant in Zephyrhills.
- **Tampa Brass & Aluminum** is
  [investing](https://tbbwmag.com/2026/09/14/tampa-brass-aluminum-expansion/)
  $58 million in machinery and equipment at its Tampa headquarters.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare Access**

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources to expand and
modernize care in Florida’s rural communities.**

- Florida [secured](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states) $210
  million in first-year funding through the Working Families Tax Cuts’
  Rural Health Transformation Program — the largest federal investment
  in rural healthcare in American history.
- In August 2026, the state
  [awarded](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2026/governor-ron-desantis-announces-nearly-188-million-expand-rural-health-care-access)
  $188 million in Rural Health Transformation Program grants across 31
  rural counties. These funds support expanded access to care, growth of
  the rural healthcare workforce, preventive and home-based services,
  specialty and acute care, value-based care models, rural and satellite
  clinics, and health and lifestyle initiatives.

## **Investing in Florida’s Infrastructure and Natural Resources**

**President Trump is accelerating critical projects that protect
Florida’s communities and natural resources while strengthening the
state’s economy and readiness.**

- **Accelerating Everglades Restoration:** Working with the U.S. Army
  Corps of Engineers, the Trump Administration
  [modified](https://www.eenews.net/articles/trump-desantis-administrations-agree-to-speed-everglades-restoration/)
  key contracts to expedite the Everglades Agricultural Area Reservoir.
  The project is now [on
  track](https://www.eenews.net/articles/trump-desantis-administrations-agree-to-speed-everglades-restoration/)
  for completion by 2029 — five years ahead of the previous timeline —
  improving water distribution to the Everglades, protecting drinking
  water supplies, and reducing harmful discharges.
- **Upgrading Critical Infrastructure:** In October 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [awarded](https://nefi.com/news-publications/recent-news/dot-investing-426-million-to-eliminate-highway-bottlenecks)
  $85 million to upgrade 20 miles of I-4 highway between State Road 434
  and State Road 472 in Seminole and Volusia counties. In August 2026,
  the Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-invests-351-million-small-shipyards)
  over $1 million to the Gulf Marine Corporation in Tampa for training
  center equipment, wheel loaders, and upgrades to three berths. In May
  2026, the Federal Aviation Administration
  [awarded](https://cbs12.com/news/local/florida-news-new-name-upgrades-10-million-dollars-headed-donald-j-trump-international-airport-djt-palm-beach-international-airport-name-change-new-restrooms-mothers-rooms-sensory-room-travelers-special-needs-us-transportation-secretary-sean-duffy)
  President Donald J. Trump International Airport $10 million to expand
  the terminal and add new facilities for mothers and travelers with
  special needs.
- **Advancing Roadway Safety:** Through [Operation Highway
  Shield](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/operation-highway-shield-trumps-departments-transportation-homeland-security-sweep),
  the Trump Administration
  [partnered](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-invests-nearly-1-billion-making-airports)
  with state and local law enforcement to conduct more than 3,300
  inspections across Florida in just one month, focusing on unqualified
  foreign drivers operating commercial vehicles. 176 drivers were placed
  out of service, including 54 for language deficiencies, while dozens
  of others were arrested on criminal charges and immigration
  violations.
- **Delivering Disaster Relief:** The Trump Administration has moved
  quickly to support Florida communities struck by disaster. In October
  2026, President Trump
  [authorized](https://katv.com/news/nation-world/trump-approves-emergency-declaration-for-florida-due-to-hurricane-isaias)
  FEMA coordination following Hurricane Isaias. Earlier actions included
  more than $116 million to
  [accelerate](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260929/fema-announces-more-116-million-florida-recovery)
  recovery after extreme storms, an
  [increase](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260702/president-donald-j-trump-increases-federal-cost-share-florida)
  in the federal cost-share for Hurricane Michael recovery, and nearly
  $416 million in
  [additional](https://floridianpress.com/2026/07/trump-announces-over-400-million-in-disaster-relief-for-florida/)
  disaster relief.
- **Funding Rail Safety:** In September 2025, the Trump Administration
  [released](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-announces-over-42-million-critical)
  $42 million in four
  [grants](https://www.wlrn.org/light/south-florida/2025-09-23/feds-release-brightline-safety-funding-to-address-unnecessary-danger)
  for critical rail safety projects along the Brightline corridor after
  the projects had been stalled in the Biden Administration’s backlog.
- **Advancing American Space Leadership:** Artemis II
  [launched](https://www.wesh.com/article/artemis-ii-florida-launch-moon-mission-watch-live/70898167)
  from Florida’s Space Coast in April 2026. Artemis III is also
  scheduled to [launch](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-iii/) from
  NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, reinforcing Florida’s central role in
  America’s return to the Moon and beyond.

## **Honoring and Supporting Florida Veterans**

**President Trump is expanding resources that strengthen care, housing
stability, and prevention services for the heroes who have served our
nation.**

- In October 2026, the VA
  [awarded](https://www.va.gov/west-palm-beach-health-care/news-releases/va-awards-more-than-600000-in-grants-for-adaptive-sports-programs-in-florida/)
  more than $600,000 to Florida community organizations for adaptive
  sports programs that help veterans build independence, well-being, and
  quality of life.
- In September 2026, the VA
  [awarded](https://www.va.gov/bay-pines-health-care/news-releases/va-awards-113-million-in-grants-to-fight-veteran-homelessness-in-florida/)
  more than $113 million in grants to 42 Florida organizations to assist
  veterans who are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless.
- The VA also
  [awarded](https://www.va.gov/west-palm-beach-health-care/news-releases/va-awards-62-million-to-organizations-working-to-strengthen-community-based-suicide-prevention-efforts-in/)
  more than $6 million to 12 Florida organizations providing suicide
  prevention services to veterans.
- In June 2026, the VA [broke
  ground](https://www.va.gov/bay-pines-health-care/news-releases/bay-pines-va-city-community-leaders-break-ground-on-new-north-port-va-clinic/)
  on a new clinic in North Port that will replace the Port Charlotte
  clinic and serve more than 10,000 veterans annually.

## **Making Florida Safer and More Secure**

**President Trump is delivering decisive action that is driving down
crime in Florida communities while securing the border and ending the
failed policies of the past.**

- **Historic Drops in Violent Crime:** Violent crime
  [declined](https://www.facebook.com/FBIMiami/posts/from-2024-to-2025-violent-crime-dropped-97-nationwidethe-largest-year-to-year-de/122293712000034579/)
  across Florida last year, with murders
  [down](https://www.facebook.com/FBIMiami/posts/from-2024-to-2025-violent-crime-dropped-97-nationwidethe-largest-year-to-year-de/122293712000034579/)
  19%. In Miami, in the first half of 2026 compared with the same period
  in 2025, homicides
  [plunged](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  50%, rapes
  [dropped](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  22%, robberies
  [fell](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  22%, and aggravated assaults
  [declined](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-Midyear.pdf)
  8%.
- **Securing the Border:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.nbcmiami.com/news/local/florida-gets-608m-from-trump-administration-for-immigration-detention-efforts/3700873/)
  Florida $608 million to expand illegal alien detention capacity.
  Catch-and-release has been fully
  [ended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/securing-our-borders/),
  producing [17 straight
  months](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  of zero releases at the border and [zero Border Patrol
  releases](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  in FY2026 — the first fiscal year in history with that result. Illegal
  southern border crossings were
  [slashed](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  by 95% from Biden-era levels in FY2026, the
  [lowest](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  total on record. Refugee admissions have also been [sharply
  reduced](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/08/17/refugee-admissions-to-the-us-are-dropping-sharply-in-trumps-second-term/).
