**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to the Peach State with a
proven record of America First results that put Georgia workers,
families, seniors, and communities first.** Under the leadership of
President Trump and Republicans, Georgians are seeing historic tax
relief, a surge of private investment and job creation, lower energy
costs, support for farmers, the most secure border in a generation, and
safer streets.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Georgians**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of Georgia workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 1.8 million Georgia
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Georgia-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay zero
  federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Georgia taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,169](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=12) 198,000
  Georgia jobs, $36 billion in state GDP, and $18 billion in workers’
  wages.
- **Rural Health
  Investments:** Georgia [secured](https://scdailygazette.com/2025/12/30/sc-to-receive-200m-in-first-rural-hospital-funding-allocation/) $219
  million in first-year Rural Health Transformation Program funding from
  the Working Families Tax Cuts.

## **Supporting Georgia Families, Farmers, and Communities**

**President Trump is shielding Georgia families from higher costs and
delivering support where it is needed most.**

- **Protecting Ratepayers:** Georgia Power is
  [freezing](https://www.georgiapower.com/news-hub/press-releases/georgia-psc-approves-plan-to-freeze-base-rates-through-2028.html)
  base rates through 2029 and
  [delivering](https://www.georgiapower.com/news-hub/press-releases/contract-openai-approved-part-portfolio-delivering-950-million-annual-savings.html)
  more than $2.8 billion in savings — $180 per year in savings for the
  typical residential consumer. Georgia’s major utilities have
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/ratepayer-protection-pledge/)
  President Trump’s [Ratepayer Protection
  Pledge](https://www.whitehouse.gov/ratepayer-protection-pledge/),
  ensuring large data centers — not Georgia ratepayers — pay for the
  power and infrastructure they require.
- **Delivering for Farmers:** The Trump Administration has delivered
  [more than $177
  million](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/emergency-commodity-assistance-program/dashboard)
  to over 4,300 Georgia farmers through the Emergency Commodity
  Assistance Program and [more than $171
  million](https://publicdashboards.dl.usda.gov/t/FPAC_PUB/views/FSA-DAFP-FBADashboard/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity?%3Aembed=y&%3AisGuestRedirectFromVizportal=y)
  to over 4,000 producers under a [$12
  billion](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
  national bridge program for trade disruptions and higher input costs
  left by the Biden Administration.
- **Delivering Disaster Relief:** After Hurricane Helene, President
  Trump
  [raised](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260703/president-donald-j-trump-increases-federal-cost-share-georgia)
  the federal cost-share for Georgia disaster relief from 75% to 90%,
  [approved](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/116841674801545467)
  a $318.3 million Disaster Declaration Request, and
  [announced](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/12/12/secretary-noem-announces-1-billion-fema-funding-georgia)
  more than $1 billion in FEMA funding for communities and healthcare
  facilities.

## **Securing the Border and Ending the Biden-era Betrayal**

**President Trump ended the open borders policies that enabled tragedies
like the murder of Laken Riley and restored the most secure border in a
generation.**

- President
  Trump [signed](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/1884690966667231272) the
  Laken Riley Act into law,
  [requiring](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-signs-laken-riley-act-law-first-legislative-victory-new-administration)
  detention of illegal immigrants charged with theft or violence —
  honoring the legacy of Laken Riley, a Georgia student murdered by an
  illegal alien previously released into the country.
- The Trump Administration [has not
  released](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border)
  a single illegal alien from the border into the U.S. interior for [17
  straight
  months](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/10/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest-fiscal-year-border).
- Refugee admissions are
  [down](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/08/17/refugee-admissions-to-the-us-are-dropping-sharply-in-trumps-second-term/#:~:text=The%20federal%20government%20has%20admitted%2010%2C258%20refugees%20through%20the%20first%2010%20months%20of%20fiscal%20year%202026%2C%20which%20ends%20on%20Sept.%2030.%20That%E2%80%99s%20down%20from%2038%2C102%20refugees%20in%20fiscal%202025%20and%20100%2C034%20in%20fiscal%202024.%20(President%20Joe%20Biden%20was%20in%20office%20for%20part%20of%20fiscal%202025%20and%20all%20of%20fiscal%202024.))
  nearly 90% from 2024 levels.
- Southern border apprehensions in August were [94%
  lower](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/16/trump-administration-delivers-16-straight-months-zero-releases-border)
  than the monthly average under the Biden Administration.
- The U.S.
  [recorded](https://abcnews.com/US/us-1st-time-50-years-experienced-negative-net/story?id=129175522)
  negative net migration in 2025 for the first time in half a century,
  with more than three million illegal aliens having
  [left](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/01/20/dhs-sets-stage-another-historic-record-breaking-year-under-president-trump)
  the country since President Trump returned to office.
- ICE has
  [arrested](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/06/24/making-america-safe-again-ice-hits-milestone-more-10000-gang-members-arrested-under)
  more than 10,000 gang members during President Trump’s second term.

## **Making Georgia’s Streets Safer**

**Crime is falling across Georgia as President Trump’s immigration
enforcement, support for law enforcement, and tough-on-crime policies
Make America Safe Again.**

- In Atlanta, murders
  [dropped](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2025-and-2024-Year-End.pdf)
  22% last year and continued plunging in 2026 — with homicides
  [down](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2026-and-2025-First-Quarter.pdf)
  36% in the first quarter compared to last year.
- In DeKalb County, murders
  [fell](https://majorcitieschiefs.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MCCA-Violent-Crime-Report-2025-and-2024-Year-End.pdf)
  nearly 30% last year.
