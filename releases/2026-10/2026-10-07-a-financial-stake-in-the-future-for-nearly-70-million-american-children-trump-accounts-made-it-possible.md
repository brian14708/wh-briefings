Trump Accounts—tax-advantaged investment accounts for kids—are a
game-changing tool designed to help every American child build savings
and a stronger financial future.

With automatic enrollment now complete, the Trump Administration has
made it easier than ever for families to participate and give their
children a jump-start on the American Dream. Nearly 70 million Trump
Accounts have now been created, including more than 60 million through
automatic enrollment, giving every American child under 18, with a
Social Security number, an account in their name. Now, parents or
guardians just have to claim their child’s Trump Account on the [Trump
Accounts App](https://trumpaccounts.gov/) and unlock what’s waiting for
their child.

Today, President Donald J. Trump celebrated this historic milestone
alongside Trump Account recipients, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent,
IRS CEO Frank Bisignano, philanthropists Michael and Susan Dell, and
Brad Gerstner, whose historic contributions have helped fund millions of
Trump Accounts.

**<span style="text-decoration: underline">Trump Accounts By The
Numbers:</span>**

- **Nearly 70 million Trump Accounts have been created, with more than
  60 million through automatic enrollment. **The Trump Administration’s
  automatic enrollment has created millions of Trump Accounts, making it
  easier than ever for families to help secure their children’s
  financial futures. Every American child under 18 with a valid Social
  Security number now has a Trump Account in their name.
- **Parents and guardians simply need to claim their child’s account
  through the [Trump Accounts App](https://trumpaccounts.gov/)**.
  Claiming an account allows parents to manage it, unlock contributions
  from family and friends, and receive the one-time $1,000 seed
  contribution from for eligible children.
- **Trump Accounts are already investing billions of dollars for
  American children.** Since
  their [launch](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0554?) on
  July 4th, more than $4.5 billion has been deposited into Trump
  Accounts, including $1.3 billion in $1,000 seed contributions, over
  $600 million in direct contributions from family and friends, and $2.6
  billion in philanthropic gifts—with more on the way.
- **Trump Accounts are reaching hardworking families across the
  country.** An estimated 80% of accounts are linked to families earning
  less than $200,000 annually, putting the opportunity to build
  long-term savings and wealth within reach for millions of Americans.
- **Families, employers, and philanthropists can all help children build
  their accounts.** Family and friends can contribute up to $5,000 per
  year; employers can contribute up to $2,500 per year, subject to the
  overall $5,000 annual contribution limit, and children can qualify for
  philanthropic contributions that are automatically deposited into
  their accounts. To date, more than 70 companies have already committed
  to Trump Account contributions for their employees.
- **Trump Accounts give children a financial foundation that can grow
  with them. **Over time, children can watch their balance grow from a
  couple of thousand dollars in kindergarten to an [estimated half a
  million](https://www.whitehouse.gov/research/2025/08/trump-accounts-give-the-next-generation-a-jump-start-on-saving/#:~:text=%24303%2C800%20by%20age%2018%20and%20%241%2C091%2C900%20by%20age%2028%20if%20maximum%20contributions%20are%20made.) dollars
  by retirement—while learning essential financial skills like saving,
  investing, compound growth, and diversification.

Trump Accounts are made possible by President Trump and Republicans’
historic Working Families Tax Cuts legislation. These tax-advantaged
investment accounts— **that every single Democrat voted against**— are
giving millions of kids a head start on building wealth and a financial
stake in their future.
