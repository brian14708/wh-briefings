**President Donald J. Trump returns to Iowa today to highlight a clear
record of results for workers, families, seniors, farmers, and rural
communities.** Under President Trump and Republican leadership, Iowans
are seeing historic tax relief, major private investment, stronger rural
healthcare, and direct support for the producers who feed the country.

## **Delivering Historic Tax Relief for Iowans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are delivering real relief
to Hawkeye State workers, families, farmers, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 600,000 Iowa seniors
  [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Iowa-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf)
  from the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay
  zero federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Iowa taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,365](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=17) 57,000
  Iowa jobs, $10 billion in state GDP, and $5 billion in workers’ wages.
- **Lowering Taxes for Family Farms:** The Working Families Tax Cuts
  [made](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/INVESTS-IN-RURAL-AMERICA.pdf)
  the increased death tax exemption permanent and indexed it for
  inflation, helping keep generational farms in the family. The package
  also
  [made](https://home.treasury.gov/wftc/fact-sheets/Delivering%20Relief%20to%20America%27s%20Heartland.pdf)
  100% expensing permanent, doubled the Section 179 limit, made the 20%
  small business deduction permanent, expanded crop insurance, and
  [increased](https://www.fb.org/intel/markets/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-final-agricultural-provisions)
  reference prices for the first time in over a decade.
- **Expanding Workforce Grants:** In August 2026, the Trump
  Administration
  [approved](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-approves-nations-first-workforce-pell-grant-program-iowa)
  the nation’s first Workforce Pell Grant program, allowing students at
  Iowa Central Community College to use Pell dollars for the Emergency
  Medical Technician program.

## **Standing with Iowa Farmers and Producers**

**President Trump’s America First agenda is delivering direct support,
lower costs, and expanded markets to the farmers and producers who feed
the country.**

- **Year-Round E15 and Stronger Biofuels:** In 2025, the Trump
  Administration
  [cleared](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-fortifies-domestic-fuel-supply-provides-americans-relief-pump-approving-nationwide)
  summer sales of E15 nationwide,
  [waived](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-fortifies-domestic-fuel-supply-provides-americans-relief-pump-approving-nationwide) all
  federal barriers to E10, and
  [implemented](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-finalizes-historic-new-renewable-fuel-standards-strengthen-american-energy)
  the
  [highest-ever](https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard/final-renewable-fuel-standards-2026-and-2027)
  renewable fuel volume requirements.
- **Historic Production:** In 2025, Iowa farmers
  [posted](https://iowaagriculture.gov/news/usda-crop-production-summary)
  the state’s second-highest corn yield, a record corn production (2.77
  billion bushels), a record soybean yield, and its third-highest
  soybean production.
- **Direct Relief:** USDA has delivered more than [$833
  million](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/emergency-commodity-assistance-program/dashboard)
  to 57,000 Iowa farmers through the Emergency Commodity Assistance
  Program and more than [$867
  million](https://publicdashboards.dl.usda.gov/t/FPAC_PUB/views/FSA-DAFP-FBADashboard/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity?%3Aembed=y&%3AisGuestRedirectFromVizportal=y)
  in [Farmer Bridge
  Assistance](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
  payments. The Trump Administration also
  [announced](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/10/19/trump-administration-announces-8-billion-harvest-fuel-relief-program)
  an $8 billion Harvest Fuel Relief program to help address temporary
  higher input costs and market disruptions.
- **Reducing Costs:** President Trump
  [suspended](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-declares-an-emergency-and-authorizes-the-temporary-suspension-of-certain-duties-on-phosphate-fertilizer-from-morocco/)
  certain phosphate fertilizer duties,
  [designated](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/06/29/president-trump-takes-action-lower-fertilizer-costs-and-support-american-farmers#:~:text=The%20Administration%20has%20designated%20phosphate%20and%20potash%20as%20critical%20minerals%2C)
  phosphate and potash as critical minerals,
  [extended](https://www.rfdtv.com/jones-act-waiver-keeps-fertilizer-shipping-options-open)
  a Jones Act waiver to improve fertilizer logistics, and
  [launched](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/07/01/secretary-rollins-announces-500-million-fertilizer-investment-and-expansion-program-strengthen)
  the $500 million FIELDS Program to expand domestic fertilizer
  production. The Administration also temporarily
  [allowed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-promotes-diesel-affordability/)
  off-road dyed diesel for highway use and deferred the federal excise
  tax to lower fuel costs.
- **Expanding Market Access:** Ethanol exports reached a record high in
  2025, up 13% from the year prior and 18% since President Trump
  returned to office, while U.S. food and agricultural exports have
  [risen](https://x.com/USTradeRep/status/2036435169456857269/photo/1)
  under
  [expanded](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/april/liberation-day-one-year-later-protecting-american-jobs-and-delivering-greater-market-access-american#:~:text=%E2%80%9COn%20Liberation%20Day,critical%20supply%20chains.%E2%80%9D)
  global market access.
- **Cutting Red Tape:** President Trump’s “[Right to
  Repair](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/epa-advances-farmers-right-repair-their-own-equipment-saving-repair-costs-and)”
  effort allows farmers to lawfully repair their equipment, saving
  thousands of dollars per repair. The Administration also
  [fixed](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/trump-administration-announces-latest-action-address-diesel-exhaust-fluid-def-system)
  diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) system issues that were forcing farm
  equipment and trucks to derate or shut down.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare Across Iowa**

**President Trump is directing targeted resources to expand access and
modernize care in Iowa’s rural communities.**

- **Rural Health Transformation:** Iowa
  [secured](https://hhs.iowa.gov/news-release/2026-10-09/iowa-leads-nation-early-rural-health-transformation-progress-obligating-20904006371-first-year-award)
  $209 million in first-year
  [funding](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states)
  through the Working Families Tax Cuts’ Rural Health Transformation
  Program to expand clinical services, strengthen the rural health
  workforce, modernize technology and infrastructure, and improve
  coordination.
- **Modernizing Local Facilities:** Funding from the program is
  [supporting](https://x.com/ZachNunn/status/2107562714146947110?s=20)
  upgrades that include an MRI at Lucas County Health Center, a PET/CT
  system and new providers at Mahaska Health, a robotic bronchoscopy
  system at North Iowa Medical Center, expanded prenatal care at Sioux
  Center Health, a new CT scanner at Washington County Hospital, and a
  radiation oncology center at Wayne County Hospital.

## **Unleashing Private Investment and High-Quality Jobs**

**President Trump’s pro-growth, America First agenda is driving major
private investment and job creation across Iowa.**

- **Mesabi Metallics**
  [announced](https://www.wsj.com/politics/trump-to-unveil-planned-15-billion-iowa-steel-project-051bc7d8)
  a $15 billion investment to build a major steelmaking complex in Lee
  County, creating thousands of construction jobs and at least 1,750
  permanent positions.
- **John Deere**
  [announced](https://www.cultivationcorridor.org/news-events/industry-news/john-deere-pledges-20-billion-us-expansion)
  a $20 billion U.S. investment, including new high-horsepower tractor
  assembly lines at its Waterloo plant.
- **Google**
  [announced](https://blog.google/feed/new-7-billion-investment-iowa/) a
  $7 billion investment in cloud and Super Intelligence infrastructure
  in Iowa, including a new data center in Cedar Rapids and expansion in
  Council Bluffs.
- **Kraft Heinz**
  [announced](https://www.tradeandindustrydev.com/region/illinois/news/pa-il-kraft-heinz-investing-3-billion-upgrade-us-34518)
  a $3 billion investment in its U.S. manufacturing operations,
  including its Davenport distribution center.
- **JBS USA**
  [announced](https://jbsfoodsgroup.com/articles/jbs-usa-to-invest-135-million-in-new-sausage-plant-in-perry-iowa)
  a $135 million investment in a production facility in Perry, expected
  to create hundreds of jobs.
- **Electrical Power Products**
  [announced](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55396433/four-iowa-manufacturers-invest-more-than-62-million-to-expand-operations)
  a $42 million expansion of its engineered-to-order electrical power
  control and protection systems manufacturing facility in Des Moines,
  creating 200 jobs.
- **Greater Machining and Manufacturing**
  [announced](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55396433/four-iowa-manufacturers-invest-more-than-62-million-to-expand-operations)
  a $16 million expansion in Independence, creating 100 jobs.
- **Webster City Custom Meats**
  [announced](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55396433/four-iowa-manufacturers-invest-more-than-62-million-to-expand-operations)
  a $2.5 million expansion of its meat processing facility in Webster
  City.
- **S&J Tube**
  [announced](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55396433/four-iowa-manufacturers-invest-more-than-62-million-to-expand-operations)
  a $2.4 million expansion in Wapello.
- **Protecting Ratepayers:** Under President Trump’s [Ratepayer
  Protection
  Pledge](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/03/ratepayer-protection-pledge/),
  data center developers partnering with Alliant Energy, including QTS,
  must
  [cover](https://q.com/commitments/#:~:text=QTS%20funds%20100,data%20centers%20use.)
  100% of the energy and infrastructure costs for their facilities while
  supporting a five-year [rate
  freeze](https://corridorbusiness.com/alliant-ceo-cedar-rapids-data-centers-will-help-enable-five-year-rate-freeze/#:~:text=and%20a%20five%2Dyear%20freeze%20on%20electrical%20rates%2C%20according%20to%20Alliant%20CEO%20Lisa%20Barton.)
  for Iowans.
- **Restarting Duane Arnold:** The Trump Administration
  [announced](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-closes-19-billion-loan-restart-duane-arnold-nuclear-plant)
  a loan of up to $1.9 billion to NextEra to restart the Duane Arnold
  Energy Center, bringing Iowa’s only nuclear plant back online with 615
  megawatts of baseload power — enough for nearly 500,000 homes.

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources that strengthen care,
housing stability, and prevention services for the veterans who have
served our country.**

  [awarded](https://www.va.gov/central-iowa-health-care/news-releases/va-awards-925536-to-organizations-working-to-strengthen-community-based-suicide-prevention-efforts-in-iowa/)
  funding to Iowa community organizations providing suicide prevention
  services to veterans.

  [announced](https://www.va.gov/iowa-city-health-care/news-releases/iowa-city-va-health-care-system-upgrades-health-care-infrastructure/)

  to support safe and effective patient care.

  funding to five Iowa organizations to help veterans who are homeless
  or at risk of becoming homeless.
