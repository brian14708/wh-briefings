title: Nearly Two Years In, President Trump Is Still Stacking Historic Wins at Record
  Speed
---
 
**On Day One, President Donald J. Trump**
[**promised**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/remarks/2025/01/the-inaugural-address/)
**to “move with purpose and speed to bring back hope, prosperity,
safety, and peace.”** Nearly two years into his second term, he has done
exactly that — delivering results at a pace Washington has never seen.

**Here are 32 landmark policy wins from the past few weeks alone:**

- President Trump [clawed
  back](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/09/president-trump-takes-historic-action-to-eliminate-wasteful-and-harmful-spending/)
  nearly $1 billion in wasteful spending aimed at illegal aliens and DEI
  programs.
- The Department of State
  [surpassed](https://www.foxnews.com/us/state-department-revokes-250000-visas-broad-immigration-vetting-crackdown)
  250,000 visa revocations, tightening vetting and removing criminal
  illegal aliens.
- President Trump
  [launched](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-streamlines-access-to-government-services-through-america-gov/)
  America.gov, a first-of-its-kind platform putting government services
  and information directly in Americans’ hands.
- President Trump
  [secured](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/mined-melted-and-poured-in-america-president-trump-reverses-years-of-anti-mining-policy/)
  a $15 billion Iowa steel mill set to add $95 billion to the economy,
  produce up to 10 million tons of steel per year, and create thousands
  of permanent jobs.
- The Department of the Treasury
  [began](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/obamacare-aca-refund-checks.html)
  sending Obamacare refund checks to nearly one million Americans who
  were overcharged under the Biden Administration.
- The Department of the Treasury
  [announced](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/treasury-trump-accounts-auto-enroll-more-than-60-million-children.html)
  that more than 60 million children have been automatically enrolled in
  Trump Accounts.
- The Department of the Treasury
  [sanctioned](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0636/) 21
  individuals and 25 companies tied to the Sinaloa Cartel.
- The Department of Education formally
  [rescinded](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-formally-rescinds-biden-administrations-illegal-title-ix-rewrite-reinstating-2020-regulation?utm_source=NOTUS+Newsletter+Audience&utm_campaign=df43448bd0-EMAIL_CAMPAIGN_2026_09_23_08_44&utm_medium=email&utm_term=0_-df43448bd0-746235601&mc_cid=df43448bd0&mc_eid=f5c0c43bca)
  the Biden Administration’s illegal Title IX rewrite.
- The Department of Energy
  [announced](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-speed-power-investments-across-26-states-lower-electricity)
  nearly $2 billion for grid upgrades across the country.
- The Department of Transportation
  [finalized](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/president-trump-transportation-secretary-duffy-finalize-freedom-means-affordable-cars)
  new vehicle efficiency standards that will save Americans nearly $140
  billion over five years.
- The Department of Commerce [locked
  in](https://www.wiwynn.com/news/wiwynn-strengthens-global-supply-chain-resilience-with-grand-opening-of-advanced-manufacturing-facility-in-texas?srsltid=AU7gw4VmtTNASfq1F27izYTfTHBKCILI_nAc7qw4PICAeZVrlHnhJUjT)
  a $1.6 billion advanced manufacturing investment in Texas.
- The Department of State
  [secured](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/nigeria-us-sign-agreement-boost-mining-investment-2026-09-24/)
  a historic agreement to develop Nigeria’s $700 billion worth of
  mineral resources.
- The Department of State
  [imposed](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesman/2026/09/protecting-the-sanctity-of-u-s-citizenshipa-new-visa-restriction-policy-for-those-who-engage-in-or-facilitate-birth-tourism)
  new visa restrictions to combat birth tourism and protect U.S.
  citizenship.
- The White House budget office
  [moved](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/white-house-inventories-us-capacity-ahead-plans-reshore-production-86-essential-medicines)
  to reshore production of 86 essential medicines, including penicillin,
  epinephrine, and ibuprofen.
- Federal regulators
  [authorized](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2876304-ferc-approves-more-gas-pipelines-in-southeast-us)
  the Kosciusko Junction pipeline to deliver an extra 1.2 billion cubic
  feet of natural gas per day to the Southeast.
- The Department of the Interior
  [completed](https://www.blm.gov/press-release/blm-oil-and-gas-lease-sale-utah-generates-nearly-45-million-revenue)
  a Utah oil and gas lease sale that brought in nearly $5 million, with
  Trump-era revenues already
  [exceeding](https://www.blm.gov/press-release/blms-second-quarter-2026-oil-and-gas-lease-sales-generate-41-billion)
  the entire Biden term.
- The Departments of Commerce and Energy
  [secured](https://naturalgasintel.com/news/eqt-expands-lng-reach-with-10-year-supply-deal-in-lithuania/)
  a ten-year LNG supply deal between Lithuania’s Ignitis and U.S.
  producer EQT.
- The Department of State
  [facilitated](https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2026/Biman-Bangladesh-Airlines-Orders-11-More-Boeing-Jets-to-Expand-Fleet/default.aspx)
  a landmark deal for Biman Bangladesh Airlines to purchase 11
  additional Boeing aircraft.
- The Department of the Treasury
  [expanded](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-proposed-regulations-on-installment-payments-for-tax-on-certain-farmland-sales)
  tax-payment flexibility for American farmers.
- The Office of Personnel Management
  [uncovered](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/office-personnel-management-uncovers-500m-fraud-waste-federal-health-benefits-programs)
  $500 million in fraud and waste in federal health benefit programs.
- Medicare officials
  [cancelled](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-cracks-down-fraud-waste-abuse-federal-health-insurance-marketplacer)
  315,000 unauthorized Obamacare enrollments, saving taxpayers roughly
  $2.2 billion.
- The Department of Justice
  [charged](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/dojs-fraud-division-uncovers-134m-stolen-social-security-benefits-one-month-takedown)
  17 defendants in a $1.3 million Social Security fraud scheme.
- The Department of Justice
  [sued](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-sues-minnesota-st-paul-and-minneapolis-over-firearm-purchase-waiting)
  the state of Minnesota and the cities of Minneapolis and Saint Paul
  over a gun scheme that violates the Second Amendment.
- The EEOC
  [resolved](https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/eeoc-defends-womens-rights-single-sex-spaces-work-illinois)
  a case against a municipal employer that refused to protect women’s
  single-sex spaces.
- Health officials
  [awarded](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-funds-tick-control-research-lyme-disease-alpha-gal-syndrome.html)
  $3 million to develop new ways to cut tick populations and fight Lyme
  and alpha-gal.
- The Export-Import Bank
  [secured](https://mercuria.com/mercuria-commits-500-million-to-vaultco-u-s-critical-mineral-stockpile-in-partnership-with-the-u-s-export-import-bank/)
  a $500 million private commitment to build a U.S. Strategic Critical
  Minerals Reserve.
- The Department of War
  [unveiled](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/sep/28/pete-hegseth-memo-lays-pentagon-plan-protect-elections-foreign/?srsltid=AU7gw4WRJrQox2c8DnCl8OHbQl5PujY2RqDY1DuxvOqiZT32yxroQErU)
  a plan to protect the 2026 midterm elections from foreign
  interference.
- The Department of War
  [raised](https://www.dailywire.com/news/exclusive-war-department-raises-hazard-pay-for-first-time-in-two-decades)
  hazard pay for U.S. service members for the first time in over two
  decades.
- The Department of War
  [awarded](https://www.rtx.com/news/news-center/2026/09/28/rtxs-raytheon-awarded-20-7-billion-multi-year-contract-for-amraam-under-landmar)
  a historic $20.7 billion contract to surge domestic missile
  production. 
- The Department of War
  [convened](https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4607679/military-spouse-commission-holds-first-meeting-identifies-top-issues/)
  the first Military Spouse Commission meeting at the White House to
  improve life for military families.
- The Coast Guard
  [seized](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/09/24/us-coast-guard-surpasses-260000-total-pounds-cocaine-seized-operation-pacific-viper)
  more than 260,000 pounds of cocaine under Operation Pacific Viper.
- The Department of Energy and the Department of War
  [signed](https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/articles/nnsa-and-joint-interagency-task-force-401-strengthen-partnership-counter-drone)
  an agreement to strengthen joint defenses against drone threats.
- President Trump’s Memphis Safe Task Force
  [surpassed](https://www.usmarshals.gov/news/press-release/memphis-safe-task-force-commemorates-first-year-of-relentless-action-against)
  13,100 criminal arrests and 2,200 illegal gun seizures, while his D.C.
  Safe and Beautiful Task Force neared 18,000 arrests and more than
  2,100 illegal gun seizures.
- First Lady Melania Trump
  [secured](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/09/adobe-amazon-intel-starlink-google-and-zoom-partner-with-first-lady-melania-trumps-fostering-the-future-together/)
  partnerships with Adobe, Amazon, Intel, Starlink, Google, and Zoom to
  expand technology and education for children through her Fostering the
  Future Together initiative.
