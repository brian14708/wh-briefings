title: President Trump Delivers Historic Wins for Kansas Workers, Families, Farmers,
  and Rural Communities
---
 
**Today, President Donald J. Trump returns to Kansas to showcase his
proven record of results that put Kansas workers, families, seniors,
farmers, and communities first.** Under the leadership of President
Trump and Republicans, Kansans are benefiting from historic tax relief,
surging private investment and job creation, stronger rural healthcare,
major support for farmers and ranchers, and new investments in the
state’s military installations.

## **Delivering Tax Relief for Hardworking Kansans**

**President Trump’s Working Families Tax Cuts are putting real money
back in the pockets of Kansas workers, families, and seniors.**

- **No Tax on Social Security:** Approximately 500,000 Kansas
  seniors [benefit](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Kansas-WH-OBBB-Savings-by-State.pdf) from
  the new senior deduction, with the average senior expected to pay zero
  federal taxes on Social Security benefits.
- **Bigger Refunds:** Kansas taxpayers saw average refunds of
  [$3,453](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/average-tax-refunds-by-state-county/)
  in 2026.
- **Protecting Jobs and Wages:** The Working Families Tax
  Cuts [safeguarded](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NAM_Tax_Wins_Collection_Final.pdf#page=18) 53,000
  Kansas jobs, $10 billion in state GDP, and $5 billion in workers’
  wages.

## **Strengthening Rural Healthcare**

**President Trump is delivering targeted resources to expand care in
Kansas’s rural communities.**

- **Rural Health
  Transformation:** Kansas [secured](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states) $222
  million in first-year funding from the Working Families Tax Cuts’
  Rural Health Transformation Program — the sixth-largest award among
  all states.
- **Modernizing Care Delivery:** The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/trump-administration-announces-nearly-17-million-adopt-emerging-healthcare-technologies-improve)
  nearly $17 million to 14 rural Kansas providers to adopt new
  diagnostic tools and remote patient monitoring, plus $780,000 to
  improve patient transport between facilities.

## **Unleashing Private Investment and Job Creation**

**President Trump’s pro-growth, America First agenda is driving major
private investment and high-quality job creation throughout the
Sunflower State.**

- Beale Infrastructure is
  [investing](https://bealeinfra.com/beale-infrastructure-announces-hyperscale-kansas-data-center-campus-advancing-3-billion-infrastructure-investment/)
  $3 billion to build a data center campus in De Soto.
- Merck Animal Health is
  [investing](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/merck-animal-health-announces-895-million-investment-kansas-2025-05-08/)
  $895 million to expand its De Soto manufacturing facility and R&D
  laboratories, creating 200 jobs.
- Fiserv is
  [investing](https://www.kansascommerce.gov/2025/04/governor-kelly-announces-fiserv-to-open-regional-fintech-hub-in-kansas/)
  $175 million to open a Strategic Fintech Hub in Overland Park,
  creating 2,000 jobs.
- Indra Group is
  [investing](https://www.indragroup.com/en/news/indra-group-strengthen-presence-us-50-million-investment-new-facility-200-new-jobs)
  $50 million to build and equip a new manufacturing facility in Kansas
  City, creating 200 jobs.
- U.S. Engineering Metalworks is
  [investing](https://www.kansascommerce.gov/2026/09/u-s-engineering-metalworks-expands-lawrence-manufacturing-operations/)
  $25 million to build a 110,000-square-foot production facility at
  Lawrence VenturePark, creating about 50 jobs.
- J.M. Smucker Co. is
  [investing](https://www.plantservices.com/industry-news/news/55357317/jm-smucker-co-announces-205-million-topeka-plant-expansion)
  $20.5 million to expand its Topeka pet food factory.
- The Trump Administration
  [awarded](https://hayspost.com/posts/69621c8d-69a4-4e95-9acb-aee43376ae18)
  $34 million to the Kansas City BioSecure Manufacturing Tech Hub to
  build an advanced pharmaceutical manufacturing center and train
  workers in biomanufacturing.

## **Putting Kansas Farmers and Ranchers First**

**President Trump is delivering direct support, infrastructure upgrades,
and market opportunities for Kansas agriculture.**

- **Emergency Assistance:** The Trump Administration has
  [delivered](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/programs/emergency-commodity-assistance-program/dashboard)
  more than $735 million to nearly 53,000 Kansas farmers through the
  Emergency Commodity Assistance Program for the 2024 crop year. Nearly
  [50,000](https://publicdashboards.dl.usda.gov/t/FPAC_PUB/views/FSA-DAFP-FBADashboard/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity?%3Aembed=y&%3AisGuestRedirectFromVizportal=y)
  Kansas producers have also
  [received](https://publicdashboards.dl.usda.gov/t/FPAC_PUB/views/FSA-DAFP-FBADashboard/ProgramPaymentsbyCategoryandCommodity?%3Aembed=y&%3AisGuestRedirectFromVizportal=y)
  more than $793 million in payments as part of a $12 billion [national
  bridge
  program](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/12/08/trump-administration-announces-12-billion-farmer-bridge-payments-american-farmers-impacted-unfair)
  for trade disruptions and higher input costs left by the Biden
  Administration.
- **Support for Ranchers:** In September 2026, President Trump
  [signed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-supports-americas-ranchers/)
  two Executive Orders to back American ranchers — one [directing
  action](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history)
  to help producers protect herds from wolves and other predators and
  advancing country-of-origin labeling transparency, and another
  [strengthening](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/04/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-cementing-status-most-pro-rancher-administration-history)
  enforcement of the Packers and Stockyards Act to expand market access
  without compromising food safety.
- **Infrastructure for Rural Communities:** In September 2026, the
  Department of Transportation
  [awarded](https://www.moran.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/2026/9/sen-moran-announces-over-53-million-federal-investment-for-k-96-highway-in-wichita)
  more than $53 million to reconstruct and widen K-96 between I-135 and
  I-35 in Wichita, reducing travel times and improving access for school
  buses and emergency vehicles.
- **Year-Round E15:** President Trump
  [approved](https://www.moran.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/2026/3/sen-moran-applauds-trump-administration-decision-to-permit-nationwide-year-round-e15-fuel-sales)
  year-round E15 sales, expanding markets for Kansas corn growers and
  giving drivers more affordable options at the pump.

## **Investing in Kansas’s Military Installations**

**President Trump is delivering new resources to strengthen Kansas’s
military bases and support the men and women who serve there.**

- At Fort Riley, the Trump Administration is
  [investing](https://www.moran.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/2025/11/passed-appropriations-package-short-term-funding-deal-to-reopen-the-government)
  $26 million for a new Air Traffic Control tower, $5.5 million to
  modernize security infrastructure surrounding the intermodal railyard,
  and $2.6 million to improve the staging area so the 1st Infantry
  Division can position equipment more efficiently for loading and
  unloading.
- At Fort Leavenworth, the Trump Administration is
  [investing](https://www.moran.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/2025/11/passed-appropriations-package-short-term-funding-deal-to-reopen-the-government)
  $8 million to add a new fire station and $900,000 to update the
  current facility.
- Forbes Field in Topeka was
  [named](https://www.marshall.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/senator-marshall-praises-forbes-field-being-named-candidate-location-for-kc-46a-mission/)
  one of four candidate bases in October 2026 for the KC-46A Pegasus
  mission, which would bring eight tanker aircraft to the Airmen of the
  190th Air Refueling Wing.
