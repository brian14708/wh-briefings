title: President Trump Announces Arrival of First One Million Barrels of Oil in Texas
  Thanks to Historic Oil Deal
---
 
*“This historic win gives us control over a massive part of the world’s
oil supply.” – President Donald J. Trump*

Yesterday in Corpus Christi, Texas, President Donald J. Trump announced
the arrival of **the first million barrels of
oil [secured](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-historic-oil-agreement-to-secure-american-energy-dominance-and-drive-venezuelas-economic-recovery/)
by the Trump Administration** as part of the President’s historic
Venezuela oil deal—with all oil coming to America at no cost to the
United States or the American taxpayer. 

This first shipment of oil will be refined in the Lone Star State,
supporting American jobs, and **increasing domestic supply to lower
Americans’ prices at the pump.**

From Alaska to Texas, President Trump is unleashing America’s energy
dominance—making the Western Hemisphere the energy capital of the
world—and Americans will benefit as gasoline and diesel prices come
plummeting down!
