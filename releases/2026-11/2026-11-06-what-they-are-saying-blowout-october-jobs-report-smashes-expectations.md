**President Donald J. Trump has long said that America is the hottest
country in the world — and today’s** [**blockbuster jobs
report**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-was-right-tariffs-and-tax-cuts-are-reshoring-manufacturing-creating-jobs/)
**delivers the proof.** The economy added a massive 165,000 new jobs in
October, more than double what experts expected, with the unemployment
rate falling, manufacturing surging, prior months revised sharply
higher, and broad-based gains. Experts agree: this is a booming labor
market.

**Here’s what they’re saying:**

“This was a blowout jobs report. Payroll growth exceeded expectations,
revisions were positive, the unemployment rate fell, participation
improved, and wage growth strengthened.”

[EY-Parthenon Chief Economist Gregory

“It’s as good as can be, really.”

[Allianz Trade North America Senior Economist Dan
North](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/jobs-report-october-2026.html#:~:text=%E2%80%9CYou%20have%20to,unexciting%20labor%20market.%E2%80%9D):
“You have to look pretty hard to find something to dislike in there.
It’s a really, really good report, and all around I think you’d say it’s
stronger than expected and the best one in a while, and it kind of fits
with what I call the stable but perhaps unexciting labor market.”
