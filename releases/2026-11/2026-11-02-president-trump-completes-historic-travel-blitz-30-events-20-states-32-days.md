**President Donald J. Trump has just completed one of the most
relentless, far-reaching** [**travel
schedules**](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-takes-midterm-campaign-blitz-historic-stage-grand-finale-before-election-day)
**of any modern presidency: 20 states in 32 days, from Wasilla, Alaska,
to Daytona Beach, Florida.** At every stop, President Trump took his
Administration’s record of results directly to the American people.

**Here are the highlights from President Trump’s travel:**

- **30 events, 20 states, 32 days.** From October 1 to November 1,
  President Trump visited Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Florida (twice),
  Georgia (twice), Iowa (twice), Kansas (twice), Kentucky, Maryland,
  Michigan, Mississippi, Montana, Nebraska, New York, North Carolina,
  Ohio (three times), Oklahoma, South Carolina (twice), Tennessee, and
  Texas (four times).
- **21 rallies in 21 days.** From October 12 to November 1, President
  Trump held 21 events, including at least one every day for 12 straight
  days (October 19-30).
- **Four doubleheaders.** President Trump held two events in a single
  day in Denton, TX, and Durant, OK (October 1); Bullhead City, AZ, and
  Kalispell, MT (October 14); Corpus Christi, TX, and Lubbock, TX
  (October 27); and Dubuque, IA, and Salina, KS (November 1).
