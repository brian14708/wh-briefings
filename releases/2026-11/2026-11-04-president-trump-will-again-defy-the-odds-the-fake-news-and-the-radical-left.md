Every time the Radical Left, the Fake News, and the professional panic
class have written President Trump’s obituary, they have been wrong.
They will be wrong again.

**In the final two years of his presidency, Democrats will try — and
fail — to reverse everything President Trump has done:**

- Reopen the border after he secured it.
- Raise taxes after he cut them and delivered record growth.
- Weaken law enforcement after he produced the largest drop in violent
  crime in modern history.
- Flood the country with illegal aliens after he enforced mass
  deportations at scale.
- Resurrect the Green New Deal after he achieved American energy
  independence.
- Surrender energy dominance after he achieved record production.
- Restore transgender mutilization on minors after he ended the barbaric
  practice and protected kids.
- Reinstate race-based DEI after he eliminated it.
- Rebuild the censorship regime after he dismantled it.
- Reopen the floodgates of waste, fraud, and abuse after he cracked down
  on it.
- Shield criminal illegals after he prioritized their removal.
- Pack the Supreme Court after he appointed originalist justices.
- Launch another impeachment hoax after he swiftly beat back the first
  two failed efforts.

**These efforts will fail; the American people have already rejected
this agenda.** President Trump’s
[record](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trumps-second-term-promises-made-promises-kept-results-delivered/)
is a contrast they cannot erase: border security, the end of DEI, energy
independence, the protection of our kids, and much more. Attempts to
impeach him, claw back his agenda, or reverse the gains of his
Administration will meet the same fate as the Radical Left’s previous
attempts: failure.

**President Trump will never stop fighting.**
