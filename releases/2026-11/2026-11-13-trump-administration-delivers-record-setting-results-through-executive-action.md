title: Trump Administration Delivers Record-Setting Results Through Executive Action
---
 
Under President Donald J. Trump’s leadership, the Administration has
delivered historic results for the American people in just 21 months
through executive action alone. The promise of America has been quickly
reignited under the President’s common-sense leadership, making America
the hottest nation in the world and reviving the American Dream. Crime
has plummeted, merit is restored, burdensome red tape is gone, and
American energy is unleashed. 

President Trump will never stop delivering on his promises to Make
America Great Again. Time and time again, he has defied the naysayers,
achieving what they deem as impossible to deliver results that improve
the lives of the American people. 

Exercising his executive authority, the President has: 

- **Executed a record-setting 147 Executive Orders** in his first 100
  days—more than any other President in history—and over 290 Executive
  Orders since January 2025, delivering on his promises of a more
  prosperous and secure America. 
- Delivered the most secure border in
  history, [**enforcing**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/10/how-president-trump-ended-the-border-invasion-and-restored-american-sovereignty/)** an
  extraordinary crackdown on illegal
  immigration**, [resulting](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/macroeconomic-implications-of-immigration-flows-in-2025-and-2026-january-2026-update/) in
  negative net migration to the U.S. for the first time in over half a
  century. 
  - [Terminated](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/10/how-president-trump-ended-the-border-invasion-and-restored-american-sovereignty/) welfare
    for illegal aliens across government programs, including [public
    housing](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/5745544-hud-bars-undocumented-immigrants/) benefits, [food
    stamps](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2025/07/10/secretary-rollins-prevents-illegal-aliens-receiving-taxpayer-funded-snap-benefits),
    and [small business
    loans](https://www.sba.gov/article/2026/03/09/sba-bans-foreign-nationals-accessing-sba-backed-loans).
    American taxpayer benefits are for Americans only, not illegal
    aliens.[Strengthened](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/09/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-further-enhances-program-integrity-and-interagency-coordination-in-the-h-1b-visa-program/) H-1B
    enforcement to protect the jobs of hardworking Americans and prevent
    visa abuse.  

  <!-- -->

  - Ensured Americans are
    the [safest](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-drop-in-violent-crime-in-american-history/) they’ve
    been in years by removing the worst of the worst and deporting
    millions of criminal illegal aliens from our streets. 
- [**Delivered**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-drop-in-violent-crime-in-american-history/)** the
  largest drop in violent crime in American history **in 2025, and
  preliminary 2026
  data [shows](https://cde.ucr.cjis.gov/LATEST/resources/reports/UCR_Summary_of_Reported_Crimes_in_the_Nation_2025.pdf#page=4) it
  is falling even further. 
  - In one year, violent crime saw its largest decline in history;
    murders fell over 18% to the lowest rate ever seen, and robberies
    fell more than 18%.

  <!-- -->

  - Property crimes, rapes, and aggravated assaults
    all [dropped](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-drop-in-violent-crime-in-american-history/) as
    well in 2025.

  <!-- -->

  - [Captured](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/kash-patel-reveals-fbi-capture-all-10-members-most-wanted-fugitive-list-during-hearing) all
    of the FBI’s Ten Most Wanted fugitives — 2.5 times as many as the
    entire previous four-year Administration — and six of the FBI’s Most
    Wanted Fraudsters.
- [Cracked down](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fraud/) on** fraud, waste,
  and corruption across the federal government at an unprecedented
  rate**, and restored integrity to federal programs designed to help
  Americans in need.
  - [Uncovered](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fraud/) more than $260
    billion in fraud of taxpayer dollars
    and [stopped](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fraud/) $72 billion a year,
    breaking up large-scale fraud schemes and seeing through that
    fraudsters are brought to justice.

  <!-- -->

  - The Trump Administration has stopped fraudulent payments before they
    go out the door, detected high-risk fraud trends, and shut down
    suspected fraudulent providers and retailers.
- [**Ended**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-ended-the-illegal-and-discriminatory-practices-of-diversity-equity-and-inclusion/)** illegal
  and discriminatory diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI)** practices
  across government, education, and the private sector. 
  - Reformed higher education by ending race-based discrimination in
    admissions and employment, DEI programming, and antisemitism.

  <!-- -->

  - Secured historic settlements of hundreds of millions with elite
    universities for their DEI practices,
    including [Columbia](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-major-settlement-with-columbia-university/), [Brown](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-major-settlement-with-brown-university/), [Cornell](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-major-settlement-with-cornell-university/),
    the [University of
    Virginia](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-agreement-university-virginia-0), [Northwestern](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/united-states-announces-agreement-northwestern-university),
    and [the University of North
    Carolina](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-settlement-ending-antisemitism-and-race-discrimination-unc).

  <!-- -->

  - Restored equal treatment and equal opportunity under the law
    by [eliminating](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/04/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-landmark-order-to-restore-equality-of-opportunity-and-meritocracy/) race-based
    policies and practices to rebalance outcomes along racial lines.

  <!-- -->

  - [Secured
    deals](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/doj-secures-agreements-nyu-and-upmc-end-pediatric-gender-affirming-care) with
    six major hospital systems to end their transgender mutilization of
    minors, protecting America’s children.
- [**Spearheaded**](https://x.com/ENERGY/status/2115145257469780051?a=EPW5K_MGaFEmW0oojI4&s=20)** a
  nuclear energy renaissance
  and **[**restored**](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/once-an-arab-oil-embargo-victim-us-becomes-worlds-top-oil-exporter-2026-06-11/)** America’s
  position as the world’s leading oil producer and exporter. **
  - [Revolutionized and
    time-bound](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-directs-reform-of-the-nuclear-regulatory-commission/) the
    nuclear regulatory process.Enabled four new advanced reactors
    to [reach](https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-golden-era-american-nuclear-energy-has-arrived) criticality
    by July 4th, 2026. Today, a total of five have [achieved
    criticality](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/us-says-oklo-s-small-nuclear-reactor-reaches-criticality?srnd=homepage-americas) following
    President Trump’s historic Executive
    Orders.[Completed](https://www.nrc.gov/sites/default/files/cdn/doc-collection-news/2025/25-071.pdf) more
    than 700 nuclear-energy licensing actions that will supply Americans
    with tens of thousands of megawatts in energy.

  <!-- -->

  - [Delivered](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-announces-historic-oil-agreement-to-secure-american-energy-dominance-and-drive-venezuelas-economic-recovery/) the
    largest oil deal in the history of the world to secure America’s
    energy future.
- **Enacted President Trump’s record-shattering deregulation
  agenda, **[saving](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/environment-and-energy/trump-push-to-slash-regulatory-red-tape-opens-a-crucial-chapter) a
  family of four over $17,500—or $1.5 trillion total—in one year alone
  by [cutting](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/environment-and-energy/trump-push-to-slash-regulatory-red-tape-opens-a-crucial-chapter) over
  400 regulations.
  - Thanks to these cuts, the Trump Administration is the first in
    history to reduce the regulatory burden on the American people.

With more than two years to go, President Trump will not hesitate to use
the power and authority of the Executive Branch to deliver historic
victories for the American people.
