***National Rural Health Day honors the people who keep rural America
strong.***

As our nation celebrates [Rural Health
Day](https://x.com/SecKennedy/status/2123505828095218168?a=HoPXj6rbIcOYOZrjyN8&s=20),
Americans across the country are benefiting from President Trump’s
leadership, which has delivered the single largest investment in rural
health in American history. The landmark $50 billion Rural Health
Transformation Fund—which every single Democrat voted AGAINST—is already
working to improve healthcare across all 50 states. These funds are
[expanding](https://nbcmontana.com/news/local/trump-administration-to-invest-87m-in-montana-rural-ems-ambulances-equipment)
[](https://www.unr.edu/nevada-today/news/2026/nevada-rural-health-transformation-program)
workforce programs in rural America, promoting access to
[telemedicine](https://www.cbsnews.com/atlanta/news/millions-headed-to-rural-georgia-hospitals-for-telehealth-staff-and-new-technology/),
and helping
[upgrade](https://www.wbiw.com/2026/09/03/trump-administration-announces-120-million-to-expand-maternal-and-infant-health-services-increase-primary-care-access-and-grow-the-healthcare-workforce-across-indiana/)
rural hospitals, [among numerous other
initiatives](https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/rural-health).
 

The Trump Administration is also relentlessly working to lower costs and
strengthen the healthcare system for all Americans. President Trump’s
efforts to ensure Americans get the same low prices available in other
countries for prescription drugs have delivered [the largest drop in
drug prices in OVER 60
years](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-prescription-drug-price-drop-in-over-60-years/);
made weight loss medicines available to seniors for just [$50 per
month](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-launches-medicare-glp-1-bridge-expanding-access-glp-1-medications);
and has secured more than [$500 billion in
investments](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/global-drugmakers-rush-boost-us-presence-tariff-threat-looms-2026-10-06/)
to reshore innovative pharmaceutical development, creating jobs and
opportunity for hardworking Americans.  

In today’s *RealClearHealth*, Health and Human Services Secretary Robert
F. Kennedy Jr., CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz, and HRSA Administrator
Thomas Engels highlighted how President Donald J. Trump is delivering on
his promise to put rural America first by transforming rural healthcare.
