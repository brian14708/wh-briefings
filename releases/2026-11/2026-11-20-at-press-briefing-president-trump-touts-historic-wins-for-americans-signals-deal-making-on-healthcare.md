title: At Press Briefing, President Trump Touts Historic Wins for Americans, Signals
  Deal-Making on Healthcare
---
 
**In a White House press briefing today, President Donald J. Trump took
questions from reporters alongside Vice President JD Vance and Secretary
of State Marco Rubio.** Fresh off his return from Asia, President Trump
used the briefing to underscore that America is negotiating from a
position of restored strength — highlighting [more than $17
billion](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/11/president-trump-brings-home-billions-in-economic-growth-and-strengthens-americas-security/)
in deals that put American workers, farmers, and families first. He also
made clear that he is ready to work across the aisle, especially on
healthcare, to deliver results for the American people.

- **On the historic Asia trip:** “Last night, we returned home from one
  of the most successful presidential visits to Asia in American
  history… In four days, I met more than 20 world leaders, secured two
  new trade deals and about ten others coming very soon, attended four
  major international summits, reaffirmed America’s alliances throughout
  the region, and advanced the cause of peace and security around the
  world.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123832470822932512))
- **On working with Democrats:** “Because of the power of the veto,
  there’s a real possibility that we’ll get a lot of great things done,
  especially on healthcare. I’m open to great healthcare. Now, can it be
  what they want? No, but it can be a combination of what we all want —
  and we could end up with great healthcare in our country.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123848950146826371))
- **On trade with Thailand:** “Thailand signed an agreement to eliminate
  all tariffs on American products… and remove their non-tariff
  barriers, and they want to buy $2.6 billion a year in U.S.
  agricultural products, and we’re going to increase that number… Our
  farmers are a big beneficiary of this trip.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123837956054261930))
- **On trade with the Philippines:** “Philippine Airlines will purchase…
  20 brand new Boeing 787 Dreamliners with General Electric engines.
  It’s a big contract, supporting 47,000 American jobs.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123837519439773947))
- **On permitting reform:** “Last night, landmark permitting reform
  legislation passed the Senate. It’s one of the biggest things that you
  will see. It’s a big deal… Now, it goes to the House — and if passed,
  it’s projected to put at least $1,300/year back in the pockets of
  hardworking Americans. But it’s even beyond that; it’s going to cause
  tremendous amounts of new building.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123834359153111161?s=20))
- **On the trilateral summit and nuclear security:** “We discussed the
  responsibility of the world’s three largest nuclear powers… to reduce
  the risk that these weapons pose to the world… We spoke about the need
  to cooperate to stop the proliferation of that nuclear threat
  throughout our planet. And in particular, we talked extensively about
  ensuring that Iran will never have a nuclear weapon.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123840519067287987))
- **On meeting President Putin:** “I held a bilateral meeting with
  President Putin of Russia, who clearly expressed his desire to see the
  war between Russia and Ukraine end by diplomatic means.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123838429884744163))
- **On economic strength:** “The United States entered this trip from a
  position of unmatched economic and diplomatic strength… Already,
  gasoline is down 60 cents a gallon from just one month ago, and it’s
  going to be dropping fast.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123833100207620343))
- **On Iran:** “They want to make a deal, but they don’t want to make a
  deal that’s acceptable to me. We want to make a real deal, not a fake
  deal that they’ve been doing for 51 years… They’ve been bullshitting
  people for 51 years.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123833098551038180))
- **On American strength:** “Everywhere I went on this trip and every
  leader I met, it was clear that America is respected again like never
  before. We are respected.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123835436397478267))
- **On keeping critical infrastructure in America:** “Whoever wins SI
  wins… We’re not going to allow our companies to build data centers
  outside of our country. We’re not going to allow that to happen. You
  know, they allowed that to happen with chips and everything else and
  it was very destructive to our country… We want them to build it
  here.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123836545346285698))
