Over the past month, President Donald J. Trump and Vice President JD
Vance maintained an unmatched pace of
[travel](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-completes-historic-travel-blitz-30-events-20-states-32-days/)
across the country, meeting with Americans face-to-face to highlight the
Administration’s [Promises Made, Promises
Kept](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trumps-second-term-promises-made-promises-kept-results-delivered/)
record.

**Recent coverage underscored the scale of the travel:**

[**The Daily
Wire**](https://www.dailywire.com/news/trump-touts-drug-discounts-patient-payments-in-final-midterm-push)**:
Trump Touts Drug Discounts, Patient Payments In Final Midterm Push**  
*“As President Donald Trump made prescription drug discounts and direct
payments to patients part of his closing pitch to voters, highlighting
the administration’s health care initiatives… The president participated
in 29 campaign rallies since the convention, as well as multiple
tele-rallies on Monday evening, and he often used the rallies as an
opportunity to highlight major health care announcements. Meanwhile, his
administration was rolling out a series of healthcare initiatives, such
as the $500 Obamacare refund checks, $90 Medicare checks for seniors,
$275 million devoted to recruiting nurses for nursing homes, and
historic investments in rural healthcare.”*

[**The Daily
Signal**](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/11/03/trumps-midterm-travel-blitz-surpasses-predecessors/)**:

*“President Donald Trump has held more than 30 events from Oct. 1 to
Election Day as part of his ‘midterm blitz,’ far exceeding the travel of
his Democrat predecessors. Trump has held 30 events in 20 states in 32
days. From Oct. 1 to Nov. 1, he visited Alabama, Alaska, Arizona,
Florida (twice), Georgia (twice), Iowa (twice), Kansas (twice),
Kentucky, Maryland, Michigan, Mississippi, Montana, Nebraska, New York,
North Carolina, Ohio (three times), Oklahoma, South Carolina (twice),
Tennessee, and Texas (four times).”*

[**Washington

*“President Donald Trump closed out the 2026 midterm elections on a

waning days of the 2022 midterms. In the three months running up to the

gaggles, and standard remarks… The 170 press-facing events that Trump
has done since August 1 total over 85 hours, and include 79 remarks, 62
gaggles, and 29 sprays. At 83 of his events, Trump has answered
questions from the press.”*
