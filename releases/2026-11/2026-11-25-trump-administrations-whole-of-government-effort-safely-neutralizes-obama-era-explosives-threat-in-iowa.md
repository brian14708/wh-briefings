title: Trump Administration Leads Whole-of-Government Effort to Safely Neutralize
  Obama-Era Explosives Threat in Iowa
---
 
**Under President Donald J. Trump’s leadership, the U.S. Army this
morning safely destroyed nearly five tons of degraded explosives at the
Iowa Army Ammunition Plant.** All initial indications show the
controlled detonation to be highly successful, with EPA monitors
[reporting](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/initial-air-monitoring-shows-zero-detection-airborne-lead-following-controlled)
zero detection of airborne lead and [no secondary
explosions](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/25/us/live-news/iowa-lead-azide-detonation?post-id=cmwwdhdkn001y356uojo8y5en)
or infrastructure damage.

**This is what happens when competence runs the government.** Handed a
dangerous mess the Obama Administration left behind, the Trump
Administration acted decisively, marshaling a whole-of-government
approach that involved meticulous planning, pre-positioning hundreds of
on-the-ground personnel, mitigation engineering, and real-time data
collection to protect the safety, health, and wellbeing of nearby
residents and livestock at every step.

Experts remain on the ground to coordinate community health assessments,
answer questions, and provide ongoing support. Real-time air sampling
and monitoring data can be viewed as it becomes available at
[epa.gov/iowadata](https://epa.gov/iowadata).
