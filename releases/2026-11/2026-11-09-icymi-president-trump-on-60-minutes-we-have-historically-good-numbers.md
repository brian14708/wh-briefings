---
 
**Last night, President Donald J. Trump sat down with *60 Minutes*
following the midterm elections and made a powerful case for his America
First record.** President Trump highlighted the booming Trump economy —
165,000 new jobs in October, record 401(k) balances, and hundreds of
billions of dollars in new factories — touted the most secure border in
history, detailed the decisive blows he has dealt to Iran’s nuclear
program, explained why America must win the Super Intelligence race, and
much more.

**Key exchanges:**

- **On the midterms:** “I think if I was on the ballot, it would’ve been
  a much different situation… But there’s something beyond that. They
  didn’t like the war. They didn’t like the fact that gasoline went up…
  And there’s a great dislike, incredibly, for Israel.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119583809418981768?a=K9ttbJfX7vt_4R574no&s=20))
- **On the stock market:** “We have historically good numbers. We have
  the highest stock market in history.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=310s))
  - Since President Trump was elected, the [S&P
    500](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EGSPC/) has hit 77 new record
    highs, the [Nasdaq](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EIXIC/) has
    hit 70 record highs, and the
    [Dow](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EDJI/) has hit 51 record
    highs. Since Inauguration Day, the S&P 500 has hit 67 record highs,
    the Nasdaq has hit 60 record highs, and the Dow has hit 43 record
    highs.
- **On jobs numbers:** “The numbers are way beyond anybody’s
  expectations… We had 165,000 jobs created just in a short time.”
  ([Watch](https://youtu.be/m2aC8lAOQbg?t=379))
  - The U.S. economy
    [added](https://www.newsweek.com/trump-gets-major-jobs-report-boost-after-midterms-rattle-markets-12676939)
    165,000 new jobs in October — smashing economists’ expectations;
    manufacturing
    [added](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/jobs-report-october-2026.html)
    19,000 jobs in October and the sector has
    [added](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/us-added-jobs-october-risks-stagnation-rcna607812)
    more than 100,000 jobs since its December 2025 low.
- **On retirement savings:** “401(k)s are at their all-time high by a
  lot. Under other Presidents, they were a disaster. 401(k)s have made
  people rich.” 
  ([Watch](https://www.cbsnews.com/video/president-donald-trumps-extended-november-2026-60-minutes-interview/))
  - 401(k), 403(b), and IRA balances
    [hit](https://newsroom.fidelity.com/pressreleases/fidelity--q2-2026-retirement-analysis--record-savings-rates-help-drive-401-k---403-b--and-ira-balanc/s/bca5d71f-ebda-4289-bdd5-a90abde7feba)
    new highs in the second quarter of 2026, with the average 401(k)
    balance reaching a record $155,800 — up 13.1% from a year earlier.
- **On private investment:** “There has never been a time where there is
  the activity — trillions and trillions of dollars right now, as we
  sit, is being built. In a year from now… many of those plants are
  gonna be open, operating.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=1228s))
  - Companies are pouring money into America, including
    [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/08/apple-increases-us-commitment-to-600-billion-usd-announces-ambitious-program/)
    ($600 billion and 20,000 new American hires),
    [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-manufacture-american-made-ai-supercomputers-us/)
    (up to $500 billion in American-made SI infrastructure),
    [TSMC](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3210) ($165 billion in the
    largest single foreign direct investment in U.S. history), and
    [Johnson &
    Johnson](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-increases-u-s-investment-to-more-than-55-billion-over-the-next-four-years)
    (more than $55 billion).
- **On reshoring auto manufacturing:** “We’re also building automobile
  factories like we’ve never built before.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=1204s))
  - Audi is
    [building](https://www.audi.com/en/media-releases/audi-to-establish-first-us-production-site-in-georgia-investing-4-billion-and-creating-4500-jobs-18420)
    its first-ever U.S. plant in
    [Georgia](https://gov.georgia.gov/press-releases/2026-10-24/gov-kemp-audi-ag-selects-georgia-invest-4-billion-first-us-manufacturing),
    with a $4 billion investment that will create about 4,500 new jobs.
- **On reshoring pharmaceutical manufacturing:** “How about
  pharmaceutical? Eli Lilly, Merck, Pfizer — they’re building hundreds
  of billions of \[investment\].”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=1221s))
  - Eli Lilly has
    [committed](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-plans-more-double-us-manufacturing-investment-2020)
    more than $50 billion to American manufacturing, Merck has
    [committed](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-breaks-ground-on-3-billion-center-of-excellence-for-pharmaceutical-manufacturing-in-elkton-virginia/)
    more than $70 billion, and Pfizer has
    [committed](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizer-reaches-landmark-agreement-us-government-lower-drug)
    an additional $70 billion as part of its landmark agreement with the
    Trump Administration to lower drug prices.
- **On securing the border:** “I think I got an A+ for closing up the
  border… I get very high marks for getting the bad ones out.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=842s))
  - Border Patrol recorded just 98,708 southwest border apprehensions in
    FY2026 — the
    [lowest](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest)
    in agency history and a 95% drop from the Biden-era average — while
    September marked the [17th straight
    month](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/11/03/making-america-safe-again-under-president-trump-dhs-delivered-most-secure-border)
    of zero releases at the border.
- **On Iran:** “You can’t let Iran have a nuclear weapon. I think,
  historically, this will be a very good decision. Is it a good decision
  for today and tomorrow? Maybe not, but I have to go with what’s right
  for our country.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119584903813607745))
- **On the Strait of Hormuz:** “The strait is open, Ross… Your favorite
  newspaper, *The Wall Street Journal*… had an article that said,
  ‘There’s more oil going through the Hormuz Strait than there was at
  the beginning of the war.’ Think of that.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=650s))
  - *The Wall Street Journal*
    [reports](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/more-oil-is-now-coming-out-of-the-persian-gulf-than-before-the-iran-war-6a6d1670)
    that Persian Gulf oil exports have surpassed prewar levels — more
    than 24 million barrels a day — thanks to a shipping system under
    U.S. protection.
- **On Venezuela:** “I just say the Venezuela attack will go down in
  military histories… It was one of the most incredible attacks anyone’s
  ever seen. They left the aircraft carriers at 1:00 in the morning,
  pitch black. Everything went perfect.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=1676s))
- **On the Russia-Ukraine war:** “I believe that war is going to end and
  it’s going to end soon, and the only reason it’s going to end is
  because of me.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119590880709628372))
- **On Super Intelligence:** “The reason I’m an AI bull, one of the
  reasons, is because President Xi left here two weeks ago — we had a
  great meeting — he’s all-in to SI… Whoever wins that race wins.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119594621638791227))
- **On data centers:** “China has no problem with data centers. China
  has no problem with electricity. And, by the way, we don’t either
  because… I’m demanding that they build their own electric plant.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=1302s))
- **On MAGA:** “MAGA is basically common sense. MAGA is strong borders,
  good military — we have created wealth at a level that our country has
  never seen before.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119593391109632147))
- **On 2028:** “We have a tremendous bench.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119593221001261077))
- **On how his presidency will be remembered:** “That I made America
  great again.”
  ([Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2aC8lAOQbg&t=3092s))
