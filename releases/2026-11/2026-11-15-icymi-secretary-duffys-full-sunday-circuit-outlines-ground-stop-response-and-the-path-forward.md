**This morning, Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy appeared on all
five Sunday shows to level with the American people about Friday’s**
[**nationwide ground
stop**](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/icymi-trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-provides-update-across-sunday)**.**
Secretary Duffy laid out the Trump Administration’s decisive actions to
isolate the problem and restore the airspace and shared preliminary
findings from the investigation.

While Democrats shamefully lie and resist [finishing the
job](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2026-09/Modern%20Skies%20Act%20fact%20sheet.pdf),
Secretary Duffy also reiterated that the $12.5 billion provided in the
Working Families Tax Cuts (which not a
[single](https://clerk.house.gov/Votes/2025190)
[Democrat](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1191/vote_119_1_00372.htm)
voted for) was written specifically for radar, radios, and related
upgrades — not a new telecom network or software. As President Donald J.
Trump [made
clear](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2121965402641334452),
Congress must approve the rest of the funding so the modernization can
be finished.

**Key exchanges:**

- **On the Trump Administration’s immediate response:** “We were talking
  to the President… He said, ‘Whatever resource in any other Department
  that you need, let me know. You can pull them. I want everybody to
  work on this’ — because the safety of the traveling public is number
  one for him, like it is for me.” ([State of the
  Union](https://x.com/CNNSOTU/status/2121974895764455717))
- **On what caused the ground stop:** “The telecom in El Dorado, it was
  an old copper wire that basically was going to be decommissioned. They
  said it can’t be used anymore, so we were forced by the telecom
  provider to actually update that copper to fiber and we brought in our
  routers and did the work, and that’s when we saw the flooding of the
  network.” ([Face the
  Nation](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122001848882156002))
- **On the need for Congress to fully fund a modernized air traffic
  control network:** “I have systems that are from the ‘90s. I have
  radar that are from the ‘60s and ‘70s… \[We’re\] replacing radar and
  radios, we’re putting in voice switches, we’re doing all of this work
  with the $12.5 billion, but one of the biggest problems we continue to
  see is our network — and the architecture of our network is from 1980.
  We need a modern technology and architecture. I have AT&T, Verizon,
  and Lumen all ready to go. We have the architecture ready to build a
  state-of-the-art telecom system that will give us resiliency. All I
  need is the money to finish it.” ([State of the
  Union](https://x.com/CNNSOTU/status/2121974185740439942))
- **On what the $12.5 billion downpayment can — and can’t — do:** “When
  I asked for a massive amount of money to fix the system, what the
  Congress did is they said, ‘Okay, we’ll give you a partial funding,
  $12.5 billion,’ in the One Big Beautiful Bill, and they were very
  specific in how we spend it… But what I don’t have money for is a new
  telecom system. I don’t have money for new software… Let’s finish the
  job. Let’s not see what happened on Friday. Let’s not let it happen
  again.” ([Face the
  Nation](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122004653336584629))
- **On Democrats’ “unspent money” talking point:** “Whoever’s saying
  that, they have to actually read the bill that gave me the money. It
  was very specific… There’s no money for towers. Our towers are old.
  Our air traffic controllers oftentimes don’t have elevators or HVAC…
  Their roofs leak. Let’s build a new system that is worthy to be called
  American.” ([State of the
  Union](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/15/politics/video/sean-duffy-faa-sotu))
- **On the broad consensus for fully modernizing the system:** “If you
  don’t give us the resources, we can’t complete the job… This is the
  most bipartisan thing in all of Washington — Democrats, Republicans,
  the President, everyone agrees on this. Let’s just do it. Do it for
  the American people.” ([Meet the
  Press](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_TTu-Equ3Yg&t=499s))
- **On Democrats’ push to pull money from border security:** “We should
  be able to keep our borders safe, we should be able to deport
  illegals, and we should be able to invest in safe air travel in
  America. We should be able to invest in new networks, invest in
  software. We can do all things; we don’t have to pick one versus the
  other.” ([Fox News
  Sunday](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2121991215394754849))
- **On the safety of American air travel:** “It is the safest air system
  in the world, bar none… It’s going to remain safe. But let’s not lie
  to each other — we have to make investments into a network that has
  been neglected for decades.” ([Meet the
  Press](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2121980681689383114))
- **On delivering results:** “President Trump and I are going to fix
  it.” ([Meet the
  Press](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_TTu-Equ3Yg&t=404s))
