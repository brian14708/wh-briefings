*“Last night, landmark permitting reform legislation passed the Senate.
It’s one of the biggest things that you will see. It’s a big deal… It’s
projected to put at least $1,300 a year back in the pockets of
hardworking Americans.”*** **– President Donald J. Trump

President Donald J. Trump is committed to fixing America’s broken
permitting system — standing behind the Bipartisan American
Affordability and Jobs Act (BAAJA), legislation that will modernize
federal environmental reviews and permitting while delivering cheaper
energy, high-paying jobs, and economic growth across the United States.

Last night, the Senate advanced landmark permitting reform legislation,
and now the House has the opportunity to finish the job and send the
bill to President Trump’s desk to lock in generations of affordability
and energy dominance for the American people. 

**Here’s what they’re saying about the Bipartisan American Affordability
and Jobs Act**

Bradbury:](https://axpc.org/education-resource/the-house-should-seize-this-generational-opportunity-to-lower-costs-by-passing-baaja-now/)</u> **“AXPC
applauds the Senate for taking decisive bipartisan action to remove
longstanding barriers to American energy and infrastructure development.
BAAJA offers a generational opportunity to cut through permitting delays
and lower costs across America without compromising critical
environmental protections. With meaningful relief for American families
within reach, we respectfully urge the House to send BAAJA to the
President’s desk before the end of the year.”
