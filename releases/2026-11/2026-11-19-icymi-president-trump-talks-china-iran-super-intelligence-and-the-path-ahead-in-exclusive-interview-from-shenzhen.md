**Last night, President Donald J. Trump delivered a message of American
resurgence in an exclusive live interview on Fox News from Shenzhen,
China — just hours before his historic meeting with President Xi Jinping
and President Vladimir Putin.** He highlighted the respect America now
commands under his leadership, the decisive steps he is taking to
modernize critical infrastructure, America’s clear lead in Super
Intelligence, and his confidence in working with Congress over the next
two years as he negotiates from a position of strength.

**Key moments:**

- **On China and President Xi:** “The relationship that I have with
  China is a great one. You know, it’s a friendship relationship. It’s a
  respect relationship. You saw the seating last night. You saw the
  greeting yesterday… This is a great President, one of the great
  Presidents of China, and he’s never gone to the airport to meet
  somebody — and not only that, the fanfare, the beauty, the pageantry,
  the thousands of people… the soldiers, the whole thing. It was a great
  respect, and I appreciate that. That’s a big win for us. It’s a big
  win for China, too.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123215548075192560))
- **On air traffic control:** “This is 50-year-old equipment, and it has
  to be changed… They spent billions of dollars, the previous
  Administration, and then they tried to hook it up and it didn’t work;
  not only didn’t it work, it set it back. So, we want it fixed, and we
  want it fixed right… When it’s finished, we will have the best system
  anywhere in the world.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123213109598810455))
- **On recent tech outages:** “Everything’s good. These are individual
  events… I also think that the press is sort of having a lot of fun
  with it. If these things would have happened two years ago, three
  years ago in the Biden Administration, which was grossly incompetent
  and corrupt, nobody would have mentioned it… If anybody did that to us
  and we found out about it, they know what the repercussions would be
  with me. Nobody’s going to play games with us.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123213992139034978))
- **On Iran:** “They want to make a deal, but I want a deal that can be
  lasting… If we have a bad President, a weak President, I want a deal
  that will supersede the President… One way or the other, we’ll finish
  it off.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123221203812811092))
- **On working with Democrats in the new Congress:** “I’ve been called
  by Democrats at a level that’s pretty amazing. Nobody would even
  believe it… They want to make deals. I have the veto power… I have a
  feeling that this is going to be an unbelievably productive two years.
  Because they’ll send me a bill on healthcare, and I’ll say no way, and
  I’ll veto it, and then they’ll call me… and we’ll sit down.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123222245325590850))
- **On Super Intelligence**: “I was with President Xi all night last
  night, sitting next to him at the dinner, right on his right-hand
  side, and we spoke the whole evening, and we talked about that, and
  he’s amazed at how well we’ve done with that. We’re leading China,
  we’re leading the whole world.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123217406462210127))
- **On reports of an Iranian dirty bomb plot:** “There is indication
  that Iran is looking at gas and they’re looking at the bio — the
  biological weapons, not nice — and other things, and we’ll see… These
  are nasty people… Look, if it does happen, we wipe them out.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123219231051288808))
- **On Obama driving China and Russia together:** “The relationship
  should have never been allowed to happen. You know, they were natural
  enemies, but because of Obama, not Biden, it was Obama before, he
  drove them together because of energy. … All my life I read you never
  want to have Russia and China team up… What can I tell you? They both
  like Trump.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123220226934178120))
