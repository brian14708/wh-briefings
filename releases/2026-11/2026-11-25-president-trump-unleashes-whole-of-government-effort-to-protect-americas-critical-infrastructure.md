title: "President Trump Unleashes Whole-of-Government Effort to Protect America\u2019\
  s Critical Infrastructure"

At President Donald J. Trump’s direction, the Administration is pursuing
a coordinated, whole-of-government effort to modernize federal systems,
strengthen our defenses, and protect America’s critical infrastructure.
The Trump Administration has assembled a highly capable interagency team
that remains hypervigilant in protecting our nation’s critical systems,
confronting emerging threats, and ensuring America is secure, resilient,
and positioned to lead in the digital age.

As part of his historic effort to strengthen America’s cyber
capabilities, President Trump has established new leadership structures,
made key appointments, and expanded the cyber workforce. He has
[nominated](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-taps-pentagon-cyber-chief-kirsten-davies-as-national-cyber-director/) battle-tested
cyber leader and Pentagon Chief Information Officer Kirsten Davies to
lead the Office of the National Cyber Director, which will be integrated
into a reorganized White House operational and policy structure, and
he [appointed](https://www.nsa.gov/About/Leadership/Leadership-View/article/4439868/general-joshua-m-rudd-usa/)
General Joshua M. Rudd to lead the National Security Agency and U.S.
Cyber Command. The U.S. Office of Personnel Management’s United States
Tech Force has brought in nearly 500 high-skilled engineers to modernize
and secure essential systems across the government. The Cybersecurity
and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added more than 200
personnel to mission-critical roles, and will have more than 400
additional personnel added in the coming months.

Recognizing the strategic importance of winning the Super Intelligence
(SI) race, President Trump established the Super Intelligence Force,
chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton. The Force is
advancing American leadership in SI, maintaining our technological
advantage over foreign adversaries, and helping safeguard our critical
infrastructure. By leveraging SI and assembling top experts across each
federal agency, the Administration is supercharging its ability to
identify, deter, and disrupt malicious activity before it can ever
compromise our systems.

**President Trump is committed to ensuring our systems remain secure,
resilient, and reliable and has taken a historic all-of-government
approach to deliver on his cyber agenda:**

- **BUILDING AN ELITE CYBERSECURITY TEAM AND ORGANIZATION: **President
  Trump established the Super Intelligence
  Force, [nominated](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/president-trump-taps-pentagon-cyber-chief-kirsten-davies-as-national-cyber-director/) highly-qualified
  Kirsten Davies to lead the Office of the National Cyber Director, and
  he [selected](https://www.nsa.gov/About/Leadership/Leadership-View/article/4439868/general-joshua-m-rudd-usa/) General
  Joshua M. Rudd to helm the NSA and U.S. Cyber Command. The White House
  cyber policy and operations teams are rapidly staffing up with
  additional experts and will be reorganized under the National Security
  Council and Homeland Security Council to ensure effective integration
  across all government cybersecurity programs. CISA has waived
  otherwise applicable limits on its hiring to scale up employment,
  adding more than 200 mission-critical personnel, with another 400-plus
  expected. The U.S. Office of Personnel Management’s new [United States
  Tech Force](https://techforce.gov/) has hired nearly 500
  highly-skilled, private sector engineers to modernize and secure
  government systems across every major agency in under a year.
- **PROTECTING THE SYSTEMS AMERICANS DEPEND ON:** CISA launched [CI
  Fortify](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/industrial-control-systems/ci-fortify) to
  help critical infrastructure operators maintain essential services
  during major disruptions and established a requirement for federal
  agencies to aggressively patch high-risk vulnerabilities within three
  days**. **Next week, DOJ and FBI will unveil their latest cyber
  defense initiative as a follow on to [Operation Winter
  SHIELD](https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/ten-actions-to-improve-cyber-resiliency).
- **SECURING AMERICA’S NETWORKS:** The FCC prohibited
  new [foreign-made](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-420034A1.pdf)
  consumer routers identified as posing unacceptable national security
  risks, while federal agencies are also strengthening protections for
  telecommunications and transportation infrastructure.
- **CRACKING DOWN ON CYBERCRIME: **Federal law enforcement
  has [made](https://www.justice.gov/ola/media/1462211/dl?inline=) more
  than 150 cybercrime arrests, disrupted more than 220 malicious cyber
  operations, and extradited more than 50 cyber actors in 2026 alone.
- **DISMANTLING GLOBAL SCAM OPERATIONS AND SAVING AMERICANS
  BILLIONS: **Just yesterday, the Department of
  Justice [announced](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-largest-ever-crackdown-scammers-targeting-over-800-defendants)
  that it has brought more than 400 cases against criminal scam networks
  since March 2026, identifying more than $8 billion in intended fraud
  losses. The FBI’s Operation Blackout has
  [seized](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2125265400728260844?a=2XuvJgDVwUK5UuHwDmA&s=20)
  nearly $17 billion in illicit funds, taken control of more than 14,000
  digital devices, and dismantled 29 scam compounds across four
  continents. Federal law enforcement has also helped prevent at least
  $1.6 billion in losses by warning more than 10,000 potential victims,
  frozen nearly $1 billion in cryptocurrency tied to foreign scammers,
  and worked with technology companies to disrupt millions of criminal
  online accounts. 
- **HELPING PROTECT AMERICA’S BUSINESSES IN THE DIGITAL AGE: **The Trump
  Administration is implementing [GOLD
  EAGLE](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/white-house-launches-gold-eagle-initiative-for-unprecedented-cybersecurity-vulnerability-coordination/) to
  coordinate cybersecurity vulnerability identification across critical
  infrastructure sectors and expanding private-sector access to advanced
  models to strengthen defenses.
- **HOLDING FOREIGN HACKERS ACCOUNTABLE: **The Department of Justice
  charged [12 Chinese
  hackers](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-charges-12-chinese-contract-hackers-and-law-enforcement-officers-global)
  and law enforcement officers for their alleged roles in global hacking
  campaigns targeting U.S. agencies. Treasury also sanctioned
  four [entities](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0319)
  supporting [ransomware
  attacks](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0559) against
  Americans and [five Iranian
  nationals](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0613) for
  targeting U.S. critical infrastructure, government offices, and
  digital assets.
- **STRENGTHENING AMERICA’S MILITARY CYBER DEFENSES: **President
  Trump [modernized](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-defends-americas-warfighters-and-intelligence-officers-against-cyber-threats/)
  cybersecurity standards for America’s military and intelligence
  systems for the first time in more than 35 years.
- **LEADING THE WORLD IN SUPER INTELLIGENCE, STOPPING HACKERS AND
  FOREIGN THREATS: **The Trump Administration is working with leading
  technology companies to test advanced models, identify cybersecurity
  vulnerabilities, and strengthen defenses before adversaries
  can [exploit](https://www.ic3.gov/CSA/2026/261113.pdf) weaknesses.
- **PROTECTING AMERICA’S CHILDREN ONLINE: **The Trump Administration
  is [aggressively
  confronting](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2112983495505215793?a=vKIFc2J282NE8P56OzM&s=20)
  the digital extortion rackets that have wrecked the lives of too many
  young people, a top priority for First Lady Melania Trump, who led a
  successful bipartisan effort to pass the [Take It Down
  Act](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/05/icymi-president-trump-signs-take-it-down-act-into-law/).
