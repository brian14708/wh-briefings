**Today, President Donald J. Trump**
[**announced**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2118778661243634138)
**the appointment of Stephen Miller as the 33rd White House Chief of
Staff, succeeding Susie Wiles after her highly successful tenure.**
Miller has served alongside President Trump since the very beginning and
has been a driving force behind the Administration’s major successes —
driving violent crime to historic lows, securing the border at
unprecedented levels, and dismantling large-scale fraud. He brings deep
experience, broad institutional knowledge, and unwavering commitment to
the role.
