This morning, Attorney General Todd Blanche appeared on the Sunday shows
and made clear that the Department of Justice will fulfill its
Constitutional duty to represent the Executive Branch — and that
Democrats’ explicit threats of endless investigations, impeachments, and
retribution against President Trump, Cabinet officials, and private
citizens will not succeed.

**Key exchanges:**

- **On Democrats’ threats against donors, students, and private
  citizens:** “They’ve threatened donors, they’ve threatened individuals
  who are in college and might want to work for conservative
  institutions or for a conservative government or for President Trump,
  and that is extremely troubling. It should be troubling to everybody
  in this country.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119436352001307106))
- **On Democrats’ claims of “oversight” vs. political retribution:**
  “Have you been listening to the rhetoric the past couple days? You
  honestly think that all they’re doing is talking about their oversight
  obligations when they’re talking about going after basically every
  member of the cabinet? They’re talking about impeachments; they’re
  talking about going after private companies who happen to have made
  investments in this country over the past couple years. That is NOT
  oversight.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119437421213356473))
- **On the DOJ’s role in representing Administration officials:** “In
  every Administration, the Department of Justice represents members of
  the executive who are brought in front of Congress. So, there is
  nothing unique to me saying that. Every Attorney General in history
  represents members of the Administration… I would suggest \[Rep.
  Garcia\] read the Constitution. If he doesn’t have a copy, I can
  arrange to send him one — but the Constitution absolutely protects
  members of the executive.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119440100908056608))
- **On Democrats recycling the same playbook:** “This is literally déjà
  vu all over again. They’ve tried this before. It didn’t work last
  time; it’s not going to work this time.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119443005492838759))
- **On Stephen Miller as Chief of Staff:** “\[Miller\] will be a
  phenomenal Chief of Staff. The reason why you saw immediate complaints
  about Stephen is really fear, and they’re worried about what’s going
  to happen because he’s so good at his job.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2119444556211204422))
