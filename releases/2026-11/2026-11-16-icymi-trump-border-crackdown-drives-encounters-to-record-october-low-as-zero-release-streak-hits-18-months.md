**For 18 straight months, zero illegal aliens have been released into
the United States as President Donald J. Trump continues to deliver the
most secure border in American history.** It’s a direct consequence of
restored enforcement, deterrence, and operational control that has
reversed years of chaos — and the numbers aren’t even close.

**As Fox News**
[**reports**](https://www.foxnews.com/us/trump-border-crackdown-drives-encounters-record-october-low-zero-release-streak-hits-18-months-dhs-says)**:**

*“Border Patrol agents caught fewer illegal immigrants at the southwest
border in all of October than they did in just five days of the same
month under former President Joe Biden in 2024, according to new federal
figures.*

*The sharp drop comes as the Trump administration reports 18 straight
months of zero releases at the border and the lowest nationwide
encounter total ever recorded for the opening month of a fiscal year…*

*Border Patrol recorded 8,288 apprehensions along the southwest border
in October, down 95% from the Biden administration’s monthly average and
97% from its December 2023 peak, according to the Department of Homeland
Security and U.S. Customs and Border Protection…*

*The latest southwest border apprehension total fell 7% from September,
carrying the decline into the first month of fiscal year 2027.
Nationwide, CBP recorded 27,890 encounters in October, also down 7% from
September. That was the lowest October total on record and the lowest
for any month since February, the agencies said.*

*The new figures follow preliminary DHS data showing 98,708 southwest
border apprehensions for all of fiscal year 2026, roughly 95% below the
Biden administration’s annual average of more than 1.8 million.”*
