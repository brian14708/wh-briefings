**Just as President Donald J. Trump**
[**predicted**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123180022659723313)**,
gas prices are tumbling down — and American families are feeling the
relief just in time for Thanksgiving.** From coast to coast, prices at
the pump are falling fast, with the national average falling to its
[lowest](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/us-gas-pump-price-hits-lowest-since-march-as-oil-supply-recovers)
price in eight months after the
[biggest](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/economy/gas-prices-fall)
three-week dive since 2008. As millions of Americans hit the road to
gather with the people they love, President Trump is delivering just
what he promised: real relief at the pump and more money in families’
pockets this holiday season.
