**Traffic fatalities have**
[**fallen**](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/decline-traffic-fatalities-first-half-2026)
**to the second-lowest level in American history following President
Donald J. Trump’s crackdown on illegal alien truck drivers.** That good
news joins sharp drops in traffic deaths, overdoses, violent crime, and
border fatalities after the largest immigration enforcement surge in
modern history — while Democrats’ open-border policies left chaos,
missing kids, and body counts.

Saving lives is at the heart of President Trump’s border security and
law enforcement agenda. At his direction, the Administration is
[arresting](https://www.dhs.gov/news-releases/press-releases?field_taxonomy_topics_tid=171&items_per_page=50&page=0)
criminal illegals,
[removing](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-yanks-133-illegal-electronic-logging)
unqualified truck drivers from the roads, and
[rescuing](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/three-people-charged-conspiracy-commit-sex-trafficking-minors-and-related-charges)
victims of human trafficking.

### **Here are six ways President Trump’s immigration policy is saving lives:**

**It is making America’s roads safer.** Traffic deaths
[dropped](https://www.nhtsa.gov/press-releases/decline-traffic-fatalities-first-half-2026)
sharply in the first half of this year — the second-lowest half-year
fatality rate on record. This follows President Trump’s
[order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/04/enforcing-commonsense-rules-of-the-road-for-americas-truck-drivers/)
to enforce English proficiency requirements and review non-domiciled
commercial licenses —
[taking](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-yanks-133-illegal-electronic-logging#:~:text=Placed%2034%2C000%20drivers,unvetted%20foreign%20drivers.)
34,000 drivers out of service for failing basic English standards and
revoking more than 31,000 illegally issued licenses. For years,
Democrats looked the other way while foreign drivers who could not read
road signs or communicate with law enforcement operated 80,000-pound
trucks on American highways.

**It is crushing the overdose crisis.** Overdose deaths
[fell](https://www.cdc.gov/overdose-prevention/data-channel/index.html)
11.5% amid
[record](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/largest-fentanyl-bust-dea-history-authorities-seize-over-400-kilograms-fentanyl-record)
fentanyl seizures and the
[lowest](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/11/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-cbps-nationwide-encounters-october-were#:~:text=Drug%20Interdictions%20That%20Save%20Lives)
levels of cross-border drug trafficking in years, [driving
down](https://abc7news.com/post/disruption-fentanyl-supply-chain-is-working-purity-levels-take-drastic-drop-dea-says/18296991/)
fentanyl pill purity. In fact, drug overdose deaths have now
[declined](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/u-s-drug-overdose-deaths-decrease-for-18th-consecutive-month-under-trump-administration/)
for 18 straight months under President Trump. After Democrats refused to
secure the border and allowed cartels to flood American communities with
poison, that pipeline is now being choked off.

**It is confronting violent crime and criminal aliens.** Violent crime
[dropped](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/fbi-releases-2025-reported-crimes-in-the-nation-statistics)
9.3% in 2025, including an 18.1% drop in murder and nonnegligent
manslaughter — the
[largest](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/08/president-trump-delivers-largest-drop-in-violent-crime-in-american-history/)
annual drop in violent crime ever recorded — while ICE
[conducted](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/ice-breaks-records-fiscal-year-2026-most-arrests-and-deportations-agencys-history)
a record 491,000 immigration arrests and 483,000 deportations in FY26.
Criminals shielded and released under the previous Administration are
now being removed from the country faster than ever before.

**It is dramatically lowering deaths at the border.** Migrant deaths
[fell](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-dhs-announces-lowest)
nearly 80% in FY26 from Biden-era peaks as the Trump Administration
[delivered](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/11/16/under-president-trump-and-secretary-mullin-cbps-nationwide-encounters-october-were)
18 straight months of zero releases at the southern border and
[drove](https://www.foxnews.com/us/trump-border-crackdown-drives-encounters-record-october-low-zero-release-streak-hits-18-months-dhs-says)
illegal crossings down 95% from the Biden-era monthly average. Southwest
border apprehensions fell to just a tiny fraction of the more than 1.8
million annually under Biden. The prior Administration’s
catch-and-release policies turned the border into a humanitarian
disaster; that era is over.

**It is bringing endangered children to safety.** Approximately 157,000
of the roughly 300,000 unaccompanied migrant children lost under Biden
have been
[located](https://californiainsider.com/news/homan-says-157000-missing-unaccompanied-immigrant-children-located-6095104)
in President Trump’s second term. The scale of the Biden-era failure on
missing migrant children remains one of the most serious scandals of
that presidency.

**It is rescuing victims from human traffickers.** The Trump
Administration has
[rescued](https://x.com/FBIDirectorKash/status/2070103655991157132) more
than 7,200 children from traffickers and predators (a 42% increase over
the previous Administration) and
[arrested](https://x.com/FBIDirectorKash/status/2070103655991157132)
more than 3,500 predators and human traffickers (a 102% increase).
Immigration enforcement saves lives; non-enforcement enables
exploitation.
