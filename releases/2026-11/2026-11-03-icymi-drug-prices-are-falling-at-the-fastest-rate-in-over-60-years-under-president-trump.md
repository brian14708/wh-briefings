**Prescription drug prices are plummeting for the first time in decades
under the leadership of President Donald J. Trump — delivering real,
measurable relief to millions of Americans.** In *City Journal*, Dr.
Joel Zinberg
[explains](https://www.city-journal.org/article/prescription-drug-costs-glp1-trumprx)
why this is a major accomplishment:

*“Yet few Americans realize that drug prices—a major complaint—are
actually falling.*

*The Consumer Price Index for prescription drugs has either fallen or
been flat every month of 2026. The latest inflation data from the Bureau
of Labor Statistics show that while the overall CPI rose 3.7 percent
year to year, prescription drug prices fell 4.5 percent—the largest
year-over-year decline since 1963—and nonprescription prices fell 0.9
percent…*

*The Trump administration has pursued multiple policies to bring drug
prices down. TrumpRx, launched in February 2026, provides cash-paying
patients with large discounts on over 800 drugs, including many of the
most popular medicines. Chief among these are the GLP-1 weight-loss
drugs. U.S. prescription drug spending surged in 2025, driven by
increased utilization of the GLP-1s tirzepatide and semaglutide at
approximately $60 billion each. Consumers’ use of the TrumpRx
discounts—particularly for GLP-1s—has been a major factor in lowering
prescription prices throughout 2026.*

*Agreements with pharmaceutical companies Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk
reduce the cost of GLP-1 medications for government programs and TrumpRx
while expanding Medicare coverage for obesity with a $50 monthly copay.
TrumpRx prices are most valuable to uninsured patients and those whose
private plans do not cover GLP-1s to treat obesity. (Less than a third
of employer-provided health plans cover GLP-1s for obesity.) By
providing low, transparent prices to consumers, these programs create
downward pressure on drug prices in all insurance plans.”*
