  Creating Jobs'

**The October [jobs
report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/jobs-report-october-2026.html)
crushed expectations, confirming that President Trump’s America First
agenda is delivering a manufacturing renaissance.** Tariffs and tax cuts
are bringing production home, strengthening supply chains, raising
blue-collar wages, and adding hundreds of thousands of jobs for American
workers.

**Here’s what you need to know:**

- 165,000 jobs were added in October — blowing out expectations by over
  100,000.
  - 19,000 manufacturing jobs were added — the strongest monthly gain
    since 2023.

  <!-- -->

  - More than 100,000 manufacturing jobs and 120,000 factory
    construction jobs have been added since President Trump returned to
    office.
- Manufacturing employment is rising for the first time in three years,
  reversing the Biden-era loss of more than 200,000 factory jobs.
- September’s jobs report was revised up to 60,000 jobs (double the
  initial estimates); August’s report was revised up to 186,000 jobs.
- Wage growth reached its highest for the year, average hourly earnings
  beat expectations, and the unemployment rate fell.

**Since day one, the Trump Administration has delivered on its promise
to fight for American workers**:

- More than 1.25 million private sector jobs have been
  [added](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/09/made-in-america-manufacturing-private-hiring-power-explosive-august-jobs-report/)
  since President Trump returned to office.
- Jobless claims
  [fell](https://www.breitbart.com/economy/2026/11/05/jobless-claims-fall-to-57-year-low-of-189000/)
  to a new 57-year low.
- The trade deficit
  [dropped](https://www.wsj.com/economy/trade/u-s-trade-deficit-contracted-in-september-e9eeff46)
  over 12% in September; U.S. goods exports hit a record high, up nearly
  15% year-over-year.
- Capital goods imports — the machinery and equipment needed to rebuild
  domestic industry —
  [reached](https://www.reuters.com/business/us-trade-deficit-narrows-slightly-september-imports-race-record-high-2026-11-04/)
  a record 46% share of total imports.
- 36% of equipment manufacturers have [reshored or are
  reshoring](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/reshoring-foreign-direct-investment-247k-jobs-2025-report-reshoring-initiative/873433/),
  and 63% are
  [making](https://reshorenow.org/content/pdf/2026_Reshoring_Survey_Report.pdf?_sp=fce11f61-9c9b-4988-af9c-301a155a88eb.1793800878309)
  capital investments to expand U.S. production.
- U.S. manufacturing
  [expanded](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/october/)
  for a 10<sup>th</sup> straight month.
- Core capital goods orders are
  [up](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/NEWORDER) almost 14% from a
  year ago and are on track for a record share this year.
- The economy
  [grew](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/10/president-trump-unleashes-the-american-economy-gdp-surges-as-investment-hits-multi-year-high/)
  3.4% in the third quarter, once again defying the experts.
