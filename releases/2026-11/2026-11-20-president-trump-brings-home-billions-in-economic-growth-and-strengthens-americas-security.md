President Donald J. Trump has concluded his four-day visit to the
Philippines and China where he advanced his America First agenda,
secured billions in deals that support American workers, ensured a free
and open Indo-Pacific that benefits everyday Americans, and bolstered
regional security to protect Americans from illicit activity
abroad. **At home or abroad, President Trump is delivering for
Americans.**

This week, in the Indo-Pacific, President Trump secured real and
tangible wins for the American people on trade, energy, economic growth,
and cracking down on crime. 

- [**Secured**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-historic-economic-deals-for-american-workers-and-strengthens-americas-leadership-in-asia/)** over
  $17 billion in deals benefiting tens of thousands of American workers
  and American companies.**
  - These deals support the jobs of American farmers, manufacturers, and
    technicians, and help rebuild our defense industrial base. 
- [Signed](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122670007964356775?a=MeIPy-y-8beiW-77L5o&s=20) a** trade
  deal with
  Thailand** – [eliminating](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/joint-statement-on-an-agreement-between-the-united-states-of-america-and-the-kingdom-of-thailand-on-reciprocal-trade/) Thailand’s
  tariffs on 99% of all U.S. exports and unlocking market access for a
  variety of U.S. industrial and agricultural exports such as vehicles,
  medical devices, pharmaceuticals, ethanol, and poultry. 
  - Thailand will also buy an additional $2.6 billion in U.S.
    agricultural products each year, supporting our great farmers and
    ranchers. President Trump
    also [announced](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-historic-economic-deals-for-american-workers-and-strengthens-americas-leadership-in-asia/#:~:text=The%20leaders%20also%20committed%20to%20eliminate%20the%20U.S.%20trade%20deficit%20with%20the%20Philippines%20through%20increased%20Philippine%20imports%20of%20American%20energy%20and%20agriculture%20products%2C) the
    Philippines’ commitment to eliminate the U.S. trade deficit with the
    Philippines.

  <!-- -->

  - Thanks to President Trump’s America First trade policies, **American
    exports are at an **[**all-time
    high**](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/exports)**.**
- [Announced](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122680681276776607?a=NXJhtNl79CZkN_T7_Xw&s=20) the
  approval of a massive LNG pipeline in Alaska that South Korea and
  Taiwan helped fund, which will produce record amounts of abundant and
  affordable American Energy, **lowering Americans’ energy costs and
  bolstering our energy security and dominance**.
  - Because of the Trump Administration’s energy dominance agenda, U.S.
    energy production is at a record high.
- [Met](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/u-s-russia-china-heads-of-state-declaration-toward-peace/) with
  the President of Russia and the President of China in a first-ever
  trilateral meeting of the Presidents. **President Trump will meet with
  anyone who is willing to work together to make the lives of Americans
  – and the world – more prosperous and secure.**
  - President
    Trump [emphasized](https://x.com/WhiteHouse/status/2123401811327619388?a=hIGEW2BM7fqayFRCQms&s=20) the
    special responsibility the three global leaders have to maintaining
    open, respectful, and productive dialogue.President Trump called for
    the [reduction](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123338944624685475?a=3yq2z0D9R-VGJqhG5t4&s=20) of
    the number of nuclear weapons in the world and
    the [end](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123339626610119150?a=w0XrUjl2tTwp33DoMjQ&s=20) of the
    war between Russia and Ukraine.

  - Additionally, the
    leaders [committed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/u-s-russia-china-heads-of-state-declaration-toward-peace/) to
    advancing peace across the world.
- Thanks to President Trump’s leadership in [cracking
  down](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-expands-capabilities-to-combat-transnational-cyber-enabled-crime/) on
  transnational criminal organizations that target and attack Americans,
  ASEAN [adopted](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122675997367799957?a=LtAOt-kZn58_7H-1P8o&s=20) a
  comprehensive work plan to tackle online scams.
  - **President Trump is committed to addressing crime targeting
    Americans regardless of where it originates.**
- Held
  a [meeting](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/quad-leaders-summit-joint-press-release/) with
  Quad Leaders to tackle the region’s toughest challenges and ensure a
  free and open Indo-Pacific.
- At this year’s APEC Summit, President
  Trump [championed](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123250057747046857?a=rYu6FjZOg97wA65qIsA&s=20) America’s
  economic growth
  and [highlighted](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123249678032466075?a=jAYj9any8BSoyeLlqj4&s=20) the
  extraordinary prosperity that Super Intelligence is unleashing to all
  people all around the world.
