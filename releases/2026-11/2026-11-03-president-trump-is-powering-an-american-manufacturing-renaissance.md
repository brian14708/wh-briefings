title: President Trump Is Powering an American Manufacturing Renaissance
---
 
**American manufacturing is surging under President Donald J. Trump.** A
new
[report](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/october/)
shows the sector expanding for the tenth consecutive month, with
production growing at its fastest pace in nearly five years. Factories
are expanding. Jobs are returning. Paychecks are rising faster than
inflation. And companies are investing trillions to build their futures
in America.

- **Manufacturing jobs are coming back.** Manufacturers have
  [added](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm) 72,000 jobs
  this year, reversing the 202,000 manufacturing jobs lost in the final
  two years of the Biden Administration. Employment in factory
  construction has climbed by nearly 112,000 since President Trump took
  office.
- **Paychecks are growing.** Manufacturing workers’ weekly earnings are
  [up](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm) 5% over the past
  year — outpacing inflation and boosting purchasing power.
- **Investment is accelerating.** Business investment
  [jumped](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-3rd-quarter-2026)
  12.7% in the third quarter of 2026, the fastest growth in 15 years.
  Private investment in plants and equipment rose 9.4% — more than twice
  the 2025 rate and triple the pace of Biden’s final year. Capital goods
  imports
  [hit](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-3rd-quarter-2026)
  a record share of total goods imports in September while consumer
  goods imports fell to a historic low, signaling businesses are
  equipping American factories, not just importing finished products.
- **Demand for American machinery is strong.** U.S. machine tool orders
  [reached](https://metrology.news/us-machine-tool-orders-hit-third-highest-monthly-total-on-record/)
  their third-highest monthly total on record, up more than 36% from a
  year earlier.

**Companies are matching the momentum with major new commitments. In
just the past few weeks:**

- **Audi**
  [announced](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-24/volkswagen-plans-new-audi-plant-in-georgia-handing-trump-a-win)
  a $4 billion factory in Georgia — its first U.S. manufacturing plant —
  creating 4,500 jobs.
- **Westinghouse Electric**
  [announced](https://whosonthemove.com/westinghouse-announces-landmark-expansion-in-south-carolina-to-advance-next-generation-nuclear-fuel/)
  a $1 billion investment in South Carolina to build a next-generation
  nuclear fuel factory.
- **U.S. Steel**
  [announced](https://www.wsj.com/business/nippons-u-s-steel-to-invest-nearly-3-billion-in-hot-strip-mill-near-pittsburgh-b9784176)
  a $3 billion investment near Pittsburgh to construct a new hot strip
  mill and increase annual capacity by roughly 1.3 million tons.
- **Apple**
  [announced](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-announces-american-manufacturing-program-partners-for-latest-iphone-lineup/)
  up to $100 million to strengthen American titanium production.
- **Bombardier**
  [announced](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/jetmaker-bombardier-doubles-down-us-investment-deal-touted-trump)
  $270 million in U.S. manufacturing investment, creating thousands of
  jobs.
- **Carrier**
  [announced](https://www.carrier.com/us/en/news/corporate/2026/carrierto-invest-more-than-600-million-to-expand-advanced-us-manufacturing-and-engineering-capacity/)
  more than $600 million in an advanced manufacturing facility in Fort
  Worth.

More jobs. Rising wages. New factories. Record investment. Under
President Trump, American manufacturing is being rebuilt — and made
great again.
