title: "\u201CNo Indication of a Cyberattack\u201D: DNI Clayton and Secretary Rubio\
  \ on Recent Tech Outages"
---
 
**The Trump Administration sees no indication of a coordinated
cyberattack, state-sponsored activity, or malign actor behind the recent
tech outages.** Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton and
National Security Adviser Marco Rubio made clear that these are separate
incidents caused by companies patching vulnerabilities and upgrading
outdated systems, with America leading the effort to harden its digital
infrastructure.

- **DNI Clayton:** “This is not a cyberattack, and this is not a
  coordinated effort by a nation state. We’ve looked very closely.
  Anytime we see something like this, those are the first questions that
  we ask on behalf of the American people, and we go across and we say,
  ‘Is that what’s going on here?’ **That is not what’s going on here.**”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122799793218969745))
- **Secretary Rubio:** “We have no indication, no evidence, and no
  reason to believe at all that any of these incidents are related to
  each other. In essence, **the Microsoft incident has nothing to do
  with what we saw with the FAA. The FAA has nothing to do with what we
  saw with the banks a few days ago**… We have zero evidence or any
  reason to believe that any of these isolated or individual instances
  have anything to do with nefarious actors, with cyber hackers, with
  criminals, with another nation-state, with a malign actor of any
  kind.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122861266146587028))
- **DNI Clayton:** “We do not see the indicia of a coordinated
  cyberattack — let me just be clear about that — and we do not see the
  indicia of state-sponsored activity here. And we can be fairly
  confident in that because we know those things exist, and we’ve been
  looking for them in the past.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122800369075994738))
- **Secretary Rubio:** “People are fearful, people are uncertain — and
  the best way to deal with people’s uncertainty and discomfort is to
  share with them transparently what we know and what we believe in
  terms of the information we have before us… **We are not under
  systemic attack**… Every indicator we have is that every single one of
  these events has an explanation or a cause that has nothing to do with
  a cyber-criminal, has nothing to do with a hostile actor or another
  country… **The American people should have confidence in that.**”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122863623504556066))
- **DNI Clayton:** “Let me tell you what we think this is, and we think
  that it is with confidence. Lots of institutions, because these
  vulnerabilities have been identified, are patching. They’re taking
  their systems down, they’re upgrading them, they’re putting patches in
  place… This is being done on much larger scale because everybody, and
  it’s a good news story, everybody recognizes it’s time to upgrade
  their systems. **And as they upgrade them, they’re not coming back as
  expected in all cases. And in some cases, systems have to hand off to
  other systems, and those handoffs are not coming back as expected.**
  But this is just an upgrade in patching, an upgrade in systems.”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122800369075994738))
- **Secretary Rubio:** “Sometimes when you are going in to fix your
  system because you’ve identified a vulnerability, it creates a
  short-term disruption in the way your system works… **Companies, which
  is a good thing, are now immediately sharing vulnerabilities because
  they don’t want to be accused of covering up something.**”
  ([Watch](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/marco-rubio-holds-presser-to-try-and-reassure-the-public-amid-outages/))
- **DNI Clayton:** “As a result of SI and the ability to scan systems
  more quickly, the ability to identify vulnerabilities has increased.
  That is why it is so incumbent upon all of us to work together to
  patch, to report vulnerabilities, and **it is where the U.S. is
  leading.**” ([Watch](https://www.foxbusiness.com/video/6408513620112))
- **DNI Clayton:** “**Our systems need upgrades. They need patching.**
  We have legacy systems, and they’re more and more interconnected, and
  they’re handling more and more traffic… Thankfully, the U.S. is
  leading and is far ahead in that step change in technology… **We’re
  going to come out the other side much better.**”
  ([Watch](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122878235125879293))
