  Director

**Yesterday, President Donald J. Trump**
[**named**](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/white-house/national-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-leaving-kirsten-davies-replaci-rcna610329)
**Kirsten A. Davies to lead the White House Office of the National Cyber
Director.** A battle-tested cyber leader and the Pentagon’s Chief
Information Officer, Davies will drive an America First cyber strategy
that treats cybersecurity as national security and keeps the United
States dominant and secure in cyberspace.

**Her selection immediately drew praise from leaders across the
Administration:**

[Secretary of State Marco
Rubio](https://x.com/SecRubio/status/2123591426814460410):
“Congratulations to the new National Cyber Director Kirsten Davies —
look forward to working together to strengthen America’s cybersecurity.”

[Secretary of War Pete
Hegseth](https://x.com/SecWar/status/2123594274721644701): “The
Department congratulates Kirsten Davies on her new role as the National
Cyber Director. Throughout her tenure at DOW, she has proven herself to
be a dedicated patriot and professional, and we are confident she will
advance President Trump’s America First agenda across the cybersecurity
landscape.”

[Director of National Intelligence Jay
Clayton](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2123598853639119139):

**President Trump has made cybersecurity a core pillar of his
Administration. Under his leadership, the United States has:**

- [Released](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/03/white-house-unveils-president-trumps-cyber-strategy-for-america/)
  a Cyber Strategy for America built on six pillars to keep the United
  States unrivaled in cyberspace, on offense and defense;
- [Overhauled](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/national-security-presidential-memorandum-nspm-12/)
  cybersecurity for America’s military and intelligence systems, holding
  agency heads accountable and modernizing oversight for the first time
  in over 35 years;
- [Seized](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-and-fbi-seize-platforms-operated-and-used-china-state-sponsored-hackers)
  Chinese state-sponsored hacking platforms targeting U.S. critical
  infrastructure, including NASA and the Federal Reserve;
- [Unleashed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/expanding-capabilities-to-combat-transnational-cyber-enabled-crime/)
  vetted American companies, under federal oversight, to go after
  foreign cybercriminal networks;
- [Mandated](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/securing-the-nation-against-advanced-cryptographic-attacks/)
  post-quantum cryptography for high-value federal systems by 2030;
- [Harnessed](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)
  AI to defend federal and military networks, creating a clearinghouse
  to find and patch vulnerabilities;
- [Ordered](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/combating-cybercrime-fraud-and-predatory-schemes-against-american-citizens/)
  a plan to dismantle the transnational criminal networks and scam
  centers preying on Americans;
- [Charged](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-charges-12-chinese-contract-hackers-and-law-enforcement-officers-global)
  12 Chinese contract hackers and law enforcement officers behind global
  hacking campaigns that hit U.S. agencies;
- Sanctioned ransomware enablers [First
  VPN](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0559), [Media
  Land](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0319), [Aeza
  Group](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0185), and
  [Zservers](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0018);
- [Blocked](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-updates-covered-list-include-foreign-made-consumer-routers)
  new foreign-made consumer routers deemed a national security threat;
- [Hardened](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/sustaining-select-efforts-to-strengthen-the-nations-cybersecurity-and-amending-executive-order-13694-and-executive-order-14144/)
  federal software, internet routing, and encryption standards against
  foreign threats;
- Signed laws
  [funding](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/7147/all-info)
  the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency,
  [authorizing](https://therecord.media/trump-signs-ndaa-cyber-command)
  Cyber Command operations, and
  [extending](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/guidance-assist-non-federal-entities-share-cyber-threat-indicators-and-defensive-measures-federal)
  legal protections for companies that share cyber threat intelligence;
  and
- Taken decisive first-term action,
  [releasing](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/National-Cyber-Strategy.pdf)
  the first fully articulated national cyber strategy in 15 years,
  [elevating](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefings-statements/statement-president-donald-j-trump-elevation-cyber-command/)
  U.S. Cyber Command,
  [creating](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2018/11/19/cybersecurity-and-infrastructure-security-agency)
  CISA,
  [securing](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/presidential-actions/executive-order-securing-information-communications-technology-services-supply-chain/)
  the tech supply chain from foreign adversaries, [ripping
  out](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefings-statements/president-donald-j-trump-committed-safeguarding-americas-vital-communications-networks-securing-5g-technology/)
  untrusted telecom equipment, and
  [purging](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-17-01-removal-kaspersky-branded-products)
  untrusted software from federal systems.
