title: S. 858 Signed into Law
---
 
On Thursday, September 10, 2026, the President signed into law:

S. 858, the ” Hershel ‘Woody’ Williams National Medal of Honor Monument
Location Act,” which authorizes the National Medal of Honor Museum
Foundation to establish a commemorative work on the National Mall to
honor the extraordinary acts of valor, selfless service, and sacrifice
displayed by Medal of Honor recipients.
