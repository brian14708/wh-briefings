title: "Adobe, Amazon, Intel, Starlink, Google, and Zoom partner with First Lady Melania\
  \ Trump\u2019s Fostering the Future Together"
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump secured six new private-sector technology
partnerships for Fostering the Future Together, including Adobe, Amazon,
Intel, Starlink, Google, and Zoom.

“When I established Fostering the Future Together, the mission was
clear: empower children through technology and education. Delivering on
that mission demands alliances between best-in-class innovators and
global leaders, especially as super intelligence transforms how the next
generation learns,” said First Lady Melania Trump.

Combined, the new technology partners committed more than $23 million
towards education, tools, and expertise, for children across the globe.

Google committed $4 million to expand its SI Educator Series globally
through its new Fostering the Future Together partnership. “Google is
proud to support First Lady Melania Trump’s vital effort,” said Google’s
Maggie Johnson. “This investment demonstrates our belief in keeping
educators in the lead.”

Intel will deploy its technology across ten countries through its SI for
Citizens Program, SI for Youth Program, and Semiconductor Education
Pathways Program. Adobe will provide students worldwide with free
SI-powered study and creation tools through Student Spaces. Amazon will
expand free access to its Kiro SI coding assistant, reaching 5 million
students through 132 universities, while AWS Academy will provide free
training to more than 2 million students. Zoom Cares will provide $2.75
million in SI for Good grants across Europe, Africa, Latin America, and
the Asia-Pacific region.

“I deeply value the commitment of these technology companies and welcome
them to the Fostering the Future Together movement as we scale across
continents,” said First Lady Melania Trump.

**About Fostering the Future Together:**  
Fostering the Future Together is a global coalition of nations unified
to empower children through technology and education.

First Lady Melania Trump announced Fostering the Future Together during
the 80th United Nations General Assembly. In spring 2026, she convened
the initiative’s two-day meeting in Washington DC, bringing together
First Spouses and technology-sector leaders at the United Sates
Department of State. The gathering culminated at the White House, where
the First Lady welcomed the largest assembly of First Spouses ever
convened there for diplomatic purposes in U.S. history. During the 81st
United Nations General Assembly, First Lady Melania Trump unveiled
several new 2026 Fostering the Future Together regional events,
including the G20 (USA), the ASEAN Conference (Philippines), Europe
(Poland), and the Caribbean (Saint Kitts and Nevis).
