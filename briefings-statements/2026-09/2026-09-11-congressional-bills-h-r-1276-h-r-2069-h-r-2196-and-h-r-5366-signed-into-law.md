title: Congressional Bills H.R. 1276, H.R. 2069, H.R. 2196 and H.R. 5366 Signed into
  Law
---
 
On Friday, September 11, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 1276, which directs the Department of the Interior to remove all
deed restriction from an approximately 3.62-acre parcel of land conveyed
from the Department to the City of Paducah, Kentucky;

H.R. 2069, the “Stop Secret Spending Act of 2025,” which expands Federal
spending transparency requirements to include other transaction
agreements, and to modify related standards, oversight, and reporting;

H.R. 2196, the “National Emergency Medical Services Memorial Extension
Act,” which extends the authority of the National Emergency Medical
Services Memorial Foundation to establish a commemorative work on
Federal lands in the District of Columbia to commemorate the commitment
and service represented by emergency medical services personnel; and

H.R. 5366, the “Doug LaMalfa Federal Disaster Tax Relief Certainty Act,”
which extends and codifies the Federal tax deduction for qualified
disaster-related personal casualty losses and the exclusion from gross
income of qualified wildfire relief payments.
