title: Presidential Message on National Preparedness Month
---
 
America has always been a nation of strong, capable, and determined
people who understand that strength must be built before crisis.
 Preparedness builds that strength, and readiness is the result.  This
National Preparedness Month, I call on every American citizen to ready
themselves and their families, homes, businesses, and communities for
natural and man-made threats.

Readiness begins with understanding the risks that matter most.  Before
a crisis, Americans can take practical steps to protect their homes and
property, purchase affordable insurance against the risks they face,
know evacuation routes, maintain an emergency kit with essential
supplies, and stay informed through trusted local news and real-time
alerts.  These simple investments save lives, protect livelihoods, and
help families and communities return to normal.

The same principle applies to the systems that power our Nation.
 American businesses and operators of critical infrastructure have an
essential role and must also be ready.  The energy, communications,
transportation, financial, water, food, and health systems that power
American life must remain available when we need them most.  My
Administration is working to strengthen the reliability of these
systems, secure vital supply chains, reduce dangerous dependencies, and
ensure services continue operating through crisis.

On June 23, 2026, I issued the National Resilience Strategy that
articulates how the Nation, through federalism, will achieve a readiness
posture.  As President, my Administration continues transforming
emergency management in the United States around readiness,
responsibility, and results.  States and communities must participate in
this transformation by building their own fiscal and operational
capacity to manage the emergencies they routinely face and support one
another through mutual aid, drastically speeding up deployment and
delivery of critical resources when disaster strikes.  Americans deserve
state and local governments that respond quickly to their needs,
enabling communities to rebuild stronger and more resilient than ever
before.  This allows the Federal Government to concentrate its resources
on the greatest risks and catastrophic incidents—regionally or
nationally.  It also makes our country harder to threaten.  When America
functions through crisis, our adversaries have fewer opportunities to
harm us and fewer opportunities to achieve their objectives.

Strength begins with a Nation that is ready to act and such readiness is
embodied by Americans who stand strong, who never back down, and who
always put America First.  This month, we honor our brave first
responders, law enforcement officers, emergency personnel, and selfless
volunteers.  When the alarm sounds, they do not hesitate.  They answer
the call, protect their fellow Americans, and help keep our communities
and our Nation safe and secure.  Together, we resolve to build a more
prepared, ready, and resilient America.
