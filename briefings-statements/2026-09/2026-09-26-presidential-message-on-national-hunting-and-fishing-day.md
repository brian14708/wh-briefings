title: Presidential Message on National Hunting and Fishing Day
---
 
For over 250 years, America’s hunters and anglers have been faithful
stewards of our great outdoors—respecting the land, pursuing their sport
with honor, and preserving our lands and waters.  On National Hunting
and Fishing Day, we pay tribute to the American sportsmen, hunters, and
fishermen who protect our abundant resources, cherish our Nation’s
natural beauty, and sustain one of the oldest and proudest customs in
American life.

Hunting and fishing have fed our families, tamed our frontier, and
shaped the character of our people since the very beginning of our
national story.  Today, American outdoorsmen are the backbone of
conservation, passing their skills and respect for the land from one
generation to the next.  They also support a thriving outdoor economy
that sustains jobs, strengthens rural communities, and contributes
billions of dollars into the wild places they love through every
license, tag, and piece of gear they buy. 

My Administration has proudly carried forward this great tradition by
giving Americans more access to their public lands than ever before. 
During my first term, I signed the Great American Outdoors Act—the
single largest investment in America’s national parks and public lands
in history and the most significant conservation achievement since Teddy
Roosevelt’s Presidency.  I also enacted landmark legislation protecting
1.3 million new acres of wilderness and unlocked more than 2.3 million
acres for American sportsmen, and we recovered more endangered and
threatened species than any prior administration in a single term.

Just this month, I was proud to sign an Executive Order to reinvigorate
America’s hunting heritage, opening new hunting opportunities on federal
lands—including places long cut off from sportsmen due to Federal
overreach—and expanding Sunday hunting across our Nation.  This order
also directs my Cabinet to work alongside the States to promote thriving
populations of America’s iconic game species, and it prioritizes
incentives that cultivate the hunting tradition in young Americans and
veterans like never before.  In addition, I signed an Executive Order to
restore American saltwater angling and recreation, slashing burdensome
regulations on boaters, anglers, and outdoor businesses; modernizing
catch reporting; rebuilding prized stocks like the Atlantic Striped
Bass; and turning retired offshore platforms into flourishing artificial
reefs. 

My Administration was also proud to reestablish the Hunting and Shooting
Conservation and Access Council earlier this year, calling on
distinguished sportsmen and conservation leaders from across the country
to council the Federal Government on conserving our wildlife and opening
more public land to hunting and shooting sports.  Through these historic
initiatives, my Administration will continue to cut red tape, expand
access for every American sportsman, and strengthen our great outdoor
economy—restoring the integrity of our national lands and keeping their
splendor intact for generations to come, guaranteeing that these wonders
remain the birthright of every American, now and forever.

Today, our Nation proudly celebrates the hunters and fishermen who
champion their sport and take to the field and stream—whose heritage is
America’s heritage.  Together, we will preserve this cherished
tradition, and as long as I am President, the best hunting and fishing
will always be found here in the United States of America.
