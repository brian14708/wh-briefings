title: Congressional Bills S. 32 and S. 307 Signed into Law
---
 
On Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the President signed into law:

S. 32, the “Local Access to Courts Act,” which adds College Station,
Texas, and El Centro, California, to the list of locations where court
is authorized to be held in the Southern District of Texas and in the
Southern District of California, respectively; and

S. 307, the “Prison Staff safety Enhancement Act,” which establishes
requirements for the Bureau of Prisons within the Department of Justice
to address sexual harassment and sexual assault on Bureau of Prisons
staff by incarcerated individuals.
