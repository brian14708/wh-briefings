title: Congressional Bill H.R. 5334 Signed into Law
---
 
On Friday, September 18, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 5334, the “Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of
2026,” which authorizes and expands statutory sanctions, tariffs, and
prohibitions on Russia and extends existing sanctions on Iran.
