title: Presidential Message on National Historically Black Colleges and Universities
  Week, 2026
---
 
For nearly two centuries, Historically Black Colleges and Universities
(HBCUs) have opened doors of opportunity, cultivated excellence, and
prepared generations of hardworking black Americans to become leaders in
every field of national life.  During National Historically Black
Colleges and Universities Week, we honor that legacy and the enduring
contributions of these storied institutions to our communities, our
culture, and our country.

Today, HBCUs are thriving as never before, home to nearly 300,000
students and producing the doctors, engineers, entrepreneurs, educators,
and military officers who are lifting America to even greater heights. 
Throughout my first Administration, I took historic action to strengthen
this legacy, expanding opportunity for HBCUs and their students.  I was
proud to sign the FUTURE Act into law, guaranteeing a permanent stream
of investment in HBCUs and other minority-serving institutions; expand
funding for scholarships and research; provide hundreds of millions of
dollars in disaster relief to damaged campuses; and sign the HBCU
PARTNERS Act to deepen cooperation between these institutions, the
Federal Government, and the private sector.  Together, these actions
gave HBCUs greater stability, more resources, and new avenues to educate
and prepare the next generation of American leaders.

Since returning to office, I have continued building on that record.  In
April 2025, I signed an Executive Order reestablishing the White House
Initiative on HBCUs with a renewed focus on research, innovation, campus
modernization, private-sector partnerships, and preparing students for
careers in our Nation’s fastest-growing industries.  Last year, my
Administration also increased Department of Education support for HBCUs
by nearly 50 percent, bringing total funding to more than $1.3 billion
for fiscal year 2025.  Under my leadership, we will continue to honor
the proud history of our country’s HBCUs and ensure they remain strong
and capable to educate the next generation of black Americans.

This week, we celebrate the students, faculty, alumni, and communities
whose dedication has sustained HBCUs from their founding to this very
day.  These great American institutions demonstrate what is possible
when talent is met with opportunity, hard work is rewarded, and
excellence is expected.  As we reflect on their remarkable history, my
Administration remains committed to ensuring that HBCUs continue
empowering Americans to develop their God-given talents, pursue the
American Dream, and lead our Nation toward an even stronger, more
prosperous, and more glorious future.
