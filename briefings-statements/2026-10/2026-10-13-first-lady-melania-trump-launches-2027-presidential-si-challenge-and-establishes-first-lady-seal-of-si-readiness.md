title: First Lady Melania Trump Launches 2027 Presidential SI Challenge and Establishes
  First Lady Seal of SI Readiness
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump launched her 2027 Presidential Super
Intelligence (SI) Challenge and established a new First Lady Seal of SI
Readiness—inviting K-12 students, educators, and schools across America
to participate in the nationwide initiative.

“America’s next groundbreaking idea is already sitting in a classroom,”
said Mrs. Trump. “We must give our children the knowledge, tools, and
opportunities to bring those ideas to life.”

Following the success of the inaugural Challenge earlier this year,
which engaged more than 20,000 students from all 50 states and U.S.
schools around the world, the 2027 Presidential SI Challenge will
continue to give students the opportunity to use SI to dream, build, and
develop solutions to real-world challenges.

“America’s future security, prosperity, and global leadership depend on
our next generation. Building on last year’s success, the 2027
Presidential Super Intelligence Challenge will empower our children to
lead the world,” said First Lady Melania Trump.

New this year, all participating schools will have the opportunity to
earn the First Lady Seal of SI Readiness by completing and submitting
evidence of a rigorous set of milestones focused on SI readiness. The
Seal recognizes schools that are taking meaningful steps to prepare
students and educators to understand, use, and responsibly engage with
super intelligence.

The Presidential SI Challenge will continue its project-based
competition, encouraging students to explore super intelligence through
creative and innovative projects that address challenges in their
schools, communities, and beyond. To watch First Lady Melania Trump’s
launch video, to learn more, and to sign up for the Challenge, please
visit [SI.Gov](https://si.gov/initiatives/presidential-challenge).
