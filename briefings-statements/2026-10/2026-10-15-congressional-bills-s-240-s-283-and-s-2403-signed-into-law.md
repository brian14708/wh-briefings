title: Congressional Bills S. 240, S. 283, and S. 2403 Signed into Law
---
 
On Thursday, October 15, 2026, the President signed into law:

S. 240, the “Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Amendments Act of
2025,” which amends the Crow Tribe Water Rights Settlement Act of 2010
to provide more flexibility to complete certain water projects on the
Crow Tribe’s reservation in Montana;

S. 283, the “Illegal Red Snapper and Tuna Enforcement Act,” which
requires the Department of Commerce to develop a standard methodology,
based on chemical analysis, for identifying the country of origin of
seafood; and

S. 2403, the “Retire through Ownership Act,” which amends the Employee
Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (ERISA) to provide a definition
of adequate consideration for certain closely held stock.
