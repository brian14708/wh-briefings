title: Presidential Message on National Domestic Violence Awareness Month
---
 
In communities across our great Nation, domestic violence destroys
families, harms innocent children, and leaves lasting pain in our
neighborhoods.  For America to be strong, children must feel safe in
their homes, spouses and significant others must be respected, and it
must be a universal truth that violence in any form is unacceptable. 
This National Domestic Violence Awareness Month, my Administration
reaffirms its commitment to permanently ending domestic violence and
ensuring every American can live free from the threat of violence.

For as many as 10 million Americans, the unrelenting fear of violence in
our homes, places of work, and other social settings make it impossible
to enjoy life and foster healthy relationships.  Behind every one of
these numbers is a fellow American robbed of the safety and peace that
our Nation promises to all its people.  As President, I will always
uplift survivors of domestic violence and make certain that shelters,
counselors, advocates, and hotlines have the resources they need to
answer each and every call for help.  Help is always within reach, and
you are never alone.  If you are in need of immediate and confidential
support, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233. 

This month, we commit that for our mothers, our fathers, our daughters,
and our sons, we will end the scourge of domestic violence.  My
Administration remains steadfast in safeguarding the safety and security
of the American People, giving law enforcement the tools and resources
they need to prevent domestic violence, and ensuring that those who
commit acts of violence are held accountable.  Together, we will protect
the innocent, defend the vulnerable, and build a safer and stronger
America.
