title: Presidential Message on Leif Erikson Day
---
 
Half a millennium before legendary American hero Christopher Columbus
set foot in the New World, Norse explorer Leif Erikson made history as
the first European to arrive on the North American continent.  This Leif
Erikson Day, we celebrate his exceptional legacy of bravery, daring, and
exploration as we build upon our extraordinary relationships with our
Nordic partners and open a glorious new chapter in America’s Arctic
story.

Born in 970, Leif Erikson developed a love for exploration and adventure
that he would carry through his entire life, a calling that would change
the world for centuries to come.  His steadfast Christian faith gave him
the conviction to sail into the unknown, voyaging over 1,800 miles
across the Atlantic with a small crew of 35 men and overcoming raging
winds, crashing waves, and crippling storms to reach the shores of
L’Anse aux Meadows in northern Newfoundland, Canada—the land he named
“Vinland.”  In doing so, he opened the door between two worlds and
proved that no ocean could hold back the course of discovery—the same
daring spirit that would one day build the greatest Nation on Earth.

A millennium later, that resolve lives on in America’s outstanding
relationships with Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, and Sweden—nations
bound to us by adventure, fortitude, and faith in Almighty God.  Nowhere
is that bond stronger today than in Greenland, where Lief Erikson once
led a settlement of his own and where my Administration has now secured
Greenland’s future as a bastion for U.S. security and transatlantic
defense.

Last month, I entered into a historic agreement with Denmark and
Greenland that gives the United States permanent control over security
in Greenland, guaranteeing our ability to safeguard America and our
allies for all time.  In concluding this agreement, I did what no
President in the last 100 years was willing to do—I permanently
recognized Greenland as an essential national security partner and made
the Arctic a pillar of American strength once again.  For centuries to
come, Americans will be safer, our Arctic frontier will be stronger, and
our bond with the Nordic peoples will be closer than ever before.

As we reflect on the enduring legacy of Leif Erikson, we recognize his
rightful place in our glorious American story, we honor the millions of
Nordic Americans whose contributions have shaped the life of our Nation,
and we commit to carrying the spirit of his marvelous discovery boldly
into America’s next chapter of exploration, friendship, and greatness.
