title: First Lady Melania Trump Secures Ceasefire Between Russia and Ukraine, Localized
  to Oleshky
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump successfully secured a ceasefire between Russia
and Ukraine, localized to the city of Oleshky. Both nations agreed to
temporarily stop all military action in Oleshky to allow for
humanitarian aid to enter and to prepare a civilian evacuation through a
designated safe passageway.

The localized ceasefire will begin on October 29, 2026.

“The situation in Oleshky is dire. Oleshky is a front-line war city on
the brink of disaster.  Oleshky’s remaining population is comprised
primarily of elderly residents who urgently need medical attention,
medicine, food, and clean water,” said First Lady Melania Trump. “The
people of Oleshky have limited electricity, live amid mined roads, and
endure regular military attacks. It was abundantly clear that without my
intervention, the possibility to bring humanitarian aid into the city
and to evacuate the elderly would be impossible.”

First Lady Melania Trump and her representative worked directly with
Russian and Ukrainian leadership to define the terms of the localized
ceasefire. The First Lady initiated discussions with both nations and
aligned the parties on the agreement’s objectives, technical procedures,
timing, processes, and terms, in her formal role as Exclusive
Intermediary.

“Non-military, civilian leaders from both countries will work together
to visit Oleshky residents in their homes and determine the level of
assistance they require. Residents who wish to evacuate will receive
coordinated support and transportation to their preferred destination. I
anticipate some will want to go to Russia, and some to Ukraine,” said
First Lady Melania Trump.  

The First Lady continued, “I have also secured an impartial American
media organization to accompany and document Russian and Ukrainian teams
on the ground throughout the process. This will play a significant role
in ensuring that the wishes of each family are faithfully respected.”

The exigent circumstances of Oleshky came to First Lady Melania Trump’s
attention during a private discussion at her Fostering the Future
Together event in New York City during the 81st United Nations General
Assembly.

“My representative and I immediately leveraged the communication channel
we established with Russia and Ukraine through the previous six
reunifications of 34 children. Both nations promptly assigned teams to
coordinate the complex mechanics involved, including demining. All
parties have worked around the clock since that moment,” explained First
Lady Melania Trump.

**About Oleshky**

Oleshky is located in southern Ukraine, directly across from the Dnipro
River in Kherson. Before the war, roughly 24,000 people lived in
Oleshky. Today, an estimated 1700-2000 residents remain, primarily older
people who have been unable to leave, along with approximately 50
children.   There has not been a formal demographic count in Oleshky
since 2022. Since the war began, the city has remained on the front
line, with much of its population displaced or evacuated. Following the
destruction of the Kakhovka Dam, approximately 90 percent of the city’s
territory was flooded.
