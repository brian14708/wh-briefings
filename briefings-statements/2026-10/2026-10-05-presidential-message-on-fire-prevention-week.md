title: Presidential Message on Fire Prevention Week
---
 
During Fire Prevention Week, my Administration honors the memory of
America’s courageous firefighters and first responders for their
extraordinary service and sacrifice. We are reaffirming our commitment
to strengthening fire prevention and emergency response across the
Nation.  I encourage every American to take simple steps to protect
their homes, families, and communities from preventable fires. 

Fire safety begins at home.  Every family can take precautions to
prevent fires, and to better protect themselves and their loved ones.
 These can include mapping out a home escape plan, testing smoke and
carbon monoxide alarms every month, replacing the worn wiring and
overloaded outlets that spark so many household fires, or practicing
safe charging habits that minimize the potential of fires from
ubiquitous electronic devices.  These commonsense steps can save lives
by preventing fire or by ensuring families are prepared in the event of
an emergency.   

My Administration remains committed to protecting American lives from
the threat of fires and improving our country’s ability to prevent and
respond to them.  Last year, I signed an Executive Order to streamline
Federal wildfire response, strengthen local preparedness, and put modern
technology to work detecting and fighting fires.  We are also working to
establish the United States Wildland Fire Service to better coordinate
Federal wildfire programs and respond more effectively when fires break
out.

This week, we also honor America’s extraordinary firefighters, first
responders, emergency personnel, and volunteers who selflessly respond
whenever the alarm sounds.  Their determination to aid fellow Americans
in distress embodies the highest American ideals of heroism, courage,
and service to others.  Together, we recommit to preventing fires,
protecting our communities, and building a Nation more prepared, more
ready, and more resilient than ever before.
