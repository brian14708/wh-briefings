title: "First Lady Melania Trump Champions New U.S. Women\u2019s Health Initiative"
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump advanced a landmark program to improve women’s
health through a new executive order that strengthens research,
prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and care for girls and women
throughout every stage of life.

“Previous generations fought for women’s rights. Our generation will
fight to discover the cures that will ensure a woman can live her life
in health and to the fullest,” The First Lady said from the White House.

The Executive Order establishes a White House Women’s Health Task Force
and establishes a new Women’s Health Division within the National
Institutes of Health to accelerate scientific discoveries to improve
women’s health outcomes.

First Lady Melania Trump highlighted her “First Lady’s Five Pillars of
Women’s Health Innovation:”

1.  Direct research funding towards finding cures that affect women in a
    unique way.  
2.  Use super intelligence to enhance our insight of female biology and
    speed up medical discoveries.
3.  Accelerate the process of getting promising therapeutics from
    laboratories to female patients, while maintaining strict safety
    standards.  
4.  Ensure that medical breakthroughs are accessible and affordable to
    women, regardless of their financial circumstances.  
5.  Replace generations of silence with open conversation, education,
    and compassion.

The **[Executive Order on Advancing Women’s Health to Improve Outcomes
in All Phases of
Life](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/10/advancing-womens-health-to-improve-outcomes-in-all-phases-of-life/)**
also recognizes the importance of collaboration with the private sector
in advancing new technologies and solutions for women’s health. Through
these efforts, the Trump Administration will work to ensure American
women have access to the highest-quality, scientifically supported
medical information and care while advancing new treatments, therapies,
and cures.

To learn more about the Executive Order, read the [**fact
sheet**](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-and-first-lady-melania-trump-advance-womens-health-to-improve-outcomes-in-all-phases-of-life/)
released by the White House.

First Lady Melania Trump’s full remarks, as prepared for delivery, are
below.

**Five Pillars of Women’s Health Innovation**

Cabinet members, doctors, directors, administrators, and all in
attendance today … thank you for standing behind this important issue.

Now is the time. This is the time.  

This is a transformative moment for women in America. Beginning today,
we will change the reality of women’s health.

Previous generations fought for women’s rights. Our generation will
fight to discover the cures that will ensure a woman can live her life
in health and to the fullest.

The Advancing Women’s Health to Improve Outcomes in All Phases of Life
Executive Order establishes a new standard for women’s health in
America.

We now possess extraordinary scientific capabilities, such as super
intelligence, advanced biotechnology, and groundbreaking therapies. At
the same time, social norms are changing, and topics that were once
taboo are becoming open, well-informed, and necessary.

Together, these developments present a unique chance to reinvent women’s
healthcare.

We will move our hopes into action, beginning with the distinguished
individuals and great minds present here today.

This afternoon, the first task force working session will convene. As
you embark on this important work, I ask you to keep in mind the
priorities that form my “First Lady’s Five Pillars of Women’s Health
Innovation.”

Start by directing research funding towards finding cures for diseases
that affect women in a unique way.

Second, use super intelligence to enhance our understanding of female
biology and speed up medical discoveries- all to the benefit of women.

Third, accelerate the process of getting promising therapies from
laboratories to female patients, while still keeping strict safety
standards.

Fourth, ensure that medical breakthroughs are accessible and affordable
to women, regardless of their financial circumstances.

And fifth, replace generations of silence with open conversation,
education, and compassion.

Our mission must serve women at every stage of life, from young girls
just starting out to mothers dealing with a complex range of
responsibilities, to mature women who deserve to have vitality, dignity,
and independence.

In 2026, medical advances can do more than just prolong life. It should
give women the freedom to live life to its fullest- to pursue our
dreams, experience love, discover, create, travel, and lead.

Today, we establish a new ambition: transforming hardship and pain into
scientific discovery, effective treatments, and lasting cures.

We Are Women. We are resilient, capable, and deserving of lives defined
not by the burdens we carry, but by the possibilities before us.  

Our health should never limit the fullness of our lives.

I would like to thank the President for his support and for signing this
very important Executive Order.  

Thank you.
