title: Presidential Message on the Birthday of the United States Navy
---
 
For over 250 years, American sailors have defended our Nation upon the
seas, forging the most lethal Navy in the history of the world.  Today,
on the birthday of the United States Navy, Icelebrate this incredible
milestone with our active duty and reserve sailors, past and present,
whose unparalleled expertise and extraordinary devotion to duty in times
of war and peace have carried our Navy to these unrivaled heights.   

On October 13, 1775, our Navy was formally established when the Second
Continental Congress authorized a pair of armed vessels to sail against
the mighty British Empire, intercept enemy supply lines, and defend the
cause of American liberty at sea during the Revolutionary War.  Since
its humble beginnings, the Navy has grown into the most formidable
seafaring force the world has ever known. 

Today’s sailors are part of a storied legacy of maritime supremacy that
has safeguarded our sovereignty in every major conflict since.  Our
warships are unmistakable symbols of American military might, but the
most sophisticated, state-of-the-art fleet is nothing without the elite
men and women who carry out every mission at home and abroad with
unwavering resolve and professionalism.  In home ports and on carrier
decks, submarine patrols, and distant oceans, our sailor stand watch
around the clock, deterring aggression, keeping the world’s great sea
lanes open and free, and carrying American strength to every corner of
the globe.  Their skill and sacrifice keep our Republic safe and secure
in the blessings of liberty, earning the everlasting gratitude of the
American People.

As President, I am rebuilding our incredible Navy and restoring the
mighty American shipbuilding industry that sustains it, ensuring our
fleet is prepared to meet every challenge of our age.  Since returning
to office, my Administration has secured over $150 billion in
investments to usher in the Golden Age of American shipbuilding.  In
August, I was proud to sign a National Security Presidential Memorandum
that directs the Department of War to pursue more direct investments in
the American shipbuilding industrial base, expanding our capacity to
build warships and creating good-paying jobs for American workers.  The
Memorandum further directs the Secretary of War to establish a fifth
Naval shipyard, the first in more than eight decades, to increase our
Nation’s submarine and aircraft carrier repair capacity.  Just last
week we saw the fruit of those actions as construction began on
the largest new shipyard in the United States since World War II, which
will build the next generation of American submarines. Under my
leadership, our Navy will be bigger, better, and stronger than ever
before.

Since the dawn of our glorious Republic, generation after generation of
valiant sailors have answered the Navy’s stern call for “faith, courage,
service true, with honor over all.”  Today, the First Lady joins me in
saluting over two and a half centuries of American dominance upon the
seas and honor the heroes who secure the freedom and safety of the
American People.
