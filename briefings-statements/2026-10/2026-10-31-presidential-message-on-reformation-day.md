title: Presidential Message on Reformation Day
---
 
From the earliest days of our Republic, prayer and faith in Almighty God
have stood as the foundation of our strength, the source of our unity,
and the fount of our greatness.  This Reformation Day, I send my best
wishes to the countless Christians across our country commemorating the
Protestant Reformation and its enduring message of salvation by God’s
grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

Five hundred years ago, William Tyndale—inspired by the work of Martin
Luther—advanced the Protestant Reformation’s devotion to Holy Scripture
through the publication of the first New Testament printed in English,
shaping the language of the Bibles that would fortify the faith and
resolve of America’s founding generation.  The Sacred Scriptures helped
form the religious convictions that formed our young Nation’s founding
ideals of God-given rights and individual liberty, a spiritual
inheritance we honor as we continue to reflect on 250 glorious years of
American Independence.  The Bible remains a cornerstone of our Nation’s
character and upholds our steadfast devotion to life, liberty, and the
pursuit of happiness.

Guided by an unwavering faith in Jesus Christ, my Administration is
working to restore the moral and religious foundations that have
sustained our Republic for generations.  As President, I will always
support citizens of faith and defend their place in the public square,
honoring the enduring role of our churches in strengthening families,
serving communities, and preserving the core values that have anchored
us across the centuries.

As Christians across our country unite in faith and fellowship this
Reformation Day, I send my heartfelt prayers for a meaningful
celebration and encourage each of you to draw renewed strength from
God’s Word as we carry forward the blessings of faith and freedom
throughout our beloved Nation.
