title: Presidential Message on Child Health Day
---
 
This Child Health Day, we renew our commitment to ensure the health and
well-being of America’s children – our Nation’s future – so that they
may live full, lasting, and happy lives free from the burden of health
concerns.

As our children grow, we must protect their health and give them every
opportunity to thrive.  American children now face chronic health
conditions and serious medical issues at rates far higher than in
previous generations.  With little explanation, we have witnessed sharp
rises in the prevalence of obesity, diabetes, severe allergies, autism,
autoimmune diseases, and mental health challenges such as anxiety and
depression in our Nation’s youth.  As President, I have a solemn duty to
safeguard the health of the coming generation, and I will not rest until
these conditions are understood and defeated and every child in America
can grow up strong and whole.

For this reason, my Administration is confronting the chronic health
crisis plaguing our Nation’s most vulnerable citizens by reversing the
failed policies that have fueled America’s childhood disease epidemic
and ensuring parents are informed with the Gold Standard Science to make
the decisions for their children.  Through the Make Our Children Healthy
Again Strategy—an initiative launched by the Make America Healthy Again
Commission—we have taken over 120 specific actions to halt the rise in
childhood chronic diseases and discover the root causes of serious
medical conditions by improving preventative care, getting artificial
dyes out of our food, accelerating breakthroughs and cures, and
encouraging physical activity and limited screen time for children.  

While more work remains to fully reverse these concerning trends, in
just the first year of my Administration, we experienced the lowest
death rate in recorded in our country’s history. Our Nation’s future
relies upon the health and leadership of the next generation, which is
why I remain committed to ensuring that Americans and their children
receive the best medical advice to keep them healthy and the best care
in the world if they do get sick.  Under my leadership, we are
delivering a healthcare system that restores trust and truly puts the
health of children and families first, and together, we will make
American children healthy again for generations to come.
