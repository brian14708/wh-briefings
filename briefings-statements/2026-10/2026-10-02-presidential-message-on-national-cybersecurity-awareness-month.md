title: Presidential Message on National Cybersecurity Awareness Month
---
 
In an age of rapid digital advancement, cyberspace has emerged as the
new frontier of American innovation, prosperity, and strength.  As new
technologies open doors to discovery and opportunity that were once
unimaginable, a secure cyberspace is essential to ensuring that America
continues to lead the world in finance, military power, emerging
technology, and manufacturing.  During National Cybersecurity Awareness
Month, my Administration reaffirms our dedication to bolstering our
Nation’s cybersecurity, protecting the American People, and unleashing
the Golden Age of Cyberspace. 

Every American relies on the digital world for information, economic
opportunity, and the simple routines of daily life.  Freedom and
security in cyberspace, however, can never be taken for granted.  For
too long, adversaries and cybercriminals exploited our connected way of
life to target our citizens, communities, and businesses without
consequence.  My Administration has put an end to this era of weakness.

As President, I signed an Executive Order to combat cybercrime, fraud,
and predatory schemes and stop foreign criminal networks from preying on
vulnerable Americans.  In March, I released my Cyber Strategy for
America—a bold vision for the future of American cybersecurity and
innovation.  My Administration is already putting this strategy into
action through the creation of “GOLD EAGLE,” a partnership between the
Federal Government and leading American companies to harness the power
of American Super Intelligence to identify cybersecurity vulnerabilities
at rapid speed and stay ahead of our adversaries, defend the American
People from emerging threats, and safeguard the critical infrastructure
vital to our national security and global competitiveness. 

As we secure America’s digital future, we are also preparing the next
generation to lead it.  Through First Lady Melania Trump’s leadership on
the Presidential Super Intelligence Challenge, we have inspired young
Americans to embrace emerging technologies, develop new skills, and find
solutions to challenges in their own communities.  The First Lady’s
Fostering the Future Together initiative also continues to empower
students, parents, and educators to learn, grow, and thrive through the
safe and innovative use of advanced technology. This month, I encourage
every American to do their own part to stay safe online by creating
strong passwords, keeping software up to date, and remaining vigilant
against scams.  Together, we can strengthen our Nation’s cybersecurity,
safeguard the technologies that power our daily lives, and secure
America’s place as the leader in the world’s digital age.
