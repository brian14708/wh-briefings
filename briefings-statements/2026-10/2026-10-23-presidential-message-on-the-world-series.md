title: Presidential Message on the World Series
---
 
This week, Americans across our great country are coming together to
celebrate the 2026 World Series, the crown jewel of our Nation’s
favorite pastime.  Baseball has long held a cherished place in our
culture, and now, the Fall Classic once again unites us around this
storied game.

For nearly two centuries, baseball has shaped the character of American
communities, from neighborhood sandlots and local ballparks to the
bright stadium lights of the Major Leagues.  The game has carried our
country through times of hardship and times of triumph, lifting the
spirits of our Republic and instilling the enduring values of
excellence, dedication, and sportsmanship in generations of Americans.

This October, the finest athletes in baseball will take the field in
pursuit of the legendary Commissioner’s Trophy.  Melania and I send our
best wishes to the millions of fans across America for a memorable World
Series and to the Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers as they
compete to earn a lasting place in the history of our Nation’s pastime. 
Play ball!
