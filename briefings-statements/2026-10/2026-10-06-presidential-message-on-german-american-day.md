title: Presidential Message on German American Day
---
 
The German American story began on October 6, 1683, when 13 families
from Krefeld, after braving the Atlantic in search of opportunity,
landed on our shores and soon founded Germantown, Pennsylvania—the first
permanent German settlement in America.

In the centuries since, German Americans have farmed the fields of our
heartland and powered the industries that turned our young country into
the mightiest economic powerhouse in the world.  Their sons and
daughters have defended American freedom in every era, serving valiantly
in both World Wars and standing watch through the long struggle against
communism during the Cold War.  In our own time, their descendants
continue to serve in our Armed Forces, work the factories and family
farms that sustain our Nation, and lead the businesses and communities
that keep the American Dream alive for all of our citizens.

This spirit endures in the long-standing relationship between the United
States and Germany—a relationship sustained by our mutual respect, and a
shared desire for peace and prosperity.  As President, I have led a
historic renewal of the NATO alliance, securing a historic commitment
from our NATO allies to increase their investment in our common
defense.  Since that historic summit in The Hague last year, Germany has
taken bold steps toward the realization of a stronger, more balanced
NATO.  The impact of this commitment will be felt beyond Europe, as
billions of dollars in new investments strengthen the transatlantic
defense industrial base and create thousands of high-paying jobs for
American workers.

Today, we give thanks for the generations of German Americans whose
labor and love of liberty helped make the United States the greatest
Nation on Earth.  Their story is woven into the fabric of America, and
together, we commit ourselves to building on this legacy to create an
even brighter, more prosperous future for all Americans.
