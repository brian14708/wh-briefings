title: Presidential Message on Down Syndrome Awareness Month
---
 
Every child is a gift from God, created in His image and likeness,
bringing hope, love, and a future filled with promise to our Nation and
the world.  This Down Syndrome Awareness Month, I join millions of
Americans in celebrating the lives, accomplishments, and contributions
of people with Down syndrome and honor the families and communities who
stand beside them each and every day.

Americans with Down syndrome enrich every life they touch, meeting the
world with a sincerity and joy that make our classrooms brighter and our
communities stronger.  Their condition arises from a single extra
chromosome, a small difference that can slow a child’s development and
bring challenges to families who love and care for them.  In recent
decades, our Nation’s doctors and researchers have answered these
challenges with remarkable scientific discoveries, and in so doing have
greatly enhanced the quality of life for children and adults living with
Down syndrome. 

As President, I have taken action to protect every innocent life and
empower young Americans, including those living with Down syndrome, to
lead long, productive, and fulfilling lives.  My Administration is
leading the charge, advancing the research that delivers new treatments
and opening every door for these individuals to pursue their dreams and
thrive in their communities.  The families, friends, and caregivers who
pour their hearts into looking after children with developmental
disabilities are some of the most extraordinary, caring, and generous
Americans—ensuring the more than 5,000 babies born with Down syndrome
every year can live their American Dream just like every other citizen.

My Administration will never waver in our devotion to the men, women,
and children living with Down syndrome, and I will always fight to
ensure that they, like every other American, can be proud to live in a
country with a government and a society that upholds the dignity of
every precious life and recognizes the beauty and humanity of every
person.  This month, and every month, we celebrate, love, and cherish
the incredible Americans living with Down syndrome.
