  3620, H.R. 4307, H.R. 4467, H.R. 6380, and H.R. 7831 Signed into Law
---
 
On Tuesday, October 20, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 227, the “Clergy Act,” which allows a 2-year period in which
ministers of a church, members of a religious order, and Christian
Science practitioners may submit an application to the Internal Revenue
Service to revoke their exemption from Social Security coverage;

H.R. 837, which requires the Secretary of Agriculture to convey the
Pleasant Valley Ranger District Administrative Site, located on the
Tonto National Forest, to Gila County, Arizona;

H.R. 1721, the “Critical Infrastructure Manufacturing Feasibility Act,”
which requires the Department of Commerce to conduct a study on the
costs, benefits, and feasibility of manufacturing any high-demand
product in the United States that is being imported due to a
manufacturing, material, or supply chain constraint; 

H.R. 2388, the “Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe Project Lands Restoration
Act,” which takes certain land in the State of Washington into trust for
the benefit of the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe;

H.R. 2481, the “Romance Scam Prevention Act,” which requires online
dating service providers to provide to a member of the online dating
service a fraud ban notification if the member has received a message
through the service from a member who was banned due to fraudulent
activity;

H.R. 3620, the “Southcentral Foundation Land Transfer Act of 2025,”
which requires the warranty deed conveyance of land in Anchorage,
Alaska, to the Southcentral Foundation;

H.R. 4307, the “Enhancing Detection of Human Trafficking Act,” which
requires the Secretary of Labor to provide training to certain employees
of the Department of Labor on how to detect and assist law enforcement
in preventing human trafficking during the course of their official
duties;

H.R. 4467, the “Vicksburg National Military Park Boundary Modification
Act,” which directs the Secretary of the Interior to convey
approximately seven acres of Federal land, located within the boundaries
of the Vicksburg National Military Park, to the State of Mississippi for
construction of a new interpretive center;

H.R. 6380, the “Chiricahua National Park Act,” which redesignates the
Chiricahua National Monument, located in southeastern Arizona, as the
Chiricahua National Park;

H.R. 7831, the “License to Drill Act,” which extends the authority of
the Department of the Interior to collect certain oil and gas permit
application processing fees.
