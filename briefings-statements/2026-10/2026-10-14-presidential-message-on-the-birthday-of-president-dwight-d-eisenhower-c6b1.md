title: Presidential Message on the Birthday of President Dwight D. Eisenhower
---
 
On this day, we mark the 136th birthday of President Dwight David
Eisenhower—a visionary leader, respected warrior, distinguished
statesman, and noble public servant.  In command of great armies and in
the White House, he met every trial with steadiness, courage, and
resolve, earning the enduring trust of the American People and the
lasting gratitude of the free world.

Born in Denison, Texas, and raised in Abilene, Kansas, President
Eisenhower began his legendary life of service to our great Nation as a
graduate of West Point.  His stellar military career spanned nearly four
decades and included appointments such as the Supreme Commander of the
Allied Expeditionary Force during World War II, during which he
orchestrated the historic D-Day Invasion of Normandy, and as NATO’s
first Supreme Allied Commander Europe.  In his first bid for the
Presidency in 1952, the celebrated general and political outsider was
elected in a landslide victory.  After four years, Americans still liked
“Ike” and handed him another decisive win in the 1956 election.

Eisenhower’s tenure in the White House was marked by peace, growth,
economic prosperity, and a remarkable record of foreign and domestic
accomplishments.  He negotiated an armistice in the Korean War, signed
the Civil Rights Act of 1957, created the National Aeronautics and Space
Administration and the Small Business Administration, and championed the
Interstate Highway System, which connected communities across our
country and boosted American commerce.  Eisenhower’s commitment to
military might and the preservation of our birthright of freedom is
reflected in the cautionary words from his 1953 Inaugural Address:
 “History does not long entrust the care of freedom to the weak or the
timid.”

My Administration is proudly advancing the ideals that shaped
Eisenhower’s Presidency, including economic prosperity, a strong
national defense, secure borders, an unwavering commitment to peace
through strength, and the tireless pursuit of American exceptionalism.
 Like President Eisenhower, I believe lasting peace is secured only
through unquestioned American strength, and my Administration is
rebuilding our military into the most powerful fighting force on Earth.
 We are upholding the law and order he championed, making our streets
and communities safe again, igniting an economic boom unlike anything
our country has ever seen, and renewing the strength and spirit of
American life at every turn.  More than seven decades later,
Eisenhower’s example continues to guide our Nation as we restore the
peace, prosperity, and confidence that defined his era and usher in a
new Golden Age for the American People.

As we commemorate the birthday of our 34th Commander in Chief, we honor
the extraordinary life and legacy of this patriot, hero, and American
icon, whose devotion to duty and country will inspire Americans for
generations to come.

Happy Birthday, President Eisenhower!
