title: Presidential Message on National Breast Cancer Awareness Month
---
 
During National Breast Cancer Awareness Month, we celebrate the
resilience and courage of our Nation’s four million breast cancer
survivors, extend our unwavering support to those currently in the
fight, and honor the memory of beloved family members and friends lost
to this terrible disease. 

In an instant, a breast cancer diagnosis forever changes the life of the
patient and the lives of those around them.  This year in the United
States, more than 380,000 women will receive this devastating news, and
approximately 40,000 souls will be lost to breast cancer.  Thanks to
medical breakthroughs, increased public awareness, and early detection,
survival rates have drastically improved—but these numbers represent
shattered lives and families, and that is unacceptable.

Through our historic mission to Make America Healthy Again, my
Administration is committed to conquering chronic illness and advancing
cutting-edge research, treatment options, and innovative therapies that
will enhance the quality of life for men and women battling breast
cancer and other diseases.  Under my leadership, we will never stop
working to deliver the breakthroughs, treatments, and cures that
American patients and families deserve.

Breast cancer has caused immeasurable pain, fear, and uncertainty on too
many American families, and throughout this month, we stand in
compassionate solidarity with all cancer warriors.  The First Lady and I
encourage every person to know their family medical history and risk
factors and to become a fierce advocate for their own health.  May God
comfort those who mourn, give strength to those suffering, and offer
grace to the caregivers as we anticipate, with hope and confidence, the
day this horrific disease is defeated once and for all.
