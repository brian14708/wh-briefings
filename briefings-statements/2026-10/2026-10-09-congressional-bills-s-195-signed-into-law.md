title: Congressional Bills S. 195 Signed into Law
---
 
On Friday, October 9, 2026, the President signed into law:

S. 195, the “American Music Tourism Act of 2025,” which requires the
Department of Commerce to promote domestic and international travel and
tourism to locations and events in the United States that are important
to music tourism.
