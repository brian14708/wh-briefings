title: Presidential Message on National Forest Products Week
---
 
From the misty evergreens of the Pacific Northwest to the hardwood
ridges of Appalachia, America’s forests are among our Nation’s greatest
natural treasures.  During National Forest Products Week, we renew our
commitment to conserving these incredible landscapes and responsibly
harnessing their abundance to sustain households, strengthen American
jobs and industry, and secure lasting prosperity for generations to
come.

The woodlands of America helped build our Nation from the ground up,
giving generations of citizens the resources to raise homes, establish
businesses, and pursue their dreams.  That proud tradition continues
today in our logging, forestry, manufacturing, and construction
industries, where the bounty of our land meets the skill and resolve of
our people in communities across our country. 

As President, I am committed to supporting these vital industries and
continuing to drive economic growth.  Last year, I was proud to sign two
Executive Orders to increase domestic timber supply and reduce reliance
on imports from foreign markets. As a result, over half a billion
dollars in new domestic industry investments were announced and the U.S.
Department of Agriculture’s Forest Service has sold over three billion
board feet of American timber this year, the highest volume sold in
almost three decades. Under my leadership, the USDA Forest Service has
also championed sustainable forests, achieving 116 percent of our annual
reforestation goal, the highest level in twenty-six years.

We are cutting red tape and rolling back unnecessary environmental
regulations on industries such as logging and paper manufacturing,
unleashing the full productive power of American workers and natural
resources.  I also established the Make America Beautiful Again
Commission, which brings together members of my Administration to lead
active forest management and historic conservation efforts that will
preserve the magnificent abundance of our wildlands for the Americans
who will inherit them in the centuries ahead.

This week, we reflect on God’s gift of our beautiful and immense natural
landscape, and we refocus our efforts to responsibly and boldly utilize
these resources to grow our Republic, strengthen our economy, and
empower American families with the tools and opportunities they need to
build our Nation’s future.
