title: Congressional Bills H.R. 5345 and S. 766 Signed into Law
---
 
On Wednesday, October 7, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 5345, the “Improving Social Security’s Service to Victims of
Identity Theft Act,” which establishes a single point of contact at the
Social Security Administration for individuals whose Social Security
Number has been misused or whose Social Security card has been lost; and

S. 766, the “Billion Dollar Boondoggle Act of 2025,” which requires an
annual report on federally-funded projects that are more than 5 years
behind schedule or more than $1 billion over the original cost estimate.
