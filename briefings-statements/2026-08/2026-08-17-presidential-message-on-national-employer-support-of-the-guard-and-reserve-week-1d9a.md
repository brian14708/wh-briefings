title: Presidential Message on National Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve
  Week
---
 
The citizen warriors of the National Guard and Reserve carry forward a
tradition as old as our Republic itself, building careers in the
communities they serve while answering our Nation’s call in its hour of
need.  Their service strengthens our communities, powers our economy,
and keeps our military second to none.  During National Employer Support
of the Guard and Reserve Week, we honor our heroes in uniform and the
patriotic employers who stand behind them—a partnership that sustains
our all-volunteer force and secures the cause of American freedom.

National Guardsmen and Reservists across our great country are fellow
Americans who teach our children, raise our buildings, grow our food,
care for our sick, and strengthen our economy.  When their country
summons them, they answer without hesitation by deploying overseas as
part of our Armed Forces or responding here at home to hurricanes,
floods, wildfires, and other crises with fearless resolve.

Since returning to office, I have directed the National Guard to restore
law and order, secure our borders, and protect our communities.  I have
mobilized National Guardsmen to some of America’s most dangerous cities,
where they stand constant vigil over transit hubs, public spaces, and
federal facilities, freeing local authorities to pursue the violent
criminals who have terrorized law-abiding citizens for too long.  Thanks
to their efforts, in 2025 violent crime fell at a pace unmatched in
modern American history, the property crime dropped by over 12% from the
year before – the second largest in history, and our Nation is on track
for its lowest homicide rate in more than a century this year after
tying the record in 2025. In the first half of this year alone, murders
across our major cities are falling 18 percent, and carjackings are
dropping by nearly half.  Business owners who once closed their shops at
sundown now stay open into the evening, employers can staff late-night
shifts they struggled to fill a year ago, and parents can let their
children live in their communities without fear of tragedy.  In town
centers and neighborhoods written off for decades, Americans are opening
more businesses, buying more homes, and putting down roots once more,
thanks to the heroic actions of our National Guardsmen. 

None of the National Guard and Reserve’s tremendous accomplishments
would be possible without America’s employers, who keep faith with their
workforce wherever duty calls.  From family-owned shops on Main Street
to the largest corporations in our country, these employers hold
positions open, keep benefits in place, and welcome their employees home
after every mission.  They give National Guardsmen and Reservists the
flexibility and support they need to train, deploy, and fulfill their
military duties without sacrificing the careers they have worked hard to
build.  In return, they gain some of America’s best workers, whose
leadership, discipline, and skills are strengthened through their
service.

This week, the First Lady and I salute every National Guardsman and
Reservist, the military spouses and families whose sacrifice at home
makes every mission possible, and the employers who stand behind them. 
Together, we will keep our communities thriving and our Armed Forces the
strongest and most respected the world has ever known.
