title: Presidential Message on the Birthday of the United States Coast Guard
---
 
Today, our Nation celebrates the 236th birthday of the United States
Coast Guard, a force of American law enforcement professionals and Coast
Guardsmen who stand constant watch over our shores, our waters, and our
glorious way of life.  From the earliest days of our Republic, Coast
Guardsmen have answered every call with the vigilance and grit that
define the very best of the American Spirit. 

Ever since its founding under Alexander Hamilton in 1790, the Coast
Guard has been indispensable to the security and prosperity of our
Nation, serving as America’s maritime first responders and the living
standard-bearers of the Long Blue Line, the oldest unbroken tradition of
American valor upon the sea.  These crews are defending our maritime
border against the narco-terrorist cartels and smugglers who traffic in
drugs and human beings.  Every day, they are bravely turning back the
illegal invasion at sea and seizing hundreds of billions of dollars in
deadly narcotics before this poison can ever reach American shores. 
They are the faithful guardians of our ports, harbors, and shipping
lanes through which the lifeblood of our economy flows, and they are the
first to act when an American citizen is in peril far from shore. 
Through gale-force winds and freezing waves, through storms of shrapnel
and ash, there is nothing these tenacious warriors will not face in
defense of our homeland. 

Coast Guardsmen of this caliber have earned the lasting support of the
Nation they protect, and my Administration is meeting that obligation by
reclaiming the maritime dominance upon which American liberty has always
depended.  Last year, I signed The Working Families Tax Cuts Act,
delivering a record $25 billion investment to rebuild the Coast Guard’s
fleet and restore the military and law enforcement strength that was
allowed to decay under my predecessor.  This historic investment is
putting 21 new cutters and 40 new search and rescue helicopters into
service alongside 17 state-of-the-art icebreakers, giving our Coast
Guardsmen unequaled reach to patrol our coasts from the Caribbean to the
Arctic. 

This past December, I was proud to issue every one of these selfless
patriots a $2,000 Devotion to Duty payment in recognition of an
extraordinary year of service—a fitting expression of the gratitude our
Republic owes to those who dedicate their lives to our security.  Thanks
to my Administration’s policies, recruiting numbers have climbed to
their highest levels in a generation, and the Coast Guard is once again
operating with a confidence and spirit unmatched in its long and
glorious history.  As Commander in Chief, I pledge to the men and women
of the Coast Guard that I will fight for them at every turn and that, in
this new Golden Age for America, they will sail farther, soar higher,
and strike harder than ever before.

On this day, we honor the incredible legacy and unique mission of the
United States Coast Guard and pay tribute to every Coast Guardsman who
has ever served our Nation as well as those who remain “Always Ready”
this very hour at home and abroad.  Their courage remains the standard
by which American resolve is measured, and so long as they guard our
waters, they will forever keep safe the freedom, the prosperity, and the
security that are the birthright of every American.

*Semper Paratus*!
