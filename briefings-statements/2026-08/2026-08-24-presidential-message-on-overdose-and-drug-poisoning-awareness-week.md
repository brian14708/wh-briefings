title: Presidential Message on Overdose and Drug Poisoning Awareness Week
---
 
During Overdose and Drug Poisoning Awareness Week, our Nation renews its
resolve to defeat the scourge of illicit drugs and synthetic poisons
that have devastated hundreds of thousands of American lives.  My
Administration is relentlessly confronting this threat at every source,
and we will always put our national security and the safety, health, and
well-being of the American People first.

When I returned to office, I vowed to stop the flow of fentanyl and
other deadly drugs into our country, and my Administration immediately
took action.  We created the Homeland Security Task Force to bring
together all federal, state and local partners to achieve the national
policy goal of eliminating the cartel presence in America once and for
all.  By doing so, my Administration secured our southern border and
began dismantling fentanyl supply chains at every link, from the
narco-terrorist cartels and gangs that traffic these deadly drugs to the
foreign suppliers that enable them.  I proudly signed the HALT Fentanyl
Act, permanently classifying fentanyl-related substances as Schedule I
drugs, ensuring dealers and traffickers will receive the maximum force
of the law.  I designated fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction and
the worst cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, unleashing our
national security resources against this attack on American soil.  I
have also directed military forces to conduct strikes on dozens of
narcotrafficking boats to disrupt the flow of maritime supply routes.
 As a result, fentanyl deaths dropped 22 percent in a single year, total
drug overdose deaths fell 14 percent, and thousands of American lives
have been saved.

The strength of our country rests upon the health of its people, and my
Administration is restoring hope in every community touched by
addiction.  Earlier this year, I launched the Great American Recovery
Initiative, uniting government, healthcare, faith communities, and the
private sector behind one mission of prevention, treatment, and
recovery.  This program is making proven treatments more accessible,
supporting Americans and families on the road to recovery, and giving
every person the opportunity to build a healthy and hopeful future.

This week, we remember every American lost to overdose and drug
poisoning, stand with the families who carry their memory, and support
all those working to overcome addiction.  Above all, we vow to end the
devastation wrought by deadly drugs and build a stronger, healthier, and
safer America.
