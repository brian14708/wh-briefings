title: Presidential Message on National Native American Heritage Month
---
 
Alaska Natives and Native Americans are inextricably linked to the
history of our Republic and are integral to our glorious future.  During
National Native American Heritage Month, we honor the sacrifices and the
innumerable contributions of tribal communities to the prosperity and
success of our great Nation. 

Native Americans impact communities nationwide with innovation and
achievement in every area of life and culture, including business,
healthcare, law enforcement, and the highest levels of Government.  They
bravely answer the call to safeguard our liberty—at five times the
national average—and have served with distinction in every major
conflict since the Revolutionary War. This month, we pay special tribute
to the Indigenous men and women who have proudly worn the uniform, past
and present, in every branch of our Armed Forces.

As President, I support the empowerment of all Tribes through
self-governance, self-determination, and a strong partnership with the
Federal Government to foster economic development and address the most
pressing challenges for our Native Americans.  Last year, I returned 680
acres of federal land to the Spirit Lake Nation of North Dakota—a
long-overdue restoration—now theirs to build on, farm on, and pass down
to future generations.  I also directed more than $1.1 billion in Indian
Housing Block Grants to hundreds of Tribes, Alaska Native Villages, and
Tribally Designated Housing Entities for safe and stable homes in Native
communities nationwide.  In January, the Bureau of Indian Education
reached its highest graduation rate in its history, reflecting sustained
reforms, accountability measures, and quality instruction to bolster
student achievement.  My Administration will continue to prioritize
educational options, training, and meaningful pathways to the workforce
of tomorrow so that Native students and communities can enjoy a bright
and successful future.  

As our Nation marks National Native American Heritage Month, we
celebrate the vibrant traditions and rich cultural heritage of Tribal
peoples.  We uphold the shared values of family, faith, and freedom, and
we will never stop fighting to ensure every Native American has the
opportunity to pursue the hope and promise of the American Dream.
