***Historic Donation Creates Permanent Scholarships for LSU Students
From Foster Care***

First Lady Melania Trump and Louisiana State University (LSU) have
secured $7.2 Million to establish a permanent endowment fund supporting
FOSTERING THE FUTURE scholarships for LSU students who experienced
foster care.

“A great education does more than prepare someone for work life.  It
gives something no one can ever take away.  There is nothing more
powerful we can give a young person than a world-class education,” said
First Lady Melania Trump.  

The $7.2 Million endowment advances First Lady Melania Trump’s FOSTERING
THE FUTURE initiative which provides individuals from foster care with
academic scholarships to prepare them to secure an entry-level job and
become financially independent.

“As adults, we have a moral obligation to our children, especially those
in foster care.  But, as you know, we must act to truly make a
difference.  The foster care community doesn’t need words,” Mrs. Trump
highlighted the importance of the LSU endowment.    

MMR Group committed $5 million to First Lady Melania Trump’s FOSTERING
THE FUTURE, with contributions from additional LSU Foundation donors.

“Access is a top priority for LSU, because circumstances should not
determine whether talent and ambition thrive.  MMR Group’s visionary
scholarship funding for First Lady Melania Trump’s FOSTERING THE FUTURE
is transformative for youth from foster care who aspire to earn an LSU
degree,” said LSU President Wade Rousse.

To celebrate the initiative, First Lady Melania Trump visited Baton
Rouge for the LSU-Alabama NCAA football game, where FOSTERING THE FUTURE
was prominently showcased across the stadium.

The high-visibility marketing campaign featured branded BE BEST
FOSTERING THE FUTURE patches on LSU players’ jerseys, helmet decals,
cheerleader uniforms during the pre-game announcement event, mascot
attire, commemorative fan towels, and a limited-edition ceremonial
football.  SEC officials also agreed to use a First Lady Melania Trump
Challenge Coin for the game’s official coin toss.

The University of Alabama is also a proud Fostering the Future partner.
 “Today, we have fourteen scholars at LSU and nine at Alabama.  Across
the country, 26 universities are supporting 100 Fostering the Future
scholars this semester,” said Mrs. Trump.

In closing, First Lady Melania Trump spoke directly to America’s
children and youth with experience in foster care, “To the Fostering the
Future scholars here today, and throughout America: I see you. I believe
in you. And I will keep working to create new opportunities with you.”

First Lady Melania Trump’s full remarks, as prepared for delivery, are
provided below.

**I see you. I believe in you.**

At some point in a young person’s life, the world can feel like it’s
moving too fast.  When things are uncertain, it’s hard to know which way
to go, and the future can seem out of reach.

For children navigating foster care, that feeling of drifting can feel
even stronger.

Today, Louisiana State University is launching a Seven Million Two
Hundred Thousand Dollar FOSTERING THE FUTURE scholarship endowment fund.

This achievement will give young people from foster care an anchor of
stability, a strong educational foundation, and a chance to build a
wonderful life.

Thank you, LSU, for giving the foster care community this advantage.
 After meeting LSU’s leadership and this remarkable community, I am not
surprised by your achievement.  You have united by giving young people
with few resources the tools to chart their own futures.

MMR and the generous donors who made this seven million
two-hundred-thousand-dollar milestone possible, know that investing in
young people will create opportunities for Baton Rouge for many
generations.  This is very good for America.

LSU defines excellence.  Your high standards start in the classroom,
amplifies on the football field, and shapes America’s future leaders.

Thank you for welcoming me to Baton Rouge for one of this year’s
greatest college football games: LSU versus Alabama.  Both universities
are part of FOSTERING THE FUTURE.

While most eyes will be on the athletes today, I encourage you to
recognize LSU’s Fostering the Future scholars, who are just as
remarkable.  Today, we have 14 scholars at LSU and nine at Alabama.
 Across the country, 26 universities are supporting 100 Fostering the
Future scholars this semester.

When I started FOSTERING THE FUTURE in 2021, my vision was clear: every
young person from foster care should have the chance to build an
extraordinary life. Education is the center of that plan.

A great education does more than prepare someone for work life.  It
gives something no one can ever take away.  There is nothing more
powerful we can give a young person than a world-class education.

As adults, we have a moral obligation to our children, especially those
in foster care.  But, as you know, we must act to truly make a
difference.  The foster care community doesn’t need words.

To the FOSTERING THE FUTURE scholars here today, and throughout America:
I see you. I believe in you. And I will keep working to create new
opportunities with you.  Explore the programs, scholarships, and
resources we have created, and make the most of them.

You can control your own destiny now.

**FOSTERING THE FUTURE**  
FOSTERING THE FUTURE, a BE BEST initiative established by First Lady
Melania Trump in 2021, secures scholarship opportunities at 26
prestigious colleges and universities across the nation for young people
with lived experience in foster care.

Through Mrs. Trump’s leadership, FOSTERING THE FUTURE has advanced
landmark federal reforms to strengthen the child welfare system,
expanded educational and housing opportunities for youth transitioning
out of foster care, and secured $30 million for the Melania Trump Foster
Youth to Independence program. Her FOSTERING THE FUTURE Executive Order
advances greater access to education, employment, housing, and financial
independence. She also launched FOSTERING THE FUTURE Accounts, America’s
first savings and investment initiative dedicated to building long-term
financial security for youth in foster care, with 26 governors already
pledging to establish accounts for children in their states.

**Louisiana State University**  
Rooted in Louisiana and reaching across the world, Louisiana State
University educates and prepares future leaders, drives transformative
research and innovation, and translates knowledge into solutions that
improve health, strengthen communities and expand human potential.
