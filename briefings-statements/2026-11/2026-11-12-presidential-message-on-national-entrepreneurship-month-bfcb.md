title: Presidential Message on National Entrepreneurship Month
---
 
The entrepreneurial spirit of America has been forged by the
determination of generations who envisioned a Nation where every
hardworking citizen can live their glorious American Dream.  For over
250 years, entrepreneurs have created the strong foundation that our
country has built upon to become the economic powerhouse that we are
today.  This National Entrepreneurship Month, we recognize the patriotic
innovators whose drive and vision have created the strongest business
economy the world has ever seen.

Entrepreneurs are the heart of our Nation and represent a way of life
that has kept America competitive, growing, and at the forefront of
every serious conversation about our economy.  In my first term, we
built the strongest economy in the history of the world, and now we are
doing it again.  Through The Working Families Tax Cuts Act, my
Administration made the Small Business Tax Deduction permanent, secured
No Taxes on Tips and Overtime, and delivered historic tax cuts to
hardworking families across our country.

I have also launched the most aggressive deregulatory agenda in American
history.  In my first weeks back in office, I signed the Unleashing
Prosperity Through Deregulation Executive Order, directing federal
agencies to repeal ten old regulations for every new one issued. My
Administration has far surpassed that goal, cutting over 1,500
regulations and saving Americans over $1.5 trillion. These actions free
small businesses from the costly red tape that for too long has stifled
American entrepreneurs.  This was followed by the Reducing
Anti-Competitive Regulatory Barriers Executive Order, which roots out
the obstacles that smother competition, innovation, and the formation of
new businesses.  Under my leadership, American entrepreneurs will have
the freedom to compete, grow, and lead the world once again.

As President, I remain committed to keeping America the greatest place
in the world to start and grow a business.  My Administration will
continue reducing regulatory burdens, combating unfair foreign trade
practices, and ensuring entrepreneurs have the freedom to make decisions
that will enhance their products and services.

This National Entrepreneurship Month, we reaffirm our commitment to the
entrepreneurs and small business owners who keep our communities
thriving with quality job opportunities and economic activity.  When we
support these brilliant innovators, America is stronger, our communities
excel, and our ability to dream big and turn those visions into reality
is unparalleled.
