*Declaring precision and mental strength the foundation of global
military superiority*

As families across America prepare to celebrate Thanksgiving, First Lady
Melania Trump addressed members of America’s armed forces at Joint Base
Andrews, “The US military is the most powerful military in the world
because of its people.  Our warriors train relentlessly, pushing the
boundaries of physical and mental strength.”  Mrs. Trump analyzed,
“Perfect precision is not an accident.  It is the result of repetition,
measured pacing, and flawless execution.”

First Lady Melania Trump has long been an ardent supporter of America’s
military.  Her unwavering commitment is reflected in her visit to an
active combat zone in Iraq in 2018, her presence with grieving families
of fallen American soldiers at Dover Air Force Base in March of 2026,
and her presentation of a Purple Heart to a combat-wounded service
member in 2019.  Throughout her years of service as First Lady, Mrs.
Trump has consistently championed military families, recognizing those
who have been wounded or have made the ultimate sacrifice in defense of
America.

Mrs. Trump brought a deeply personal dimension to America’s military as
the enduring traditions of Thanksgiving mark the beginning of the
holiday season, “But I know our military is not a machine.  It’s made up
of human beings, sons and daughters, who are taking on this inherently
big risk.”

First Lady Melania Trump has been committed to peace throughout the
world, recently securing a ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine in
Oleshky.  Mrs. Trump explained, “The strength of our military helps us
prevent wars.  We perfect our capabilities to protect human life,
preserve peace, and secure our nation’s future.  This high level of
precision of our Armed Forces sends an unmistakable message to the rest
of the world: America’s children must inherit peace.”  

First Lady Melania Trump’s remarks come just days before American
families gather to celebrate Thanksgiving.  Mrs. Trump also participated
in assembling holiday care packages alongside military spouses, helping
prepare special deliveries for service members deployed overseas.  The
long-standing, traditional event was made possible through the efforts
of the American Red Cross.

First Lady Melania Trump’s full remarks, as prepared for delivery, are
included below.

**Remarks by First Lady Melania Trump**

Thank you, Usha, for your kind words and for the dedication and service
that you and the Vice President, a proud United States Marine veteran,
have provided to our nation.

It is an honor to be welcomed by all of the members of our armed forces
and their beautiful families here at Joint Base Andrews.  I am delighted
to share this special afternoon with you.  And, thank you the spouses of
our cabinet members who have joined me today to support America’s
military.

Perfect precision is not an accident.  It is the result of repetition,
measured pacing, and flawless execution.  

The US military is the most powerful military in the world because of
its people.  Our warriors train relentlessly, pushing the boundaries of
physical and mental strength.  

Every movement is deliberate.  Everything is designed to achieve
overpowering military superiority.  Through relentless training,
technical mastery, and a very measured approach to everything they do,
our warriors make the very complex look effortless.  

But I know our military is not a machine.  

It’s made up of human beings, sons and daughters, who are taking on this
inherently big risk.  Our brave men and women have families who are
praying for their safety every day.  Those families know that absolute
precision and coordination are vital to protecting the people they love.
 There is no room for mistakes.  

Our warriors are united by a vison greater than themselves.  Each
individual contributes to a larger plan with their fellow warriors.
 They have unwavering trust in each other.  There cannot be any
accidents.  

We must always remember that their service is not just about the
transaction of a single mission.  It is about the future of our children
and grandchildren.  It is about maintaining America’s lead position in
the world, creating opportunities for American ingenuity and innovation,
and preserving the safety and freedom of future generations.  Our
leaders don’t always talk about this, but we must always remember this
is the end result of our military action.

The American Red Cross assists members of the military and their
families, and for this I am grateful.  They serve troops and their
families worldwide, and today’s tradition will deliver packages filled
with warm holiday season wishes, made with love, which will be well
received.

The strength of our military helps us prevent wars.  We perfect our
capabilities to protect human life, preserve peace, and secure our
nation’s future.  This high level of precision of our Armed Forces sends
an unmistakable message to the rest of the world:

America’s children must inherit peace.   I wish America a very Happy
Thanksgiving, safety, and peace during this upcoming holiday season.
