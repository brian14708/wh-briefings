***Bringing Total to 43 Amid Russia-Ukraine War***

First Lady Melania Trump has successfully facilitated her seventh
reunion of Russian and Ukrainian children with their families, bringing
the total number of children reunited through her humanitarian efforts
to 43.

“We can heal the world through peace, and peace begins with our
children. Every child reunited with their parents brings humanity one
step closer to a better world for everyone.

“Children in this region of the world, like children everywhere, cannot
possibly understand why geography and government have put them into the
horrors of war.

“As adults, it is our moral imperative to elevate our children above the
divisions that power war in order to restore the simple joys of life,
immediately.

“Today, the reunification of nine children with their families occurred
with the full cooperation of the Russian and Ukrainian leaders. I remain
determined to continue my humanitarian work and bring families
together,” stated First Lady Melania Trump.

This latest humanitarian achievement comes shortly after the First Lady
secured a localized ceasefire in Oleshky and the greater Kherson region.
The First Lady’s intervention saved nearly 800 lives and enabled the
delivery of critically needed food, clean water, medicine, and medical
assistance.
