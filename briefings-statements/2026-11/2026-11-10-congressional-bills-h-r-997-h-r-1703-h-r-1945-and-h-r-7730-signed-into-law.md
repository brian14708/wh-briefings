title: Congressional Bills H.R. 997, H.R. 1703, H.R. 1945, and H.R. 7730 Signed into
  Law
---
 
On Tuesday, November 10, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 997, the “National Taxpayer Advocate Enhancement Act of 2025,”
which authorizes the National Taxpayer Advocate to appoint legal counsel
within the Office of the National Taxpayer Advocate that reports
directly to the National Taxpayer Advocate, or delegate thereof, and
further expands the authority of National Taxpayer Advocate to take
personnel actions with respect to any Taxpayer Advocate Service
employees;

H.R. 1703, the “Choices for Increased Mobility Act of 2026,” which
modifies Medicare payment rules for certain wheelchairs;

H.R. 1945, the “America’s National Churchill Museum National Historic
Landmark Act,” which designates the America’s National Churchill Museum,
located in Fulton, Missouri, as a National Historic Landmark; and

H.R. 7730, the “Bankruptcy Threshold Adjustment Act,” which permanently
raises bankruptcy threshold limits for small business Chapter 11
proceedings and consumer bankruptcy Chapter 13 proceedings.
