title: Congressional Bills H.R. 5284, H.R. 8352, and H.R. 10159 Signed into Law
---
 
On Friday, November 20, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 5284, the “Claiming Age Clarity Act,” which changes certain
terminology used by the Social Security Administration;

H.R. 8352, the “Criminal History Access Act of 2026,” which authorizes
the Department of Justice to exchange criminal identification records
with peace officer standards and training agencies of a State, the
District of Columbia, or a United States territory; and

H.R. 10159, which authorizes the Secretary of the Army to convey to the
State of North Carolina a certain parcel of real property located at
Fort Bragg, North Carolina, on which the State shall construct a
veterans’ State home, and for other purposes.
