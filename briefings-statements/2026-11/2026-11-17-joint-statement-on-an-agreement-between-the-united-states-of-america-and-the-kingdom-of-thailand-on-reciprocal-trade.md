title: Joint Statement on an Agreement Between the United States of America and the
  Kingdom of Thailand on Reciprocal Trade
---
 
On November 17, 2026, in Manila, Philippines, President Donald J. Trump
and Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul confirmed that the United States
of America (“the United States”) and the Kingdom of Thailand
(“Thailand”) have reached substantial conclusion in their negotiations
for an *Agreement Between the United States of America and the Kingdom
of Thailand on Reciprocal Trade* (“ART”).

The ART will strengthen our bilateral economic relationship and will
build upon our longstanding economic relationship, including the *Treaty
of Amity and Economic Relations Between the Kingdom of Thailand and the
United States of America*, signed in 1966, and the *Trade and Investment
Framework Agreement* *Between the United States of America and the
Kingdom of Thailand*, signed in 2002.

The United States and Thailand will now begin their respective necessary
internal processes to prepare the ART for signature and entry into
force. 

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the undersigned, being duly authorized thereto by
their respective Governments, have signed this Joint Statement.

DONE in duplicate at Manila, in English, this 17th day of November,
2026.
