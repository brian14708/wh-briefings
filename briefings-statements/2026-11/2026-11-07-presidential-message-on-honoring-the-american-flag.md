title: Presidential Message on Honoring the American Flag
---
 
For nearly two and a half centuries, the American Flag has stood as the
most iconic and magnificent symbol of freedom, liberty, and justice that
the world has ever known.  Today, we recommit ourselves to honoring our
beloved Stars and Stripes by ensuring it is always treated with the
utmost respect, dignity, and reverence that it so rightly deserves.

From the fateful moment the Star-Spangled Banner defiantly waved above
the ramparts at Fort McHenry to the triumphant raising of the red,
white, and blue atop Mount Suribachi during the Battle of Iwo Jima,
throughout every generation and across every battlefield, the American
Flag has shone as a radiant beacon of hope and liberty in the face of
tyranny and oppression.  It is the solemn duty of every American patriot
to honor our Flag with unfailing loyalty, respect, and devotion—never
allowing it to be dishonored or desecrated.  

The United States Code, Title 4, Section 8, sets forth the proper
conduct owed to our Flag.  It reminds us that the Flag “represents a
living country and is itself considered a living thing.”  When our Flag
has served its purpose and is no longer a fitting emblem for display, it
should be retired in a dignified way, preferably by burning.  Countless
veterans service organizations, scouting groups, and patriotic Americans
across our great Nation carry out these retirement ceremonies with
solemn respect, and we remain grateful for their devotion to this sacred
tradition. 

To fully restore pride in our American Flag, last year I signed an
Executive Order to prosecute those who desecrate this sacred symbol of
our freedom to the fullest extent of the law.  Under my leadership,
anyone who defiles our Flag will face swift and unrelenting
justice—including the radical rioters, agitators, and foreign nationals
who despise our country, break our laws, incite violence, and seek to
intimidate our citizens.  This month, the Department of Veterans Affairs
is launching a brand-new initiative online where Americans can access
all the necessary information on how to properly respect, treat, and
retire our great American Flag.  Together, we will ensure the premier
symbol of our great Republic is preserved for generations to come.

Today, on the Saturday before Veterans Day, we honor the sacred emblem
of our beloved country—a Flag that has flown over battlefields and
homesteads, been carried into combat by our bravest warriors, and been
draped over the caskets of heroes who paid the ultimate price to
preserve our freedom.  May we continue to righteously defend the
timeless values so beautifully embodied by our Stars and Stripes, and
may Old Glory always be treated with the dignity and honor befitting the
Flag of the greatest Nation in all of human history.
