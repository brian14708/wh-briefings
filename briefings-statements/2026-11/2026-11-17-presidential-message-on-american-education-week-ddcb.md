title: Presidential Message on American Education Week
---
 
Education is among the most important investments we make in America’s
future, equipping the next generation with the knowledge, skills, and
confidence to lead our country toward greater prosperity.  This American
Education Week, my Administration reaffirms its commitment to restoring
greatness to our education system, ending the reign of far-left
indoctrination in our classrooms, and empowering parents to choose the
education that best suits their children’s needs and values.

For decades, American students, teachers, and parents faced an education
system that failed to deliver the results they deserved.  Since its
inception in 1979, the Department of Education has managed to waste over
3 trillion taxpayer dollars without directly educating students or
improving their academic performance.  National assessments in recent
years reveal alarming trends, with mathematics and reading scores
plummeting as a result of nonsensical leftist policies and Federal
Government ineffectiveness.  Our children deserve better.  These results
underscore the urgent need to restore common sense to our classrooms and
equip our students with the tools they need to build an independent and
prosperous future.

Since I returned to office, my Administration has taken decisive action
to advance a new era of academic excellence and return educational
authority where it belongs:  with States, parents, and local
communities.  Through historic Executive Orders expanding educational
freedom and opportunity for families, we are reducing federal
bureaucracy and putting students and families first.  As part of that
effort, I proudly directed the Secretary of Education to facilitate the
closure of the Department of Education to put an end to the federal
control that has failed generations of American children. 

At the same time, my Executive Orders bar programs that receive
Department of Education funds from advancing unlawful “Diversity,
Equity, and Inclusion” mandates.  Under my leadership, taxpayers will no
longer be burdened with billions of dollars wasted on progressive social
experiments and obsolete programs.  We are delivering an education
system that shifts the focus from dividing students by race and identity
to uniting them around the timeless ideals of advancement through sheer
hard work, merit, and excellence.

America’s greatness has always depended on the promise of the next
generation.  This week, we recognize the dedicated teachers, devoted
parents, and hardworking students who form the foundation of our
education system, and we promise them our unwavering commitment to
building a system worthy of our Nation’s extraordinary potential. 
Together, we will restore greatness to our classrooms, renew confidence
in American education, and ensure that every child has the opportunity
to pursue their own unique American Dream.
