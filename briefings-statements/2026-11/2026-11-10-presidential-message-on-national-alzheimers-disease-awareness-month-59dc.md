title: "Presidential Message on National Alzheimer\u2019s Disease Awareness Month"
---
 
This National Alzheimer’s Disease Awareness Month, we stand with every
American living with this cruel disease, every family walking beside a
loved one through its difficult course, and every caregiver who devotes
themselves to others day after day with extraordinary patience and
love.  We also pause to remember the millions of precious moments,
cherished memories, and beloved lives that Alzheimer’s has taken from
families across our Nation.

Few illnesses challenge the human spirit like Alzheimer’s.  It steals
the memories that tell the story of a life—the names of old friends, the
sound of a child’s voice, and the familiar places we once called home. 
Yet no disease can erase the dignity of our citizens, and no diagnosis
diminishes the love of a family that will not leave their side.  Across
America, spouses become caregivers; children return the devotion once
given to them by their parents; and neighbors, doctors, nurses,
researchers, and volunteers come together to offer comfort and hope in
moments of profound uncertainty.  These acts of love and devotion to
those suffering from Alzheimer’s represent the very best of America’s
heart and soul.

For too long, our Nation has accepted chronic illness as an unavoidable
part of American life, but my Administration refuses to accept that
answer any longer.  Through our historic Make America Healthy Again
agenda, we are asking the fundamental questions about why Americans are
becoming sick, pursuing the root causes of chronic disease, restoring
integrity to scientific research, and placing preventive innovation at
the center of our national mission.  We are unleashing the power of
Super Intelligence and the genius of American scientists, doctors, and
entrepreneurs to pursue medical breakthroughs that once seemed beyond
our grasp.  America has never been a country that resigns itself to the
impossible—we have conquered countless diseases that once seemed
unbeatable and transformed modern medicine for the world.  With that
same spirit of discovery, compassion, and determination, we will keep
searching for better treatments, earlier answers, and—one day—a cure for
Alzheimer’s.

This month, we honor those fighting Alzheimer’s and the caregivers who
stand beside them.  We remember those we have lost, embrace the families
who carry their memories forward, and renew our promise to build an
America where longer lives are also healthier lives. Together, we will
Make America Healthy Again and give future generations the priceless
gift of more years, more memories, and more time with the people they
love.
