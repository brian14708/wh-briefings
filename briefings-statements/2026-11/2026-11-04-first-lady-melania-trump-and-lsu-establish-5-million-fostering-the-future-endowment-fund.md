*Historic Donation Creates Permanent Scholarships for LSU Students from
Foster Care*

First Lady Melania Trump and Louisiana State University (LSU) have
secured $5 million to establish a permanent endowment fund supporting
FOSTERING THE FUTURE scholarships for LSU students who experienced
foster care.

First Lady Melania Trump will travel to Baton Rouge, Louisiana to
advance her Fostering the Future initiative with a special announcement
at Louisiana State University.  

During her visit, Mrs. Trump will attend the highly anticipated LSU
Tigers versus Alabama Crimson Tide football game at Tiger Stadium, where
a groundbreaking Fostering the Future campaign will activate across the
campus and on the field.
