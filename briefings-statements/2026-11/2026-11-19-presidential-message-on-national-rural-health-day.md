title: Presidential Message on National Rural Health Day
---
 
On National Rural Health Day, we honor the doctors, nurses, first
responders, caregivers, and other healthcare professionals who serve
millions of Americans in rural communities across our country.  Their
work is essential to the strength of our Nation, and my Administration
is committed to ensuring that Americans in every rural town receive
high-quality healthcare, from the hills of Appalachia to the great
Alaskan frontier.

For decades, career politicians treated rural America as an
afterthought, and small-town families paid the price.  Hospitals
struggled to recruit enough doctors and nurses, and families were often
forced to travel long distances to reach specialists and services that
should have been available closer to home.  Rural America should not
have to trade its way of life for better health.  The communities that
feed our Nation and carry on our proudest traditions deserve a
healthcare system that works for them.

Through the historic Working Families Tax Cuts Act, which every Democrat
voted against, Republicans and I created the $50 billion Rural Health
Transformation Program, a 5-year investment now at work in all 50
States, helping rural communities strengthen their healthcare workforce,
modernize hospitals and clinics, expand access to telehealth, and bring
more services closer to the patients who need them.  The program puts
States in charge, giving them the resources and flexibility to develop
solutions that reflect the needs of their own communities.

This investment is also bringing a revolution of common sense to
American health.  Guided by our Make America Healthy Again agenda, it
confronts the chronic disease crisis and puts prevention first, funding
mobile clinics and screenings that catch illness early, remote
monitoring that helps patients manage diabetes and high blood pressure
from home, and nutrition and fitness programs that keep families
thriving.  Under my leadership, we will stop diseases before they ever
take hold and give every citizen the chance to live a long, healthy
life.

Today, we thank every healthcare professional who has chosen to serve
rural America.  Together, we are strengthening rural hospitals,
improving access to care, and building a healthier future for the proud
communities that feed, fuel, and defend our Nation.  As we Make Rural
America Healthy Again, our heartland will rise stronger, prouder, and
greater than ever before.
