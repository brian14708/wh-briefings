title: "First Lady Melania Trump\u2019s Fostering the Future Together Grows Its International\
  \ Momentum Across the United States, Asia, Europe, and the Caribbean"
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump’s Fostering the Future Together is accelerating
its global mission with four major international gatherings before the
end of 2026. The member nation leaders are mobilizing to empower
children through technology and education.

Mrs. Trump’s international initiative, Fostering the Future Together,
will convene world leaders at four significant gatherings:

- United States: G20 Leaders’ Summit, Miami, hosted by First Lady
  Melania Trump
- Philippines: ASEAN Conference, hosted by First Lady Liza
  Araneta-Marcos
- Poland: European Regional Conference, hosted by First Lady Marta
  Nawrocka
- St. Kitts and Nevis: Caribbean Regional Conference, hosted by First
  Lady Diani Prince-Drew

“I applaud First Ladies Liza Araneta-Marcos, Marta Nawrocka, and Diani
Prince-Drew for their extraordinary leadership,” said First Lady Melania
Trump. “Together, we are uniting nations around the shared
responsibility of equipping the next generation with the knowledge,
technology, and resources to shape a better world.”

The four gatherings build on Fostering the Future Together’s momentum at
the 81st United Nations General Assembly in New York, where Mrs. Trump
announced new global technology partnerships with more than $23 million
in commitments from Adobe, Amazon, Intel, Starlink, Google, and Zoom.

The landmark moment in Manhattan also welcomed prominent technology
executives, including Apple’s Executive Chair Tim Cook and PayPal
President and CEO Enrique Lores, underscoring the private sector’s
growing engagement in Fostering the Future Together’s mission.

“Our children are growing up in an era of technological transformation,
driven by the emergence of super intelligence,” said Mrs. Trump. “From
Miami and Manila to Europe and the Caribbean, we are building a future
to ensure children everywhere can thrive in this new technological era.”

First Lady Melania Trump’s Fostering the Future Together is a global
coalition of nearly 60 nations united to empower children through
technology and education.
