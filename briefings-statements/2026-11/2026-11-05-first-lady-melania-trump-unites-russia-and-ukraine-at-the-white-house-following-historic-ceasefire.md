title: First Lady Melania Trump Unites Russia and Ukraine at The White House Following
  Historic Ceasefire
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump gathered leaders from Russia and Ukraine at the
White House today. The occasion is believed to mark the first time in
American history that a First Lady has personally brought
representatives of two nations actively at war together to the White
House for diplomacy.

The unprecedented gathering united key officials from both nations who
played instrumental roles in establishing the ceasefire in Oleshky.

“Now, we must use the courage we brought to Oleshky to find common
ground through a shared concern for the suffering of innocent people.
Together, we can create something more enduring than a temporary
ceasefire. Let us see Oleshky as a spark in a world of darkness, capable
of illuminating the best in humanity. Build a powerful flame of peace,
visible to the world. Ignite hope, foster cooperation, and brighten a
path toward lasting peace,” said First Lady Melania Trump.

Mrs. Trump detailed her role as Exclusive Intermediary in securing the
ceasefire, facilitating the delivery of humanitarian aid to the region,
and organizing the safe evacuation of nearly 800 people.

“My role as Exclusive Intermediary was memorialized in written
agreements drafted and signed by both Russia and Ukraine,” the First
Lady shared. “I was involved in every stage of the planning. I reviewed
demining plans, aerial images, assessed roadways, and discussed
evacuation routes, hospital access, vehicles, and medical resources. I
was made aware of the preferred dates and timing of the operation.”

First Lady Melania Trump acknowledged, “The ceasefire in Oleshky would
not be possible without President Putin and President Zelensky. Their
cooperation establishes a meaningful precedent for advancing peace
throughout the region.”

Ukrainian representatives presented First Lady Melania Trump with
President Zelensky’s award, Ukraine’s Order of Princess Olga, First
Class, the highest degree of this prestigious state honor. The award
recognizes Mrs. Trump’s leadership in securing the ceasefire in Oleshky,
which enabled the evacuation of 800 individuals.

Mrs. Trump concluded her historic speech by presenting the
highest-ranking leaders in attendance with letters she had written to
President Putin and President Zelensky.

Following her remarks, Mrs. Trump invited the distinguished guests to
view two historic artifacts from the White House collection, which she
personally selected as powerful symbols of peace.

The first, a late 18th-century medicine chest, was removed from the
White House after British forces set the building on fire during the War
of 1812. Once adversaries, the United States and Great Britain
ultimately established a strong alliance

The First Lady also presented an 1899 Presentation Cup, gifted to French
Ambassador M. Jules Cambon in recognition of France’s diplomatic efforts
to negotiate peace and bring the Spanish-American War to an end.

Mrs. Trump’s remarks, as prepared for delivery, are below.

**Oleshky, a Spark in a World of Darkness that Can Illuminate the Best
in Humanity**

Oleshky will forever unite the brave individuals gathered at the White
House today.

We have chosen humanity over war.

Undeniably, the lives of innocent people must transcend the divisions of
war.

I hope Oleshky inspires greater peace. Every significant transformation
begins with a small spark.

What matters is not the modesty of this beginning, but the determination
to turn it into something extraordinary.

Now, we must use the courage we brought to Oleshky to find common ground
through a shared concern for the suffering of innocent people. Together,
we can create something more enduring than a temporary ceasefire.

Let us see Oleshky as a spark in a world of darkness, capable of
illuminating the best in humanity. Build a powerful flame of peace,
visible to the world. Ignite hope, foster cooperation, and brighten a
path toward lasting peace.

I became aware of the severity of the humanitarian crisis in Oleshky in
September. Recognizing the urgency, I acted immediately and directed my
representative, Marc Beckman, to engage with Russia and Ukraine on my
behalf, to pursue peace in Oleshky.

Both nations welcomed my participation, acknowledging that previous
efforts to establish a ceasefire in the region had not succeeded.

Russia and Ukraine honored our relationship and agreed to work with me
in the capacity of Exclusive Intermediary to establish a ceasefire in
Oleshky and the surrounding Kherson region.

Our shared objective was to deliver critical humanitarian aid to Oleshky
residents and establish a safe evacuation route for civilians.

My role as Exclusive Intermediary was memorialized in written agreements
drafted and signed by both Russia and Ukraine.

Marc and I began our work using the communication channels we had
previously established to reunite children with their families in Russia
and Ukraine. Humanitarian, military, and government officials from
Russia and Ukraine worked diligently to coordinate every aspect of the
operation.

I was involved in every stage of the planning. I reviewed demining
plans, aerial images, assessed roadways, and discussed evacuation
routes, hospital access, vehicles, and medical resources. I was made
aware of the preferred dates and timing of the operation.

My communication with Russia and Ukraine was extensive, professional,
detailed, and highly cooperative. Marc and I did everything possible to
support both nations in advancing their shared commitment to reach a
ceasefire in Oleshky.

We fulfilled our responsibilities at every stage, collaborating with
Russian and Ukrainian teams through completion. Russia and Ukraine
signed the agreements in both languages on Friday, October 23rd, 2026.

I commend both sides for your good-faith efforts to define and implement
all components of this sophisticated ceasefire.

I would like to welcome Mr. Kirill Dmitriev, Special Envoy to President
Putin of the Russian Federation, to the podium to say a few words. Thank
you for your cooperation and professionalism.

My deepest appreciation to Ms. Yana Lantratova, Commissioner for Human
Rights in the Russian Federation. Your dedication brings hope and
dignity to those devastated by the war.

Freedom exists only when the mind is without fear. It is both an ethical
and intellectual condition.

A free world is one where people can live with dignity, truth, and
knowledge, without fear. To soar freely beneath the sun, we must have
the promise of life without oppression.

For a brief period, Oleshky residents enjoyed relief from the
psychological horrors of war. For innocent people living in the shadow
of war, freedom is more personal than we can fully understand. It is
found in the simplest moments of life.

Last week in Oleshky and neighboring areas of Kherson, people
experienced the simple joys of life for the first time since 2022. They
walked down familiar streets without fear of explosions.

Four brave American journalists entered the war-torn region to document
the wishes of Oleshky’s residents. Working in the true spirit of
journalism, Newsmax provided transparency, accountability, and
protection to safeguard the truth. Thanks to these journalists, who are
with us here today.

The leaders of Russia and Ukraine worked to establish calm. Food, clean
water, and medicine entered the area, and nearly 800 people left their
homes in search of safety, loved ones, and a renewed sense of being.

Mr. David Arakhamina, faction leader of the Servant of the People party
of Ukraine, is also with us today. Please join me and share a few words.

Thank you President Zelensky and the nation of Ukraine for this highest
honor – the Order of Princess Olga, First Class.

My tremendous gratitude to Mr. Dmytro Lubinets, Ukrainian Parliament
Commissioner for Human Rights, for the incredible bravery and heart you
show in serving your people every day.

Imagine a young child confined to her home, unable to dream of her
future because she lives in constant fear. We were not born to spend our
lives hiding in terror, watching the sky for drones and missiles, or
scanning the ground for mines.

What benefit is there when the lives of innocents are lost forever?

We are alive to laugh, create, explore, and love.

Let Oleshky be the modest spark that grows into a lasting flame of
peace.

The ceasefire in Oleshky would not be possible without President Putin
and President Zelensky. Their cooperation establishes a meaningful
precedent for advancing peace throughout the region

As this historic moment concluded, I wrote personal letters to President
Putin and to President Zelensky.

David and Kirill, please deliver these letters to your presidents on my
behalf. I trust you will ensure their safe delivery.
