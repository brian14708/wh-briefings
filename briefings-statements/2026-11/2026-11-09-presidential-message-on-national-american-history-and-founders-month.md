title: Presidential Message on National American History and Founders Month
---
 
The story of America is the greatest story of freedom in all of human
history.  During National American History and Founders Month, we honor
the legendary patriots whose vision gave birth to our Republic; we
celebrate the magnificent heritage they entrusted to us; and we pledge
to carry their spirit of courage, faith, and patriotism into America’s
next great chapter.

From the battlefields of the Revolution to the stunning achievements
that propelled our civilization into the heavens, Americans have
advanced the cause of freedom and lifted our Nation to extraordinary new
heights.  Their triumphs are our triumphs, their legacy is our
inheritance, and their unyielding spirit of greatness courses through
the veins of every American patriot—filling us with immense pride in our
past and tremendous optimism for our future.

In recent decades, however, across our schools, government, and most
cherished institutions, radical left-wing ideologues have worked to
tarnish this indelible history and portray our past as a source of
shame.  At my direction, my Administration issued a report revealing
that the Smithsonian’s National Museum of American History—a
taxpayer-funded organization entrusted with preserving our national
treasures—has become a battleground in this campaign to undermine our
founding principles and diminish our Nation’s extraordinary
achievements.

I proudly took action to reverse this heinous betrayal of our history
and restore patriotism and truth to the Smithsonian, signing an
Executive Order to utilize all resources available to correct the
injustices found in the report; inform visitors of the museum’s
ideological bias; and ensure the Smithsonian presents an accurate, fair,
and truthful understanding of American history.  My Administration has
restored our beloved landmarks and capital to their original beauty and
moved to combat anti-American indoctrination in the classroom.  In the
past year, Task Force 250 has worked tirelessly to deliver the greatest,
grandest, and most spectacular birthday celebration in the history of
the world.  Under my leadership, our national treasures are being
preserved with care, our history is being presented with integrity, and
our founders are once again being honored for their extraordinary
contributions to human freedom.  

During this National American History and Founders Month, as we
celebrate our exceptional heritage and our heroic Founding Fathers, we
renew our sacred promise to always honor the pioneers who built our
Nation and to continue safeguarding the blessings of liberty for
generations to come.  I encourage all Americans—especially our Nation’s
youth—to learn more about our incredible history and to draw inspiration
from the noble example of those who forged our Republic.  With faith in
Almighty God, unwavering patriotism in our hearts, and supreme
confidence in our Nation’s destiny, we affirm that the greatest chapters
of the American story are yet to come.
