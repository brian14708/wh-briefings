title: Presidential Message on the Birthday of the United States Marine Corps
---
 
For over two and a half centuries, the United States Marine Corps has
stood as a living embodiment of the American Spirit.  With honor,
courage, and unwavering commitment to preserving and advancing the
interests of the United States, Marines have proven time and again that
they will answer every call and complete any mission no matter the
odds.  As the Marine Corps celebrates 251 years of protecting America
and defeating her enemies, I extend my deepest gratitude to every Marine
who has fought and sacrificed for our Nation and pay tribute to their
unmatched legacy of valor.

From the dawn of our Republic, Marines have defended the pillars of
freedom and liberty that America was founded upon.  During the
Revolutionary War, Marines took to the sea to seize the port of Nassau
and reinforce the Continental Army with large quantities of military
supplies.  Throughout World War II, Marines played a critical role
across the Pacific Theater and were essential to American victories at
Guam, Guadalcanal, Okinawa, and Iwo Jima.  These incredible Devil Dogs
have served on the front lines of countless conflicts throughout
American history, never failed to protect our citizens at home and
abroad, and fearlessly demonstrated American might and power in the face
of our adversaries.  Today, America is made safer through the heroic and
selfless service of our brave Marines.

The patriotic men and women of the United States Marine Corps are
enduring reminders of the true source of American greatness and glory. 
As President, I have been the strongest advocate for our Marines in the
history of our country.  Through advancements in Super Intelligence and
investments in missile defense, next-generation capabilities, and
shipbuilding, we are providing our Marines with every resource they need
to defeat America’s enemies wherever they may be.

Under my leadership, our Marines also return home to the care and
support they have so rightly earned.  My Administration has restored
common sense at the Department of Veterans Affairs, empowering veterans
to seek the healthcare providers of their choice, lowering costs for
veterans and their families, and slashing harmful backlogs.  As
Commander in Chief, I will never stop standing up for our Marines and
upholding their reputation as the most lethal fighting force the world
has ever seen.

Guided by their motto, Semper Fidelis, meaning “Always Faithful,” the
United States Marines have left their mark on the history of our Nation
and all of mankind.  The steadfast strength of our Marines is a
testament to the power of the American Spirit and the might of the
United States Armed Forces.  Generation after generation, Marines have
faithfully served as champions of America, and they will carry on as
faithful defenders of liberty for centuries to come.  Semper Fi!
