title: Presidential Message on Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month
---
 
America’s critical infrastructure is the foundation of our prosperity,
the backbone of our national defense, and the bedrock of our resilience.
 For more than two centuries, American workers, through their own
ingenuity and grit, have built the infrastructure that enables the
world’s greatest economy and strongest military.  During Critical
Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month, we celebrate this long,
rich legacy and reaffirm our commitment to expanding opportunity,
fortifying our national security, and unleashing American innovation to
keep our Nation free and resilient for generations to come.

My America First Resilience Strategy establishes a bold vision to secure
such a future through commonsense transformation.  We are confronting
the gravest threats to our homeland head-on to protect American lives,
strengthen our communities, and safeguard livelihoods; upgrading our
aging technology and infrastructure to guarantee dependable power, clean
water, and modern communications for households nationwide while keeping
our country ahead of its rivals; returning authority to the States and
equipping governors, local leaders, and industries with the tools to
prepare for and recover from any crisis; and streamlining government to
deliver faster results and greater value for every hardworking citizen. 
Our goal is clear:  to secure the supply chains and systems on which our
Nation depends so that no adversary and no disaster will ever hold
America or our core interests hostage.

For America to remain the champion of the world’s economy, our critical
infrastructure must be built by American companies, supported by
American workers, and secured against those who seek to exploit it. 
Since returning to office, I have taken decisive action to reshore the
manufacturing our critical infrastructure relies on.  To harness our
abundant energy and natural resources, I declared a National Energy
Emergency and signed an Executive Order on Unleashing American Energy,
directing every Federal agency to expedite major energy projects.  I
also established the National Energy Dominance Council, marshalling the
full power of the Federal Government behind that vital mission, and
invoked the Defense Production Act to strengthen domestic production of
the transformers, transmission equipment, and other critical components
essential to our electric grid and national defense.  Through this
America First approach, we are expanding American oil, natural gas, and
nuclear energy production and eliminating disastrous, unnecessary, or
burdensome regulations to finally restore the reliable and affordable
energy that powers the greatest Nation on Earth.

That America First commitment is more critical than ever as we enter a
new era of digital infrastructure—the age of Super Intelligence (SI). 
Across America, we are investing in SI data centers to power the
American economy and ensure that we remain the global leader in this
cutting-edge technology, especially as our adversaries compete against
us for dominance.  Every new data center will unlock countless
high-paying jobs in America’s SI industry.  Thanks to the work of my
Administration, America’s largest technology companies have also signed
the Ratepayer Protection Pledge, agreeing to pay for the new power
generation and grid upgrades their data centers require instead of
passing those costs on to American families.  As President, I will
always stand up for the middle class and ensure that our companies put
the American People first.

Our infrastructure is as strong as the Americans who build, operate, and
defend it.  This Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month,
we honor the linemen, engineers, technicians, operators, cybersecurity
professionals, and skilled workers whose dedication keeps America
running.  Their unrivaled genius and determination represent the very
best of our Nation, and together with them, we will build and maintain
the infrastructure that makes America great.
