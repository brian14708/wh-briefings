title: Congressional Bills H.R. 1550, H.R. 4930, and H.R. 8552 Signed into Law
---
 
On Monday, November 23, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 1550, the “Strengthening America’s Turning Point Act,” which
redesignates the Saratoga National Historical Park as the Saratoga
National Battlefield Park;

H.R. 4930, which expands U.S. Customs and Border Protection’s authority
to share information about imports suspected of violating United States
intellectual-property-rights with the rights holders, online
marketplaces, shippers, and other stakeholders; and

H.R. 8552, the “Veterans’ Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act of
2026,” which provides for a cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for
veterans’ disability compensation and dependency and indemnity
compensation beneficiaries equal to the Social Security COLA.
