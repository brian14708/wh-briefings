title: First Lady Melania Trump to Convene Russian and Ukrainian Representatives at
  the White House
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump will personally bring representatives of Russia
and Ukraine, two nations actively at war, together at the White House.
This event represents an extraordinary moment in American history.

Mrs. Trump independently initiated engagement between the two warring
nations and successfully secured a ceasefire in Oleshky and the
surrounding Kherson region. This achievement marks an unprecedented
diplomatic initiative by an American First Lady.  

First Lady Melania Trump will deliver remarks on the ceasefire in
Oleshky at 11:00 am ET on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at the White
House.
