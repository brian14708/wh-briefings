title: U.S.-Russia-China Heads of State Declaration toward Peace
---
 
Donald J. Trump, President of the United States of America, Vladimir
Putin, President of the Russian Federation, and Xi Jinping, State
Chairman of the People’s Republic of China, met in Shenzhen on November
19, 2026. The U.S. and Russian heads of state expressed appreciation to
China for hosting this historic trilateral Summit. The three heads of
state had an in-depth exchange of views on the current international
situation and committed to maintaining strategic stability and
international security.

1.  As permanent members of the United Nations Security Council, the
    United States, Russia, and China committed to reform the UN so that
    the UN can respond more effectively to global challenges and conform
    to its fundamental mission of international peace and security.

<!-- -->

1.  The three countries plan to increase the production and supply of
    refined petroleum products for the benefit of all nations. They
    reaffirmed the importance of stable energy flows.

<!-- -->

1.  The three heads of state recognized that the principle of state
    consent and respect for immunities of State officials are of
    paramount importance, and concurred that the International Criminal
    Court and other similar bodies must not abuse their jurisdiction.

<!-- -->

1.  The three heads of state reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won
    and must never be fought. The three countries will conduct a
    dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation
    of nuclear weapons.
