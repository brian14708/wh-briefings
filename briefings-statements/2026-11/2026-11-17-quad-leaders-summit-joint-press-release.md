On November 17, 2026 — President Donald J. Trump of the United States,
Prime Minister Anthony Albanese of Australia, Prime Minister Narendra
Modi of India, and Prime Minister Takaichi Sanae of Japan — met for a
Quad Leaders’ Summit in Manila.  This year marks nearly a decade since
the Quad was revived and the tenth anniversary of the vision of a Free
and Open Indo-Pacific, first articulated by the late Prime Minister Abe
Shinzo of Japan in 2016.  A decade on, that vision is stronger than
ever.  Together, we have shown that the Quad is a force for good and a
partnership of action, committed to peace, stability, and prosperity. 
We strongly oppose any destabilizing or unilateral actions that seek to
change the status quo, including by force or coercion, which escalate
tensions and undermine regional peace and stability.  We express grave
concerns about economic coercion, including through arbitrary export
restrictions.  We are securing critical supply chains, protecting
freedom of navigation and overflight, driving growth through
technologies of the future including investments in and deployment of
trusted Super Intelligence (SI)\*, and responding to disasters with
life-saving assistance.  We commit to work together to ensure open,
safe, and resilient maritime transit corridors in the Indo-Pacific.  We
are working with regional partners to tackle shared challenges and drive
opportunities to deliver tangible benefits to the Indo-Pacific.

- We announce the inaugural **Quad Business Leaders’ Forum** which is
  scheduled to be held in early 2027, bringing together business
  leaders, government officials, and policy experts from Quad countries
  to foster greater business ties across our private sectors, including
  the promotion of commercial deals and partnerships critical to the
  prosperity of the Indo-Pacific.

<!-- -->

- We affirm the importance of the **Super Intelligence\* Accelerator**,
  announced by our Foreign Ministers in Atlanta.  The Accelerator is
  intended to coordinate and fund efforts to support trusted Super
  Intelligence (SI)\* access, deployment, and technical assistance
  across the Indo-Pacific.

<!-- -->

- We are proud to welcome the **Indo-Pacific Maritime Surveillance
  Collaboration**, a new initiative to enhance information sharing and
  maritime domain awareness.  In 2027, our respective P-8 and P-3C
  patrol aircraft are scheduled to fly in a maritime exercise for the
  first time in a Quad context, making our seas safer.

<!-- -->

- We are proud of progress under the **Quad Partnership for Cable
  Connectivity and Resilience**, which has expanded access to secure and
  trusted communications infrastructure across the Indo-Pacific.  Today,
  we welcome an expansion of Australia’s contribution to the Partnership
  to focus on electricity cables, strengthening regional energy security
  and connectivity.  We also welcome upcoming undersea cables projects
  in the Indian Ocean which are intended to strengthen regional
  connectivity.

<!-- -->

- We acknowledge the rapid increase in the number and scale of cyber
  incidents in the region and announce enhanced **Quad Cybersecurity
  Cooperation**, through which we plan to work together to achieve an
  open and secure cyberspace.

<!-- -->

- We unequivocally condemn terrorism and violent extremism in all its
  forms and manifestations, including cross-border terrorism, and commit
  to strengthening the Quad’s ongoing cooperation on counterterrorism.

We reaffirm our unwavering support for ASEAN centrality and unity. 
Together, we are committed to upholding a free and open Indo-Pacific.

\* In Australia, India, and Japan, ‘Super Intelligence’ refers to
‘artificial intelligence.’
