title: Presidential Message on National Diabetes Month
---
 
Nearly every American family has been touched by diabetes—a parent, a
grandparent, a child, or a neighbor whose daily life is shaped by this
disease.  This National Diabetes Month, we recognize the millions of
Americans struggling with diabetes, and we reaffirm our commitment to
reverse the chronic disease epidemic in our Nation and Make America
Healthy Again.

In recent decades, the number of adults diagnosed with diabetes has
doubled, with more than 40 million men and women in the United States
living with the disease.  An estimated 25 percent of adults living with
diabetes are unaware they have it, putting their health at serious risk
every single day.  For too long, the Federal Government accepted these
numbers as the cost of modern life.  My Administration refuses to accept
that answer, and we are fighting every day to help patients and families
receive the care and medicine they need to stay healthy, including
insulin. 

When I returned to office, I made a promise to pursue groundbreaking
medical advancements and expand access to care for American families and
patients, which is why I proudly established the Make America Healthy
Again Commission to gain new insights into health conditions like
diabetes.  Through my Great Healthcare Plan, unveiled earlier this year,
I am continuing to deliver on my commitment.  This plan is slashing the
price of prescription drugs, including insulin, by securing
Most-Favored-Nation pricing so that Americans never again pay more than
patients in other countries for the same medications.  It will cut
insurance premiums by putting money directly into the pockets of
American families instead of insurance companies.  It will hold Big
Insurance accountable by requiring insurers to disclose, in plain
English, how often they deny claims and how much of every premium dollar
actually goes to care.  Furthermore, it will bring total price
transparency to American healthcare so that patients know the cost of
care before they receive it.  Together, these reforms will deliver a
healthcare system that puts patients first, restores trust, and truly
serves the American People.

Through platforms like TrumpRx, we are lowering the cost of medications
and ensuring individuals with diabetes are not left without the care
they depend on.  Under my leadership, prescription drug prices recorded
their largest annual decline in more than 60 years, and we have
delivered massive price cuts on GLP-1s and other drugs used to manage
diabetes. Patients who buy GLP-1s through TrumpRx are saving $1,800 this
year, and those savings will soar to $3,000 by 2028.  Thanks to TrumpRx,
any American can also purchase insulin for $35 per month or less.  With
bold and decisive America First policies, my Administration will always
stand up for individuals with diabetes and ensure that their necessary
medications remain affordable.

This month, our Nation unites to recognize the strength of those living
with diabetes, honor the strides we have made in prevention and
treatment, and renew our commitment to defeating this disease once and
for all.  As we continue building a bigger and brighter future filled
with unprecedented technology, research, and treatment, I encourage all
Americans to take a proactive role in their health and understand their
risk for diabetes.  Together, we will ensure that every American has the
tools and support they need to build a healthy and prosperous life.
