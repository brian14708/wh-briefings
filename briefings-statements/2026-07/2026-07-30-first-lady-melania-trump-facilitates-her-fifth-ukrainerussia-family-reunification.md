title: "First Lady Melania Trump Facilitates Her Fifth Ukraine\u2013Russia Family\
  \ Reunification"
---
 
First Lady Melania Trump has helped reunite a fifth group of Ukrainian
and Russian children with their loved ones, continuing her humanitarian
work on behalf of those affected by the war. A total of five children
were reunited today.

This milestone marks another step toward ensuring that innocent kids can
safely return to their homes, communities, and the people who care for
them.

The First Lady remarked, “While diplomacy and peacebuilding are
important to this effort, they are surpassed by our collective ability
to place children above international disputes. Every reunion is a
reminder that compassion, dignity, and family can transcend even the
darkest of times: war. My representative and I continue working with the
governments of Russia and Ukraine to safely return children to their
families and communities.”

The First Lady’s leadership has bridged political divides in pursuit of
a singular humanitarian mission: placing the well-being of children
above conflict and helping restore families torn apart by war.
