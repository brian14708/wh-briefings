Executive Order 14438

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the
laws of the United States of America, it is hereby ordered:

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Section</span>
<span style="text-decoration: underline">1</span>.
 <span style="text-decoration: underline">Purpose</span>.  The American
Dream of homeownership is a vital pillar of our great country.  It is
the policy of my Administration to reduce regulatory barriers to
mortgage origination and costs impeding homeownership; embrace
innovative risk and value assessment technologies that incorporate Super
Intelligence to drive down mortgage and homeownership costs; and promote
competition and innovation throughout the housing and mortgage market.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">2</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Proactive Cancellation of
Excessive Mortgage Insurance</span>.  (a)  The Director of the Federal
Housing Finance Agency (Director) shall, if he determines that it is in
the best interests of the Federal National Mortgage Association and the
Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Enterprises) or the Federal
Housing Finance Agency (FHFA), and the American public, direct the
Enterprises to establish a pilot program, applicable only to mortgages
made after the establishment of the pilot program, that periodically
reviews single-family mortgage loans they own, guarantee, or securitize,
using automated valuation models and other available data to assess
current market value, and to identify borrowers who appear to satisfy
equity and other requirements for cancellation of mortgage insurance.

(b)  The Director shall direct the Enterprises to establish procedures
for the pilot program, including financial safety and soundness
standards and payment-history requirements, under which mortgage
insurance is canceled proactively when an Enterprise determines that:

\(i\)    the borrower satisfies applicable equity requirements based on
the property’s current market value, as determined through an automated
valuation model or other means approved by the Director;

(ii)   cancellation is consistent with financial safety and soundness
and with the Enterprise’s contractual obligations; and

(iii)  the borrower satisfies all other applicable statutory,
contractual, regulatory, and Enterprise requirements for cancellation.

(c)  The Director shall direct the Enterprises to require servicers, to
the extent permitted by law and operationally feasible, to notify
borrowers promptly when mortgage insurance may be canceled under the
pilot program, and to reduce or eliminate unnecessary documentation
requests, appraisal costs, and other administrative barriers to
cancellation.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">3</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Standardizing the Permitting of
Homes</span>.  (a)  To increase the quality and lower the cost of
building a home, there is hereby established the Task Force on
Manufactured Housing Innovation (Task Force), which shall be co-chaired
by the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development and the Director of
the Federal Housing Finance Agency, or their designees, and shall
include the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of Commerce, and the
Secretary of Veterans Affairs, or their designees.  The co-chairs may
invite the heads of other executive departments and agencies (agencies)
to participate, as appropriate.

(b)  Within 180 days of the date of this order, the Task Force shall
submit to the President, through the Assistant to the President for
Economic Policy, recommendations to facilitate the use of modern
manufacturing methods in the production of manufactured and other
factory-built housing eligible for Federal housing programs, including
actions to:

(i)    promote specifications, certifications, inspections, and
approvals across Federal housing and housing-finance programs;

\(ii\)   establish reciprocal recognition among agencies of licenses,
certifications, approvals, and inspections, where doing so would avoid
unnecessary duplication;

(iii)  eliminate Federal requirements and duplicative reviews that
unnecessarily impede automation, modularization, standardized
production, or other advanced manufacturing techniques, or that increase
the time or cost of bringing homes to market;

\(iv\)   use Federal financing, insurance, guarantee, purchasing, and
other program authorities to encourage innovation and increased
production of affordable manufactured and factory-built homes; and

(v)    promote competition among manufacturers and other market
participants while maintaining appropriate standards for safety,
quality, durability, and consumer protection.

(c)  The Task Force shall also evaluate whether, and under what existing
statutory authorities, Federal grant funding may be conditioned on, or
be used to incentivize, State and local adoption of standardized
permitting and licensing for manufactured and other factory-built
housing, consistent with the best practices issued under section 4(a) of
Executive Order 14394 of March 13, 2026 (Removing Regulatory Barriers to
Affordable Home Construction), and shall prepare legislative
recommendations to codify the policies set forth in this subsection.

(d)  The heads of the agencies represented on the Task Force shall,
within their respective authorities, take appropriate action to
implement the Task Force’s recommendations.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">4</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Reducing Excessive Title
Insurance, Especially in the Refinancing of Americans Homes</span>. 
(a)  The Director shall, if he determines that it is in the best
interests of the Enterprises or the FHFA, and the American public, and
consistent with financial safety and soundness, the Director’s
authorities as regulator and conservator, and applicable law, direct the
Enterprises to expand the title acceptance pilot into an ongoing
program, available nationwide through both Enterprises, under which an
Enterprise may accept an automated title review or other title-risk
assessment approved by the FHFA in lieu of a lender’s title insurance
policy on eligible refinance transactions.

(b)  In expanding the program, the Director shall consider:

(i)    extending eligibility to refinance transactions with
loan-to-value ratios above the current 80 percent limit where title risk
remains low;

\(ii\)   removing geographic limitations and certifying additional
title-assessment providers to promote competition;

(iii)  waiving appraisals, income re-verification, and other
documentation requirements for limited cash-out refinances that reduce
the borrower’s monthly payment, where the Enterprise’s existing data
support doing so; and

\(iv\)   reducing or eliminating loan-level price adjustments and other
Enterprise fees on such refinances.

(c)  The Director shall require that cost savings resulting from the
program be passed through to borrowers and that lenders inform borrowers
of those savings.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">5</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Department of Agriculture and
Department of Veterans Affairs Direct Loan Rates</span>.  The Secretary
of Agriculture and the Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall, consistent
with applicable law, including 42 U.S.C. 1490a(a)(1)(B) and 38 U.S.C.
3762(c)(1), and available appropriations, reduce the interest rates
charged on single‑family home loans made directly by their respective
departments.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">6</span>.
 <span style="text-decoration: underline">General Provisions</span>. 
(a)  Nothing in this order shall be construed to impair or otherwise
affect:

(i)   the authority granted by law to an executive department or agency,
or the head thereof; or

\(ii\)  the functions of the Director of the Office of Management and
Budget relating to budgetary, administrative, or legislative proposals.

\(b\)  This order shall be implemented consistent with applicable law
and subject to the availability of appropriations.

\(c\)  This order is not intended to, and does not, create any right or
benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity by
any party against the United States, its departments, agencies, or
entities, its officers, employees, or agents, or any other person.

\(d\)  The costs for publication of this order shall be borne by the
Federal Housing Finance Agency.

DONALD J. TRUMP

THE WHITE HOUSE,

         October 22, 2026.
