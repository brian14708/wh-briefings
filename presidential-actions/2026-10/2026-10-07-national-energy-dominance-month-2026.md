BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

A PROCLAMATION

America’s abundant energy resources are the backbone of our booming
economy and the foundation of our enduring prosperity.  During National
Energy Dominance Month, we recommit to unleashing the full might of
America’s vast natural resources, strengthening our Nation’s energy
independence, and ushering in a new era of American energy dominance.

In less than 2 years since returning to office, my Administration has
transformed the United States into the most dominant energy superpower
in the history of the world.  We have reversed the disastrous oil, gas,
and vehicle regulations that crippled our industries, burdened
consumers, and stifled economic growth, including rescinding the
Obama-era Endangerment Finding this past February — a ruling that handed
Washington bureaucrats sweeping power to over regulate our vehicles and
power plants in the name of climate alarmism.  Its repeal stands as the
single largest deregulatory action in American history. 

We have unleashed millions of acres of Federal land for oil, gas, and
coal production, approving over 6,000 drilling permits — more than in
any year in over a decade.  We have also committed over $1 billion to
the revitalization of America’s beautiful, clean coal industry; saved
over 100 coal plants; ended the freeze on new natural gas export
terminals; secured historic investments in nuclear energy; ended the
Green New Scam; and withdrawn from the restrictive international climate
agreements crafted by globalist politicians to plunder our wealth and
undermine our sovereignty.  We are also restoring the reliable,
affordable power America needs to lead the world in manufacturing, super
intelligence, and advanced technology; cutting the red tape that has
delayed critical generation and grid infrastructure; strengthening our
electric system; and ensuring that American workers and businesses have
the abundant power needed to build, innovate, and compete — without
depending on foreign adversaries or unreliable energy schemes.

Last month, my Administration took a historic step in achieving
America’s full energy potential by securing the largest oil deal in all
of human history.  This landmark agreement gives our Nation majority
control of more than 65 billion barrels of proven oil reserves in
Venezuela — vastly increasing America’s oil supply, asserting our
unrivaled power in the Western Hemisphere, and securing our energy
dominance for the next century and beyond.

While this work is far from finished, our energy industry and
hardworking families are reaping the benefits of these tremendous
actions.  America achieved record domestic oil and gas production along
with record levels of liquefied natural gas exports in 2025, and 2026 is
on pace to shatter those records once again.  Under my leadership,
American energy is roaring back to life with new pipelines, export
terminals, power plants, and nuclear reactors set to come online across
the country, creating thousands of new high-paying jobs for American
workers in the years ahead.  As President, I will continue fighting to
drive down the cost of the energy that fills our tanks and powers our
homes, and I will not rest until the full benefits of American
prosperity are felt in every home across our land.  On March 4, 2026, I
arranged for the Ratepayer Protection Pledge, addressing electric power
affordability for American households and businesses.

This month, we pledge to continue to chart a bold new course for
American energy — harnessing the extraordinary abundance beneath our
soil and securing a future of strength, prosperity, and independence. 
Our Nation’s energy renaissance is only just beginning, and the best is
still yet to come.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim October 2026 as
National Energy Dominance Month. IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set
my hand this seventh day of October, in the year of our Lord
two thousand twenty-six, and of the Independence of the United States
of America the two hundred and fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
