By the President of the United States of America

A Proclamation

Across our Nation’s harbors and fishing communities, the unrivaled skill
and resolve of American fishermen and seafood producers turn the bounty
of our waters into food for our families and enduring economic
strength.  Food security is national security — we recognize that our
fisheries are a vital pillar of American independence.  This National
Seafood Month, I join the American people in celebrating these
hardworking men and women and reaffirm my commitment to helping them
compete, prosper, and lead the world in seafood production.

From the fishing ports of early America to today’s seafood farms and
processing plants, this great industry has rewarded enterprise,
sustained family businesses, and helped forge the unmatched spirit of
determination that defines our maritime workforce.  For generations, our
fishermen have braved rough seas and punishing storms, spending long
days far from home to earn an honest living and provide for the people
they love.  Across our land, the incredible American seafood industry
harvests nearly 8.5 billion pounds of world-class food annually,
sustains more than a million jobs, and fuels more than $173 billion in
commerce, bringing wealth and opportunity to communities across our
great Nation.  And now, as we usher in a new Golden Age of seafood
production, we are ensuring our fishing communities remain places where
the next generation can raise a family, build a business, and pursue
their own share of the American Dream.

Since returning to office, I have taken decisive action to restore
American seafood competitiveness and give our fishing communities
greater freedom to grow and succeed.  Last year, I signed an Executive
Order to restore American seafood competitiveness, and my Administration
recently launched the Department of Agriculture Office of Seafood.  I
have also signed three proclamations to restore commercial fishing
access to nearly 5,000 square miles in the Atlantic Ocean and to
hundreds of thousands of square miles in the Pacific, enabling American
vessels to work additional fishing grounds and compete more effectively
in the global seafood market.  Under my leadership, we delivered the
largest single-year quota increase in the history of the United States
Atlantic bluefin tuna fishery, secured a massive 1,200 percent quota
increase for Atlantic mackerel, and revived the West Coast salmon season
to yield the first full commercial fishery in 3 years.  My
Administration was also proud to allocate $123.6 million in fishery
disaster assistance to help communities recover from devastating losses,
strengthen shoreside infrastructure, and support job retraining to help
workers get back on their feet.  We will continue slashing burdensome
regulations so our fishermen can reinvest in their fleets, expand their
operations, and keep more of what they earn.  Through these historic
efforts, we are putting American fishermen first and fighting to make
our Nation the strongest, most successful seafood producer in the world.

As our Nation celebrates National Seafood Month, we give thanks to the
families whose skill and sacrifice have built and sustained this
essential American industry for over 250 years.  We will stand with them
as we build a future of thriving ports, successful businesses, and an
American seafood industry that is stronger, more competitive, and more
prosperous than ever before.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim October 2026 as
National Seafood Month.  I call upon all Americans to express their
thanks for the American citizens who sustain this crucial industry, and
to mark this month with appropriate events and ceremonies.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this twenty-second day
of October, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
