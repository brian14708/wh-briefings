BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

A PROCLAMATION

For 250 years, American workers have been the driving force behind our
Nation’s strength, prosperity, and industrial might.  Their skill,
ingenuity, and determination have fueled our economy and carried
American manufacturing to new heights.  On National Manufacturing Day,
we honor the men and women who make, build, and create in America — and
we celebrate the extraordinary comeback of American manufacturing.

As President, my highest priority has been putting America First by
restoring our Nation’s manufacturing and bringing back good-paying
American jobs.  I have imposed powerful tariffs to protect American
workers and businesses from unfair trade practices, and as a result, we
are seeing the greatest reshoring of American manufacturing in our
Nation’s history, alongside a surge in demand for products Made in
America.  Since I returned to office, I have secured more than $11.2
trillion in United States and foreign investments across manufacturing,
critical infrastructure, and technology sectors.  Groundbreakings of new
factories are surging with 112,000 new factory construction workers
building since I returned to office.  The factory construction jobs of
today mean more good-paying manufacturing jobs for decades to come. 
These investments fuel job growth, innovation, and opportunity across
every corner of our country.

I was also proud to sign the Working Families Tax Cuts Act into law —
landmark legislation that makes the Small Business Tax Deduction
permanent and allows American manufacturers to immediately expense 100
percent of their factory, machinery, equipment, and domestic research
and development investments.  At the same time, this historic law is
putting more money back into the pockets of hardworking Americans and
increasing take-home pay, including new provisions like No Tax on
Overtime.  After declining by nearly $850 under the previous
administration, the real wage for the average manufacturing worker has
surged by $2,500 annually since I took office.  These efforts are
driving a revival of American manufacturing and making our country the
best place in the world to build, produce, and invest.

After years of failed policies that hollowed out our industrial base and
left American workers behind, manufacturing is once again powering
America’s economic resurgence.  After years of decline, manufacturing
employment is rising once again with 72,000 new manufacturing jobs added
in 2026 so far.  Today, we honor the generations of Americans who have
built our country through their hard work, and we look proudly to the
next generation, who will carry on our great tradition of American
innovation and enterprise.  Together, we will make American
manufacturing bigger, better, and stronger than ever before.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim October 2, 2026, as
National Manufacturing Day.  I call upon all Americans to celebrate the
entrepreneurs, innovators, and workers in manufacturing who are making
our communities strong.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this second day of
October, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
