BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

A PROCLAMATION

For over 250 years, the liberties and freedoms of every American have
been safeguarded by the heroic men and women who have faithfully served
in the United States Armed Forces.  This Veterans Day, we honor our
Nation’s cherished veterans and recommit to strengthen the support and
resources they have so rightly earned.

From the battle for our independence during the Revolutionary War and
the fight for the future of humanity in World War II to the ongoing
battles to defend America’s security and protect her people, the
countless veterans who have taken up arms have ensured that generations
of Americans could enjoy the many blessings of our great Nation.  These
selfless men and women have committed their lives to defending our
families, our freedom, and our great American values.  We will never
forget the price they have paid for our homeland.

As they sacrificed for our freedom, so too must our Nation ensure their
welfare.  At my direction, the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is
restructuring to put veterans at the forefront of every decision.  The
VA processed a record 3.1 million disability claims this year.  In my
second term, we have also slashed the VA’s claims backlog by more than
70 percent and opened 47 new health care facilities, expanding access to
world-class care for veterans in communities across our country.

Every American owes our veterans an eternal debt of gratitude, and no
one who has worn our Nation’s uniform should ever be homeless.  That is
why I will never stop fighting until every veteran has the housing and
mental health support and treatment they need.  Additionally, I
established the National Center for Warrior Independence last year with
a goal of housing up to 6,000 homeless veterans by 2028.  Last year
alone, we housed nearly 52,000 homeless veterans, the most in 7 years,
and under my leadership, every veteran who served our Nation in the
Armed Forces will have a place to call home.

My Administration is working tirelessly to provide our veterans with the
resources and support they so dearly deserve.  As President, I have
eliminated burdensome and needless red tape and ensured that every
veteran receives the exceptional care they are entitled to.  With an
Executive Order I signed in September, we are also accelerating
veterans’ access to benefits and employment opportunities through
modernized data management and sharing between the VA and the Department
of War.  So long as I am President, we will continue to relentlessly
expand veterans’ access to health care, education, and job opportunities
and aggressively deliver this life-changing support.

Millions of brave men and women have served in the United States Armed
Forces with heroic determination and sacrifice, safeguarding our
security, prosperity, and sovereignty.  Today, we are united in
gratitude for the courage of our Airmen, Coast Guardsmen, Marines,
Sailors, Soldiers, and Guardians, and we thank the families who have
stood with them in their service to our country.  Through the unmatched
prowess and strength of America’s veterans, the freedoms we cherish, our
glorious American way of life, and the greatest country the world has
ever known will endure for centuries to come.  We will always uphold the
legacy of our veterans and ensure our Nation remains safe, strong, and
secure in the blessings of liberty.

In respectful recognition of the contributions our service members have
made to advance peace and freedom around the world, the Congress has
provided (5 U.S.C. 6103(a)) that November 11 of each year shall be set
aside as a legal public holiday to honor our Nation’s veterans.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, do hereby proclaim November 11, 2026, as Veterans Day.  I
encourage all Americans to reflect on the fearlessness and sacrifice of
our veterans through public ceremonies and private thoughts and prayers
of gratitude.  I call upon Federal, State, and local officials to
display the flag of the United States and to participate in patriotic
activities in their communities.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this tenth day of
November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
