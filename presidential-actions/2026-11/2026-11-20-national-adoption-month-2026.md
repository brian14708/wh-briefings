title: NATIONAL ADOPTION MONTH, 2026
---
 
From the moment a child is conceived, they are a gift from God, bringing
hope, love, and a future filled with promise into the world.  For
countless children across our Nation, adoption provides the gift of a
loving family and nurturing home where they can feel safe, supported,
and encouraged to pursue their own unique American Dream.  This National
Adoption Month, we recognize the parents who devote their lives to
protecting our Nation’s children and youth, and we rededicate ourselves
to supporting those still waiting to find the loving homes they deserve.

Tragically, many children who age out of foster care face long-term
socioeconomic hardships that hold them back from building a stable and
prosperous future.  To combat this issue, my Administration has remained
committed to aligning Federal resources to improve adoption outcomes and
remove unnecessary barriers to permanency.  Last year, I proudly
expanded access to the Adoption Tax Credit and made the Child Tax Credit
permanent through the historic Working Families Tax Cuts Act to ease the
financial challenges of adoption.  With the leadership of our great
First Lady Melania Trump, I also signed the Fostering the Future for
American Children and Families Executive Order, which increases
educational, employment, and mentorship opportunities for youth as they
transition from foster care into adulthood.

As President, I will always ensure that American children transitioning
in and out of foster care have the resources they need for lifelong
independence and success.  To give the next generation the head start
that they deserve, the First Lady also launched Fostering the Future
Accounts, America’s first savings and investment vehicle providing
children in foster care with the opportunity to build long-term
financial stability and expand their prospects for future success.  With
this historic resource, as with Trump Accounts, America’s foster care
system will harness the unparalleled generosity and resources of our
Nation to serve every child.

Building on First Lady Melania Trump’s results-driven leadership, my
Administration will continue to champion new policies that empower
families and communities to lift up our country’s children for
generations to come.  As part of this effort, we proudly launched
FosteringTheFuture.gov to provide youth with accessible, reliable, and
Super Intelligence-enabled support for life after foster care. 
Faith-based organizations, houses of worship, businesses, nonprofits,
and community groups also play a vital role in supporting adoptive
parents and their children, and I encourage all Americans to put their
faith and convictions into action through service, generosity, and
enduring support for adoptive families.

Adoption is a beautiful reminder of what is possible when children are
surrounded by love, safety, and the security to thrive.  This month, we
remember those still awaiting adoption and the prospective parents
preparing to welcome them, understanding that their hopes call us to
purposeful action.  Strong families are sustained by strong communities,
and together, we can cultivate change for others and become a source of
hope and encouragement for our Nation’s children. 

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do

hereby proclaim November 2026 as National Adoption Month.  I call upon
all Americans to honor this month by ensuring that every child has the
love and support they need to live a long, healthy, and prosperous life.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this twentieth day of
November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
