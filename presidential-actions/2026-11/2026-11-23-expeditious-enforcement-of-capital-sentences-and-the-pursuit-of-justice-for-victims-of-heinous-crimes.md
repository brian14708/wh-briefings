Memorandum for THE ATTORNEY GENERAL

SUBJECT:       Expeditious Enforcement of Capital Sentences and the
Pursuit of Justice for Victims of Heinous Crimes

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the
laws of the United States of America, I hereby direct:

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Section</span>
<span style="text-decoration: underline">1</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Background</span>.  A prolonged
delay in carrying out a death sentence is a grave offense to the justice
system that denies a victim’s family the closure they deserve.  Yet
capital sentences imposed upon the most vile criminals are too often
frustrated by years, or even decades, of litigation.  The Department of
Justice must take every step to ensure that those who commit the most
heinous crimes promptly face the full consequences of their actions.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">2</span>.
 <span style="text-decoration: underline">Pursuing Capital Punishment
for Heinous Federal Crimes</span>.  The Attorney General, when
appropriate and consistent with applicable law, shall pursue Federal
jurisdiction and seek the death penalty for particularly heinous and
egregious Federal capital crimes, in addition to those identified in
section 3(b) of Executive Order 14164 of January 20, 2025 (Restoring the
Death Penalty and Protecting Public Safety), to include:

(a)  murder in the first degree of a minor;

(b)  murder committed in the course of rape, sexual abuse, sexual
exploitation of a child, or sex trafficking; and

(c)  murder committed in the course of kidnapping or human trafficking.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">3</span>. 
<span style="text-decoration: underline">Expediting the Enforcement of
Capital Sentences</span>.  The Attorney General shall take all lawful
and appropriate measures to ensure that federally imposed sentences of
death are carried out as expeditiously as possible.  These measures
shall include:

(a)  prioritizing the prompt resolution of direct appeals, petitions for
writs of habeas corpus, and other post-conviction proceedings;

(b)  opposing dilatory litigation tactics;

(c)  seeking expedited judicial consideration wherever appropriate; and

(d)  eliminating unnecessary administrative delays.

<span style="text-decoration: underline">Sec</span>.
<span style="text-decoration: underline">4</span>.
 <span style="text-decoration: underline">General Provisions</span>.
 (a)  Nothing in this memorandum shall be construed to impair or
otherwise affect:

(i)   the authority granted by law to an executive department or agency,
or the head thereof; or

\(ii\)  the functions of the Director of the Office of Management and
Budget relating to budgetary, administrative, or legislative proposals.

\(b\)  This memorandum shall be implemented consistent with applicable
law and subject to the availability of appropriations.

\(c\)  This memorandum is not intended to, and does not, create any
right or benefit, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in
equity by any party against the United States, its departments,
agencies, or entities, its officers, employees, or agents, or any other
person.

(d)  The costs for publication of this memorandum shall be borne by the
Department of Justice.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
