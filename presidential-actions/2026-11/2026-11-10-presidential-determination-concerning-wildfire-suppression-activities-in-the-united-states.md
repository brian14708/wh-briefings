MEMORANDUM FOR THE SECRETARY OF THE INTERIOR, THE SECRETARY OF
AGRICULTURE, THE ADMINISTRATOR OF THE ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

SUBJECT:       Presidential Determination Concerning Wildfire
Suppression Activities in the United States

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the
laws of the United States of America, including section 313 of the
Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended (33 U.S.C. 1323), I
determine that it is in the paramount interest of the United States to
exempt firefighting activities, including the aerial use, deployment,
and discharge of fire retardants, conducted by the United States Forest
Service, the Department of the Interior, and any other executive
departments and agencies (agencies) and their contractors (aspects of
which are the subject of litigation in *Forest Service Employees for
Environmental Ethics v. U.S. Forest Service*, No. CV-22-168-M-DLC (D.
Mont.)) from Federal, State, interstate, and local requirements,
administrative authority, and process and sanctions respecting the
control and abatement of water pollution.  

Therefore, pursuant to subsection (a) of section 313 of the Federal
Water Pollution Control Act, as amended (33 U.S.C.  1323(a)), I hereby
exempt firefighting activities conducted by the United States Forest
Service, the Department of the Interior, and any other agencies and
their contractors, including the aerial use, deployment, and discharge
of fire retardants, from Federal, State, interstate, and local
requirements, administrative authority, and process and sanctions
respecting the control and abatement of water pollution; except that no
exemption is hereby granted from the requirements of 33 U.S.C. 1316 and
1317.

The exemption granted by this memorandum shall be for the 1-year period
beginning November 10, 2026, and ending November 10, 2027.

Nothing herein is intended to:  (a) imply that in the absence of such a
Presidential exemption, the Clean Water Act or any other provision of
law would require the United States Forest Service or other agencies to
obtain permits pertaining to the control and abatement of water
pollution for the exempted operations; or (b) limit the applicability or
enforcement of any other requirement of law applicable to the United
States Forest Service, the Department of the Interior, or any other
agencies and their contractors regarding such agencies’ firefighting
activities.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized and directed to publish this
determination in the *Federal Register*.

                               DONALD J. TRUMP
