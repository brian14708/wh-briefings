BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

A PROCLAMATION

For more than a century, human freedom has been threatened by communist
and socialist ideologies that eliminate individual opportunity,
subjugate individuals to the state, and extinguish the God-given rights
upon which free nations are built.  This Anti-Communism Week, we are
reminded of the devastating consequences of state control over human
thought, creativity, and political expression, resulting in suffering,
poverty, exploitation, violence, and misery for millions, and we renew
our commitment to protect the sacred values of life, liberty, and the
pursuit of happiness from the chains of tyranny.

Our Republic was founded upon the enduring conviction that our rights
are inherent, our liberties are sacred, and our Government derives its
just powers from the consent of the governed.  These principles have
defined our national character for over 250 years and continue to
distinguish our way of life from that of all other countries on Earth.

Today, however, our American experiment faces renewed threats as
socialist, communist, and radical leftist ideologies find increased
expression in our Nation.  We must remain vigilant in defending the
principles that sustain our Republic and ensure the lessons of history
are never forgotten nor repeated.  Under my leadership, America will
never fall victim to these evil ideologies, and the United States will
never become a communist country.

The story of America is, above all, a story of a Nation uniting as one,
standing up to tyranny, and charting its own destiny as a free and
self-governing Republic.  As President, I will always protect the
founding principles for which so many fought and died.  Together, we
will defend our Constitution, which protects our freedoms; uphold the
dignity of every individual; strengthen the free market, which has made
us the most prosperous country ever to exist; and ensure that the
promise of America remains alive and well for generations to come.

This week, we honor the victims of socialism and communism by
remembering their lives, defending the freedoms that were denied,
renewing our resolve to safeguard the Republic entrusted to us, and
reminding ourselves that what we have created as a Nation stands as the
greatest testament to human freedom in the history of the world.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim the week of November 1
through November 7 as Anti-Communism Week.  I call upon all Americans to
stand firm in their defense against any threat to the American way of
life and to protect our burning flame of liberty from the darkness of
communism. 

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this third day of
November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
