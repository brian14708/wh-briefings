NOMINATIONS SENT TO THE SENATE:

Nathan Anonick, of Virginia, to be a Commissioner of the Commodity
Futures Trading Commission for a term expiring June 19, 2031, vice
Rostin Behnam, term expired.

Kristin N. Johnson, of Michigan, to be a Member of the Securities and
Exchange Commission for a term expiring June 5, 2029, vice Caroline A.
Crenshaw, term expired.

Steven Levine, of Maryland, to be a Member of the Securities and
Exchange Commission for a term expiring June 5, 2030, vice Hester Maria
Peirce, term expired.

Matthew Rowland, of the District of Columbia, to be a Commissioner of
the Commodity Futures Trading Commission for the remainder of the term
expiring April 13, 2028, vice Summer K. Mersinger.
