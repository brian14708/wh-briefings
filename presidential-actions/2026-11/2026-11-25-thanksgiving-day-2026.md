As families and friends gather across our country to celebrate
Thanksgiving in this 250th anniversary year of American Independence, we
pledge to carry forward the spirit of faith and fellowship that marked
the first Thanksgiving feast, and we renew our mission to continue
ushering in the Golden Age of America so that every citizen has the
opportunity to achieve their American Dream. 

More than four centuries ago, 102 passengers set sail aboard the
*Mayflower* on a daring voyage across the Atlantic Ocean that would
forever change the course of humanity.  Fleeing religious persecution
and seeking the promise of freedom found in the New World, these brave
Pilgrims endured a treacherous journey and reached the shores of New
England in November of 1620.  A brutal winter followed, bringing
unimaginable hardship and claiming the lives of nearly half of the souls
who had made the crossing.  Sustained only by their unfailing faith in
Jesus Christ and the bonds they formed in caring for one another, this
small community persisted with remarkable strength of spirit and laid
the foundations for our civilization — forging a friendship with the
Wampanoag people, learning to cultivate the land, and gathering a
successful harvest in the following autumn.  Filled with gratitude to
our Lord for these magnificent blessings, the Pilgrims joined the
Wampanoag in a celebration that became the first Thanksgiving.

The faith and gratitude that sustained our forebears still remain at the
heart of our Thanksgiving traditions and national character.  In every
generation since, these enduring values have inspired proud citizens to
persevere through adversity, build a land of opportunity, and pass the
blessings of liberty to their children — making our Nation stronger, our
future brighter, and the promise of America greater than ever before.

On this blessed day, we celebrate the glorious land that has allowed all
people to worship freely and openly since its founding, and we remember
that this solemn conviction anchors our national identity:  America is —
and always will be — one Nation under the eternal providence and
goodness of God.  Through every trial and triumph, our trust in Him has
strengthened our people and inspired us to carry the flame of liberty
forward.

As we gather with our loved ones to celebrate the innumerable blessings
of our Republic, we also pay special tribute to the brave men and women
of our Armed Forces whose commitment to duty draws them far from home
and family.  Their courage and sacrifice secure the way of life we
cherish, and we salute them for their unwavering dedication to defending
our Nation and keeping the American People safe throughout this holiday
season.

This Thanksgiving, we uphold a beloved American tradition older than our
Nation itself.  May we remain steadfast in our mission to restore
American greatness, faithful in our devotion to Almighty God, and united
in our resolve to preserve His blessings of freedom and prosperity for
centuries to come.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim Thursday, November 26,
2026, as a National Day of Thanksgiving.  I encourage all Americans to
gather in homes and places of worship and offer a prayer of thanks to
God for our many blessings. 

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this twenty-fourth day
of November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.                             

DONALD J. TRUMP
