BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

A PROCLAMATION

It is America’s destiny to explore and expand our frontiers beyond Earth
to the farthest depths of space.  Today, my Administration reaffirms
that our solar system and all of outer space beyond Earth are the
province of all humans, and that our Nation will lead the way in the
exploration, development, use, and settlement of these new frontiers —
forging an interplanetary civilization founded on the enduring and
inalienable rights of freedom and individual liberty.  

From the intrepid Apollo 11 astronauts who took humanity’s first steps
on the Moon to the pioneering rovers that explored the surface of Mars,
the United States has always blazed a trail into the great unknown,
shattering boundaries and pushing mankind to extraordinary new heights. 
Behind each triumph are generations of astronauts, scientists,
engineers, and workers whose tireless dedication and unmatched ingenuity
turn impossible dreams into reality. 

Under my leadership, the United States is once again writing a bold new
chapter in space exploration.  This past April, the Artemis II mission
successfully carried astronauts around the Moon and farther from Earth
than any human had ever traveled before.  This historic voyage is only
the beginning.  Next summer, Artemis III will undertake one of the most
complex and consequential test flight campaigns in the history of our
Nation’s space program.

This proud legacy of innovation is our sacred inheritance, and our
Nation bears the solemn responsibility to advance it by continuing to
open new horizons of exploration and discovery.  Through the landmark
Executive Orders and directives I have signed, we are reinvigorating
human spaceflight, unleashing a competitive and thriving commercial
space industry, and ensuring American space superiority for all
eternity.  We will return to the Moon by 2028, lay the foundation for a
permanent American lunar presence, and hasten the day when an American
becomes the first human to set foot on the red dunes of Mars.  Together,
we will build the facilities, develop the resources, and establish a
permanent presence in space to extend the reach of American exploration,
preserve our frontier spirit, and strengthen our national security for
centuries to come.  The nation that leads in space will shape the future
of humanity — and that nation will be the United States of America. 

Today, we renew our solemn and righteous mission to propel humanity
toward the farthest reaches of the universe.  We reaffirm that the
United States will lead the way in this bold endeavor, for the nation
with the best scientific understanding of space, the most advanced space
economy, and the greatest spacefaring citizens, will be the most
prosperous nation for centuries to come.  Above all, we pledge to carry
our sacred values of faith and freedom wherever we journey, so that
freedom may forever ring among the stars and beyond.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, DONALD J. TRUMP, President of the United States of
America, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and
the laws of the United States, do hereby proclaim that the solar system
and all of outer space beyond Earth are the province of all humans and
are open to exploration, development, use, and the establishment of a
permanent presence by American citizens consistent with United States
law and the international obligations of the United States.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand this third day of
November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, and of the
Independence of the United States of America the two hundred and
fifty-first.

                             DONALD J. TRUMP
