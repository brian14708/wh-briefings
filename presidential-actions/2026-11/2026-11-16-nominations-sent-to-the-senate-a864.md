NOMINATIONS SENT TO THE SENATE:

Kara Frederick, of California, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Plurinational
State of Bolivia.

Eric Matthew Ueland, of Virginia, to be Comptroller General of the
United States for a term of fifteen years, vice Eugene Louis Dodaro,
term expired.

T. Elliot Gaiser, of Ohio, to be United States District Judge for the
Northern District of Texas, vice Jane J. Boyle, retired.

William Grayson Lambert, of South Carolina, to be United States District
Judge for the District of South Carolina, vice Timothy M. Cain, retired.

James P. Sullivan, of Texas, to be United States District Judge for the
Western District of Texas, vice Alan D. Albright, resigned.
