NOMINATIONS SENT TO THE SENATE:

Mark Aesch, of Florida, to be Federal Transit Administrator, vice Marcus
Molinaro.

Mikhail Bogachek, of Florida, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Republic of
Kosovo.

Andreas Borgeas, of California, to be a Member of the Foreign Claims
Settlement Commission of the United States for the term expiring
September 30, 2028, vice Sylvia M. Becker, term expired.

John Burns, of South Carolina, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Democratic
Republic of the Congo.

Pedro Cabrera, of Florida, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Republic of
Guatemala.

Hugo Dante Jr., of Alabama, to be Director, Office of Financial
Research, Department of the Treasury, for a term of six years, vice Dino
Falaschetti.

Julia Diaz-Rex, of Puerto Rico, to be United States Attorney for the
District of Puerto Rico for the term of four years, vice W. Stephen
Muldrow, resigned.

Peter A. Feldman, of the District of Columbia, to be Chairman of the
Consumer Product Safety Commission, vice Alexander Hoehn-Saric,
resigned.

Peter A. Feldman, of the District of Columbia, to be a Commissioner of
the Consumer Product Safety Commission for a term of seven years from
October 27, 2026.  (Reappointment)

Rebekah Jurata, of California, to be an Assistant Secretary of the
Treasury, vice Luke Pettit, resigned.

Matthew Millhollin, of Tennessee, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Sultanate of
Oman.

William Stephens, of Virginia, a Career Member of the Senior Executive
Service, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the
United States of America to the Independent State of Papua New Guinea,
and to serve concurrently and without additional compensation as
Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the United States of
America to the Solomon Islands and Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Republic of
Vanuatu.

Joseph Weirsky, of Virginia, to be Director of the National
Counterterrorism Center, Office of the Director of National
Intelligence, vice Joseph Kent, resigned.

Daniel Zimmerman, of North Carolina, to be an Assistant Secretary of
Defense.  (New Position)

Bernard Michael Desrosiers, of the  District of Columbia, to be an
Associate Judge of the Superior  Court of the District of Columbia for
the term of fifteen years, vice Hiram E. Puig-Lugo, retired.

James Cody Hiland, of Arkansas, to be United States Circuit Judge for
the Eighth Circuit, vice Bobby E. Shepherd, retiring.

WITHDRAWALS SENT TO THE SENATE:

 Lee Philip Rudofsky, of Arkansas, to be United States Circuit Judge for
the Eighth Circuit, vice Bobby E. Shepherd, retiring, which was sent to
the Senate on September 14, 2026.
