NOMINATIONS SENT TO THE SENATE:

Carl Bednarski, of Michigan, to be a Member of the Farm Credit
Administration Board, Farm Credit Administration, for a term expiring
May 21, 2034.

John Grunewald II, of Oklahoma, to be a Member of the Farm Credit
Administration Board, Farm Credit Administration, for a term expiring
May 21, 2032.

James Russell Middleton, of Virginia, to be a Member of the Farm Credit
Administration Board, Farm Credit Administration, for a term expiring
October 13, 2030.

John Coale, of Maryland, to be Special Presidential Envoy for Hostage
Affairs, with the rank and status of Ambassador.

Dino LaVerghetta, of Virginia, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the State of Kuwait.

William Makaneole, of Hawaii, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to Turkmenistan.

Mark Priebe, of Virginia, to be Inspector General, Department of
Education.

Justin Schwab, of the District of Columbia, to be an Assistant
Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.

Reid McAlister Creswell, of South Carolina, to be United States Marshal
for the District of South Carolina for the term of four years.

James Christopher Edge, of North Carolina, to be United States Marshal
for the Western District of North Carolina for the term of four years.

Christian Ehrhardt, of New York, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and
Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the Republic of
Cameroon.

Gregory Ibach, of Nebraska, to be Under Secretary of Agriculture for
Trade and Foreign Agricultural Affairs.

James Richard Jackson, of Texas, to be United States Marshal for the
Eastern District of Texas for the term of four years.

Scott Krueger, of Indiana, to be United States Marshal for the Northern
District of Indiana for the term of four years.

Robert Steinau, of Maryland, to be Inspector General, National
Aeronautics and Space Administration.

Jeffrey Zisk, of Texas, to be a Commissioner of the Postal Regulatory
Commission for the remainder of the term expiring November 22, 2026.

Jeffrey Zisk, of Texas, to be a Commissioner of the Postal Regulatory
Commission for a term expiring November 22, 2032.
